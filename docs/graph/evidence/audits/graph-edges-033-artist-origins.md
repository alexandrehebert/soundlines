# Artist origin coverage

Reviewed on 2026-11-15. The audit looked for missing direct links between
artists and places already in the catalogue. Candidates came from artist
descriptions, existing genre and scene relationships, and sources documenting
birthplaces, hometowns, or group formation.

The graph gained 106 artist origin edges across 42 places. Detroit went from
three direct artist links to 18. The [coverage tracker](./graph-edge-coverage.csv)
records the review status and confidence for each edge. Its linked evidence
notes record the claim, sources and assessment for each pair.

The place panel labels this relationship “birthplace of,” so its wording
informed the audit. Kevin Saunderson remains linked to Detroit techno and now
has an origin edge to his birthplace, New York City. His teenage move to
Belleville and later role in Detroit techno are documented by the
[Detroit Historical Society](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/saunderson-kevin).
Joy Division's formation in Salford calls for a place at that geographic
precision. Jelly Roll Morton and Abdelkader Chaou need further research because
sources differ on their birthplaces.
