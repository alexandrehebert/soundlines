## Summary

The strongest exact-place claims are the Detroit/Chicago/Berlin labels and venues. Several genre/cultural edges are stronger when interpreted as major local centers rather than exclusive birthplaces: San Juan reggaetón, Seattle grunge, Seoul K-pop, London UK garage and Second Summer of Love, Tuva xöömei, Zanzibar taarab, and Wigan Northern Soul. Source Records–Paris remains unverified and should stay low/pending until a contemporary company record or address is found.
