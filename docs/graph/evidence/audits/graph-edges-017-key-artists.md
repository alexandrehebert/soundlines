## Source notes

- National museums, university libraries, scholarly publications, established music institutions and specialist music journalism were prioritized. AllMusic and music-platform editorial were used as corroboration where institutional genre taxonomies were unavailable.
- Confidence describes support for the graph’s broad “key artist” relation, not a claim that the genre defines the artist’s entire career.
- TimesLIVE supports the caveat that Maphorisa took the genre seriously after being introduced to amapiano, rather than establishing him as an originator.
