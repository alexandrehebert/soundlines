## Summary

Several links are well supported when their scope is kept specific: Moroder/synth-pop, Kraftwerk/synth-pop and electro (the latter through Bambaataa), Marley Marl’s documented influence on Premier and Pete Rock, Cooke’s mentorship of Franklin, Siti binti Saad’s mentorship of Bi Kidude, and jungle’s rhythmic contribution to UK garage. The weakest current claims are synth-pop → electro, Music Institute → Detroit techno as one-way influence, and the broad Second Summer of Love → breakbeat hardcore relation. Their current high ratings should not stand without a more precise relation or stronger direct evidence. The breakbeat edges are supported as rhythmic inputs, not as exclusive genre descent.
