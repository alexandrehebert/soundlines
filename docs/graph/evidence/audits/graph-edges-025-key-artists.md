## Summary

All 25 live pending edges were source-checked. Most central genre connections support high confidence. Lower or qualified confidence is appropriate for broad, hybrid, scene-based or alias-specific claims: New Edition/New Jack Swing, Nine Inch Nails/industrial metal, N.W.A./West Coast rap, Oneohtrix Point Never/vaporwave, Osibisa/highlife, OutKast/alternative rap, Oakenfold/Second Summer of Love, Peggy Gou/house, and Perfume/J-pop. Osibisa is the strongest candidate for eventual retyping/removal if the graph distinguishes highlife influence from direct key-artist status.
