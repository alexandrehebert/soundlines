## Summary

Evidence is robust where edges refer to specific musical practices, movements, or documented artists: breakbeat rhythms in jungle, disco sampling in French house, gamelan’s effect on some minimalists, Hindustani exchanges with particular jazz artists, metal/punk in grunge, and post-punk in Bristol trip-hop. Several historical-period or instrument-family links need relation changes or narrower wording. The strongest corrections are to remove the direct lute → guitar claim, retype Congolese rumba → soukous as derivation, and lower confidence on broad high-rated ambient/downtempo and post-punk/trip-hop edges. Mbalax sources support multiple transnational influences but do not establish equally direct causation for salsa and Congolese rumba.
