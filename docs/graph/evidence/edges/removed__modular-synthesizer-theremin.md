# Removed edge: lineage:modular-synthesizer-theremin

Former graph ID `lineage:modular-synthesizer-theremin`

**Disposition** · Removed (2026-11-13)

The Met’s theremin record and the Bob Moog Foundation timelines establish the instrument’s historical context and Moog’s practical experience with theremins. The Foundation links his transistor experience and a theremin demonstration to his later work with Herbert Deutsch. This supports a connection in Moog’s career and use of general electronics, but does not identify a theremin design feature that directly shaped modular architecture or establish a causal edge to the catalog’s Moog-and-Buchla instrument category. No replacement edge.
