**Finding** · Scholarship says UK hardcore-rave breakbeats drew on hip-hop/electro break sampling, then were accelerated in rave. This supports a rhythmic/production influence from hip-hop, but the genre emerged from a wider mix of house, techno, reggae, sound-system and rave practices. Medium, not a single-parent claim.
