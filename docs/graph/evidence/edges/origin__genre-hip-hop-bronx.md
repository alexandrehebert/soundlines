# Hip-hop → The Bronx

Graph ID `origin:genre-hip-hop-bronx`

**Review status** · Source-checked

**Relationship** · originated_in · medium

**Finding** · The Library of Congress describes hip-hop’s 1973 beginning as commonly associated with a South Bronx party hosted by DJ Kool Herc. PBS’s *History Detectives* presents 1520 Sedgwick Avenue as a claimed birthplace and explicitly frames the exact-site story as something to investigate. The Bronx connection is strong, but a single party/site should not be treated as settled sole origin.

**Sources** · [Library of Congress](https://blogs.loc.gov/music/2023/08/early-hip-hop-at-the-library-of-congress/); [PBS History Detectives](https://www.pbs.org/opb/historydetectives/investigation/birthplace-of-hip-hop/).
