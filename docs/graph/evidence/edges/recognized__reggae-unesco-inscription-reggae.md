## Assessment

The inscription formally recognizes Reggae as practised by the communities named in the nomination. This link does not date the origin of the genre.

UNESCO adds the reggae music of Jamaica to its Representative List. Its decision highlights reggae’s role in social commentary and its reach beyond Jamaica.
