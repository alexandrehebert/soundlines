## Sources checked

- [Yamaha, The invention of the valve](https://www.yamaha.com/en/musical_instrument_guide/trumpet/structure/structure002.html) — Explains how valves let trumpets change the length of their air column and play chromatically.

## Assessment

The target of this ancestry is the valved form, the focus of the trumpet entry’s 1818 chronology anchor. The edge does not imply that the entire trumpet family arose in 1818 or had a single path.
