## Sources checked

- [The Sinaloan Tuba in the Renewal of 21st Century Mexican Regional Music](https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?lng=en&nrm=iso&pid=S0187-73722024000100109&script=sci_arttext&tlng=en) — Identifies the helicon as a precursor and describes Pepper’s modification of a BB-flat helicon to make the first sousaphone.
- [Duke University Musical Instrument Collections, Helicon Tuba in E-Flat](https://sites.duke.edu/dumic/instruments/brass/north-america/helicon-tuba-in-e-flat/) — Documents the circular layout, valves and surviving instrument dated 1862–68.
- [Museum of Making Music, Bb Sousaphone](https://museumofmakingmusic.org/momm_artifact/bb-sousaphone/) — Confirms the commission to Pepper and describes the upright-bell design.

## Assessment

The evidence identifies a direct design adaptation of the helicon’s wearable body for the original upright-bell sousaphone.
