## Sources checked

- [The Met, The Pipa](https://www.metmuseum.org/es/essays/the-pipa) — Identifies the Chinese pipa as ancestral to the Japanese biwa, which preserves aspects of early playing practice.
- [The Met, Female musician with lute](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/44801) — Identifies pipa-to-biwa derivation in a Tang-period comparative object record.

## Assessment

Early pipa is the ancestor, rather than the modern concert instrument. The two Met sources are from one institution.
