## Assessment

The inscription formally recognizes Merengue as practised by the communities named in the nomination. This link does not date the origin of the genre.

UNESCO adds the music and dance of merengue in the Dominican Republic to its Representative List, documenting a tradition passed on at gatherings, celebrations and festivals.
