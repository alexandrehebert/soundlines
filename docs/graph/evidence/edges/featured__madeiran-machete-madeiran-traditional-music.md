## Sources checked

- [Musical Instruments Museum, Braguinha](https://www.mim.be/en/collection-piece/braguinha) — Documents four single strings, Madeiran use and the connection to the Hawaiian ukulele.
- [Madeira Tourism Board, Folklore](https://www.visitmadeira.com/en/what-to-do/culture-passionates/folklore/) — Lists the machete and braguinha among instruments used in regional folklore.
- [Xarabanda, Tangedores do Atlântico](https://xarabanda.pt/tangedores-do-atlantico/) — Describes the braguinha as a central instrument in rural and urban repertoire and in modern arrangements of historical pieces.

## Assessment

The instrument appears in regional folklore and is central to the traditional string repertoire preserved and performed by Tangedores do Atlântico.
