## Sources checked
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, “Ras Shorty I: The Soul of Calypso”](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2016/06/ras-shorty-i-the-soul-of-calypso/) — describes Soca as a revisioning of calypso and recounts early experiments blending calypso with East Indian rhythmic elements.
- [Caribbean Beat, “Soca gold”](https://www.caribbean-beat.com/issue-174/soca-gold-backstory) — describes Soca as an evolution/redefinition of calypso and as a style that updated calypso for Carnival.

## Assessment
The sources describe Soca as developing from and reworking calypso, which supports the child-to-parent direction `genre:soca` → `genre:calypso` and the precise `derived_from` type. The documented East Indian elements in early experiments are not enough to assert a simple two-parent `fusion_of` lineage. Confidence is medium because the genre’s evolution is gradual.
