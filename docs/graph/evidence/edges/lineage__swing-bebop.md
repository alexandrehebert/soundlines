# Swing → Bebop

Graph ID `lineage:swing-bebop`

**Review status** · Source checked (2026-11-05)

**Relationship** · influences · medium

**Graph note** · Bebop arose in the 1940s through jazz musicians’ reworking of swing-era practice into a more experimental idiom. It grew out of the swing-era environment while developing distinct small-group improvisational language.

**Sources checked** · [Oxford Academic, The History of Jazz: “Modern Jazz”](https://academic.oup.com/book/39631/chapter-abstract/339587065) — identifies the rise and musical transformation of 1940s bebop. [Oxford Academic, Bebop: The Music and Its Players: “The Beginnings”](https://academic.oup.com/book/2103/chapter-abstract/142032491) — scholarly account of bebop’s beginnings and swing-era context.

**Assessment** · Oxford’s jazz history dates bebop’s rise to the 1940s and describes it as a radical, experimental transformation of jazz. The relationship is best treated as historical development from the swing-era scene, not a simple one-source derivation.
