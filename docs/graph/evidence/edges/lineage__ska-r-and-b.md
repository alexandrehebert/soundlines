# Rhythm and blues → Ska

Graph ID `lineage:ska-r-and-b`

**Review status** · Source checked (2026-11-05)

**Relationship** · influences · medium

**Graph note** · Jamaican ska emerged as urban musicians adapted North American R&B, in combination with jazz and local Afro-revival rhythms and mento. R&B is a major strand in ska’s formation, not its sole source.

**Sources checked** · [Jamaica Information Service, “Black History in Roots Reggae Music”](https://jis.gov.jm/jamaica/black-history-in-roots-reggae-music/) — R&B, jazz, and Afro-revival context. [Jamaica Information Service, “Jamaica’s Heritage in Music”](https://jis.gov.jm/jamaicas-heritage-music/) — calls ska a Jamaicanized version of North American R&B.

**Assessment** · Jamaica Information Service describes ska’s emergence when urban Jamaican musicians began playing North American R&B, with jazz and Afro-revival rhythms also contributing. Its heritage overview likewise characterizes ska as a Jamaicanized version of North American R&B.
