## Sources checked

- [The Met, The Lute](https://www.metmuseum.org/de/essays/the-lute) — Documents medieval transmission of the Arab oud to Spain and when Western lute forms become distinguishable in the iconography.
- [Cleveland Museum of Art, Oud 1918.350](https://www.clevelandart.org/print/art/1918.350) — Explicitly connects the oud to the evolutionary history of the European lute. Only that branch of the record’s broader ancestry claim is used here.

## Assessment

Historical transmission and explicit descent support the European lute only. The precise origins and routes cannot be reconstructed from the surviving depictions alone.
