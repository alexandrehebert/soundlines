## Evidence review

Trax issued the Frankie Knuckles version of “Your Love” in 1987. The independent account documents the release and Trax lists the artist in its retrospective catalog. The sources establish a release relationship. Confidence remains low for the signing claim because the sources give no terms or direct evidence of a label contract. [Release account](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/Your-Love_Snoman.pdf), [Trax retrospective](https://www.traxrecords.com/40th-anniversary)

## Correction (2026-11-05)

Removed as an inaccurate signing claim. The LOC and Trax sources support a Trax release association, but not a signing; Knuckles said he had nothing to do with Trax Records. Correct signing relationship is now `signed:frankie-knuckles-virgin-records` (1991), supported by The HistoryMakers and Knuckles’s Red Bull Music Academy lecture.
