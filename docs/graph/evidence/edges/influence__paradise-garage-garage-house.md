# Paradise Garage → Garage house

Graph ID `influence:paradise-garage-garage-house`

**Review status** · Source-checked

**Relationship** · influences · high

**Finding** · Carnegie Hall’s house history says New York clubs including the Paradise Garage and Chicago’s Warehouse contributed to house music’s emergence, and specifically describes Frankie Knuckles bringing Paradise Garage club culture and sounds to Chicago. NYC LGBTQ Historic Sites independently documents the Garage and Levan’s role. The evidence supports a direct scene-to-genre influence, while the graph note’s wording “the genre is named after the club” is too simplistic: Carnegie Hall ties the word “house” to both the Warehouse club name and record-store labeling.

**Sources** · [Carnegie Hall, History of House](https://timeline.carnegiehall.org/genres/house); [NYC LGBTQ Historic Sites, Paradise Garage](https://www.nyclgbtsites.org/site/paradise-garage/).
