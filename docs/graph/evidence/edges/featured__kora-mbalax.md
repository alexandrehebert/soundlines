## Assessment

RFI lists the kora among instruments on Éclairer le monde, which revisits mbalax. The ethnographic study Mbalax: Cosmopolitanism in Senegalese Urban Popular Music independently documents its use in a modern group. It is a Mandé instrument with documented use in mbalax.
