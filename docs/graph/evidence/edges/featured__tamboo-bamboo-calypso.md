## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian Folkways, Calypso Awakening liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/SFW40453.pdf) — Identifies tamboo bamboo as a root tradition of calypso and a precursor to steelband.
- [Smithsonian Folkways, Wilmoth Houdini liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/ARH07010.pdf) — Identifies a Jules Sims kalenda recording with tamboo bamboo and situates the practice among the early Carnival song traditions that fed calypso.
- [National Carnival Commission, History of Carnival and its Elements](https://ncctt.org/history-of-carnival-and-its-elements/) — Describes varied bamboo tube lengths, chant accompaniment and the transition to metal containers around 1935.

## Assessment

The association concerns early Carnival song and calypso’s roots. The documented kalenda performance supports a specific use of the instrument with song.
