## Sources checked
- [Cuban Cultural Center of New York, Son](https://cubansinamerica.cubaninstitute.org/cuban-music/son/) — Describes mambo as a descendant of Cuban son and emphasizes the faster forms of the 1940s and 1950s.
- [David F. García, Contesting that Damned Mambo](https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/377/37716112.pdf) — Original research on early Cuban son montuno and mambo, New York’s Palladium Ballroom, and the bands of Machito and Tito Puente.

## Assessment
The mambo drew from Cuban son through the syncopated figures of son montuno. Cuban danzón was also a documented source.
