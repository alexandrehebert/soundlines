## Sources checked

- [Encyclopedia of Peking Opera, Cheng Yanqiu biography (X-Boorman)](https://xboorman.enpchina.eu/biographie/cheng-yanqiu/) — Specialist encyclopedia gives his 1904–1958 dates, says he began working as an actor at 14 (c. 1918), and describes his roles and vocal style.
- [University of Hong Kong CCCH9051 collection, Cheng Yan Qiu](https://learning.hku.hk/ccch9051/group-35/items/show/14) — Student-curated university collection says he began stage work at age 11, records the 1904–1958 lifespan, and identifies his Peking opera occupation; lower authority than the specialist encyclopedia.
- [Heidelberg University Press chapter](https://heiup.uni-heidelberg.de/catalog/view/550/857/87180) — Attempted scholarly corroboration; page returned access denied and was not used for claims.

## Assessment

Replace the unsupported circa-1910 start with 1918, derived from the specialist encyclopedia’s statement that he began acting professionally at age 14 and its 1904 birth date. The HKU collection gives a conflicting stage age of 11, so this graph marker represents professional career onset, not first stage appearance or training. 1958 death is consistently reported. Keep “actor” as the role; sources describe him as a Peking opera actor/singer.

## Remaining uncertainty

The existing Hong Kong Chinese Opera Festival PDF citation now redirects to the festival homepage and could not be re-opened. HKU’s age-11 stage claim conflicts with the specialist encyclopedia’s age-14 professional start; clarify the distinction rather than treating them as the same milestone.
