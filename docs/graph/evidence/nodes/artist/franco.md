## Sources checked

- [Franco biography, Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/literature-and-arts/music-popular-and-jazz-biographies/franco) — Chronology distinguishes first band work (1950), solo debut (1953), and O.K. Jazz (1956).
- [Franco biography, AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/franco-mn0000159406/biography) — Confirms O.K. Jazz co-founding in 1956 and long career ending in 1989.
- [Radio Okapi retrospective](https://www.radiookapi.net/culture/2009/10/12/musique-franco-luambo-20-ans-deja) — Congolese broadcaster dates O.K. Jazz founding to 6 June 1956.

## Assessment

Kept 1950 as the professional-career start (not the better-known 1956 band launch); made the start precise to a year. 1989 is the documented death year.

## Remaining uncertainty

The earliest professional-band date comes from a biographical reference; surviving contemporary discography is needed to independently verify the 1950 event.
