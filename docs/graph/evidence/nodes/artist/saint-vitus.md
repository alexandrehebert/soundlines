## Sources checked

- [Saint Vitus official history](https://www.saintvitusband.com/history-2/) — Band history dates its formation as Tyrant to 1979 and notes later releases through 2019, contradicting the old 2010 endpoint.
- [Louder Sound interview/history](https://www.loudersound.com/features/saint-vitus-doom-metal-band-interview-2012) — Describes formation in 1978 as Tyrant and a name change to Saint Vitus in 1980; documents the 1996 split and 2003 reunion.
- [Encyclopaedia Metallum band entry](https://www.metal-archives.com/bands/Tyrant/83992) — Lists Tyrant activity before the Saint Vitus name and later reunions; secondary discography cross-check.

## Assessment

Corrected the unsupported 1970s decade marker to 1979, the official band history’s formation year. Removed the 2010 end date: the band reunited and released a studio album in 2019; current evidence does not support a definitive later end.
