## Sources checked

- [Historical Dictionary of Nigeria](https://www.elibrarynigeria.com.ng/files/books/0.771725001604240432HistoricalDictionaryofNigeriabyToyinFalolaAnnGenovaMatthewM.Heaton%28z-lib.org%29.pdf) — Reference work says Obey began in Lagos bands in the 1950s and formed International Brothers in 1964.
- [Agidigbo journal article](https://doi.org/10.53982/agidigbo.2024.1202.28) — Scholarly article says his career began in the mid-1950s in Fatai Rolling Dollar’s band.
- [Lagos State University of Education journal article](https://journals.lasued.edu.ng/index.php/OLIJHE/article/download/21/19/82) — Article also places his professional career in the mid-1950s.

## Assessment

The previous circa-1950 was too early/unspecific. Two scholarly sources and a historical dictionary converge on professional work in the mid-1950s; his own group is a later 1964 milestone. Use circa 1955 rather than falsely precise year.

## Remaining uncertainty

Biographical summaries do not establish the exact first year; circa 1955 reflects the repeated “mid-1950s” description.
