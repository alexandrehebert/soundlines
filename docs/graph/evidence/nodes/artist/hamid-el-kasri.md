## Sources checked

- [Aga Khan Trust for Culture: Hamid El Kasri](https://the.akdn/en/en/how-we-work/our-agencies/aga-khan-trust-culture/akma/hamid-el-kasri) — Institutional artist profile identifies him as Gnawa master, singer and guembri player; documents artistic practice and collaborations.
- [Festival Gnaoua official artist biography](https://www.festival-gnaoua.net/en/artist/maalem-hamid-el-kasri/) — Festival biography dates birth to 1961, training from age seven and the 2004 Joe Zawinul collaboration.
- [Le Matin: Essaouira Gnaoua festival, 2004](https://lematin.ma/journal/2004/Festival-Gnaoua-et-musiques-du-monde-d-Essaouira-du-24-au-27-juin-2004/41285.html) — Contemporary Moroccan newspaper confirms Hamid El Kasri was on the 2004 program.
- [SNRT News: El Kasri on Essaouira Festival](https://snrtnews.com/fr/article/hamid-el-kasri-le-festival-dessaouira-a-propulse-le-gnaoua-sur-la-scene-internationale) — Moroccan public broadcaster describes the 2004 collaboration as a memorable turning point for the festival.

## Assessment

Confirmed that 2004 is a documented performance/visibility marker, not a career start: his training began in childhood and he is described as a longstanding master. Kept the graph’s explicit caveat and replaced Wikipedia with institutional and Moroccan sources.

## Remaining uncertainty

The first public performance and start of professional career are not established; do not interpret the timeline date as either.
