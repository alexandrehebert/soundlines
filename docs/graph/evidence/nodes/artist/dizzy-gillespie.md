## Sources checked

- [National Endowment for the Arts, Dizzy Gillespie](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/john-birks-dizzy-gillespie) — Jazz Masters biography confirms his earliest professional work and death in 1993.
- [Smithsonian Institution, Charismic Productions Records of Dizzy Gillespie](https://www.si.edu/object/archives/sova-nmah-ac-0979) — Archival biography says he joined his first band in Philadelphia in 1935.
- [Library of Congress Vanderbilt collection guide](https://collections.library.vanderbilt.edu/repositories/2/resources/2009) — Collection biography independently confirms the 1935 first-band milestone.

## Assessment

The 1935 start is supported by archival biographies documenting his first band; the 1993 end is confirmed by NEA. This is a professional activity span, not the date bebop emerged. Confidence: high.
