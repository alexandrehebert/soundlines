## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/p2930/biography) — Cites early recordings under Co-Pilot and Shabba Ranks; documents his late-1980s rise.
- [Contemporary Musicians biography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/ranks-shabba) — Says he recorded “Heat under Sufferers Feet” as Co-Pilot in 1979, later released as a single.
- [GRAMMY, 1985 in reggae](https://www.grammy.com/news/how-1985-changed-reggae-forever-grammy-awards) — Contextualizes his crossover era and the international breakthrough of reggae and dancehall artists.

## Assessment

Corrected the imprecise 1980 start to 1979, the reported recording year of his earliest song, while noting that single release dates are variously given as 1980 or 1985. Removed the 2000 end date: checked sources do not establish that his career ended then.

## Remaining uncertainty

Early title release dates conflict; 1979 is the recording date, not a securely documented commercial issue date.
