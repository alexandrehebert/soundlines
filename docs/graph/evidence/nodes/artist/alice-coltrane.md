## Sources checked

- [The Washington Post — Alice Coltrane; Musician, Spiritual Guru](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2007/01/15/alice-coltrane/79c5b5df-824f-4271-ae86-e500d784ca57/) — Obituary documents her 1966 entry into John Coltrane’s quintet, solo sound and instrumentation, 1978 turn toward full-time spiritual work, and continued devotional music.
- [Los Angeles Times — Alice Coltrane, 69](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2007-jan-14-me-coltrane14-story.html) — Obituary records early-1960s jazz work, late-1960s solo albums, a 26-year gap before 2004’s Translinear Light, and her 2007 death.
- [San Francisco International Airport Museum — Alice Coltrane: Music as Transformation](https://www.sfomuseum.org/sites/default/files/2025-05/Women-Afrofuturism-Extended-Labels-Literature-Recording-Artists.pdf) — Museum exhibition text identifies her 1937–2007 life and work as pianist, organist, harpist, and singer; contextualizes her music’s spiritual dimension.

## Assessment

The 1960s start and 2007 end are supported, but the cited AllMusic source was only a genre page. Replaced that weak sole citation with two contemporaneous obituaries and a museum interpretation. The 1978 change in public jazz activity is not an end to music-making: devotional recording and a later jazz return continued.
