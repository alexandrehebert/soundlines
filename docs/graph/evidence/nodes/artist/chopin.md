## Sources checked

- [Polish Music Information Centre (POLMIC), Chopin chronology](https://polmic.pl/en/encyclopedia/subject-entries/c/chopin-fryderyk-en) — Specialist Polish music encyclopedia dates his first composition and first printed work to 1817, first charity-concert performance to 1818, and provides the 1810 birth year.
- [chopin.pl, Biography of Frédéric Chopin](https://www.chopin.pl/biography_chopin.en.html) — Detailed biography reports his first polonaises at age seven, early public charity concerts, and death on 17 October 1849.

## Assessment

Keep the 1817–1849 period: POLMIC supports 1817 as the year of his first composition/printed work, while chopin.pl distinguishes that from his first performance and documents his death in 1849. Replace the Wikipedia citation with specialist and dedicated Chopin biography sources. The entry has no phase list to revise; the note records that 1817 is an early composition/publication marker, not first performance.

## Remaining uncertainty

The date marker represents his earliest documented composition/publication, while performance activity began by 1818; these are separate milestones.
