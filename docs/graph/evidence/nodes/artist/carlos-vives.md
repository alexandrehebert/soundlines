## Sources checked

- [Carlos Vives official biography](https://carlosvives.com/biografia/) — Official biography traces more than 30 years of career and dates formation of La Provincia to 1993, but does not clearly detail his 1986 first album in search extract.
- [LA Phil artist biography](https://www.laphil.com/people/carlos-vives) — Performing-arts biography distinguishes acting beginning in 1982 from singing career beginning in 1986.
- [Banco de la República Cultural Encyclopedia](https://enciclopedia.banrepcultural.org/index.php/Carlos_Vives) — Colombian cultural encyclopedia states first pop record was released in 1986 and describes later vallenato work.
- [Associated Press: Clásicos anniversary](https://apnews.com/article/28e75465d79705cdd7716477f26602f6) — Independent news account identifies the 1993 album as breakthrough and describes its vallenato-pop impact.

## Assessment

Changed start from 1980s decade to 1986, the first singing/recording milestone, distinguishing his earlier television acting. Description adds the 1993 breakthrough without treating it as career start.

## Remaining uncertainty

Official artist biography should be directly checked for fuller chronology; 1986 is independently supported by a cultural encyclopedia and performing-arts source.
