# Carlos Gardel

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Oxford Bibliographies in Music: Carlos Gardel](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62400/reference-article-abstract/555412508) — Scholarly reference states early recordings date to 1912, first tango recording to 1917, and death to 1935; notes contested birth account.
- [Buenos Aires city Carlos Gardel Museum discography](https://buenosaires.gob.ar/gcaba_historico/cultura/museos/museocasacarlosgardel/discografia) — Museum discography documents recording activity from 1912 to death, nearly 800 tracks.
- [UNESCO Memory of the World nomination](https://media.unesco.org/sites/default/files/webform/mow001/uruguay_records_carlos_gardel.pdf) — Heritage nomination frames the collection as his artistic career, including early folkloric repertoire and 1916–17 tango-song work.

## Assessment

The 1910s decade start was safe but imprecise; 1912 is an institutional and scholarly documented-recording marker. His landmark tango-song recording in 1917 is a distinct milestone, not the start of his career. 1935 is well supported as the endpoint.

## Remaining uncertainty

Gardel’s birthplace and birth date have historically contested documentation; this review did not alter those biographical details.
