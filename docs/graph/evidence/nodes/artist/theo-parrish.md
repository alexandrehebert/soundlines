## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/theo-parrish-mn0000925188/biography) — Dates first solo release Baby Steps EP to 1996 and identifies the Sound Signature label and DJ profile.
- [Resident Advisor biography](https://ra.co/dj/theoparrish/biography) — Dates his move to Michigan to 1994 and describes his Detroit DJ and production work.
- [Indiana University archives, Detroit techno conference](https://archives.iu.edu/html/VAE6885.html) — Documents his 1994 move to Michigan and emergence in Detroit’s underground scene.

## Assessment

Changed 1997 to 1996, the documented first solo release; Sound Signature’s founding in 1997 is a separate career milestone. He was DJing before either date, so 1996 represents published recorded work.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some bios count 1995’s “Lake Shore Drive” appearance on a Moodymann record as his first recorded credit; 1996 is the first solo EP.
