## Sources checked

- [Death Row Records artist biography](https://deathrowrecords.com/snoop-dogg) — Label biography says his music career began in 1992 after Dr. Dre discovered him and dates Doggystyle to 1993.
- [Snoop Dogg official album page, Doggystyle](https://snoopdogg.com/album/doggystyle/) — Official artist page dates the debut album to November 23, 1993.
- [Library of Congress, The Chronic essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/the-chronic.pdf) — Documents Dre’s introduction of Snoop on The Chronic and the album’s importance to G-funk.

## Assessment

Kept 1992 as the first national exposure/career start, not the debut solo album year. Primary label and artist sources corroborate the sequence; no end date is applicable.
