## Sources checked

- [Peaceville Records: Candlemass](https://peaceville.com/bands/candlemass/) — Label biography dates formation to 1984 and 1986 debut album; describes later reformation.
- [Candlemass official biography](https://candlemass.se/doom/biography/) — Band history emphasizes 1986 breakthrough and ongoing career, including 2019 Grammy nomination/current activity.
- [Napalm Records press biography](https://label.napalmrecords.com/images/bands/candlemass/2022/Candlemass-Sweet_Evil_Sun-Info-EN.pdf) — Label press material confirms continuing activity and significance of 1986/87 albums.
- [AllMusic doom metal style page](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/doom-metal-ma0000004496) — Genre taxonomy is not evidence for the band’s formation date.

## Assessment

Changed the unsupported 1980s-decade start to formation in 1984 and kept the period open because the band continues despite hiatuses. Distinguished formation from the 1986 landmark debut.

## Remaining uncertainty

The current official bio emphasizes the 1986 first album but does not provide a formation date; corroboration comes from label material and other secondary histories.
