## Sources checked

- [Music In Africa: Boom Shaka](https://musicinafrica.net/directory/boom-shaka/) — Regional music information platform dates group emergence and debut single to 1993; identifies members and kwaito association.
- [Mail & Guardian: 30 years of Boom Shaka](https://mg.co.za/friday/2023-03-23-30-years-of-boom-shaka-with-the-mail-guardian/) — South African newspaper history places group on scene in 1993 and identifies lineup.
- [Mail & Guardian: comeback announcement](https://mg.co.za/friday/2015-01-19-the-wait-is-over-boom-shaka-is-making-a-comeback/) — Contemporary reporting describes a return after a long break, so a 2000 endpoint would incorrectly imply permanent dissolution.
- [South African History Online: music timeline](https://sahistory.org.za/article/development-music-south-africa-timeline-1600-2004) — Historical overview places Boom Shaka among influential 1990s kwaito acts.

## Assessment

Changed the decade-level start to the documented 1993 entry/single year and removed the 2000 endpoint because the group later returned and was active around its 30-year anniversary. This period models a group with hiatuses, not uninterrupted activity.

## Remaining uncertainty

Resolve the first single title/date discrepancy: sources variously describe “It’s About Time” versus “Kwere Kwere” as first single. This does not change the 1993 activity marker.
