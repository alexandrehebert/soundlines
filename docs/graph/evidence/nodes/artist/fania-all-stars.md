## Assessment

Kept the well-documented 1968 assembly and revised the former 1997 endpoint to circa 1999, reflecting the reported reunion concert after the mid-1980s core period. This span necessarily compresses a hiatus and revival.

## Remaining uncertainty

The group’s intermittent activity resists a continuous career bar; 1999 marks a documented reunion, not necessarily its final performance.
