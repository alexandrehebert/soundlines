## Sources checked

- [Music Museum of New England Pixies history](https://www.mmone.org/pixies/) — Regional music history dates the band’s formation to 1986 and Surfer Rosa to 1988.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame profile](https://rockhall.com/inductees/pixies/) — Institutional profile documents their influence and active career.
- [Official Pixies discography](https://www.pixiesmusic.com/music/) — Official releases document post-reunion albums and continuing work.

## Assessment

Kept the supported 1986 formation date. The previous description’s 1993–2004 breakup is correct as a hiatus, but should not serve as the graph’s terminal end because Pixies reunited in 2004 and remain active.

## Remaining uncertainty

Formation month and original lineup settling are separate dates; 1986 is the well-supported year.
