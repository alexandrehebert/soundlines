## Sources checked

- [Opéra national de Paris composer biography](https://www.operadeparis.fr/en/artists/claude-debussy) — dates first melodies to 1879, Prix de Rome to 1884, Prélude to 1894, Pelléas premiere to 1902, final masterpieces to 1915, and death to 1918.
- [IRCAM / Centre Pompidou composer biography](https://resources.ircam.fr/fr/composer/claude-debussy/biography) — corroborates Prix de Rome (1884), Faune (1894), Pelléas (1902), La Mer (1905), late works and 1918 death; describes a first period through Pelléas and a second modernist phase.
- [Bibliothèque nationale de France manuscript selection](https://gallica.bnf.fr/selections/fr/html/manuscrits-musicaux-de-claude-debussy) — attempted access to manuscript chronology for early and late works; page returned 403, so not used to support claims.

## Assessment

Changed approximate 1880 start to 1879, supported by Paris Opera’s chronology of Debussy’s first melodies. End 1918 is corroborated by both Paris Opera and IRCAM. Removed Wikipedia. Existing description was accurate but broad; replaced with a concise description tied to documented instrumentation, harmony and compositional evolution. IRCAM’s period framing places a major stylistic transition around the 1902 Pelléas premiere; avoid presenting “Impressionist” as a complete or uncontested genre label.

## Remaining uncertainty

Paris Opera calls 1879 his first melodies, not necessarily start of professional public career. IRCAM biography is marked as information predating 2002 and due for future update; core dates cross-check with the Paris Opera. BNF source page blocked automated retrieval.
