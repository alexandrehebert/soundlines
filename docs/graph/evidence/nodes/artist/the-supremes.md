## Sources checked

- [Motown Museum profile](https://www.motownmuseum.org/artist/supremes/) — Documents the Primettes lineup, Motown-era breakthrough and official 1977 disbandment.
- [Detroit Historical Society, Encyclopedia of Detroit](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/supremes) — Dates formation as the Primettes to 1959, renaming after signing to Motown in 1961, and disbandment in 1977.
- [Victoria and Albert Museum history](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-story-of-the-supremes) — Corroborates their 1959 start and historic chart run.

## Assessment

Kept 1959–1977 but clarified that the start is the Primettes formation, before the Supremes name, and the end is the group’s 1977 disbandment. Career timelines should not transfer Diana Ross’s solo activity to the group.
