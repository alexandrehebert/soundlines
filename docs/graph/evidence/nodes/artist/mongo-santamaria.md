## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture](https://nmaahc.si.edu/latinx/mongo-santamaria) — Museum biography says he began performing at Havana’s Tropicana Club in 1937.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of American History archive](https://www.americanhistory.si.edu/collections/archival-collection/sova-nmah-ac-0893) — Institutional archive documents his professional career and dates him 1917–2003.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mongo-santamaria-mn0000591104/biography) — Music reference corroborates his New York move in 1950, work with Tito Puente, and later bandleading.

## Assessment

The existing 1937 start is valid: Smithsonian documents professional performance at Havana’s Tropicana that year. Confirmed 2003 death year. Added substantive career context and replaced the generic citation.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some references disagree on birth year (1917 vs 1922); this graph period dates his career onset and death, not his lifespan.
