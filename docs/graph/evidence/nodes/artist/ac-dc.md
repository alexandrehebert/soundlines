## Sources checked

- [Sony Music UK — AC/DC](https://www.sonymusic.co.uk/artist/acdc/) — Label biography dates formation to November 1973 and notes classification debates: hard rock/blues rock, sometimes heavy metal, while the band calls its music rock and roll.
- [National Portrait Gallery of Australia — Angus Young](https://www.portrait.gov.au/people/angus-young-1955) — National gallery biography independently places the brothers’ band formation in Sydney in 1973 and dates Bon Scott’s joining to 1974.
- [Associated Press — Original AC/DC drummer Colin Burgess dies](https://apnews.com/article/6881f8f56c82e6d9f51a9727a5141e66) — Contemporaneous obituary reports drummer Colin Burgess was recruited in November 1973 as the band formed.

## Assessment

The existing 1973 start is corroborated by label, national gallery, and AP accounts. Kept the year-level date and sharpened the description to distinguish band self-description from genre cataloguing.
