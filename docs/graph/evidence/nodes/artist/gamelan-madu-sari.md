## Sources checked

- [Gamelan Madu Sari: About](https://www.gamelanmadusari.com/about) — Ensemble history dates its formation to Vancouver’s Expo year, 1986, and records the 2004 debut album.
- [University of Victoria dissertation: Origins, Journeys, Encounters](https://dspace.library.uvic.ca/bitstream/handle/1828/8047/Hartana_Sutrisno_PhD_2017.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=4) — Academic study identifies Kyai Madusari and the Vancouver Community Gamelan Society as a 1986 community gamelan ensemble.

## Assessment

Corrected the start from 2004, which was the first new-music CD, to the ensemble’s documented 1986 formation.

## Remaining uncertainty

The group has changed membership and activity over time; the formation date is clear, but the sources consulted do not establish a continuous yearly performance record.
