## Sources checked

- [Benny Goodman official timeline](https://www.bennygoodman.com/timeline/) — Estate/official timeline dates professional debut to 1921, recording debut with Ben Pollack to 1926, first band to 1934 and Palomar breakthrough to 1935.
- [Benny Goodman official biography](https://www.bennygoodman.com/biography/) — Official biography corroborates career arc and radio-band breakthrough.
- [PBS American Masters profile](https://www.pbs.org/wnet/americanmasters/benny-goodman-about-benny-goodman/615/) — Public broadcasting biography describes 1934 breakthrough and role in swing popularity.
- [National Association of Broadcasters Hall of Fame](https://www.nab.org/hof/inductees/bennyGoodman.asp) — Industry Hall of Fame dates first permanent band to 1934.

## Assessment

Changed start from 1926 recording debut to 1921 professional debut, as the graph models the career rather than discography. Kept death in 1986. Any swing-era prominence phase should begin around 1935, not be inferred from the earlier career start.

## Remaining uncertainty

A Library of Congress or contemporary recording catalog could independently verify the exact 1921 debut; official estate chronology is useful but not independent.
