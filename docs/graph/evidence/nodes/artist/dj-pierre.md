## Sources checked

- [Lifetime Achievement: DJ Pierre and Phuture — Roland Articles](https://articles.roland.com/lifetime-achievement-dj-pierre-and-phuture/) — Interview identifies Phuture co-founders Herb J, Spanky and Pierre and documents cassette circulation and the 1987 pressing.
- [DJ Pierre — Resident Advisor](https://ra.co/dj/djpierre/biography) — Scene biography identifies Pierre as Chicago house DJ, Phuture member and acid-house pioneer; its 1986 memory differs slightly from broader 1985 group-start references.
- [DJ Pierre — Roland Artists](https://www.roland.com/uk/artists/dance_dj_artists/dj-pierre/) — Manufacturer profile confirms Pierre’s Phuture association and TB-303 role; promotional phrasing is not used as sole evidence for invention.
- [The Story of Acid House: As Told by DJ Pierre — Red Bull Music Academy Daily](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2012/12/dj-pierre-interview/) — Long-form interview presents Pierre’s first-person account of Phuture and Acid Tracks, placing it within a collaborative Chicago lineage.

## Assessment

The graph’s Wikipedia-only citation was inadequate. Roland, Resident Advisor and an artist interview support Pierre’s identity and role, but commercial copy that says he alone “invented” acid house overstates the evidence. Start is approximate because accounts distinguish Phuture’s early formation from the TB-303 experimentation.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1985/86 date varies by source and can refer to group formation, equipment experimentation or track development; 1987 is the commercial pressing date.
