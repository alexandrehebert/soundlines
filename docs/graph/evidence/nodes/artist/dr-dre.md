## Sources checked

- [Encyclopedia.com Dr. Dre biography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/literature-and-arts/music-popular-and-jazz-biographies/dr-dre) — Biography dates World Class Wreckin’ Cru formation to 1982 and describes his early DJ career.
- [Universal Music Japan biography](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/dr-dre/biography/) — Label biography confirms music-business start with World Class Wreckin’ Cru before N.W.A. and later G-funk solo work.
- [Library of Congress-hosted scholarly volume](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/21/01/52/97/2021015297/2021015297.pdf) — Academic volume identifies his music career beginning with the Los Angeles electro group.

## Assessment

The prior 1985 start conflated the mid-80s transition toward N.W.A. with Dre’s earlier DJ/group work. The checked reference dates World Class Wreckin’ Cru formation to 1982; 1987 is the later N.W.A. album breakthrough.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some biographies date group formation differently; 1982 marks documented early crew formation, while discography milestones occur later.
