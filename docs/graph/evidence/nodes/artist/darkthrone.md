## Assessment

Replaced the broad decade start with 1986, corroborated by the label and founding member Fenriz’s first-person recollection. This marks the first incarnation as Black Death; the Darkthrone name followed in 1987. Sources challenge any single-genre description: the band moved from thrash/death/doom into black metal and later continued crossing metal styles. The graph has no phase breakdown, so the evidence note records the supported transitions without inventing dated confidence phases.

## Remaining uncertainty

Peaceville is the band’s label and has a promotional interest; Fenriz’s interview independently corroborates formation and stylistic chronology.
