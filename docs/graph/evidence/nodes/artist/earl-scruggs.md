# Earl Scruggs

## Sources checked

- [Bluegrass Music Hall of Fame & Museum](https://www.bluegrasshall.org/inductees/earl-scruggs/) — Institutional biography documents professional radio performance by age 15 (1939) and Blue Grass Boys period 1945–48.
- [National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/honors/heritage/earl-scruggs) — NEA biography dates professional-career decision and joining Monroe to 1945; credits the lineup with establishing bluegrass sound.
- [North Carolina Music Hall of Fame](https://northcarolinamusichalloffame.org/inductee-item/earl-scruggs/) — State hall biography confirms role in developing defining bluegrass banjo sound.

## Assessment

The former kind “group” was incorrect; Earl Scruggs was an individual musician. 1940 was not a grounded start: the museum says he performed professionally on radio by age 15 (1939), while NEA treats 1945 as the start of his professional career with Monroe. To avoid conflating first professional work with breakthrough, set the broad career start to 1939 and describe the 1945 landmark.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1939 milestone refers to professional radio performance; the precise start of paid/local playing is not established here.
