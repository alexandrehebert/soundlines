## Sources checked
- [Detroit Historical Society: Saunderson](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/saunderson-kevin) — the three met at Belleville High and “later became known as the ‘Belleville Three,’ pioneers” of techno.
- [Carnegie Hall timeline](https://timeline.carnegiehall.org/genres/detroit-techno) — photo caption: the Belleville Three performing at the Detroit Masonic Temple, 2017.
- [Pitchfork obituary of Atkins](https://pitchfork.com/story/juan-atkins-co-founder-of-techno-music-dies-at-64/) — Atkins’s membership of the Belleville Three; death in 2026.
- [RBMA lecture: Saunderson](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/kevin-saunderson/) — Metroplex, Transmat and KMS as the trio’s parallel labels.

## Assessment
The trio’s formative period dates from the early 1980s. The 1990 end ignored their 2017 tour as the Belleville Three. The period now ends in 2026 with Atkins’s death, and the description mentions the long gap between collaborations. The 1981 start remains circa.
