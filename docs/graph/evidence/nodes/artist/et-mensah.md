## Sources checked

- [Fondation Daniel Langlois: Ghana’s Highlife Music Collection](https://www.fondation-langlois.org/html/e/page.php?NumPage=2278) — Detailed biography dates Accra Orchestra entry to 1932, The Tempos leadership to 1950, first recording to 1952, and death to 1996.
- [University of Pittsburgh dissertation on Ghanaian highlife](https://d-scholarship.pitt.edu/10261/1/OADosunmuDissertation_ETD_1.pdf) — Academic study places him in Accra Orchestra in youth and analyzes his role in formalizing dance-band highlife.
- [Ghana Hall of Fame profile](https://myghanaonline.com/hall-of-fame.php?country=GH&id=e-t-mensah) — Ghana-focused profile corroborates 1932 ensemble entry, 1950 Tempos leadership and 1996 death.

## Assessment

The former 1940s decade start omitted documented youth ensemble work beginning in 1932; the 1950 leadership of The Tempos is the more meaningful highlife-bandleader milestone. The old end year 1990 was incorrect: he died in 1996.

## Remaining uncertainty

1932 reflects an early youth orchestra role; adult career and highlife influence became prominent later, especially with The Tempos in the 1950s.
