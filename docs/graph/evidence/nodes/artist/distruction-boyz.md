## Assessment

Changed the coarse 2015 decade marker to 2012 year precision based on a direct interview in which the duo dates its start in music production to 2012. Another profile lists “in operation since 2015”; that may describe formal operation/name use, but does not outweigh the members’ account for the start of their musical activity. Anchored the breakthrough and 2017 debut album in contemporaneous South African reporting and Apple’s catalog metadata. Did not assign phases: available evidence gives strong individual milestones but not a stable prominence curve across their temporary solo pauses and reunions. Left the end open because Goldmax explicitly said in 2023 that they had not split, only pursued solo paths temporarily.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact date of formal duo formation remains unsettled: the artists’ 2012 account concerns producing and local performances, while the directory gives a 2015 “in operation since” date. The 2023 status is a temporary pause per Goldmax; later group activity should be checked before asserting current activity or an end year.
