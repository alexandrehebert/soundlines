## Sources checked

- [Bibliothèque nationale de France authority record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb13954498s) — Authoritative identity record confirms 1926–1980 dates, chaâbi work and instruments.
- [Portraits de France, French public information report](https://www.vie-publique.fr/files/rapport/pdf/279007.pdf) — Biography documents pre-emigration performances, 1949 migration and continued café performances in France.
- [Curtin University thesis chapter on Rachid Taha and Ya Rayah](https://espace.curtin.edu.au/bitstream/handle/20.500.11937/33998/196308_196308%20.pdf?sequence=2) — Academic discussion identifies Ya Rayah as written and first recorded in the late 1960s and contextualizes its migration themes.
- [Le Monde: history of Ya Rayah](https://www.lemonde.fr/podcasts/article/2023/03/04/sommeil-musique-complotisme-nos-choix-de-podcasts_6164083_5463015.html) — Historian Naïma Huber-Yahi discusses the song’s Paris creation and migration context.

## Assessment

Kept 1956 as an early recording milestone, not his full career start. Public and academic sources document live performance activity before then and years of work in France. This distinction prevents the period start from erasing earlier performance.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact first-record date is supported here by secondary accounts but not an accessible label archive. The 1956 start should remain a recording anchor, with pre-1956 performances noted.
