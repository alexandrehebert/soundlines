## Sources checked

- [Fonoteca Nacional, Dámaso Pérez Prado timeline](https://fonotecanacional.cultura.gob.mx/escucha/miscelanea-sonora/damaso-perez-prado/linea-del-tiempo) — Documents his early 1940s orchestral work in Havana and the major stages of his career in Mexico.
- [Los Angeles Times, Pérez Prado obituary](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1989-09-16-mn-132-story.html) — Documents Prado’s illness in the late 1980s and summarizes his principal contributions to the mambo.

## Assessment
The dates place his beginnings in Havana in the 1930s and the end of his performing career in the late 1980s.

## Remaining uncertainty
