## Sources checked

- [Hong Kong Academy of Chinese Studies, The Splendid Chinese Culture](https://chiculture.org.hk/en/node/1125) — Gives Shang Xiaoyun’s dates as 1900–1976 and places him among the Four Great Dan; describes his career and style.
- [Palace Museum, Beijing](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/240677.html) — Institutional biography confirms 1900–1976 and lists representative Peking opera works.
- [Beijing government cultural profile](https://en.chinaculture.org/created/2005-12/05/content_76657.htm) — Identifies Shang among the four leading Peking opera Dan performers.

## Assessment

Retained circa 1910 as the approximate start of his stage career; the checked institutional sources establish his life dates and prominence but do not settle an exact debut year. End year 1976 is consistently corroborated.

## Remaining uncertainty

The start date remains approximate and refers to early performance, not birth or a dated professional debut.
