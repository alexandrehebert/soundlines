- [Detroit Historical Society: Mills](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/mills-jeff) — UR founded as a foil to techno’s mainstream appeal.
- [Planet E: Mike Banks](https://planet-e.net/project/mike-banks/) — Mills and Hood left in 1992.
