## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/the-sonics-mn0000428717/biography) — Dates the band’s founding to 1960 and describes its 1963–66 peak and early recordings.
- [Tacoma Music History, Celebrating The Sonics](https://tacomamusichistory.org/2022/08/02/celebrating-the-sonics/) — Local history also dates formation to 1960, while identifying a key single recorded in 1964.
- [Ace Records artist profile](https://www.acerecords.co.uk/features/artist-profiles/the-sonics) — Discographic history traces the original band’s first singles and 1966 split.

## Assessment

Kept 1960 as founding year, not the better-known 1963–66 recording/lineup period. Some local histories date the emergence of the classic lineup later; the note distinguishes those phases.
