## Sources checked

- [Chapel Royal heritage biography](https://www.chapelroyalhamptoncourt.org.uk/music/musical-heritage/) — Identifies Lincoln Cathedral (1563–72) as Byrd’s first known professional employment.
- [Lincoln Cathedral, librarian’s choice](https://lincolncathedral.com/librarians-choice/) — Dates his organist and chorister-master post to 1563–1572.
- [Society for Lincolnshire History & Archaeology](https://slha.org.uk/catalogue_item/byrd-william-composer-organist-3) — Notes the appointment as the earliest securely documented fact in his career and dates his death to 1623.

## Assessment

Changed the unsupported circa-1555 onset to 1563, the earliest known professional appointment. Byrd may have sung as a chorister earlier, but the sources do not establish a career start in 1555. End 1623 is death year.

## Remaining uncertainty

Birth and early chorister dates remain debated; 1563 is the earliest secure professional milestone.
