## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/p5727/biography) — Dates the group’s formation under the Uncle Tupelo name to 1987 and describes its breakup after Anodyne.
- [Los Angeles Times, 1996 retrospective](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1996-03-17-ca-47880-story.html) — Contemporaneous report says the band broke up in 1994 and traces Wilco and Son Volt from the split.
- [Encyclopedia.com biography](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/uncle-tupelo) — Discography/history cross-check for the band’s original run.

## Assessment

Kept 1987–1994; AllMusic supports formation as Uncle Tupelo in 1987 and the Los Angeles Times independently documents the 1994 breakup. Earlier precursor band The Primitives should not be counted as the same named act.
