## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress — National Recording Registry essay: Manteca](https://lcweb2.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/Manteca.pdf) — Registry essay documents the fall 1947 collaboration among Dizzy Gillespie, Chano Pozo and Gil Fuller and the tune's Afro-Cuban jazz significance.
- [Library of Congress — Registry entry](https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/registry-by-induction-years/2004/) — Registry description confirms Pozo co-composed Manteca with Gillespie and that the work was recorded for RCA Victor.
- [Dizzy Gillespie discography — JazzDisco](https://www.jazzdisco.org/dizzy-gillespie/discography/) — Discography lists the RCA Victor release Manteca / Cool Breeze and the big-band recording context.

## Assessment

1947 is supported for the original recording and performance period. The graph's Dizzy Gillespie attribution is right but understates Chano Pozo's contribution; the revised description credits his prominent conga performance and co-authorship context. Afro-Cuban jazz is more precise than generic jazz.

## Remaining uncertainty

The original recording session is dated December 30, 1947 in specialist discographies; the Library of Congress essay gives the broader fall 1947 period.
