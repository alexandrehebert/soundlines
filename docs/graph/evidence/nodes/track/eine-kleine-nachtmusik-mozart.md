## Sources checked

- [International Mozarteum Foundation — Köchel catalog K. 525](https://kv.mozarteum.at/en/work/eine-kleine-nachtmusik-a-little-night-music-6049) — Scholarly catalog dates the entry to Vienna, August 10, 1787, gives its instrumentation and status, and explains the later Serenade designation and lost movement.
- [Morgan Library & Museum — A Little Night Music](https://www.themorgan.org/exhibitions/online/mozart/435) — Manuscript exhibit states the entry was completed August 10, 1787 and explains the title’s literal sense.
- [Bärenreiter — Mozart preface](https://www.barenreiter.co.uk/prefaces/9790006200177_Innenansicht.pdf) — Critical-edition preface corroborates the catalog date and the historical context of the work.

## Assessment

1787 is verified. The graph should describe the work as a string divertimento rather than confidently calling it a serenade: the critical Mozarteum catalog explains that this genre designation appears only in the later first print. The work’s incomplete state is an important qualification.

## Remaining uncertainty

The circumstances of composition are unknown; the catalog-entry date is used instead of inferring a longer composition period.
