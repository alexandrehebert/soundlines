## Sources checked

- [Red Saunders Research Foundation — Chess Label Part II](https://campber.people.clemson.edu/chess2.html) — Detailed Chess discography gives Chess 1602, recorded May 24, 1955 and released June 1955 under the original spelling Manish Boy.
- [Rock and Roll Hall of Fame archival filing](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/ttabvue/ttabvue-92057288-CAN-30.pdf) — Lists Muddy Waters' 1955 Mannish Boy among songs that shaped rock and roll.
- [Chess Records — Muddy Waters](https://www.chessrecords.com/artist/muddy-waters/) — Official label history identifies Waters as a central Chess artist and names his influential original recordings.

## Assessment

The 1955 year and Muddy Waters attribution are supported. The first Chess label used the spelling Manish Boy; Mannish Boy became the common later title. The song is blues, with influence beyond the genre.

## Remaining uncertainty

Songwriting credits include Muddy Waters, Mel London and Bo Diddley; this source pass did not verify ownership/credit metadata in the original label scan.
