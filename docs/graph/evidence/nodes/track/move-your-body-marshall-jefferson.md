## Sources checked

- [Trax Records — Marshall Jefferson](https://www.traxrecords.com/marshall-jefferson) — Original label identifies Move Your Body as a 1986 Trax single and a landmark house record.
- [Insomniac — Move Your Body](https://www.insomniac.com/music/marshall-jefferson-move-your-body/) — Dance-music history identifies the 1986 Trax release and distinguishes a later rerecorded version.

## Assessment

1986, Marshall Jefferson and house context are supported by the original label and specialist coverage. The song is also known as The House Music Anthem. The label's claim that it was the first house song with piano is a promotional superlative and should not be treated as settled without comparative evidence.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some compilations feature a rerecording; this graph node should refer to the original Trax version.
