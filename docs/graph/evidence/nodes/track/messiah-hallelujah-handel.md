## Sources checked

- [German History in Documents and Images — Messiah](https://germanhistorydocs.org/en/the-holy-roman-empire-1648-1815/ghdi%3Aaudio-5027) — Historical source distinguishes composition in 1741 from the Dublin premiere in 1742 and identifies the excerpt as the Hallelujah chorus.
- [Handel and Haydn Society — Messiah program notes](https://handelandhaydn.org/enhanced-program-notes-handel-messiah/) — Specialist society discusses the 1742 premiere and places the Hallelujah chorus in Part II.
- [University of Chicago Divinity School — Messiah history](https://divinity.uchicago.edu/sightings/articles/handels-messiah-christmas-oratorio-emily-c-hoyler) — Academic essay dates the oratorio's composition to 1741 and premiere to April 13, 1742.

## Assessment

1741 is supported as the composition year, but the first performance was in Dublin in 1742. The node does not identify a specific recording. Changed description to clarify work/chorus and removed the unsupported recorded_by edge that described Handel as performer; he is the composer and conducted the premiere.

## Remaining uncertainty

The genre edge should describe Baroque composition, not a recording. A specific recorded performance would need a performer/recording source and separate date.
