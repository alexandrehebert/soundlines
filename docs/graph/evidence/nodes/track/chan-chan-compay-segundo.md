## Sources checked

- [Buena Vista Social Club / World Circuit — album page](https://www.buenavistasocialclub.com/albums/buena-vista-social-club/) — Official album page dates the album to June 1997, lists Chan Chan as track one, identifies the 1996 Havana sessions, and credits it as a recent Compay Segundo composition.
- [PBS — Chan Chan](https://www.pbs.org/buenavista/music/songs/chan_chan.html) — PBS music guide identifies Compay Segundo as the signature artist and classifies Chan Chan as a classic son within traditional Cuban music.
- [El País — Eliades Ochoa interview](https://elpais.com/eps/2026-03-27/eliades-ochoa-el-ultimo-gran-trovador.html) — Interview with Ochoa recounts Compay Segundo giving him the song and its later role as a Buena Vista Social Club signature.

## Assessment

1997 accurately dates the globally known Buena Vista Social Club recording, not the song’s composition. Official album materials confirm the recording context and track order; son cubano is strongly supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

The original composition’s precise year is not established by the label’s album page, so it is deliberately not stated.
