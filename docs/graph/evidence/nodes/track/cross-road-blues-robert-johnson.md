## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress — Robert Johnson test pressings](https://www.loc.gov/item/prn-06-088/) — LOC American Folklife Center confirms Johnson’s recorded repertoire was made in 1936 and 1937 and preserves test pressings and first-person session documentation.
- [Smithsonian — Robert Johnson centennial collection](https://www.si.edu/object/centennial-collection-robert-johnson%3Asiris_sil_1090504) — Smithsonian catalog lists Cross Road Blues and places the original Johnson recordings in San Antonio in 1936 and Dallas in 1937.
- [Duke University — Delta Blues sonic dictionary](https://sonicdictionary.duke.edu/items/delta-blues.html) — Educational archive dates Cross Road Blues to a November 1936 recording and May 1937 release.

## Assessment

Graph’s 1936 date refers to the recording session, not commercial issue, which was in 1937. Blues classification is secure. The folklore of Johnson selling his soul at a crossroads is not evidence about the song’s title or recording and is not repeated here.

## Remaining uncertainty

LOC’s page was blocked on direct access in this check; Smithsonian and Duke educational materials independently support the session and issue distinction.
