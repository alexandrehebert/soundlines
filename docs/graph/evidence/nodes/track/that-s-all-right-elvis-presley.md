## Sources checked

- [Elvis official — That's All Right](https://www.elvisthemusic.com/music/thats-all-right/) — Official estate page gives July 19, 1954 single release and July 5–6 recording-session narrative; credits Moore and Black as backing musicians.
- [Elvis official — RCA single version](https://www.elvisthemusic.com/track/thats-all-right-rca-single-version/) — Official track catalog says recorded July 5, 1954 at Sun Studio.
- [U.S. Congress — Elvis Presley resolution](https://www.congress.gov/108/crec/2004/01/21/CREC-2004-01-21-pt1-PgS62.pdf) — Congressional Record documents the 1954 Sun recording and its historic context.

## Assessment

1954 release and Sun recording confirmed. This is an adaptation of Arthur Crudup's 1946 blues, and the early Presley version is commonly classified rockabilly; lowered generic rock-and-roll association to medium rather than imply an uncontested 'first rock record.'

## Remaining uncertainty

Graph identifies Presley as lead artist, while Moore and Black are integral to the credited performance.
