## Sources checked

- [Motown Museum — Marvin Gaye](https://www.motownmuseum.org/artist/marvin-gaye/) — Museum states Gaye's recording was made in 1968 and identifies its commercial success.
- [Universal Music Canada — 50th anniversary release note](https://www.universalmusic.ca/2018/10/31/marvin-gayes-1968-international-chart-topper-i-heard-it-through-the-grapevine-celebrated-with-new-black-and-purple-vinyl-lp-releases/) — Label group confirms the 1968 Marvin Gaye version and distinguishes it as the celebrated Motown hit.
- [Official Marvin Gaye channel audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U7eXLTYa19M) — UMG recording metadata credits vocalist Marvin Gaye and producers Norman Whitfield, with ℗1968.

## Assessment

The graph's 1968 year refers to Marvin Gaye's recording and single; it is not the first released version of the song. Gladys Knight & the Pips released their version earlier, in 1967. Graph attribution to Gaye is correct for this node.

## Remaining uncertainty

The title also exists in other Motown artists' versions; song title alone must not collapse those recordings.
