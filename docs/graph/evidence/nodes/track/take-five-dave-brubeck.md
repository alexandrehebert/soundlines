## Assessment

The 1959 version is by the Dave Brubeck Quartet, but Paul Desmond composed Take Five and is the lead alto saxophonist. Updated description and lowered Cool Jazz association confidence because Time Out is more precisely hard to confine to one subgenre.

## Remaining uncertainty

The recorded-by relation only names Brubeck, underrepresenting quartet credit; graph has no work-composer relation.
