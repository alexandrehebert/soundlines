## Sources checked

- [Philip Glass official site — Einstein on the Beach](https://philipglass.com/compositions/einstein_on_the_beach/) — Composer’s official catalog identifies the opera and its July 25, 1976 Avignon premiere.
- [Philip Glass official site — recording history](https://philipglass.com/recordings/einstein_on_the_beach_none/) — Official release history identifies the work as a pivotal 1976 composition and notes the 1978 recording was abridged to fit four LPs.
- [Library of Congress — Einstein on the Beach registry essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/EinsteinOnTheBeach.pdf) — Registry essay documents the 1976 performances and the opera’s influence on musical theatre.

## Assessment

The graph year denotes the work’s premiere, not a recording date. Recasting it as an opera avoids treating the entire five-hour stage work as a single track. Minimalism is well supported, with Robert Wilson’s staging central to the work.

## Remaining uncertainty

Production and recording versions differ in duration; the original 1976 live production is distinct from the 1978 studio release.
