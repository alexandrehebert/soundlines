## Sources checked

- [Blue Note Records — John Coltrane biography](https://www.bluenote.com/artist/john-coltrane/) — States that Giant Steps was recorded by the end of 1959 and released in early 1960.
- [John Coltrane Discography — Giant Steps](https://johncoltranediscography.org/release/giant-steps/) — Discography entry documents the May 4, 1959 session; session date is distinct from the 1960 release year.
- [Jazz in America — Giant Steps audio entry](https://www.jazzinamerica.org/Media/Audio/16?KeepThis=true&TB_iframe=true&height=640&width=500) — Catalog entry identifies John Coltrane, recording date May 4, 1959, and ℗1960 Atlantic.
- [MusicBrainz — Giant Steps release](https://musicbrainz.org/release/5c501883-817c-45aa-88a4-10f7d0a31ea9) — Release record gives the original Atlantic issue date as January 27, 1960 and lists the title track among the recordings.

## Assessment

The graph's 1960 year is correct for the album release. The title track was recorded in 1959 (including a May 4 session); recording date and release date should not be conflated. John Coltrane attribution and jazz categorization are supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some later reissues carry different dates; this node describes the original 1960 album-era recording.
