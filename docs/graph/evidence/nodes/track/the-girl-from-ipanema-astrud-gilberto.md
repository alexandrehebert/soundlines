## Assessment

The hit single was issued in 1964, while the album performance was recorded in March 1963 and released in 1964. Bossa nova is beyond dispute; raised confidence to high. Clarified this is the Astrud English-vocal single version, which also includes other instrumentalists.

## Remaining uncertainty

Credit formats differ across the album and single; graph's single-centric artist link names Astrud as its defining vocalist.
