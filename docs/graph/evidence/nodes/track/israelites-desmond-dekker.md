## Assessment

The 1968 graph year fits the Jamaican/original release era; the UK chart breakthrough came in 1969. Artist attribution is often styled Desmond Dekker & the Aces, while some catalogs shorten it to Dekker. Both ska and reggae are useful context, so the graph's ska-only representative claim is incomplete.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact release chronology differs by market; year precision should remain. Consider adding a reggae relationship if genre links are intended to reflect both ska and reggae.
