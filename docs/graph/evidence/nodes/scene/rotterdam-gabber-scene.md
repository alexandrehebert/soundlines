## Sources checked

- [Media, Culture & Society: Gabber in technoculture](https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/mms_00057_1) — Peer-reviewed article identifies Rotterdam as the epicentre of Dutch gabber in the early 1990s.
- [Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision: A Brief History of Gabber](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/ugXxcmEzFVvjKQ?hl=en) — Archive story documents Rotterdam record shops, producers, labels, and early parties.
- [Urban Studies: Dutch popular music scenes](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0308518X15595781) — Comparative research places gabber in a national network of music scenes rather than treating one city as its sole origin.

## Assessment

A Dutch hardcore network centered in Rotterdam clubs, record shops and parties; its early history includes Parkzicht, DJ Paul Elstak and Rotterdam Records. Overall confidence: high.
