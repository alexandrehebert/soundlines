## Sources checked

- [Musurgia: Elements for a musical definition of French Touch](https://musurgia.fr/lebray-sebastien-elements-pour-une-definition-plus-musicale-de-la-french-touch-musurgia-30-2-2023/) — Musicological study treats French Touch as a Parisian electronic movement and analyzes the label’s musical meaning.

- [France Music: Producers of the French Touch](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/the-producers-of-the-french-touch/wQXBKVXCbR5LJg?hl=en) — Industry overview catalogs varied producers and styles grouped under the export label.

## Assessment

Paris–Versailles electronic music network in which house, disco sampling, independent labels and club culture intersected in the 1990s and 2000s. The label is an umbrella, not a single uniform sound. Overall confidence: medium.
