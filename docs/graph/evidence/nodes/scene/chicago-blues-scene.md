## Sources checked

- [Digital Chicago History: Documenting the Black Metropolis’s Blues Culture](https://www.digitalchicagohistory.org/exhibits/show/flerlage/cultural-geography/blues-culture) — Archival essay documents postwar Chicago performers, Chess Records, and the electric blues network.
- [Library of Congress: Blues](https://www.loc.gov/collections/songs-of-america/articles-and-essays/musical-styles/popular-songs-of-the-day/blues/) — Overview describes migration and the transformation of blues in urban settings, including Chicago.
- [Chess Records history](https://www.chessrecords.com/history/) — Label archive documents the Chicago recording company and artists it recorded.

## Assessment

A city-centered electric blues network shaped by musicians who moved from the South and the independent labels and clubs of Chicago. Overall confidence: high.
