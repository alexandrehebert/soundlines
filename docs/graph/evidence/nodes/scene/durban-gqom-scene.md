## Sources checked

- [Mail & Guardian: DJ Lag and gqom](https://mg.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/edition-2023-07-14.pdf) — Reporting documents the genre’s early-2000s development in Durban and the artist’s township roots.
- [DJ Mag: Recognise DJ Lag](https://djmag.com/content/recognise-dj-lag) — Interview documents DJ Lag’s Durban roots and role in carrying gqom from local circulation to international audiences.
- [Spotify newsroom: Durban’s music culture](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-08-01/spotifys-mother-of-music-campaign-celebrates-the-citys-unmistakable-sound-and-culture/) — Supports contemporary city-scene context, but is an industry feature rather than independent historical research.

## Assessment

A Durban-born dance music network that grew through local producers, DJs, clubs and informal circulation. Overall confidence: high.
