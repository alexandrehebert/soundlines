## Sources checked

- [National Park Service: Jazz origins in New Orleans](https://www.nps.gov/jazz/learn/historyculture/history_early.htm) — Federal historical study traces jazz’s emergence through New Orleans communities and multiple musical influences, while noting ongoing debate among historians.
- [Smithsonian: Jazz](https://music.si.edu/story/jazz/) — Museum history documents New Orleans as an early center and follows the music’s spread through musicians and recording.
- [National Park Service: New Orleans jazz sites](https://www.nps.gov/jazz/learn/historyculture/upload/New-Orleans-Jazz-Sites-Then-and-Now.pdf) — Historic-site survey connects specific places and communities to jazz history.

## Assessment

A historically important jazz network whose musicians drew on African American, Caribbean, European and local traditions. The late nineteenth-century start is an approximate frame for the city’s musical antecedents, not a settled date for jazz’s invention; the NPS notes continuing historical debate. Confidence is high for New Orleans’ central role and lower for any precise origin date.
