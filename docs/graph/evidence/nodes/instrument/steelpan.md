## Sources checked

- [National Archives of Trinidad and Tobago, steelband history](https://www.natt.gov.tt/node/184) — Places invention in 1930s communities around Port of Spain.
- [Smithsonian Center for Folklife, Tamboo Bamboo](https://folklife.si.edu/magazine/art-of-rebellion-tamboo-bamboo-trinidad) — Explains 1930s move from bamboo percussion to metallic substitutes and steelpan.
- [Smithsonian Folkways, Celebrating Trinidadian Steelband](https://folkways.si.edu/celebrating-trinidadian-steelband/music/tools-for-teaching/smithsonian) — Documents steelband’s cultural role in Trinidad and Tobago.
- [National Library of Trinidad and Tobago, Steelband](https://www.nalis.gov.tt/resources/tt-content-guide/steelband/) — Places early development in 1930s–40s; association in 1950.

## Assessment

Trinidadian archival and Smithsonian sources support decade-level invention/development, not arbitrary later popularity peaks.
