## Assessment

Retained the broad c.1700 development anchor at low confidence and moved the adoption phase from 1800 to c.1750, consistent with the collection history and repertoire scholarship.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact transition to regular orchestral use varies by repertory and institution; year-level precision would overstate the evidence.
