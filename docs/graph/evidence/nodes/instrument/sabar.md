## Assessment

The Royal Museum for Central Africa lists a solo single-skin sabar in David Ames’s 1954 recording of Wolof wedding music. That recording documents this older instrument. The ethnographic study describes the distinctive one-hand-and-stick playing technique and its role in mbalax.
