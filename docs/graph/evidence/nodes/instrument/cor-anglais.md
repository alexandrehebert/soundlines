# Cor anglais

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Metropolitan Museum of Art: English Horn in F](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/501500) — Curatorial account dates bulb bell c.1700, sickle shape c.1720, angular form after mid-century, and wider use since mid-19th century.
- [Philharmonia: Cor Anglais](https://philharmonia.co.uk/resources/instruments/cor-anglais/) — Orchestral reference places earliest known specific orchestral part in Jommelli’s Ezio around 1749.
- [Vienna Symphonic Library: English Horn](https://www.vsl.co.at/academy/woodwinds/english-horn) — Supplementary history of early form and terminology.

## Assessment

Retained c.1720 as an approximate form-development marker and the mid-19th-century phase as wider use, while adding the earlier documented orchestral part c.1749 so that the latter is not mistaken for first orchestral use.

## Remaining uncertainty

Historical forms and names vary; the surviving/known repertoire does not by itself quantify adoption.
