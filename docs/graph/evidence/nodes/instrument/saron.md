## Sources checked

- [The Met, Saron Panerus Slendro](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/501374) — Catalogs a nineteenth-century Javanese example in bronze and teak and describes its role in gamelan.
- [Grinnell College, Saron peking](https://omeka-s.grinnell.edu/s/MusicalInstruments/item/1218) — Describes the bars and wooden resonator of the high-register form.

## Assessment

The century dates a museum specimen and is a documented anchor for the saron, not the beginning of gamelan.
