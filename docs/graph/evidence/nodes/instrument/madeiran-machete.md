## Sources checked

- [Musical Instruments Museum, Braguinha](https://www.mim.be/en/collection-piece/braguinha) — Documents four single strings, Madeiran use and the connection to the Hawaiian ukulele.
- [Arquipélagos, Peças para Machete](https://www.arquipelagos.pt/imagem/machete-drumond-de-vasconcelos-1846/) — Identifies Cândido Drumond de Vasconcelos’s dated 1846 collection of pieces for machete.
- [The Met, Ukulele](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/501343) — Describes Madeiran settlers bringing the braguinha to Hawaiʻi and making instruments locally.

## Assessment

1846 is a dated repertoire anchor. The instrument was already in use by then.
