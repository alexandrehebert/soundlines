# UNESCO inscribes Indonesian gamelan

Graph ID `event:gamelan-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

Indonesia’s gamelan tradition enters UNESCO’s Representative List, including the music, instruments and practices passed on through communities and schools.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
