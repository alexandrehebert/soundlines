## Assessment

A documented stage/presentation milestone that distinguishes the 1971 fair from the original founding.

The free Glastonbury Fair of 1971 introduced the festival’s first Pyramid Stage and a programme including David Bowie. Its combination of music, performing arts and countercultural gathering became part of the festival’s developing identity; this was a second edition, not its 1970 founding.
