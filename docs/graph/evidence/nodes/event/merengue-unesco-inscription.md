# UNESCO inscribes Dominican merengue

Graph ID `event:merengue-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

UNESCO adds the music and dance of merengue in the Dominican Republic to its Representative List, documenting a tradition passed on at gatherings, celebrations and festivals.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
