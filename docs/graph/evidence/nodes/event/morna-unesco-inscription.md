# UNESCO inscribes Cabo Verde’s morna

Graph ID `event:morna-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

UNESCO adds morna to its Representative List, recognizing a Cape Verdean practice that brings together music, poetry and dance.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
