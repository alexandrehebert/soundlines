# Straight Outta Compton enters the US Recording Registry

Graph ID `event:straight-outta-compton-recording-registry`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

The Library of Congress selects N.W.A.’s 1988 debut album for the National Recording Registry. Its selection recognizes a recording that helped popularize gangsta rap and brought West Coast hip-hop wider attention.

The timeline year refers to the documented registry selection, rather than the origin of the genre.
