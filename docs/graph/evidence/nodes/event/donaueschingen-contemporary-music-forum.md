## Assessment

A documented new-music presentation institution, with historical scope supported by organiser and academic accounts.

The first Donaueschingen chamber-music festival in 1921 gave new compositions a dedicated public forum. Its emerging-composer focus and later broadcasting partnership helped sustain a recurring institution for contemporary music, rather than a single stylistic school.
