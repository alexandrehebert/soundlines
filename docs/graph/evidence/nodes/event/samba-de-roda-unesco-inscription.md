# UNESCO inscribes Bahia’s samba de roda

Graph ID `event:samba-de-roda-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

UNESCO adds the music and dance tradition of Brazil’s Recôncavo of Bahia to its Representative List, following its proclamation as a Masterpiece in 2005. The inscription covers this regional tradition within the wider samba family.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
