# UNESCO inscribes Dominican bachata

Graph ID `event:bachata-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

UNESCO recognizes the music and dance of Dominican bachata, a tradition that combines influences from bolero and Afro-Caribbean styles and is practised at social gatherings.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
