## Assessment

Ancient institutionalisation of musical competition; keep the reform date distinct from an origin claim.

The reorganisation of Apollo’s festival at Delphi around 586–582 BCE expanded an older musical competition into a four-yearly Panhellenic gathering with musical and athletic contests. It documents an ancient institution for public musical competition, not the first music festival anywhere.

The 586–582 BCE range covers the conventional reform/numbering chronology; 582 BCE is a reference point, not a precisely established founding date. Earlier musical contests predate it, and no world-first is established.
