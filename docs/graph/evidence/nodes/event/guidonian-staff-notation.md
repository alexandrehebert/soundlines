## Sources checked

- [The Musical Quarterly, Origins of the Musical Staff](https://academic.oup.com/mq/article-abstract/91/3-4/327/1061299) — Musicological article reviews the conventional attribution of the four-line staff to Guido and the limits of simplified origin claims.
- [Metropolitan Museum of Art, The Guidonian Hand](https://www.metmuseum.org/pt/perspectives/musical-bodies-guidonian-hand) — Museum account dates Guido’s four-line staff system to around 1020 and describes its role in teaching pitch.
- [University of Basel, Lines and staff](https://tales.nmc.unibas.ch/en/from-ink-to-sound-32/from-sound-to-ink-early-forms-of-musical-notation-188/lines-and-staff-949) — Shows earlier line-based notation and contextualizes the gradual development of staff notation.

## Assessment

Guido’s role in systematizing pitch-specific staff notation is well supported, but he did not invent all staff-like notation from nothing. The current description’s “important step” wording is appropriately restrained; circa 1025 is a reasonable approximate marker.
