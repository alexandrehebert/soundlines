## Assessment

A documented touring-festival format in alternative music, distinct from its later permanent venue.

The first Lollapalooza tour in 1991 brought acts including Nine Inch Nails into a shared travelling programme conceived by Perry Farrell around Jane’s Addiction’s farewell. Archive material and popular-music scholarship place it within the growth of the alternative-rock festival circuit; it was not yet the later Chicago event.
