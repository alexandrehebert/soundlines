## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian archival sound recording catalog](https://www.si.edu/object/carnegie-hall-jazz-concert-sound-recording%3Asiris_arc_315450) — Catalogs the 16 January 1938 performance and the 1950 Columbia release.
- [Oxford University Press: Benny Goodman’s Famous 1938 Carnegie Hall Jazz Concert](https://academic.oup.com/book/4016) — Jazz historian Bruce Crowther documents the concert date and explains its 1950 LP release.
- [Carnegie Hall event history](https://www.carnegiehall.org/Explore/Articles/2023/01/16/Benny-Goodman-at-Carnegie-Hall) — Institutional account of the event and its place in Carnegie Hall’s history.
- [CapRadio interview with concert historian](https://www.capradio.org/music/jazz/2018/01/12/benny-goodmans-1938-carnegie-hall-concert-from-the-guy-who-wrote-the-book-on-that/) — Explains that recording was made remotely in a nearby studio and retained until its Columbia release in 1950.

## Assessment

Corrected the event title and description: the concert itself took place in Carnegie Hall, but the recording was made remotely nearby, not as a live recording in the hall. The 1950 release must not be confused with the 1938 performance date.
