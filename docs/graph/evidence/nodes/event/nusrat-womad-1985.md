# Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan performs at WOMAD

Graph ID `event:nusrat-womad-1985`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan and his ensemble perform at WOMAD in England, bringing Pakistani qawwali to a new festival audience. A recording of the performance is later issued by Real World Records.

The timeline year refers to the documented performance, rather than the origin of the genre.
