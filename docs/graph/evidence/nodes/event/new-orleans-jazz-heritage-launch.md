## Assessment

A documented festival model rooted in local musical and community heritage.

The first New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival in 1970 placed music alongside local crafts, food and community performance. Mahalia Jackson and Duke Ellington appeared at the inaugural gathering; the model presented local cultural traditions as central to the festival rather than as background to visiting headliners.
