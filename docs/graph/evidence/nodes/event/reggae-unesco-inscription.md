# UNESCO inscribes Jamaican reggae

Graph ID `event:reggae-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

UNESCO adds the reggae music of Jamaica to its Representative List. Its decision highlights reggae’s role in social commentary and its reach beyond Jamaica.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
