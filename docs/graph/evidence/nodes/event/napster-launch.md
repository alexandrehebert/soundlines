## Sources checked

- [Harvard Berkman Klein Center, Napster at 20](https://cyber.harvard.edu/events/napster20-reflections-internets-most-controversial-music-file-sharing-service) — Dates Shawn Fanning’s peer-to-peer service launch to June 1999.
- [Pew Research Center, State of Music Online: Ten Years After Napster](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2009/06/15/the-state-of-music-online-ten-years-after-napster-2/) — Retrospective dates the public launch to June 1999 and describes broader file-sharing context.

## Assessment

The 1999 launch and peer-to-peer model are supported. It popularized a particular service but did not originate file sharing; keep “popularizes” rather than implying Napster invented it.
