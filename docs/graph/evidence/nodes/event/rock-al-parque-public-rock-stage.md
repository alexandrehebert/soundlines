## Assessment

A documented public-cultural infrastructure development, with the policy claim and admission exception qualified.

The inaugural Rock al Parque in May 1995 brought Colombian bands and international guests into Bogotá’s public venues. The festival became a municipal platform for live music; its wider policy influence is a historical interpretation, and the first edition was not free at every venue.
