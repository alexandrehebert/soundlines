# Argentina and Uruguay secure UNESCO recognition for tango

Graph ID `event:tango-unesco-inscription`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-13

## Assessment

UNESCO inscribes the tango tradition jointly nominated by Argentina and Uruguay. The decision recognizes a shared musical and dance culture rooted in Buenos Aires and Montevideo.

The timeline year refers to the documented heritage inscription, rather than the origin of the genre.
