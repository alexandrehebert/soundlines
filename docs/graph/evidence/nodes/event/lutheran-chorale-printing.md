## Sources checked

- [Lutheran Theological Seminary at Gettysburg, Gruber Collection: 1524](https://gruber.lstc.edu/luthers_works/1524.php) — Documents the 1524 Achtliederbuch as the oldest Lutheran hymnal and identifies its eight hymns.
- [University of Mainz, Gesangbuch Bibliographie](https://gesangbuchbibliographie.uni-mainz.de/details.php?id=-1713624593) — Catalogs the Achtliederbuch and other 1524 Wittenberg hymn publications.

## Assessment

The 1524 print milestone is supported. Multiple early hymnbooks appeared that year; avoid implying one publication alone created Lutheran congregational song. Print supplemented oral and congregational circulation.
