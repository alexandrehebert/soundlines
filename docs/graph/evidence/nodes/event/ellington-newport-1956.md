## Assessment

A specifically documented performance/recording turning point in an existing jazz career.

Duke Ellington’s orchestra performed at Newport on 7 July 1956, with Paul Gonsalves’ extended solo on Diminuendo and Crescendo in Blue becoming the concert’s celebrated turning point. The performance and subsequent recording helped renew attention to Ellington’s orchestra; they did not create jazz or big-band music.
