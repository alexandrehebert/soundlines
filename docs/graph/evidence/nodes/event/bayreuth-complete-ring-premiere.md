## Assessment

A documented complete-cycle premiere and an influential opera-presentation model.

The first Bayreuth Festival presented Wagner’s complete Ring des Nibelungen cycle in August 1876 in a purpose-built theatre. Earlier performances of individual operas were not premieres of the complete cycle; the festival tied the musical drama to a distinctive auditorium and staging model.
