## Sources checked

- [El acordeón norteño mexicano y el transnacionalismo musical cosmopolita en las periferias](https://www.scielo.org.mx/scielo.php?pid=S0188-62662018000200083&script=sci_arttext) — Traces the formative recordings of música norteña, describes accordion and bajo sexto, and identifies Los Alegres, Los Relámpagos and Los Tigres as exponents.
- [Smithsonian Folkways, Texas Mexican Border Music, vol. III](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/ARH07016.pdf) — Documents recordings made from 1929 through 1939 in the early norteño and conjunto tradition.
