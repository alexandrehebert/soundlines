## Sources checked

- [D’Errico, “Electronic Dance Music in the Dubstep Era” (Oxford Handbook)](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/42059/chapter/355876582) — Musicologist traces dubstep from South London underground clubs through rapid transnational spread and media uptake; notes local origin narratives and later North American changes.
- [Higgins, The Evolution of Dubstep (University of Bristol PhD thesis, 2026)](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/studentTheses/the-evolution-of-dubstep/) — Doctoral research dates its transition from London subculture to North American commercial force to c.2003–13 and documents substantial stylistic change.
- [Arvidsson & Caliandro, “Market Practices in Countercultural Market Emergence” (European Journal of Marketing)](https://su.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A851001/FULLTEXT01.pdf) — Peer-reviewed study dates emergence to early 2000s South Croydon, linking Big Apple, minimalism, sub-bass, sound systems and dubplate culture; discusses early globalization.
- [Red Bull — History of Dubstep](https://www.redbull.com/gb-en/history-of-dubstep-music) — Music-history feature includes scene participants and describes early FWD>>, Croydon, and Jamaican reggae/dub influences; page yielded no readable text on direct open, so its details are not relied upon here.

## Assessment

The source record supports the early-2000s South London origin and rapid transnational diffusion, but the former end date 2012 treats one transformation as the genre’s end. The old 2005 “golden-age” and 2010 “niche” labels are not credible for a movement whose commercial force expanded across North America around 2009–13. Removed endpoint and phases; described the British underground and later commercial branches separately. Replaced Wikipedia with musicological, market-sociology and doctoral research.

## Remaining uncertainty

“Dubstep” came to cover divergent local and commercial styles; later brostep is debated as transformation versus separate genre. The exact term-coining year is not established here, so onset is decade-level.
