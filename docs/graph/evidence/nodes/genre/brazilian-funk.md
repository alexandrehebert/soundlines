## Sources checked
- [Sonic politics: technique, work and musical production in funk carioca](https://new.scielo.br/j/vb/a/LCBRksv9HrGrNDMnbtGk7TC/?ilang=en&lang=en) — Traces the scene and early Portuguese-language records, including Funk Brasil in 1989, and identifies Miami bass as a source for local recordings.
- [Contemporary Carioca: Technologies of Mixing in a Brazilian Music Scene](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/20/71/79/23/2020717923/2020717923.pdf) — Documents Rio’s funk dances and the use of electronic drum machines and Miami bass rhythms in original local tracks.

## Assessment
The 1989 release provides a landmark for a sound developed over several years in Rio de Janeiro’s bailes.

## Remaining uncertainty
The year denotes a recording landmark rather than a single origin.
