## Sources checked

- [African Music journal: Historicizing Kwaito](https://journal.ru.ac.za/index.php/africanmusic/article/view/1782) — Scholarly article reviews the standard post-apartheid emergence narrative and argues against overly reductive periodization.
- [University of Chicago Press: Kwaito’s Promise, “The Experience of the Outside”](https://doi.org/10.7208/chicago/9780226362687.003.0002) — Academic book chapter traces early kwaito and notes late-1980s use of the term alongside “international music.”
- [South African History Online: A History of Kwaito Music](https://sahistory.org.za/article/history-kwaito-music) — Historical account dates the genre’s emergence to the 1990s.
- [Popular Music and Society: South African Music after Apartheid](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/03007760500105172) — Peer-reviewed article situates kwaito in South African black youth culture and post-apartheid social history.

## Assessment

Kept a broad 1990s emergence and removed the 2010 endpoint and subjective boom/decline phases. Added scholarship that challenges a simplistic post-apartheid origin story; kwaito continues to be made and referenced.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some sources trace the term and musical ingredients into the late 1980s, while others date the recognizable genre to the 1990s.
