## Sources checked
- [Pitchfork, 2019, In the Court of the Crimson King review](https://pitchfork.com/reviews/albums/king-crimson-in-the-court-of-the-crimson-king-50th-anniversary/) — the 1969 debut refined “a nascent genre into a pinnacle of the progressive rock form”; psychedelic Beatles/Pink Floyd as building blocks.

- [Guardian, 2010, prog revival feature](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2010/jul/22/prog-rock-genesis-rush-mostly-autumn) — 2009–2010 “tidal shift in favour of prog”: Prog magazine, festival prog stages.

- [Wikipedia, Progressive rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressive_rock) — supplementary.

## Assessment
Late-1960s emergence (circa 1967) is supported. The 1975 end date was not supported: sources describe a late-1970s decline, not an end, and later neo-prog and revival activity. The period is now open-ended with phases emerging 1967, golden-age 1969, established (low) from 1978 and a modest 2009 revival; the single provisional golden-age phase was replaced. Sources added beyond the sole Wikipedia citation.

## Remaining uncertainty
The 2009 revival marker rests on one UK press feature (low confidence).
