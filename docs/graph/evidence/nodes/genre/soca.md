## Sources checked
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, “Ras Shorty I: The Soul of Calypso”](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2016/06/ras-shorty-i-the-soul-of-calypso/) — describes Lord Shorty’s 1973 “Indrani” as combining calypso with dholak and dhantal played by East Indian musicians; quotes Shorty explaining the “so”/“kah” name; describes soca proper as coalescing around 1976 and as a revisioning of calypso.
- [Caribbean Beat, “Soca gold”](https://www.caribbean-beat.com/issue-174/soca-gold-backstory) — describes a 1973 evolution/redefinition of calypso, locates soca’s emergence in Trinidad and Tobago, frames Shorty’s role in its genesis as a credible case rather than an uncontested sole-origin claim, and emphasizes contributions from multiple artists.

## Assessment
The sources support Soca as a Caribbean Carnival style originating in Trinidad and Tobago that reworks calypso, with early rhythmic experiments that included East Indian percussion. The 1973 “Indrani” milestone and RBMA’s account that soca proper coalesced around 1976 justify an estimated circa-1973 origins anchor, not a precise genre boundary. “Derived from” Calypso is more precise than a two-parent fusion claim. The `reggae` family is a browsing classification shared with Calypso, not an ancestry assertion. Overall confidence: medium.

## Remaining uncertainty
The emergence is gradual and the sources do not establish a single uncontested founding date or sole originator.
