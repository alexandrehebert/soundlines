## Sources checked
- [Jan-Peter Herbst, The Formation of the West German Power Metal Scene](https://pure.hud.ac.uk/ws/files/14390095/Herbst_Teutonic_Metal.pdf) — Documents the rise of power metal in West Germany in the mid-1980s, its heavy metal roots, melodic singing and fantasy themes, and the roles of Helloween, Blind Guardian and Gamma Ray.
- [Blind Guardian, band history](https://www.blind-guardian.com/band/) — The band’s history dates its earliest work in Krefeld to 1985 and describes its speed metal origins and the style it helped establish.

## Assessment
The mid-1980s anchor follows the documented development of the West German sound.

## Remaining uncertainty
The start places the emergence of the genre rather than a first recording or first use of the term.
