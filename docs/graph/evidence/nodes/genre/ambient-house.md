## Sources checked
- [Matt Anniss, The Story of Chill Out Music, 1988–95](https://jointhefuture.net/2020/09/10/long-read-the-story-of-chill-out/) — Dates early chill-out sets and releases to 1989 and describes the role of the Heaven nightclub in London.
- [Melody Maker, Ambient House: The Ecstasy Fantasy (1990)](https://www.808state.com/various/interview/1990-03-10-MelodyMaker-Article.html) — A contemporary account of the convergence of house beats, ambient soundscapes and samples.

## Assessment
The period is anchored by documented 1989 club sets and early records. The electronica and ambient family reflects the genre’s use in chill-out rooms and its range from rhythmic to beatless works.

## Remaining uncertainty
The emergence date is approximate because DJ practice, records and the name developed together.
