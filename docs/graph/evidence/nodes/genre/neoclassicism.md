## Sources checked
- [Breanna Corah, Neoclassical Pioneers](https://digitalcommons.du.edu/etd/1157/) — Identifies the reuse of older musical forms and the emergence of the genre in the 1920s.
- [University of Nebraska–Lincoln, Modernist Music Information](https://english.unl.edu/sbehrendt/courses/4802Modernism/ModernistMusicSp2017.html) — Lists representative postwar works, including Pulcinella and the Classical Symphony.

## Assessment
The sources document the retrospective compositional approach and its twentieth-century consolidation.

## Remaining uncertainty
Earlier works anticipate the style, and the 1920s placement marks its consolidation.
