## Sources checked
- [Beatport’s Definitive History of House Music](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/299799-beatports-definitive-history-of-house-music) — Dates the emergence of the name to about 1991 and describes the bass, breakbeats and four-on-the-floor kick of early UK records.
- [Leftfield official biography](https://www.leftfieldmusic.com/about/) — Dates the release of Not Forgotten to 1990 and identifies it as a template for a new British house sound.

## Assessment
The entry covers the house movement associated with early British releases and its development by Sasha and John Digweed.

## Remaining uncertainty
The start is approximate because records associated with the style preceded the widespread use of its name.
