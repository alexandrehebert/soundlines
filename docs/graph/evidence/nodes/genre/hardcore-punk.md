## Sources checked
- [Shayna Maskell, Politics as Unusual](https://drum.lib.umd.edu/items/dbe0407b-8e15-436e-85af-b822c3ab8d3d) — Traces the early D.C. scene, punk roots and fast, loud sound.
- [Library of Congress interview with Chris Payne](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-plan/tools-and-resources/documents/Interview_Chris-Payne.pdf) — Places the start around the turn of the 1980s and names early performers.
- [Black Flag, Official Website](https://www.blackflagband.com/) — Documents a formative Southern California band.

## Assessment
Historical accounts document regional scenes and a faster, louder punk sound around 1980.

## Remaining uncertainty
Multiple scenes developed concurrently and make an exact start year impractical.
