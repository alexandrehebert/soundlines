## Sources checked
- [Chicago History Museum, Sweet Home Chicago](https://www.chicagohistory.org/exhibition/sweet-home-chicago/) — Connects Southern migration to the rise of Chicago electric blues.
- [Little Walter Foundation, About](https://littlewalterfoundation.org/about) — Documents the lead harmonica sound in Muddy Waters’s Chicago band.
- [Mississippi Blues Trail, Howlin’ Wolf](https://msbluestrail.org/blues-trail-markers/howlin-wolf) — Places the electric Chicago style in the postwar period.

## Assessment
The museum links the style to Southern migration, and the artist sources document its electric sound and lead harmonica.

## Remaining uncertainty
Chicago had earlier blues styles, and the start refers to the emergence of the electric tradition.
