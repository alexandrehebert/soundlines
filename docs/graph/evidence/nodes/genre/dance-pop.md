## Sources checked
- [Dance-pop overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dance-pop) — Summarizes the late-1970s and early-1980s emergence, the relationship to pop and club music, and prominent performers.
- [TIME, Madonna Rocks the Land](https://time.com/3724297/madonna-rocks-the-land/) — Contemporary profile describes Madonna’s dance-pop and her early work with New York club DJ Mark Kamins.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Janet Jackson induction essay](https://www.rockhall.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Janet_Jackson_2019.pdf) — Dates her first album to 1982 and describes how Control helped establish a new dance-pop model.
- [Museum of the City of New York, Main Gallery](https://www.mcny.org/main-gallery) — Documents Madonna’s Danceteria launch in 1982, her debut album in 1983, and her worldwide breakthrough in 1984.

## Assessment
The period reflects the emergence of the style around the turn of the 1980s.

## Remaining uncertainty
