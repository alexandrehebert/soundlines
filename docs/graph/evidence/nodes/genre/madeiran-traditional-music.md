## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian Folkways, Portugal: Music and Dance from Madeira](https://folkways.si.edu/portugal-music-and-dance-from-madeira/world/music/album/smithsonian) — Documents work calls, ballads, song duels, dances, instrumental music, regional ensembles and 1996–97 field recordings.
- [Arquipélagos, Peças para Machete](https://www.arquipelagos.pt/imagem/machete-drumond-de-vasconcelos-1846/) — Identifies Cândido Drumond de Vasconcelos’s dated 1846 collection of pieces for machete.
- [Madeira Tourism Board, Folklore](https://www.visitmadeira.com/en/what-to-do/culture-passionates/folklore/) — Lists the machete and braguinha among instruments used in regional folklore.
- [Raízes do Atlântico, Xarabanda](https://raizesdoatlantico.madeira.gov.pt/artistas-2026/xarabanda-2026.html) — Documents the 1981 beginnings as Algozes, 1989 name change and the group’s collection, performance and rearrangement of Madeiran music.

## Assessment

The 1846 manuscript anchors a documented strand of the repertoire rather than a common origin for the island’s traditions. Country & Folk is a browsing lane.
