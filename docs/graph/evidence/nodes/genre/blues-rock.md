## Sources checked

- [Oxford: The Blues, A Very Short Introduction](https://academic.oup.com/book/663) — Scholarly book situates blues traditions and their continuing interaction with rock, but does not alone date blues-rock formation.
- [Cambridge Companion to the Guitar: Century of Blues Guitar](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-the-guitar/century-of-blues-guitar/ACDDDC3878C9D297F9FD5E8580D55530) — Academic chapter describes blues guitar’s long relationship with rock and warns against reducing blues origins to a single date.
- [Library of Congress: African American Song](https://www.loc.gov/collections/songs-of-america/articles-and-essays/musical-styles/ethnic/african-american-song/) — National collection contextualizes blues and later electric guitar developments but is not a blues-rock periodization source.
- [Wikipedia Blues rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blues_rock) — Prior source is broad secondary summary and does not substantiate the graph’s exact phases or endpoint.

## Assessment

Removed the unsupported 1975 end date and three narrow phase labels; revised the start to a decade-level 1960s emergence anchor. Oxford/Cambridge/LOC sources support long-running hybrid development but no precise genre-wide start or peak.
