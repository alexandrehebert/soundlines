## Sources checked
- [University of Chicago dissertation, chapter 2](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/record/15093/files/Dababneh_Dissertation.pdf) — Characterizes hyperpop through maximalist digital pop production, altered vocal pitches and distortion. Traces the earlier PC Music aesthetic and the subsequent wave led by 100 gecs.
- [Pitchfork interview with 100 gecs](https://pitchfork.com/features/podcast/100-gecs-and-the-mystery-of-hyperpop/) — Discusses the group’s 2019 debut, PC Music influences and role as a reference point for hyperpop.

## Assessment
The documented pop techniques and the 2019 breakthrough support a late-2010s placement in the Pop lane.

## Remaining uncertainty
The umbrella term covers several scenes and has no single agreed starting point.
