## Sources checked
- [Gregory McCandless, Metal as a Gradual Process](https://mtosmt.org/issues/mto.13.19.2/mto.13.19.2.mccandless.pdf) — Identifies progressive rock and heavy metal as parent styles, describes rhythmic and metrical complexity, and discusses early examples and the 1988 milestone.
- [Metal Blade Records, Fates Warning](https://www.metalblade.com/us/artists/fates-warning/) — Dates the debut album to 1984 and documents the band’s formative role in progressive metal.

## Assessment
Mid-decade is a broad placement for the development of the style, whose early examples precede the 1988 landmark.

## Remaining uncertainty
The style had precursors before its emergence as a recognizable branch of metal.
