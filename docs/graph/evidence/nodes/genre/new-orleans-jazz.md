## Sources checked
- [National Park Service, Jazz Origins in New Orleans](https://www.nps.gov/jazz/learn/historyculture/history_early.htm) — Documents late-nineteenth-century development, brass-band roots and the collective front line.
- [National Park Service, A New Orleans Jazz History, 1895–1927](https://home.nps.gov/jazz/learn/historyculture/jazz_history.htm) — Traces the regional style and its early musicians from the 1890s.

## Assessment
The National Park Service documents the regional style and the ensemble practice used to distinguish it.

## Remaining uncertainty
The formative years cannot be reduced to an exact founding date.
