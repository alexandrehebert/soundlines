## Sources checked
- [Resident Advisor interview with the Liberator DJs](https://ra.co/news/76853) — Dates the Liberator DJs’ early experiments to 1992 and describes the defining TB-303 sound.
- [Red Bull Music Academy, London Acid Techno oral history](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2016/06/london-acid-techno-feature/) — Documents the London squat parties and the combination of techno rhythms with acid textures.
- [Stay Up Forever label history](https://stayupforever.com/about-us/) — The founding label’s account identifies its music as acid techno and places the label in the early London squat scene.

## Assessment
The sources establish an early-1990s London scene and document its characteristic sound.

## Remaining uncertainty
The start anchors the documented London scene and does not claim that it was the first use of the style elsewhere.
