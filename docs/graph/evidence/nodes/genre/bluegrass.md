## Sources checked

- [Bluegrass Music Hall of Fame & Museum — Bill Monroe](https://www.bluegrasshall.org/inductees/bill-monroe/) — Museum chronology traces gradual evolution from late-1930s/early-1940s band sound; Scruggs joined in 1945, forming the classic five-piece lineup. It describes the 1945–48 recordings as a high-water mark and documents wider festival/label growth in the 1970s–80s.
- [National Endowment for the Arts — Bill Monroe](https://www.arts.gov/honors/heritage/bill-monroe) — Independent institutional account dates the sound coming together to 1946 and notes later festival growth, corroborating a range rather than a single exact birth year.
- [Country Music Hall of Fame — Bill Monroe](https://countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/bill-monroe) — Museum account calls Scruggs’s 1945 three-finger style the final building block and regards late-1940s recordings as definitive.

## Assessment

1945 was supported but overprecise as a single-year start; retained as circa, while sources show the style evolved across the late 1930s to mid-1940s and crystallized by 1945–46. Removed unsupported 1960 golden-age and 2010 established phases; replaced with a qualified 1970s institutional-growth marker.
