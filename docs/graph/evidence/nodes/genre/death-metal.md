## Sources checked

- [Chaker & Kahn-Harris, “Death Metal,” EPMOW entry (University of Kiel repository)](https://www.anglistik.uni-kiel.de/de/fachgebiete/kultur-und-medienwissenschaften/popular-culture/materialien/HeavyMetal.pdf) — Specialist genre entry dates coalescence to mid-1980s, describes stylistic markers and tape-trading networks, identifies Florida and Stockholm scenes, and explicitly dates commercial golden age 1989–93.
- [“Why Florida? Regional conditions and further developments of the Florida death metal scene” (Journal of Popular Music Studies)](https://online.ucpress.edu/jpms/article/33/3/168/118562/Why-Florida-Regional-conditions-and-further) — Peer-reviewed scene history traces Florida’s formation, tape-trading links, migrations and commercial peak, including 1992–93 album sales; notes the continuing position of pioneering bands.
- [Wallmark, death-metal timbre and scene dissertation (UC eScholarship)](https://escholarship.org/content/qt1cv7n7c1/qt1cv7n7c1_noSplash_0a0758c72bd3f0e1aa2771370e976f65.pdf) — Academic research dates formative Florida/Stockholm timbral scenes to c.1986–93, supporting the formation period while showing regional style variation.
- [Heavy metal music meets complexity and sustainability science (peer-reviewed, PMC)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5031579/) — Comparative genre study independently places death metal in the mid-1980s United States; secondary corroboration for broad date and sound description.

## Assessment

The prior 1986 exact start was over-precise and described the style as late-1980s; sources establish mid-1980s coalescence, so use decade precision. The old 1998 golden-age marker is wrong: the specialist account explicitly identifies a commercial golden age from 1989 to 1993. Removed the unsupported 2018 established boundary; used 1995 as a cautious post-peak/diversification marker, with medium confidence. AllMusic citation was replaced.

## Remaining uncertainty

Genre naming and formation are gradual; early extreme-metal roots predate the coalesced genre, and sources disagree on which demo or album should count as first. Phase 1995 is editorial and less firm than the 1989–93 peak.
