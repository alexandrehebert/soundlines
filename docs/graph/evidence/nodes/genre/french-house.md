## Sources checked

- [Sébastien Lebray, Musurgia](https://musurgia.fr/lebray-sebastien-elements-pour-une-definition-plus-musicale-de-la-french-touch-musurgia-30-2-2023/) — Musicological article defines French house/French Touch as Parisian movement with international popularity from late 1990s to early 2000s.
- [Apropos journal article on French house and French Touch](https://journals.sub.uni-hamburg.de/apropos/en/article/view/2208) — Scholarly article situates French electronic music’s global rise in the 1990s.
- [University of Évry doctoral thesis on French Touch](https://www.biblio.univ-evry.fr/theses/2015/2015SACLE021.pdf) — Thesis discusses rapid development in 1996 and Homework’s 1997 international impact.

## Assessment

The checked musicology source supports the broad late-1990s/early-2000s international wave and the late-1990s emergence context. This corroborates the existing circa-1993 to 2002 window as a broad editorial range, but not a precise first year or “golden age” score. French Touch and French house overlap but are not identical labels.

## Remaining uncertainty

The proposed 1993 start is a loose scene precursor date, not established as a singular origin; boundary varies with definition of movement versus sound.
