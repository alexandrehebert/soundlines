## Sources checked

- [University of Huddersfield, “Goatrance Travelers: Psychedelic Trance and Its Seasoned Progeny”](https://pure.hud.ac.uk/en/publications/goatrance-travelers-psychedelic-trance-and-its-seasoned-progeny/) — Scholarly study traces seasonal Goa party culture to the 1980s, Goa trance’s emergence to around 1990 and global psytrance flourishing thereafter.
- [University of Bristol doctoral thesis on psytrance](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/189626890/Final_Copy_2019_01_31_Charles_C_PhD.pdf) — Research distinguishes earlier Goa trance from the term/style in the early 1990s and explains transnational development.
- [Dancecult, “Seasoned Exodus: The Exile Mosaic of Psyculture”](https://dj.dancecult.net/index.php/dancecult/article/view/328) — Peer-reviewed electronic music and dance culture research situates Goa roots in 1970s/80s and psyculture in 1990s/2000s.
- [AllMusic Goa trance overview](https://www.allmusic.com/style/goa-trance-ma0000011977) — Specialist cross-reference for precursor style; not treated as equivalent to the full later psytrance category.

## Assessment

Kept an approximate 1990 start but clarified it marks Goa trance/psytrance codification, not the beginning of the preceding Goa party scene. Removed 2001 golden-age and 2018 established phases because they are editorial and imply unsupported comparative prominence. Ongoing global genre; confidence medium-high for broad genealogy, medium for exact naming.

## Remaining uncertainty

Goa trance and psytrance overlap but are not identical; the graph’s parent/child taxonomy should preserve that distinction.
