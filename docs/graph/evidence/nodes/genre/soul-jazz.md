## Sources checked
- [WRTI, Lou Donaldson, saxophonist who bridged bebop and soul](https://www.wrti.org/arts-desk/2024-11-11/lou-donaldson-saxophonist-who-bridged-bebop-and-soul-dies-at-98) — Describes the evolution from hard bop to soul jazz and Donaldson’s adoption of Jimmy Smith’s organ-driven groove.
- [NEA Jazz Masters, Jimmy Smith](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/jimmy-smith) — Documents Smith’s early R&B work and influential Hammond organ recordings for Blue Note beginning in 1956.
- [Blue Note Records, Soul Jazz catalogue](https://store.bluenote.com/collections/soul-jazz) — Collects Hammond-led soul jazz recordings and examples led by saxophone, piano and guitar.

## Assessment
Smith’s mid-1950s recordings and his late-1950s work with Donaldson place the emergence of the organ-led style in this period.

## Remaining uncertainty
The late 1950s are an approximate placement for a gradual stylistic development.
