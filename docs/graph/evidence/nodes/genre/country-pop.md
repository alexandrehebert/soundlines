## Sources checked
- [Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum, Glen Campbell](https://countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/glen-campbell) — Traces Campbell’s simultaneous country and pop hits from his 1967 breakthrough.
- [Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum, Dolly Parton](https://countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/dolly-parton) — Identifies Here You Come Again as Parton’s successful crossover into pop.
- [Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum, Shania Twain: Sound and Style Groundbreaker](https://cmhof.imgix.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/24125004/SCH_WM_SongwriterBios_ShaniaTwain_23-7-12.pdf) — Describes Twain’s pop-country sound and the pop and rock elements of her 1990s recordings.

## Assessment
The placement marks the start of Campbell’s sustained crossover success. The museum biographies document three different periods of the style.
