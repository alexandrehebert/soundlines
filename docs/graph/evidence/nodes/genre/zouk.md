## Sources checked

- [Musée SACEM, Les traces musicales de l’esclavage](https://musee.sacem.fr/index.php/ExhibitionCMS/ExhibitionCMS/View?id=4731) — Documents Kassav’s early recordings, Jocelyne Béroard’s voice and the major 1984 recording by Jacob Desvarieux and Georges Décimus.
- [Red Bull Music Academy, Zouk: An Introduction](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2015/12/zouk-feature/) — Dates Kassav’s creation to 1979 and describes how the zouk sound joins Antillean rhythms and Creole singing with studio electronics and influences from compas and funk.

## Assessment
French Caribbean dance music that Kassav’ helped establish around 1980. The band wove Antillean rhythms and Creole vocals together with drum machines and synthesizers.
