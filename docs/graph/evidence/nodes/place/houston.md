## Assessment

Houston's link to chopped and screwed is strong. Specialist historical writing identifies earlier Houston DJs whose “chopping” and “screwing” techniques predated Screw's synthesis, so describe him as a defining figure rather than sole inventor. Overall confidence: medium-high.
