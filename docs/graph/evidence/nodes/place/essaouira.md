## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Creative Cities Network: Essaouira](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/essaouira) — Describes the city's music culture and names the Gnaoua World Music Festival among its international cultural events.
- [UNESCO/UNDP: Cultural revival in the wake of the Essaouira Festival](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/in/rest/annotationSVC/DownloadWatermarkedAttachment/attach_import_3a642ad6-8cca-4230-bcd3-96635c041502?_=224698eng.pdf) — Case study says the festival was launched by Morocco's government in 1998 and helped bring Gnawa music to wider audiences.

## Assessment

The narrow claim that Essaouira hosts the festival is well sourced. The city's role in Gnawa should not be confused with the tradition's countrywide and community-rooted practice. Overall confidence: high.
