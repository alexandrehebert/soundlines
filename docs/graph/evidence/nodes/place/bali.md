## Sources checked

- [David Harnish, “The world of music composition in Bali,” *Journal of Musicological Research*](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01411890008574780) — Scholarly history of Balinese composition and the development of gong kebyar.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art: Distant Explosions—Kebyar and *The Rite of Spring*](https://asia.si.edu/research/essays/vitale/) — Describes kebyar’s historical development and continuing creative practice.
- [Smithsonian Folkways: Bali—Court Music and Banjar Music](https://folkways.si.edu/bali-court-music-and-banjar-music/world/music/album/smithsonian) — Collection documents Balinese ensembles and performance in kebyar style.

## Assessment

The claim is well supported. Gong kebyar is one tradition among Bali’s varied regional and ritual music practices, rather than a stand-in for all Balinese music. Overall confidence: high.
