# Tuva

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian Folkways: Throat-singing in three cultures](https://folkways.si.edu/throat-singing-unique-vocalization-three-cultures/world/music/article/smithsonian) — Describes Tuvan throat-singing as a living practice and emblem of Tuvan identity.
- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Mongolian art of singing, Khoomei](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/mongolian-art-of-singing-khoomei-00210) — Documents the practice across communities in Inner Mongolia, western Mongolia, and the Republic of Tuva.
- [UNESCO Multimedia Archives: Khoomei cultural phenomenon](https://www.unesco.org/archives/multimedia/document-1596) — Includes material on Tuvan khoomei's history, styles, and significance to Tuvan musical culture.

## Assessment

Tuva is a well-supported home of a distinctive living throat-singing tradition. Related practices also occur across Mongolian communities and neighboring regions; xöömei should not be treated as exclusive to one political territory. Overall confidence: high.
