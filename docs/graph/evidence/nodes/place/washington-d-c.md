# Washington, D.C.

## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian: Duke Ellington](https://www.si.edu/spotlight/duke-ellington) — States Ellington was born in Washington, D.C., and later rose to fame at Harlem's Cotton Club.
- [National Museum of African American History and Culture: Duke Ellington](https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/stories/jazz-appreciation-month-duke-ellington) — Confirms his 1899 birth in D.C. and provides social context for the city.
- [Library of Congress: Today in History](https://www.loc.gov/item/today-in-history/april-29/) — Independently records his birth in Washington, D.C.

## Assessment

The graph correctly separates Ellington's birthplace from his later New York career. Confidence is high for this biographical link; it does not by itself establish Washington as the origin of his musical style.
