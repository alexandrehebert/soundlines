## Sources checked

- [Transmat — official label history](https://transmat.net/en-au/pages/transmat) — Label identifies Derrick May as founder and dates Transmat to 1986.
- [Detroit Historical Society — Derrick May](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/may-derrick) — Local historical reference describes May releasing “Let’s Go” in 1986 before starting Transmat.
- [Music Week archive (1988)](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/UK/Music-Week/1988/MW-1988-04-23.pdf) — Contemporary trade press refers to Transmat Records as May’s label and discusses the early Detroit scene.

## Assessment

1986 is supported by Transmat’s own account, with historical reporting confirming the early chronology. Avoid implying Transmat alone created Detroit techno.

## Remaining uncertainty
