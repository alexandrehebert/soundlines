# Gamelan: special-case loop brief

Status: blocked on workflow support; not renderable by the existing grid pipeline.

## Musical direction

Central Javanese court gamelan in slendro tuning. Let saron and lower demung present a steady core melody while bonang embellishes, kendhang cues the pace and kenong and the deep gong ageng articulate the recurring cycle. Preserve the ringing bronze, hand damping and natural ensemble blend.

## Production constraint

Slendro tuning and colotomic gong cycles need tuning-aware generation and cycle-aware extraction.

Preserve this musical identity rather than inventing a fixed BPM, major/minor key or instrumental substitute. Plan a complete repeatable phrase/cycle, retain its natural tuning and phrasing, and check the seam over four repetitions. No audio generation is authorized by this preparation task.
