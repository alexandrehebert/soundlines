# Qawwali: special-case loop brief

Status: blocked on workflow support; not renderable by the existing grid pipeline.

## Musical direction

South Asian qawwali vocal ensemble in a small live devotional setting. A strong lead voice ornaments a rising phrase and an ensemble answers in unison, with handclaps, a harmonium doubling the line and interlocking tabla and dholak accents. Emphasize growing energy, audible breath and the communal acoustic pulse.

## Production constraint

Lead and response vocals and the devotional arc are central; select a tala and build a vocal-aware cycle workflow.

Preserve this musical identity rather than inventing a fixed BPM, major/minor key or instrumental substitute. Plan a complete repeatable phrase/cycle, retain its natural tuning and phrasing, and check the seam over four repetitions. No audio generation is authorized by this preparation task.
