## Musical direction

Traditional Tuvan khoomei overtone singing in an intimate solo performance. One singer sustains a steady low fundamental, shaping clearly stepped, flute-like upper harmonics through shifts of mouth resonance. Unaccompanied, with natural breathing and no steady drum pulse.

## Production constraint

Overtone singing is the defining sound and has no steady pulse; the instrumental/no-vocals gate is incompatible.

Preserve this musical identity rather than inventing a fixed BPM, major/minor key or instrumental substitute. Plan a complete repeatable phrase/cycle, retain its natural tuning and phrasing, and check the seam over four repetitions. No audio generation is authorized by this preparation task.
