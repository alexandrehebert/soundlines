## Musical direction

Medieval Western European plainchant in a resonant stone chapel. A small unaccompanied ensemble sings a single modal line together, moving in speech-shaped phrases with occasional short melismas and natural breaths. Clear human voices, free rhythm and a long natural room decay.

## Production constraint

Unaccompanied free-rhythm plainchant requires vocals and chant-aware phrase extraction.

Preserve this musical identity rather than inventing a fixed BPM, major/minor key or instrumental substitute. Plan a complete repeatable phrase/cycle, retain its natural tuning and phrasing, and check the seam over four repetitions. No audio generation is authorized by this preparation task.
