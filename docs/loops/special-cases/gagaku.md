## Musical direction

Traditional Japanese court gagaku, an instrumental kangen ensemble. Slow broad phrases in a piercing hichiriki reed doubled and ornamented by ryuteki transverse flute unfold against sustained sho mouth-organ clusters, with widely spaced drum and shoko gong strokes. A spacious natural hall sound with deliberate breath and silence between gestures.
