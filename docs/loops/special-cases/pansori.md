Status: blocked on workflow support; not renderable by the existing grid pipeline.

## Musical direction

Traditional Korean pansori in the sound of a bare stage performance with one singer and a single buk barrel drummer. The lead alternates short stylized Korean narration and forceful elongated sung phrases with rough-grained projection and wide expressive bends, punctuated by changing buk accents. Keep the intimate, exposed singer–drummer exchange.

## Production constraint

Narration and singing with changing drum cycles require a vocal-aware and cycle-aware workflow.

Preserve this musical identity rather than inventing a fixed BPM, major/minor key or instrumental substitute. Plan a complete repeatable phrase/cycle, retain its natural tuning and phrasing, and check the seam over four repetitions. No audio generation is authorized by this preparation task.
