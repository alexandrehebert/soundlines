## Sources checked

- [303 Day — Roland](https://www.roland.com/us/promos/303day/) — Manufacturer timeline says launched in 1981, discontinued in under two years, and recounts Phuture’s tape circulation and 1987 pressing of Acid Tracks.
- [Lifetime Achievement: DJ Pierre and Phuture — Roland Articles](https://articles.roland.com/lifetime-achievement-dj-pierre-and-phuture/) — Interview documents the 1985 pre-release cassette and 1987 Trax pressing; useful primary testimony from the group’s collaborator.
- [Flashback: TB-303 Bassline Synth — Roland Resource Centre](https://rolandcorp.com.au/blog/flashback-tb-303-bassline-synth) — Manufacturer history confirms the 1981 launch and original intended role as bass accompaniment.

## Assessment

The 1981 release is strongly corroborated by Roland. The 1987 phase must not imply that the machine became popular at launch: Roland reports it was discontinued quickly, with later Chicago reuse creating the genre association. Removed Wikipedia citation.

## Remaining uncertainty

The precise origin of the “Acid Tracks” recording and release is often simplified; early club circulation preceded its commercial pressing.
