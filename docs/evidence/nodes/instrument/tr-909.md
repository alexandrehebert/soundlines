## Sources checked

- [The TR-909 Story — Roland](https://www.roland.com/us/promos/roland_tr-909/) — Manufacturer page documents 1983 production and later use by Detroit and Chicago producers, including Derrick May’s TR-909 on Strings of Life.
- [Transmat / Rhythim Is Rhythim label archive — Detroit Historical Society](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/collection/archive/label-transmat-rhythim-rhythim) — Museum archive documents the 1987 release of “Strings of Life,” providing a contemporaneous regional landmark for the instrument’s adoption.
- [Derrick May — Encyclopedia of Detroit, Detroit Historical Society](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/may-derrick) — Museum history contextualizes the international breakthrough of May’s 1980s Detroit releases.

## Assessment

The product’s 1983 launch is supported by Roland, while its later cultural adoption is best anchored to specific Detroit and Chicago recordings. The entry already acknowledged house and techno use; the review removes Wikipedia and treats 1987 as a landmark rather than a generic global phase boundary.

## Remaining uncertainty

Specific “first use” claims are hard to establish; these sources document a significant example, not the beginning of all TR-909 use.
