## Sources checked

- [James Blades, Percussion Instruments of the Middle Ages and Renaissance](https://www.examenapium.it/cs/biblio/Blades1973.pdf) — Historical survey identifies the triangle’s entry in Hamburg Opera in 1710 and discusses late medieval evidence.
- [Grinnell College Musical Instrument Collection: Triangle](https://omeka-s.grinnell.edu/s/MusicalInstruments/item/1824) — Dates Western opera use to early 18th century and symphony use to mid-18th century.
- [Journal of the Society for American Music: Forging a Lineage](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-society-for-american-music/article/forging-a-lineage-kinship-mobility-and-the-zildjian-cymbal/71BDD1FB5865611D5F9C672C16648F2A) — Peer-reviewed context on the mid-18th-century adoption of Ottoman percussion colors, including triangle.

## Assessment

Removed the unsupported circa-1000 graph start. The revised circa-1400 start is explicitly a low-confidence documentary horizon; the well-supported milestones are opera by 1710 and symphony by mid-century.

## Remaining uncertainty

The precise earliest depiction date is debated and not independently resolved here; do not interpret c.1400 as invention.
