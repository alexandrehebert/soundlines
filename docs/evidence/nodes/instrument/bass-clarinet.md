## Sources checked

- [International Bass Clarinet Research Center: Towards a New History](https://www.circb.info/?q=node%2F12567) — Specialist research dates Mayrhofer instrument 1760–70 and earliest surviving bass clarinet solo to Mercadante’s 1834 opera.
- [Iles, The Changing Role of the Bass Clarinet](https://www.circb.info/sites/default/files/Iles_Dissertation.pdf) — Dissertation supports the 1765 Mayrhofer design anchor.
- [Vienna Symphonic Library: Bass Clarinet](https://preview.vsl.co.at/academy/woodwinds/bass-clarinet) — Meyerbeer’s 1836 Les Huguenots solo role and subsequent art-music uptake.

## Assessment

Replaced a vague c.1850 establishment phase with c.1835 as a documented repertoire milestone while clearly limiting its meaning. The graph’s 1765 start refers to a surviving/recorded design, not a settled invention date.

## Remaining uncertainty

Different early instruments have disputed classification as bass clarinet, basset horn, or other low clarinet family forms; historical boundaries are fluid.
