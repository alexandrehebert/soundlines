## Sources checked

- [Vienna Symphonic Library: Timpani](https://www.vsl.co.at/academy/percussion/timpani) — German-speaking screw-tension development at start of 16th century; orchestral use during 17th century.
- [Museums Victoria: Tympani](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/articles/2789) — Supports 16th-century development of tension screws.
- [Case Western Reserve Early Music Instrument Database: Baroque Timpani](https://caslabs.case.edu/medren/baroque-instruments/timpani-baroque/) — Late-17th-century isolated orchestral scorings, regular calls by early 18th century.

## Assessment

The graph’s European technical development and 17th-century orchestra narrative is corroborated. The c.1675 phase is an approximate adoption anchor, not a precise first use; later research source shows isolated late-17th-century examples and regular calls in the early 18th century.

## Remaining uncertainty

Historical orchestral adoption is gradual; keep the caveat that kettledrum traditions long predate European screw-tension timpani.
