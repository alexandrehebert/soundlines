## Sources checked

- [Oxford University Press: The Warehouse and The Music Box](https://academic.oup.com/book/5894/chapter-abstract/149189393) — Music scholar Micah Salkind analyzes the Warehouse and Music Box as foundational spaces for Chicago house, drawing on oral histories, press accounts, and recordings; he centers queer Black and Latino social life.
- [WTTW Chicago: How a Group of Chicago Friends Laid the Foundation for the House Music Movement](https://www.wttw.com/chicago-stories/house-music/foundation-for-the-house-music-movement) — Public-media history situates house in Chicago's underground disco scene and describes the Warehouse's West Loop location.
- [Preservation Chicago: The Warehouse](https://www.preservationchicago.org/the-warehouse-most-endangered-2023/) — Preservation account documents the club's address and its Black and LGBTQ+ clientele; useful venue context, not independent proof that one venue alone invented house.

## Assessment

Chicago as a center of early house is strongly supported. The prior phrasing already credits Black gay clubs in the plural and can remain, while sources caution against reducing the genre to the Warehouse alone. Overall confidence: high for the city association.
