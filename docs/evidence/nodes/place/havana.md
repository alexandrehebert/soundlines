# Havana

Graph ID `place:havana`

**Review status** · Reviewed · UNESCO and music scholarship support Havana’s role while showing that Cuban son also has eastern roots.

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Rumba in Cuba](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/rumba-in-cuba-01185) — Documents Havana and Matanzas as historic urban centers of rumba, while describing its Afro-Cuban and other cultural elements.
- [Peter Manuel, “From Contradanza to Son,” CUNY Academic Works](https://academicworks.cuny.edu/jj_pubs/315/) — Musicological study distinguishes son’s eastern roots from its consolidation and popularization in Havana.
- [Oxford Research Encyclopedia: Music and Eastern Cuban Identity](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/61800/chapter-abstract/546401597?login=false) — Corrects Havana-centric accounts by foregrounding eastern Cuba’s role in national genres including son.

## Assessment

Havana’s recorded and performance history is substantial, but the graph should not imply that son or Cuban music originated only there. Overall confidence: medium.
