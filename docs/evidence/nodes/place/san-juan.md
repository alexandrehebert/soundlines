# San Juan

Graph ID `place:san-juan`

**Review status** · Reviewed · Scholarly sources support San Juan's role in reggaetón's Puerto Rican development while recognizing its Panamanian roots and island-wide context.

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Duke University Press: *Remixing Reggaetón*](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv11hphs8) — Raquel Z. Rivera-Rideau traces the genre's transformation from music associated with San Juan's marginalized neighborhoods into global pop.
- [*Popular Music and Society*: “Oye mi canto”](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/03007760802218046) — Philip Samponaro traces reggaetón's evolution from Panamanian origins through Puerto Rico and its wider Latino youth culture.
- [Library of Congress: “Nación Reggaetón”](https://www.loc.gov/item/hlas-bi2013001267/) — Bibliographic annotation for an academic collection on Puerto Rican reggaetón and its cultural development.

## Assessment

San Juan is a major development and circulation center, not the sole birthplace. The genre's lineages include reggae en español in Panama and development across Puerto Rico, including neighborhoods outside the municipal boundary. Overall confidence: medium.
