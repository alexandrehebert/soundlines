## Sources checked

- [MultiRio, City of Rio de Janeiro: Cariocas in the rhythm of bossa nova](https://multirio.rio.rj.gov.br/index.php/historia-do-brasil/rio-de-janeiro/3366-a-musica-carioca-no-compasso-da-bossa-nova) — Municipal education resource locates early manifestations in Rio’s South Zone in the late 1950s.
- [University of São Paulo: “Brazilian music in the 1950s”](https://revistas.usp.br/revusp/en/article/view/13830) — Music historian Marcos Napolitano analyzes the decade and the historiography around bossa nova’s emergence.
- [Oxford Companion to Jazz: “Jazz and Brazilian Music”](https://academic.oup.com/book/49346/chapter-abstract/422390748) — Scholarly chapter situates bossa nova among Rio musicians and its exchange with samba, jazz, and other traditions.

## Assessment

The late-1950s Rio association is well supported. Bossa nova was a city-based movement within a broader Brazilian music history, not a replacement for samba or other regional traditions. Overall confidence: high.
