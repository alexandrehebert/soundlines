## Sources checked

- [Bibliothèque nationale de France: The 1932 Cairo Congress of Arab Music](https://www.bnf.fr/fr/le-congres-de-musique-arabe-du-caire-de-1932) — National library account documents the landmark international congress, its recordings, and the participating musicologists and composers.
- [Journal of Cultural Analytics: Poet-Composer Collaborations in Modern Egyptian Song](https://doi.org/10.22148/001c.117486) — Scholarly article describes Cairo's early radio stations, Egyptian state radio's role in music circulation, and Egypt's musical centrality in the Arab world.
- [Cambridge Core: Revisiting the Heritagization of Arab Music in Postcolonial Cairo](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-journal-of-middle-east-studies/article/revisiting-the-heritagization-of-arab-music-in-postcolonial-cairo/78CFD06FC4DD5654E0063A018C46EAEF) — Peer-reviewed article treats Cairo's Arab Music Conference and the later institutional shaping of Arab music heritage.

## Assessment

The broad “major centre” claim is supported by the congress, radio, and scholarly institutions. Those sources establish a major hub, not an exclusive center for Arab music. Overall confidence: medium-high.
