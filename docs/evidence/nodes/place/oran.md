## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Raï, popular folk song of Algeria](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/rai-popular-folk-song-of-algeria-01894) — UNESCO records raï as a living Algerian practice and describes its social and regional context; the inscription is national rather than Oran-specific.
- [Cambridge Core: Review of Marc Schade-Poulsen, *Men and Popular Music in Algeria*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-journal-of-middle-east-studies/article/abs/marc-schadepoulson-men-and-popularmusic-in-algeria-modern-middleeast-series-no-20-austin-university-of-texas-press1999-pp-250-3500-cloth-1276-paper/7D799206B1E5CDCF749D6EC4C0893BB6) — Scholarly review identifies raï as most closely associated with Oran and frames the genre's social significance in its place of origin.
- [University of Oran 1: Study in Oran](https://vrre.univ-oran1.dz/uploads/site/file_698672b48da8f5.83515812.pdf) — University profile calls Oran a birthplace of raï; useful local corroboration, not an independent musicological history.

## Assessment

Oran's association is strong, but raï's history spans a wider western Algerian region. The existing graph wording (“associated with the urban development”) appropriately avoids an exclusive-origin claim. Overall confidence: medium-high.
