# St Petersburg

## Sources checked

- [Rimsky-Korsakov Saint Petersburg State Conservatory: About](https://www.conservatory.ru/about-conservatory) — Institution dates its founding to 1862, identifies it as Russia's first higher professional music school, and records Tchaikovsky as its first graduate and Rimsky-Korsakov as professor.
- [Oxford Academic: *Harmony and Discord*, “The Conservatory in Russian Society”](https://academic.oup.com/book/7282/chapter-abstract/151991756) — Scholarly study situates both St Petersburg and Moscow conservatories in the institutionalization of Russian musical life.
- [Moscow Conservatory: history of early Russian composer education](https://www.mosconsv.ru/ru/publication/122396) — Research publication discusses the pedagogical work of Tchaikovsky, Rimsky-Korsakov, and others across the first Russian conservatories.

## Assessment

This supports the city's institutional and educational role. It does not mean all major Russian composers studied there: the Mighty Handful, for example, largely developed outside formal conservatory education. Overall confidence: high.
