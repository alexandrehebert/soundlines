# Santiago

## Sources checked

- [Memoria Chilena, National Library of Chile: Nueva Canción Chilena](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-702.html) — Documents the Peña de los Parra, the 1969 festival at Catholic University, and major movement events in Santiago.
- [Memoria Chilena: Peñas](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/602/w3-article-96425.html) — Describes the Parra siblings' venue as a key hub for Nueva Canción artists and related performers.
- [Memoria Chilena research PDF](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0070060.pdf) — Historical study explicitly states the Chilean movement emerged in Santiago in the mid-1960s and discusses its wider political and musical context.

## Assessment

The city's gathering-point role is supported. Nueva Canción was a Chilean movement with continental connections, not a genre confined to Santiago. Overall confidence: high.
