- [Mississippi Blues Trail: Memphis Blues](https://msbluestrail.org/blues-trail-markers/memphis-blues) — Documents Memphis studios and the many Mississippi-born artists who recorded there.
- [Smithsonian Center for Folklife and Cultural Heritage](https://folklife-media.si.edu/docs/festival/program-book-articles/FESTBK1986_12.pdf) — Discusses Memphis blues and soul labels alongside traditions and musicians from across the South.
