## Assessment

Kept the official 1990 start as the band’s formation phase, but added the important distinction that the Pearl Jam name was adopted in 1991. Their ongoing activity supports an open end.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact 1990 point refers to formation of the group from Mother Love Bone’s surviving members, before Pearl Jam was the settled name.
