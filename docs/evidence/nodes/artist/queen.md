## Sources checked

- [Queen/Sony Music UK artist biography](https://www.sonymusic.co.uk/artist/queen/) — Label biography dates the band’s origin to 1970 and classic lineup to 1971.
- [Freddie Mercury official biography](https://freddiemercury.com/bio/) — Official estate biography recounts Mercury’s role in renaming Smile and Deacon joining in 1971.
- [Queen official discography](https://www.queenonline.com/music) — Official catalog dates the debut album to 1973, distinct from band formation.
- [Associated Press profile of Queen with Adam Lambert](https://apnews.com/article/queen-adam-lambert-tour-legacy-2023) — Reports ongoing Queen touring with Lambert after Mercury’s death.

## Assessment

Corrected the 1973 start, which marks the debut album, to the documented 1970 formation. The classic four-person lineup was complete in 1971. Queen has continued as a performing act with Adam Lambert, so the graph has no terminal year.

## Remaining uncertainty

Mercury-fronted Queen ended with his illness and death in 1991, but the group continued under Queen + Adam Lambert; the graph entry covers the band identity across that change.
