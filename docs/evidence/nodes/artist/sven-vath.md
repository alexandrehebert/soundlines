## Sources checked

- [Philipps University Marburg, Hessian Music Archive](https://www.uni-marburg.de/de/ub/forschen/historische-bestaende-1/historische-bestaende/hessisches-musikarchiv/musik-in-hessen/themen/popularmusik/elektronische-musik/sven-vaeth) — Archive biography dates Väth’s DJ career to 1982 at Frankfurt airport club Dorian Gray and his later label activity.

- [Resident Advisor long-form interview](https://ra.co/features/1484) — Dates his Dorian Gray residency to 1982 and discusses his influence on Frankfurt techno.

## Assessment

Kept 1982 as the well-supported start of the Dorian Gray residency and DJ career; some accounts place his earliest public playing in 1981. The note should be read as career establishment, not first-ever set.

## Remaining uncertainty

Earliest public sets may predate the 1982 residency by a year.
