## Sources checked

- [University of Houston Libraries: Brothers in Rhyme](https://exhibits.lib.uh.edu/s/brothers-in-rhyme/page/dj-screw) — Documents early-1990s development of his techniques and mixtapes.
- [Texas State Historical Association Handbook](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/davis-robert-earl-jr-dj-screw) — Historical entry documents his 1993 breakthrough and 2000 death.
- [University of Houston Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.lib.uh.edu/collections/kk91fm37f?locale=en&page=3) — Archival collection description links his early DJ/mixtape activity to later Screwed Up shop, opened in 1998.

## Assessment

The graph’s 1987 decade start lacks support in the checked authoritative sources. University of Houston locates stylistic development and mixtape practice in the early 1990s; broader DJ practice may have started sooner, so the date is a documented creative-period marker. 2000 is a well-documented endpoint.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ on when he first began DJing versus when chopped-and-screwed production took shape; the graph period tracks the latter.
