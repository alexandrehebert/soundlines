## Sources checked

- [UKF — Origins: Altern-8](https://ukf.com/read/origins-altern-8/) — Long-form interview/history with founding producer Mark Archer describes the 1990 debut and original 1990–93 chart period, followed by a return under his leadership.
- [The Guardian — The old skool reunion](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2002/jan/11/artsfeatures) — Retrospective reports the Stafford duo and eight UK Top 75 hits before their split in 1993.
- [DJ Mag — Altern-8 for Xmas No.1?](https://djmag.com/content/altern-8-xmas-no1) — Interview with Archer confirms Activ-8 as a 1991 release and its chart rise to number three.
- [Resident Advisor — Mark Archer Exchange](https://ra.co/exchange/362) — Long-form interview listing documents the duo’s early-1990s impact and Archer’s ongoing Altern-8 performances.
- [AllMusic — Altern-8](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/altern-8-mn0000180017) — Specialist biography lists the Stafford 1990 formation, original members, and 1990–91 debut releases.

## Assessment

The 1990 start is corroborated. A simple 1990–93 lifespan would be misleading because Archer later revived the name; left the artist end open and documented the original duo’s split and later activity.
