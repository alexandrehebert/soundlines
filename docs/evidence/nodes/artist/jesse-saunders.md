## Sources checked

- [NPR / North Country Public Radio, “From the Warehouse to the world: Chicago and the birth of house music”](https://www.northcountrypublicradio.org/news/npr/1160484070/) — Describes Saunders and Vince Lawrence making “On & On” in 1984 and its status as an early house record.
- [DJ Mag, “Marshall Jefferson: Master of the house”](https://djmag.com/longreads/marshall-jefferson-master-house) — Distinguishes production in 1983 from release in early 1984 on Jes Say Records.
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, “Interview: Jesse Saunders”](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2017/03/jesse-saunders-interview/) — Interview and historical account place Saunders’ foundational house work in 1984.

## Assessment

The 1984 start is anchored to the public release of On & On; DJ Mag says it was produced in 1983 and released early 1984. Set 1983 as the more inclusive career-production marker while recording the release distinction. No evidence for an end date. Confidence: high for 1983–84 milestone; medium for career onset.

## Remaining uncertainty

The phrase “first house record” is contested in house history; use “helped establish” rather than an uncontested first.
