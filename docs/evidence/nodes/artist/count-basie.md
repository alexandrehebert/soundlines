## Sources checked

- [National Endowment for the Arts biography](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/william-count-basie) — dates his Kansas City band work from 1927 and own band from 1935; confirms ongoing work until death in 1984.
- [Rutgers University Count Basie biography](https://countbasie.rutgers.edu/biography/) — places his move to Harlem and career acceleration around 1924; distinguishes that early career start from Kansas City bands and his 1935 Barons of Rhythm.

## Assessment

The 1924 start is supported as an approximate early-career marker (move to Harlem and touring vaudeville work), not as the start of the Basie Orchestra. NEA independently anchors Kansas City band activity in 1927 and formation of his own band in 1935; Rutgers gives more granular 1928/1929 dates. End year 1984 is supported by NEA. Description now names his role and sound without treating a single signature as exclusive.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ slightly on when he joined Walter Page’s Blue Devils/Bennie Moten; retain broad graph start 1924 because it describes his career, while the best-documented bandleader era begins 1935.
