## Sources checked

- [Treccani Italian encyclopedia](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/giovanni-pierluigi-da-palestrina/) — National cultural encyclopedia gives lifespan c.1525–1594 and the 1544 organist appointment.
- [Croatian Encyclopedia](https://www.enciklopedija.hr/clanak/palestrina-giovanni-pierluigi-da) — Dates first professional music post to 1544 and death to 1594.
- [University of the Arts Helsinki study](https://taju.uniarts.fi/bitstreams/d0b727b3-ef8a-474b-a6a6-b94756e700ce/download) — Academic study corroborates 1544 as first professional appointment.

## Assessment

The existing 1544–1594 span is well supported as professional career to death. Childhood choir activity may predate it, but is not precisely dated.

## Remaining uncertainty

Birth year is disputed (1525/26); it does not affect this career span.
