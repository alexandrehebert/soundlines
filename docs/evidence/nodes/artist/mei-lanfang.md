## Sources checked

- [Museum of Chinese in America biography](https://www.mocanyc.org/collections/stories/mei-lanfang/) — Says he trained from age eight and made his stage debut by eleven.
- [National Museum of China exhibition](https://en.chnmuseum.cn/exhibition/exhibition_series/temporary_exhibitions/historical_and_cultural_exhibition/202201/t20220127_253498.html) — Institutional exhibition documents his artistic life and legacy.
- [MIT study on Mei Lanfang’s international reception](https://web.mit.edu/acyhuang/w/Publications/OnStage_English.pdf) — Scholarly study independently places first Beijing stage appearance at age eleven.
- [Palace Museum biography entry](https://www.dpm.org.cn/lemmas/239927.html) — Institutional reference confirms lifespan 1894–1961.

## Assessment

The 1910 approximate start undercounts a documented childhood stage debut. Born in 1894 and debuting by age eleven places the start around 1905; end year 1961 is corroborated by museum references.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact debut date is not established by the sources, so the start remains approximate.
