## Sources checked

- [Rob Fabrie biography — official site](https://www.robfabrie.nl/biography/) — Fabrie’s first-person career account says he and Pigmans formed 3 Steps Ahead in early 1994, then made records together; supports the collaborative origin.
- [Thunderdome: The Dutch Rave with the World’s Fastest, Hardest Music — The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2019/oct/30/thunderdome-the-dutch-rave-with-the-worlds-fastest-hardest-music) — Scene history identifies 3 Steps Ahead’s “Drop It” as a cult hit and records Pigmans’s 2003 death.
- [3 Steps Ahead — Bandcamp](https://3stepsahead.bandcamp.com/) — Artist catalog identifies the Netherlands act with Peter-Paul Pigmans and dates him 1961–2003; primary catalog context.
- [3 Steps Ahead — NTS Radio](https://www.nts.live/artists/5687-3-steps-ahead) — Music-platform biography dates the project’s first tracks to 1994 and distinguishes the brief trio from Pigmans’s later solo use of the name.
- [About Thunderdome — Thunderdome](https://www.thunderdome.com/en/about) — Festival organizer’s history documents the broader gabber movement from 1992 and its continuing revival, contextualizing the act’s scene.

## Assessment

The name is an individual producer’s alias but the project began collaboratively. Rob Fabrie’s own account provides a stronger origin source than Wikipedia; the Guardian situates “Drop It” within gabber culture. Existing 1994–2003 career dates are retained, with no unsupported fame phase added.

## Remaining uncertainty

Public biographies disagree on Pigmans’s birth details and some discography dates; this node records the alias career and avoids unsourced personal-detail precision.
