## Assessment

1990s decade precision obscures the distinction between Wiley’s general musical activity and his documented emergence in London pirate radio. Academic archival citation dates a Rinse FM appearance to April 1997; treat this as earliest checked marker, not necessarily the true beginning.

## Remaining uncertainty

The graph’s start is a sourceable public-career marker only; earlier DJ activity may predate the surviving/cited broadcast.
