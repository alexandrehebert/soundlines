## Sources checked

- [Cambridge University Press — Anton Webern at the Dawn of Modernism, chronology](https://assets.cambridge.org/97810094/53691/frontmatter/9781009453691_frontmatter.pdf) — Recent scholarly monograph chronology dates earliest surviving works to 1899, Im Sommerwind and Schoenberg study to 1904, and traces later composition.
- [IRCAM — Anton Webern biography](https://brahms.ircam.fr/en/composer/anton-webern/biography) — Research center biography documents his musical career, Schoenberg study, and Nazi-era suppression of performances.
- [Chamber Music Society of Lincoln Center — Anton Webern](https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/about-the-music/composers/anton-webern) — Concert-institution biography corroborates the 1904 Schoenberg study and the 1904 orchestral work.
- [AllMusic — Anton Webern](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/anton-webern-mn0001190857) — Existing artist reference retained as discography context; not used to establish earliest composition.

## Assessment

The graph’s 1904 start captured Schoenberg study, not Webern’s compositional beginnings. Cambridge’s chronology identifies surviving works from 1899, so moved the start earlier and separated the 1904 mentorship as a formative milestone.
