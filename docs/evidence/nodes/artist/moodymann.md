## Assessment

Refined the broad 1990s start to 1994, the documented beginning of releases under the Moodymann name/KDJ imprint. Kept the 1997 debut album distinct in the description. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources note uncertainty about which exact release was first; the year 1994 is consistent.
