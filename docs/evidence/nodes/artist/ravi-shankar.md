## Sources checked

- [Sangeet Natak Akademi biography](https://www.sangeetnatak.gov.in/public/uploads/awardees/docs/1740735753_Ravi%20Shankar.pdf) — India’s national academy biography documents his early work in Uday’s dance troupe.
- [Popular Music journal career study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/03007766.2013.767051) — Scholarly article dates his first public sitar concert to 1939 and last career phase through 2012.
- [AllMusic Ravi Shankar biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/ravi-shankars-mn0000404463) — Music reference confirms the early dance career, 1939 recitals and final concert in 2012.
- [TIME, Ravi Shankar career profile](https://time.com/4284939/ravi-shankar-sitar-history/) — Profile distinguishes his initial performance as a dancer from his later sitar career.

## Assessment

Changed the broad 1930s start, which combined his childhood dance touring with his sitar career, to 1939, when the scholarly source records his first public concert on sitar. Kept 2012, the year of his final concert and death.

## Remaining uncertainty

He was already a public performer as a dancer from age 13; 1939 specifically marks his Hindustani music career.
