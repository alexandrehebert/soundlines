## Sources checked

- [Philip Glass official biography](https://philipglass.com/biography/) — Glass’s own biography dates the ensemble’s formation to his 1967 return to New York.
- [English National Opera Philip Glass profile](https://www.eno.org/people/philip-glass/) — Opera company profile dates music for Beckett’s Play to 1965, film score to 1966, and the international breakthrough to Einstein on the Beach in 1976.
- [Praemium Imperiale biography](https://www.praemiumimperiale.org/en/laureate/glass/) — Arts award profile dates the ensemble to 1968 and Einstein premiere to 1976.
- [IRCAM composer biography](https://brahms.ircam.fr/en/composer/philip-glass/biography) — French institute source documents his early professional career and 1976 opera.

## Assessment

The prior 1964 start was unsupported by the sources reviewed. Replaced it with 1965, when Glass contributed music to a theatrical production; 1967–68 marks ensemble formation and 1976 his breakthrough.

## Remaining uncertainty

Biographies differ by a year on the ensemble’s founding (1967 vs 1968); this does not affect the 1965 career onset.
