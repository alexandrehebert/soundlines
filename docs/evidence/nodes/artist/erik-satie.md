## Sources checked

- [Oxford Bibliographies in Music: Erik Satie](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62400/reference-article-abstract/555406310) — Scholarly reference documents Gymnopédies (1889), cabaret work, and later collaborations; confirms lifespan.

- [Oxford Academic introduction to Satie the Bohemian](https://academic.oup.com/book/49319/chapter-abstract/422363833) — Dates professional involvement in Parisian popular music from 1888 to about 1909.
- [IRCAM composer biography](https://ressources.ircam.fr/fr/composer/erik-satie) — Specialist contemporary-music institute biography describes his cabaret work and major early published pieces.

## Assessment

The 1880s decade was broad. 1884 is the first known composition date in reference biographies, while professional Parisian music activity is documented from 1888. The period uses the former as earliest compositional activity; the note clarifies that public/professional career followed.

## Remaining uncertainty

1884 denotes composition, not publication or sustained professional career; dates of early works and their publication differ.
