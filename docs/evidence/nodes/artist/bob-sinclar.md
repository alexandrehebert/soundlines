## Sources checked

- [Bob Sinclar biography, AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/bob-sinclar-mn0000075329/biography) — Biography says the Bob Sinclar moniker and first album Paradise began in 1998, distinguishing that project from earlier DJ work.
- [Bob Sinclar interview, Spirit of House](https://www.spiritofhouse.com/interviews/interviewbobsinclar2.html) — Artist interview recalls 1987 as a formative moment in early DJ/music activity and says the Bob Sinclar project began in 1997.
- [Bob Sinclar profile, Generation Dance](https://generation.dance/en/artists/bob-sinclar/) — Profile dates early DJ work to 1986 under Chris the French Kiss.

## Assessment

Kept 1987 only as an approximate artist-wide DJ start; the Bob Sinclar alias itself begins around 1997–98. Clarified this distinction to avoid treating the stage name as his entire career.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ by one year (1986/1987) for early DJ activity; exact alias launch likewise varies between 1997 and 1998.
