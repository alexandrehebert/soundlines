## Sources checked

- [Death official Bandcamp / Relapse Records](https://death.bandcamp.com/) — states the band was founded in 1983 and ceased to exist after founder Chuck Schuldiner died in 2001.
- [Canadian Association of Music Libraries journal article](https://caml.journals.yorku.ca/index.php/caml/article/download/32101/29347/33362) — academic heavy-metal history identifies Death/Mantas as formed in 1983, debut album Scream Bloody Gore (1987), and ongoing releases until Schuldiner’s death in 2001.
- [Louder / Metal Hammer history of Death](https://www.loudersound.com/features/death-the-story-behind-the-iconic-death-metal-band) — traces early extreme death metal through the increasingly technical/progressive 1991–98 albums and notes the band was put on ice in 1998 as Schuldiner moved to Control Denied.

## Assessment

Replaced the 1980s/2000s decade envelope with exact band history: foundation as Mantas in 1983 and cessation after Schuldiner’s death in 2001. The bandcamp account is official under Relapse, corroborated by a music-librarianship journal. Sources also challenge any static genre label: the early death-metal sound shifted toward technical/progressive metal, and Schuldiner began Control Denied in 1998. No dedicated phase metadata exists in this graph entry, so this trajectory is recorded in description and evidence rather than invented phases.

## Remaining uncertainty

The initial group name was Mantas; the name Death was adopted later (often dated 1984). Period start denotes the band’s founding, not use of the Death name or first album.
