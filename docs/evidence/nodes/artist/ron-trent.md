## Sources checked

- [Red Bull Music Academy lecture](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/ron-trent-stoked-on-trent/) — Trent says he made “Altered States” at 14; the recording came out in 1990. He co-founded Prescription in 1993 with Chez Damier.
- [Resident Advisor biography](https://ra.co/dj/rontrent/biography) — Corroborates his Chicago house background and the 1990s Prescription label work.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/ron-trent-mn0000292019) — Lists “Altered States” as his 1990 release and identifies him as a Chicago house producer.

## Assessment

Kept 1986 as a composition/recording-origin marker rather than first release: sources say “Altered States” was made earlier but released in 1990. The descriptive emphasis now includes both his production and label contribution.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact year of initial composition varies in retellings; 1986 in graph is earlier than the well-corroborated 1990 commercial release.
