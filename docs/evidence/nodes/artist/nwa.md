## Sources checked

- [Los Angeles Times, N.W.A formation history](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/music/la-et-ms-nwa-parental-discretion-20171205-htmlstory.html) — Reports Dr. Dre, Ice Cube, MC Ren and DJ Yella formed the group in 1986.
- [Official Charts Company group profile](https://www.officialcharts.com/artist/25633/nwa/) — Chart authority lists group activity as 1987–1991.
- [AllMusic N.W.A biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/nwa-p77/biography) — Music reference documents the 1987 first album and disintegration after member departures.
- [Library of Congress, Straight Outta Compton essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/StraightOuttaCompton.pdf) — National Recording Registry essay documents the group’s breakthrough album and context.

## Assessment

Refined the 1980s marker to a 1986 formation date, supported by the Los Angeles Times. The 1987 public discography explains why some sources use 1987 as the start. Kept 1991 as the group’s terminal year, while noting its effective breakup process.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources distinguish formation (1986) from first group releases/official chart activity (1987); the graph period uses formation.
