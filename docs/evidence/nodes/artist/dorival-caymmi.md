## Sources checked

- [Fundação Joaquim Nabuco research biography](https://pesquisaescolar.fundaj.gov.br/pt-br/artigo/dorival-caymmi/) — Researcher-written biography documents 1936 songwriting contest/professional turn, 1938 move to Rio and 1939 first disc.
- [Dicionário Cravo Albin da Música Popular Brasileira](https://dicionariompb.com.br/artista/dorival-caymmi/) — Brazilian popular-music reference confirms 1939 first Odeon disc and early radio work.
- [The Guardian obituary](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2008/nov/24/1) — Independent obituary dates his first composing competition to 1936 and notes songwriting began as a teenager.

## Assessment

The former 1930s decade was broad but gave no defensible milestone. 1936 is a documented early public/professional turning point; his first commercial disc followed in 1939. These dates avoid implying the recording was the start of his composing.

## Remaining uncertainty

He wrote songs as a teenager before the 1936 contest; this graph period denotes documented public career, not first composition.
