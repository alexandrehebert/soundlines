## Sources checked

- [Le Monde, “De Motorbass à Cassius”](https://www.lemonde.fr/musiques/article/2019/06/20/de-motorbass-a-cassius-philippe-zdar-en-six-titres_5478995_1654986.html) — Reports the duo formed in 1996 as L’Homme qui valait trois milliards, then quickly renamed Cassius; first album 1999 was released three years after formation.
- [Ed Banger Records, Cassius artist page](https://www.edbangerrecords.com/pages/cassius) — Label page identifies Zdar and Boom Bass as the duo, calls them French electronic/French touch, and notes Boom Bass continued after Zdar’s 2019 death.

## Assessment

Keep 1996 as the formation year: Le Monde directly dates the duo’s formation, while distinguishing the brief precursor name from Cassius proper. The open-ended period remains appropriate because the label reports that Boom Bass continued the project after 2019. Existing AllMusic citation was not retrievable here; these two sources substantiate the core identity, origin, and continuity.

## Remaining uncertainty

A source retrieved in this pass did not give the exact date when the duo adopted the Cassius name; 1996 is the documented formation year, with the renaming described only as rapid.
