## Sources checked

- [R&B Hall of Fame, 2019 program](https://rbhalloffamemarksms.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/2019-RB-HOF-Program-Book.pdf) — Biographical program identifies Guy as formed in Harlem in 1987 by Teddy Riley, Aaron Hall and Timmy Gatling.
- [Los Angeles Times, “Guy Reunion Album: More Producers Than Inspiration”](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2000-jan-23-ca-56629-story.html) — Contemporary 2000 review confirms the group reunited and released Guy III after an early-1990s breakup.
- [AllMusic, Guy artist profile](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/guy-mn0000651156) — Specialist bio corroborates late-1980s formation and breakup context.

## Assessment

Kept 1987 as the formation year and updated the period endpoint to 2000, the documented reunion album year; the earlier 1991 endpoint wrongly implied permanent cessation. This date range represents first formation through reunion, not uninterrupted activity. Confidence: high.

## Remaining uncertainty

The group’s later touring and intermittent reunions mean the graph’s single interval compresses multiple active periods.
