## Sources checked
- [Huun-Huur-Tu official history (Ted Levin notes)](https://www.huunhuurtu.com/history) — founded 1992 as Kungurtuk by Khovalyg, Alexander and Sayan Bapa, and Kuvezin; later renamed.
- [BBC Radio 3 Awards for World Music 2004 profile](https://www.bbc.co.uk/radio3/world/awards2004/profile_huunhuurtu.shtml) — "Since their early incarnation as Kungurtuk in 1992"; debut 60 Horses in My Herd (1993).
- [BnF authority record: Huun-Huur-Tu](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb140035046) — collectivity record: "Naissance : 1992", traditional vocal-instrumental group from Tuva.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art concert notes](https://asia-archive.si.edu/podcast/throat-singers-from-tuva-huun-huur-tu/) — 2002 Freer Gallery concert, lineup and Tuvan music essay.
- Wikidata Q304391 has no AllMusic ID (P1728); MusicBrainz also lacks one. The old AllMusic URL could not be verified and was replaced.

## Assessment
Formation in 1992 is confirmed by the official site, BBC and BnF, so precision is now year. The group is still active. Sources replaced with verified institutional ones.
