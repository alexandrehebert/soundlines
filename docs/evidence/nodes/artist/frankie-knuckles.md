## Sources checked

- [The HistoryMakers: Frankie Knuckles biography](https://www.thehistorymakers.org/biography/frankie-knuckles) — Oral-history archive dates his Warehouse opening-night DJ set to March 1977 and documents his later career.
- [Chicago LGBT Hall of Fame: Frankie Knuckles](https://chicagolgbthalloffame.org/knuckles-frankie/) — Institutional biography dates the Warehouse residency to 1977–87 and links the club to house’s name.
- [Resident Advisor: Frankie Knuckles, Waiting on my angel](https://ra.co/features/2086) — Long-form scene history places his New York-to-Chicago DJ career and Warehouse residency in context.

## Assessment

Retained the established 1977 Warehouse milestone in the description but changed the career start to the 1970s, since the node period represents active creative career and Knuckles was already DJing in New York before Chicago. His death in 2014 is an evidenced career endpoint.

## Remaining uncertainty

The decade-level start reflects documented early-1970s New York work; the exact first professional date is not settled here.
