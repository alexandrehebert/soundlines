## Sources checked

- [Purna Das Baul official biography](https://baulsamrat.in/index.php/about/) — Artist biography dates his first performance outside Bengal and All India Radio appointment to 1942; says 1962 was his first international Baul tour.
- [World Music Central profile](https://worldmusiccentral.org/artist-profiles-purna-das-baul/) — Independent world-music reference also dates his professional radio appointment to 1942.
- [The Caravan, Baul touring history](https://caravanmagazine.in/reportage/sake-song) — Longform Indian reporting documents his 1962 Helsinki festival and international profile by 1967.
- [GetBengal profile](https://development.getbengal.com/home/story_detail/bob-dylan-and-purna-das-baul-an-immortal-friendship) — Regional cultural outlet reports he was the first Baul artist recorded by All India Radio in 1954.

## Assessment

Changed c.1950 to 1942, supported by his official biography and an independent profile documenting public performances and AIR work from that year. The often-cited 1954 milestone is more specifically his first AIR recording/broadcast as a Baul artist.

## Remaining uncertainty

The official source is self-published and gives a distinct 1954 Baul-recording milestone; the general career start is less independently documented than his international appearances from 1962.
