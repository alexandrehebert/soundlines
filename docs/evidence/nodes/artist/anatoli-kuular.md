## Sources checked
- [Huun-Huur-Tu official history (Ted Levin notes)](https://www.huunhuurtu.com/history) — replaced Albert Kuvezin; borbangnadyr singer, xomus and byzaanchi; visited the US in 1993 with the Tuva Ensemble.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art concert notes](https://asia-archive.si.edu/podcast/throat-singers-from-tuva-huun-huur-tu/) — performed with Huun-Huur-Tu at the Freer Gallery, 22 Aug 2002.
- [BnF authority record: Huun-Huur-Tu](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb140035046) — lists Kuular, Anatoly among members.
- [Wikipedia: Huun-Huur-Tu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huun-Huur-Tu) — joined after the first album (1993); quit in 2003, replaced by Andrey Mongush.
- MusicBrainz lists birth 1963 and death 2015-02-12, and HHT membership 1993–2002 (not cited; unverified). No Wikipedia/Wikidata article found.

## Assessment
The prior circa-1980 start had no support. Documented activity begins with the Tuva Ensemble just before 1993, so the start is now circa 1990. The end remains open because no reliable source confirms the death date reported by MusicBrainz.

## Remaining uncertainty
Pre-1993 career and later activity/death date unverified.
