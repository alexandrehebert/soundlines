## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/tabu-ley-rochereau-mn0000015762/biography) — Dates his African Jazz professional debut to 1954, an earlier date than other discographies.
- [AfroDisc release chronology](https://afrodisc.com/artists/artists-tabu-ley-seigneur-rochereau/artists-tabu-ley-seigneur-rochereau-original-releases-overview/) — Dates the start of recording with African Jazz to 1959.
- [Univers Rumba Congolaise biography](https://www.universrumbacongolaise.com/artistes/pascal-tabu-ley-rochereau/) — Places his entry into African Jazz in 1959 at age 19.

## Assessment

Changed the vague circa-1950 start to 1959, the year AfroDisc and Congolese-focused biography sources agree on for his African Jazz entry. AllMusic gives 1954 for a professional debut, so 1959 is specifically the recording/group phase. End 2013 is his death year.

## Remaining uncertainty

Early dates conflict: AllMusic’s 1954 professional-debut claim versus multiple discographic sources dating African Jazz recording/entry to 1959.
