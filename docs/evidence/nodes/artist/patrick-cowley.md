## Assessment

Changed the unsupported 1973 start to 1977, the documented professional-career onset. Cowley’s San Francisco arrival and electronic experimentation preceded commercially active work and should be treated as a preparatory phase. Death in 1982 is established.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some retrospective sources describe a longer period of musical experimentation; 1977 refers specifically to professional activity.
