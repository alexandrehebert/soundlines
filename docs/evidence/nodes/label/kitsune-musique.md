## Sources checked

- [Kitsuné founders, official Maison Kitsuné](https://maisonkitsune.com/mk/fr/les-fondateurs-maison-kitsune/) — Brand history identifies Loaëc and Kuroki as co-founders and describes the music label as part of the venture.
- [Kitsuné Musique profile, Beatportal](https://www.beatportal.com/articles/1581604-behind-the-label-kitsune-musique) — Dance-music platform dates the Paris label to 2002 and summarizes its cross-genre identity.
- [Kitsuné profile, South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/magazines/post-magazine/article/1599326/behind-label-maison-kitsune) — Independent profile confirms the 2002 music/fashion launch and founders.

## Assessment

Confirmed year and founders. Description distinguishes Kitsuné Musique as the label within a broader lifestyle company, rather than treating the entire brand as a record label.

## Remaining uncertainty

The sources often date the combined company; an exact date for the first record release was not established.
