## Sources checked

- [Def Jam profile, Universal Music Group](https://www.universalmusic.com/label/def-jam/) — Parent company dates founding to 1984 and describes early independent hip-hop identity.
- [Def Jam resolution, New York City Council](https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/View.ashx?GUID=F3FE3E59-146B-4194-9913-C40C47399B15&ID=12578812&M=F) — City Council historical statement connects the 1984 “I Need a Beat” release with the label’s official launch.
- [Def Jam at 25, TIME](https://time.com/3780916/bring-the-noise-25-years-of-def-jam/) — Retrospective details Rubin’s NYU origins and partnership with Simmons.

## Assessment

Kept 1984 as the year associated with the first release and label launch. Description clarifies Rubin initiated the imprint and Simmons became a co-founder/partner; avoids implying the fully developed business structure existed on day one.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some accounts emphasize Rubin’s initial dorm-room imprint and date the Rubin–Simmons partnership slightly later within the early period.
