## Assessment

Kept circa 1985. Accounts vary on the label’s exact legal start and who should be called its cofounder; Vince Lawrence and Jesse Saunders have claims that complicate a simple “founded by Sherman” account. The node now says it grew from Sherman’s pressing business and the wider scene, without assigning sole authorship.

## Remaining uncertainty

Trax’s early corporate history and founder credit remain contested; date describes the label’s emergence rather than a verified incorporation date.
