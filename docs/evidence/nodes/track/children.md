## Sources checked

- [Official Charts — Children](https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/robert-miles-children/) — Official chart history says the single was released in November 1995, documents its UK chart run, and describes it as a pioneering dream-house track.
- [Insomniac — Robert Miles “Children”](https://www.insomniac.com/music/from-the-crate-robert-miles-children/) — Retrospective records an initial January 1995 EP issue, later major-label release and the song’s trance/house crossover.

## Assessment

1995 is correct for the first issue and subsequent single; Official Charts refers specifically to the later single. The node now distinguishes that from the January EP appearance, avoiding confusion with the 1996 Dreamland album.

## Remaining uncertainty

First release date is based on the source’s January EP history; country-specific major-label dates vary.
