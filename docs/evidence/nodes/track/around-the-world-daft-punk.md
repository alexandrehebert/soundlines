## Sources checked

- [Daft Punk official site — Homework](https://daftpunk.com/homework/) — Official album page lists “Around the World” and dates Homework to January 20, 1997 in Europe and March 25 in the U.S.
- [Capitol Records — Daft Punk, 1997](https://www.capitolrecords.com/daft-punk/) — Virgin Legacy account dates the single to April 1997 and identifies it as the second single from Homework.
- [Official Charts — Around the World](https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/daft-punk-around-the-world/) — UK chart record documents the single’s chart history and first chart date in 1997.

## Assessment

Kept 1997. The single came after the album’s European release in January, and the graph date correctly gives the recording’s release year rather than the later chart peaks. French house is an appropriate representative association.

## Remaining uncertainty

Release dates are territory-specific; the note uses the album’s European date and the April single date without treating them as interchangeable.
