## Sources checked

- [Apple Music — Autobahn](https://music.apple.com/us/song/726155181) — Track listing dates the album version to November 1974 and distinguishes the long title track from the short single edit described in the album notes.
- [AllMusic — Autobahn](https://www.allmusic.com/album/autobahn-mw0000195085) — Specialist review describes the album as a new direction for Kraftwerk and notes roots of electro-funk, ambient, and synth-pop.
- [Academic article — NEU! Europe and Krautrock](https://doi.org/10.1080/13619462.2021.1925551) — Historical scholarship says Autobahn marks a shift in Kraftwerk’s instrumentation and sound away from other electronic Krautrock approaches.

## Assessment

Kept 1974 for the album track, not the shortened single, which followed in 1975. Challenged the simple Krautrock label: specialist and scholarly accounts describe the album as a transition toward electronic pop. Lowered the track→Krautrock association to low confidence; it is useful as a transitional example, not an unambiguous genre anchor.

## Remaining uncertainty

Classification depends on whether Krautrock is treated as a broad scene label or a narrower sound category; the entry now makes the transition explicit.
