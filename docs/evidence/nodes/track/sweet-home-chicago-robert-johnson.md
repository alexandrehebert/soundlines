## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian Folkways — Sweet Home Chicago liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/SFW40204.pdf) — Notes that Johnson originally recorded the song during his 1936–37 sessions; places it in blues tradition.
- [Smithsonian Institution — Robert Johnson collection](https://collections.si.edu/search/detail/edanmdm%3Asiris_sil_1090504?print=yes) — Catalog documents Johnson's 1936 San Antonio and 1937 Dallas recording sides, including the song.
- [The Blues Foundation — song history](https://www.blues.org/award-search/sweet-home-chicago-has-become-an) — Specialist organization identifies Johnson's 1936 Vocalion version and contextualizes earlier related blues motifs.

## Assessment

Blues identity and Johnson's canonical version are well established. Year should be recorded as 1936, matching Smithsonian and Blues Foundation evidence; keep confidence high.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some secondary sources round the session/date or give 1937; archival summaries place the relevant Johnson session in 1936.
