## Sources checked

- [Chess Records — Chuck Berry](https://www.chessrecords.com/artist/chuck-berry/) — Official label history describes Maybellene's origin from the country standard Ida Mae and its major Chess success.
- [Chuck Berry official site — About](https://www.chuckberry.com/about/) — Artist archive dates the first recording session to May 21, 1955 and the summer hit to 1955.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame — Chuck Berry](https://www.rockhall.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Chuck_Berry_1986.pdf) — Hall biography identifies Maybellene as Berry's 1955 Chess release and first major hit.
- [Chuck Berry Database — Maybellene](https://www.crlf.de/ChuckBerry/cbdb/song/135-MAYBELLENE.html) — Specialist discography details Chess 1604, July 1955 issue and session/release history.

## Assessment

The 1955 year and Chuck Berry attribution are well supported. The track adapts the western swing tune Ida Red, and its authorship/publishing history has been contested; Berry is the recording artist and credited writer in the original release context.

## Remaining uncertainty

Later legal and publishing settlements changed some credits; preserve source-specific wording rather than assuming a simple single-author history.
