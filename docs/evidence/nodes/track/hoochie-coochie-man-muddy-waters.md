## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress — National Recording Registry essay](https://lcweb2.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/HoochieCoochieMan.pdf) — Registry essay labels the 1954 recording as Muddy Waters and says Willie Dixon brought him the song in late 1953.
- [Willie Dixon official timeline](https://www.willie-dixon.com/timeline.html) — Dixon archive records that Muddy Waters recorded his song in 1954.
- [Chess Records — Muddy Waters](https://www.chessrecords.com/artist/muddy-waters/) — Label history identifies Waters as a Chess artist and names Hoochie Coochie Man among his defining recordings.

## Assessment

The 1954 date and Muddy Waters attribution are supported by the National Recording Registry and the writer's archive. Willie Dixon is the songwriter; do not mistake his authorship for the original performer. The blues classification is clear.

## Remaining uncertainty

The single was issued as I'm Your Hoochie Cooche Man; capitalization and punctuation vary across catalog records.
