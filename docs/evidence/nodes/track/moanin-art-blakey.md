## Sources checked

- [Blue Note Records — Moanin'](https://www.bluenote.com/spotlight/art-blakey-the-jazz-messengers-moanin/) — Label history identifies Bobby Timmons as composer of the opening title track and details the 1958 ensemble personnel.
- [Smithsonian Folkways — liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/SFW40820.pdf) — Liner notes give the October 30, 1958 session, personnel and original Blue Note BLP 4003 issue.
- [Universal Music Japan — Moanin'](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/p/uccu-99020/) — Label catalog gives original release date November 1, 1958.

## Assessment

1958, the Art Blakey & the Jazz Messengers performance, and hard bop classification are supported. The title track was composed by pianist Bobby Timmons; early LP cataloging used the ensemble name, while its opening track later gave the album its common title.

## Remaining uncertainty

Keep performer attribution at ensemble level; Art Blakey is the leader and drummer, not sole performer or composer.
