- [Bill Monroe Sessions — Bluegrass Discography](https://www.ibiblio.org/hillwilliam/BGdiscography/sessions/BillMonroeSessions.php) — Session discography dates the Bluebird recording to October 7, 1940.

## Assessment

The 1940 date refers to Monroe's recording and initial Bluebird release; the song itself predates it in Jimmie Rodgers' 1930 version, whose title was Blue Yodel No. 8 / Mule Skinner Blues. Bill Monroe's version was highly influential in shaping bluegrass. Keep the graph focused on Monroe's recording.

## Remaining uncertainty

Credit and naming vary: some early records used Mule Skinner's Blues; do not collapse it with the earlier Jimmie Rodgers version.
