## Sources checked

- [Juan Luis Guerra official history](https://juanluisguerra.com/historia/) — Artist biography dates Bachata Rosa to December 11, 1990 and describes its international role in popularizing bachata; the song is included on that album.
- [Official artist-channel audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BF3yLhIgkXU) — Official channel metadata credits Juan Luis Guerra 4.40 and Bachata Rosa, ℗1990.
- [University of the Americas thesis](https://dspace.udla.edu.ec/bitstream/33000/9701/1/UDLA-EC-TLMU-2018-51.pdf) — Academic thesis describes La Bilirrubina as merengue, composed/published by Guerra and 4.40 in 1990.

## Assessment

The 1990 year and artist attribution are supported. The song is merengue, although it appears on Bachata Rosa, an album important to bachata's international spread; the album title should not lead to misclassification of this track.

## Remaining uncertainty

The official store/page doesn't distinguish the song's single-release day from album release; graph year remains correct.
