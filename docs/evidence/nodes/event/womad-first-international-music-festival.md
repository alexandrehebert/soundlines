## Assessment

A documented festival-format innovation and cross-cultural circulation project, not the invention of non-Western music.

The first WOMAD took place at the Bath and West Showground in Somerset in July 1982. Its programme put musicians from many countries alongside British rock acts, giving a festival format to a project of cross-cultural musical exchange that later travelled internationally.
