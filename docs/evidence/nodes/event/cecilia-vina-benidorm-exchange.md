## Assessment

A specific, sourced transatlantic circulation mechanism rather than merely a festival founding.

Cecilia’s winning performance at Viña del Mar in 1965 was linked to an exchange with Spain’s Benidorm festival. Chile’s National Library identifies this route as helping bring Chilean Nueva Ola to Europe and strengthen its place in Chilean musical life.
