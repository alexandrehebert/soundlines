## Assessment

A documented public presentation of an established local musical legacy; organisational succession is qualified.

The first Detroit Electronic Music Festival took place at Hart Plaza in 2000 with Carl Craig involved in its organisation. It brought Detroit’s electronic-music legacy into a large public festival; later Movement and Fuse-In names involved different producers, and techno itself was already established.
