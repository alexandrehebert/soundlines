## Assessment

An early enduring choral-festival institution, dated as a convention rather than a proven first meeting.

The cathedral choirs of Gloucester, Worcester and Hereford developed Music Meetings conventionally counted from about 1715. Their recurring collaboration underlies a long-lived British choral festival and later platform for new works. The exact first meeting is uncertain, and the present festival name dates from 1838.

1715 is the conventional counting date, not a securely documented first edition; the event represents an early-eighteenth-century institutional development.
