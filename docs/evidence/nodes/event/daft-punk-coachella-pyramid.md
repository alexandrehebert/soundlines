## Assessment

A specifically documented performance innovation with an explicit institutional historical assessment.

Daft Punk’s 2006 Coachella set combined an illuminated pyramid with tightly synchronised music and multimedia. The Rock and Roll Hall of Fame identifies it as a turning point in electronic dance performance production; that assessment concerns presentation and influence, not the invention of electronic live music.
