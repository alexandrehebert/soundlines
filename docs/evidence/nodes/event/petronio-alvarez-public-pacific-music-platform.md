## Assessment

A source-backed regional musical platform; the Wikipedia discrepancy is made explicit.

The Petronio Álvarez festival began in Cali in 1997 according to Colombian broadcasting archives and university histories. It created a public platform for Pacific Colombian musical traditions, including marimba ensembles. The checked Spanish Wikipedia article instead gives 1996, so the dating discrepancy remains explicit.
