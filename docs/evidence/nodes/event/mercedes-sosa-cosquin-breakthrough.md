## Assessment

A documented career breakthrough with regional musical significance.

Jorge Cafrune introduced Mercedes Sosa at Cosquín in January 1965. The appearance became a major step in her national recognition and connected a defining voice of Latin American song with the festival’s folk audience; it was not her first musical activity.
