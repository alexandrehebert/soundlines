A dated performance tied to a documented platform for Lebanese musical creation.

Fairuz gave an outdoor concert at Baalbeck’s temple complex in 1957. The festival’s Lebanese Nights provided a platform for Fairuz, the Rahbani brothers and other Lebanese creators alongside international programming, helping establish a distinct national performance presence.
