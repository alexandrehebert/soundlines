## Assessment

A dated broadcast milestone linking festival performance and national popular-song culture.

The first Sanremo song contest was broadcast by RAI from the city’s casino in January 1951. The recurring national broadcast became an important meeting point between popular song, performers’ careers and Italian mass media.
