## Assessment

A documented development in reggae concert presentation and circulation.

The first Reggae Sunsplash took place in Montego Bay in June 1978. Its concert platform and subsequent international reach became part of reggae’s circulation beyond Jamaica; the festival was not the origin of reggae or dancehall.
