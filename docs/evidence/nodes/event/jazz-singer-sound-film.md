# The Jazz Singer brings synchronized song to feature film

**Review status** · reviewed

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Birth of Motion Picture Sound](https://americanhistory.si.edu/blog/2012/07/you-aint-heard-nothing-yet-curator-dwight-blocker-bowers-on-the-birth-of-motion-picture-sound.html) — Dates the New York premiere to 6 October 1927 and notes earlier synchronized-sound films, while describing the film’s popular impact.
- [American Film Institute Catalog, The Jazz Singer](https://catalog.afi.com/Film/1535-THE-JAZZ-SINGER) — Distinguishes the New York premiere on 6 October 1927 from the wider release date in February 1928.
- [Museum of Modern Art, The Jazz Singer](https://www.moma.org/collection/works/315769) — Characterizes its historical role as a sound film that captured public imagination while recognizing earlier sound-film precedents.

## Assessment

The 1927 date is confirmed, but it was not the first film with synchronized sound. Its significance lies in popularizing synchronized dialogue and song sequences in a feature film. Keep wording away from “first sound film.”
