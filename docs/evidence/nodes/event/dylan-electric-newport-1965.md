## Assessment

A documented performance at the folk/rock boundary, with disputed audience interpretation kept qualified.

Bob Dylan performed with an electric band at Newport Folk in July 1965, including Like a Rolling Stone. The set became a focal point in debates about the folk revival and rock; accounts differ about the audience’s reaction, and the performance was not the invention of electric folk music.
