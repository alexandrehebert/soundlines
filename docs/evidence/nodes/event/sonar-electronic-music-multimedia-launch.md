## Assessment

A dated music/multimedia presentation format, without inventing a later programme in 1994.

The first Sónar in Barcelona in 1994 joined electronic and experimental music with multimedia arts. An academic case study documents this combination as central to the festival’s format; the later Sónar+D congress should not be backdated to the first edition.
