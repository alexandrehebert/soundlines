## Sources checked
- [Cambridge Companion to Krautrock, Introduction](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-krautrock/introduction/53DFF7E1B1FBC4A09925676C4356B96D) — frames Krautrock as a sound of revolt “from the late 1960s to the mid-1970s”; traces rediscovery “beginning in the mid-1990s”.
- [Cambridge Companion, “Krautrock and the radical politics of 1968”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-krautrock/krautrock-and-the-radical-politics-of-1968/44940BF421701F6A6BCE261BF0519EE3) — links emergence to 1968 politics.
- [Adelt, Krautrock: German Music in the Seventies (JSTOR)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.3998/mpub.9206841) / [University of Michigan Press, Adelt book page](https://press.umich.edu/Books/K/Krautrock2) — academic monograph “German Music in the Seventies” (2016).
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, Jon Savage on Kosmische Musik (2011)](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2011/12/krautrock-feature) — label coined by the British press in the early 70s; Cope’s Krautrocksampler (1995); “from obscurity to global fascination” over ~20 years.
- [Guardian, 2008, Matt Bolton meets the original Krautrockers](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2008/may/09/popandrock1) — bands “all started in Germany in the late 1960s” (Irmin Schmidt); 2000s reissues and a wave of Krautrock-inspired bands.
- [The Quietus, review of Stubbs’ Future Days (2014)](https://thequietus.com/articles/15957-david-stubbs-future-days-krautrock-book-review) — “a marginal pop cultural force at the time”, now “firmly established” in rock’s canon.
- Britannica (blocked, 403).

## Assessment
Emergence c. 1968 and the early-1970s golden-age anchor are confirmed. The known issue (open-ended period rendering at peak to the present) is fixed: a niche phase from 1976 reflects the original scene’s mid-1970s end and its marginal contemporary standing, and an established phase from 1995 reflects the documented mid-1990s rediscovery and later canonisation. Description now notes the British origin of the label and adds Faust; non-Wikipedia sources were already strong and press sources were appended.

## Remaining uncertainty
The 1976/1995 boundaries are editorial approximations; Can and Kraftwerk remained active after 1976 but outside the original movement framing.
