## Assessment
Carnegie Hall supports a 1948 start through the Orioles, so “circa” precision fits better than a decade bucket. Phases are now 1948 emerging, 1955 golden-age (the mid-1950s run of crossover hits) and 1963 niche (low confidence; the style faded from the charts in the early 1960s, often linked to the British Invasion and the rise of soul). The 1965 end is kept as the conventional close of the style’s original era.

## Remaining uncertainty
The 1963 decline year and 1965 end are conventional rather than sourced to an exact year; revival groups continued afterwards.
