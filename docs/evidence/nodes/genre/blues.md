## Assessment
The 1890s start is supported by Carnegie Hall’s rural-blues history, so the period stays. The old phase set was rebuilt as 1890 emerging → 1920 golden-age (recording boom documented by Carnegie Hall) → 1950 established (low confidence). That last marker reflects the post-war Chicago electric scene on one side and the Folkways account of fading mass popularity before the 1960s revival on the other. A phaseNote now says the shading is editorial, not a popularity measure.

## Remaining uncertainty
The 1950 “established” label compresses a complex story (Chicago peak, wider decline, 1960s revival). Folkways and the Library of Congress could not be re-fetched.
