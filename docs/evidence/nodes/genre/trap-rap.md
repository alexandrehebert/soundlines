## Sources checked

- [Berklee Online, Trap music history](https://online.berklee.edu/takenote/trap-music-where-it-came-from-and-where-its-going/) — Music education account says trap came together in early-2000s Dirty South, while earlier Atlanta artists used the term in 1990s.
- [Google Arts & Culture, Atlanta hip-hop history](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/a-brief-history-of-atlanta-39-s-rise-in-hip-hop/VgXRISv_xGKfyQ?hl=en) — Local history traces conceptual references to Goodie Mob’s Soul Food (1995) and early-2000s Atlanta ascent.
- [Complex, “The Trap” history](https://www.complex.com/music/a/david-drake/the-trap) — Journalistic scene history explains that “trap” first referred to a real place/circumstance rather than a fixed genre category.
- [Music Origins Project, trap music](https://musicorigins.org/item/trap-music/) — Public-facing history distinguishes the term’s place-based meanings and later musical category.
- [AllMusic trap rap overview](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/trap-rap-ma0000013576) — Specialist category cross-reference; not used alone to assign origin.

## Assessment

Moved the graph’s decade-precision 2003 start back to c.1995 for documented Atlanta precursors/term use, with an explicit 2003 establishment marker for Trap Muzik and a more codified sound. Removed later 2010/2018 phases as generic prominence claims. Confidence: medium; sources disagree about when a place-based lyrical theme became a genre.

## Remaining uncertainty

Later electronic dance “trap” is a separate appropriation/branch; keep taxonomy clear between Southern hip-hop trap and EDM trap.
