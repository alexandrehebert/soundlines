## Sources checked

- [Cambridge, The Cambridge Introduction to Serialism](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/63414/excerpt/9780521863414_excerpt.htm) — Specialist textbook cites Schoenberg’s first use of twelve-tone rows in fall 1921 and emphasizes serial music coexists with tonality.
- [Oxford Academic, The Spread of Serialism](https://academic.oup.com/book/49294/chapter-abstract/422536119) — Music history chapter traces Schoenberg, Berg and Webern’s establishment of twelve-tone technique and later reactions.
- [Cambridge, Cambridge History of Twentieth-Century Music](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-twentiethcentury-music/proclaiming-the-mainstream-schoenberg-berg-and-webern/52CF958C178E7F1EDB53315E86B9422A) — Scholarly history cautions that the composers’ claim to represent musical mainstream was historically contested.
- [Society for American Music, Revisionist History of Twelve-Tone Serialism](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-society-for-american-music/article/abs/revisionist-history-of-twelvetone-serialism-in-american-music/DBBF1D5303FCF3452639CF2C794A51DD) — Peer-reviewed article documents serial practice in the US from late 1920s to present, challenging a 1970 endpoint.

## Assessment

Moved start from approximate 1920 to c.1921, the source-backed point when Schoenberg first used twelve-tone rows systematically. Removed 1970 as an endpoint and the golden-age/niche phases: serialism continued and remains a method, not a bounded commercial genre. Broader serial procedures also extend beyond twelve-tone practice and can predate this marker. Confidence: high for Schoenberg’s method date; medium for the broad term’s boundaries.

## Remaining uncertainty

The graph description’s early start applies chiefly to twelve-tone serialism; other serial methods and precursors require a broader separate treatment.
