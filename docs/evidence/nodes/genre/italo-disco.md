## Sources checked

- [University of Milan repository: Historical Emergence and Current Uses of the Term italodisco](https://air.unimi.it/handle/2434/1206515) — Peer-reviewed Dancecult article record; identifies the term’s emergence and uses as its subject. Abstract/full text not available in this retrieval.
- [Erasmus University Rotterdam: Italo-disco’s Post-ironization](https://pure.eur.nl/en/publications/its-easy-to-laugh-at-my-desire-italo-discos-post-ironization/) — Academic conference abstract says the genre peaked in the first half of the 1980s and persisted through niche audiences, DJ sets, reissues, covers and remixes.
- [Oxford Research Encyclopedia: Italo Disco chapter](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/62613/chapter-abstract/560908054) — Scholarly chapter listed among the graph’s sources; full content could not be retrieved in this check.

## Assessment

Kept an approximate circa-1980 start and open-ended continuation. The evidence supports an early-1980s peak and later persistence, not a fixed 1986 end or the repeated 1992/2002/2011 “established” phases; removed all unsupported phases.

## Remaining uncertainty

The start date is an editorial approximation; the history of the genre name differs from the earlier musical practices it came to describe.
