## Sources checked

- [University of Huddersfield PhD thesis on minimal techno](https://eprints.hud.ac.uk/id/eprint/18067/1/nbougaiefffinalthesis.pdf) — Academic thesis dates emergence to early 1990s and associates the style with second-generation Detroit artists.
- [DJ Mag: Minimal Nation as a defining work](https://djmag.com/features/how-robert-hoods-minimal-nation-became-defining-work-minimal-techno) — Dates Robert Hood’s landmark album to 1994 and contextualizes its influence.
- [Resident Advisor: Minimal Nation review](https://ra.co/reviews/25097) — Specialist dance publication frames Minimal Nation as a defining codification, not the only origin.
- [Journal of the Society for American Music: Minimalism and Politics of Inclusion](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-society-for-american-music/article/minimalism-and-the-politics-of-inclusion/7C493403C42F41942A79ADBA3DCE53C7) — Peer-reviewed discussion identifies minimal techno as a translocal subgenre/scene popularized in late 1990s and 2000s.

## Assessment

Retained an early-1990s Detroit emergence marker, but removed the false end date and unsupported golden-age phase. Scholarship traces broader scene popularity into the late 1990s and 2000s.

## Remaining uncertainty

“Minimal techno” labels diverse local and translocal practices; one Detroit timeline should not be treated as the whole genre.
