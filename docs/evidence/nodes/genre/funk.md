## Assessment
A start around 1965 (Brown’s mid-1960s breakthroughs) is consistent with Carnegie Hall’s “late 1960s” formation, so precision becomes circa. Phases are simplified to 1965 emerging → 1970 golden-age → 1980 established. The redundant 1978/1985/1990 markers were removed: Carnegie Hall describes traditional bands fading by the early 1980s while the sound continued through electro-funk, R&B and hip-hop sampling. A phaseNote was added.

## Remaining uncertainty
Whether 1965 or 1967 marks “emergence” is a matter of convention.
