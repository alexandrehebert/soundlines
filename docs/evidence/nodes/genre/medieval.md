## Sources checked

- [Oxford Scholarship Online: The Medieval Era](https://academic.oup.com/book/44835/chapter-abstract/384399450) — Choral-history chapter defines a medieval span around 900–1500 based on notation and polyphonic development, illustrating that musical period boundaries differ from general history.
- [Oxford History of Western Music, Volume 1](https://academic.oup.com/book/60817) — Musicology reference distinguishes early medieval music before 800 from the 800–1400 medieval period.
- [Cambridge University Press: The Cambridge History of Medieval Music](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805215/13487/excerpt/9780521513487_excerpt.pdf) — Scholarly introduction describes the broad convention of the Middle Ages from the fall of Rome through the fifteenth century and cautions against treating it as stylistically uniform.

## Assessment

Retained the conventional circa-500 to 1400 era span, acknowledging that musicological chronologies vary with surviving notation and stylistic transitions. Removed the nonsensical “emerging/golden-age/established/niche” phases, which imply a prominence curve over a millennium.

## Remaining uncertainty

The start and end depend on whether one follows general historical divisions, notation, or musical style; the bar is a navigational convention.
