# J-pop

Graph ID `genre:j-pop`

**Review status** · Reviewed: J-pop name separated from older Japanese pop history

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Inter-Asia Cultural Studies: J-pop, from the ideology of creativity to DiY music culture](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/14649370903166093) — Peer-reviewed article says the label was coined by an FM radio station in the late 1980s and broadened through the 1990s.
- [Atsushi Sasaki, Kobunsha Shinsho: The Bursting of the Bubble and the Birth of J-POP](https://shinsho.kobunsha.com/n/n8443864cef27?hl=en) — Music historian discusses the late-1980s birth and subsequent popularization of the term, distinct from the longer history of Japanese popular music.
- [Myung Joong Kim, Ritsumeikan Repository](https://ritsumei.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2001858/files/ir_37-3_kim%2Cmyung%20joong.pdf) — University-hosted academic paper dates first J-Pop use to 1988 on Tokyo FM station J-WAVE.

## Assessment

Changed the graph’s 1970 start, which conflated J-pop with its older musical antecedents, to circa 1988 for the documented label. Removed the speculative 1985 “golden age” and 2010 phase; the broad category continues and has no defensible end date.

## Remaining uncertainty

The naming event is often tied to J-WAVE’s 1988 launch; exact first use is recounted differently in secondary sources.
