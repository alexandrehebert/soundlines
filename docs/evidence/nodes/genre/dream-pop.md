## Assessment

The previous 1984 start and 1995 end incorrectly narrowed a style that scholarship places in the 1980s and says persists into the present. Reset the period to decade-level 1980s with no endpoint. Removed the 1988 golden-age and 1993 niche phases: available sources show later artists and continued practice, not a genre-wide prominence decline. Replaced generic confidence with medium because the label is broad and overlaps adjacent styles.

## Remaining uncertainty

The original academic Oxford citation could not be inspected; its bibliographic relevance is unverified and it is excluded from graph sources pending later link review. Dream pop and shoegaze boundaries are porous; scenes span the UK and US, and one origin date is too precise.
