## Assessment

The original 1958–1965 window approximates the canonical U.S. pop wave; evidence supports emergence at 1958 and mainstream prominence in early 1960s. A hard endpoint risks obscuring later groups, so removed it. “Golden age” now refers explicitly to the market wave rather than the complete history of women’s vocal groups.

## Remaining uncertainty

“Girl groups” is a broad gendered category, not one uniform sound; periods vary by geography, genre, and whether chart prominence or group activity is measured.
