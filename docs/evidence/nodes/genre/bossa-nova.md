## Sources checked

- [Agência Senado — Chega de Saudade recording and the birth of bossa nova](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2008/08/01/gravacao-de-chega-de-saudade-marca-o-nascimento-da-bossa-nova) — Brazilian Senate retrospective presents João Gilberto’s July 1958 Chega de Saudade recording as formal birth, while explicitly acknowledging a minority view that Elizete Cardoso’s April 1958 LP launched it.
- [Library of Congress — Brazil de Mis Amores](https://blogs.loc.gov/music/2023/09/brazil-de-mis-amores/) — Library of Congress account dates Jobim and de Moraes’s first bossa nova composition to 1957 and identifies later international recognition, reinforcing a gradual late-1950s formation.
- [AllMusic — Bossa Nova](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/bossa-nova-ma0000002548) — Existing reference citation retained for genre overview; not used to settle a singular origin or end date.

## Assessment

Removed 1965 as a genre end date: it confused an early concentrated wave with the style’s continuing history. Retained a circa-1958 start, lowered confidence from categorical “golden age” framing to medium-confidence emergence, and recorded the competing 1958 launch claims.
