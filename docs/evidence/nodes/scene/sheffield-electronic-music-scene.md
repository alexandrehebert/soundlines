## Sources checked

- [Sheffield City Council: Music in Sheffield](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/libraries-archives/access-archives-local-studies-library/research-guides/music-in-sheffield) — Archive guide documents local synth-pop and electronic artists and Warp Records’ Sheffield founding.
- [University of Greater Manchester: Sheffield electronic music](https://ub-ir.bolton.ac.uk/esploro/outputs/bookChapter/Resisting-that-Fascist-Groove-Thang--/999435708841) — Scholarly chapter documents the city’s lineage from Cabaret Voltaire and Human League through later electronic scenes.
- [Welcome to Sheffield: electronic music history](https://www.welcometosheffield.co.uk/living/city-culture/the-pioneering-city-of-electronic-music/) — City cultural guide documents local artists, clubs, and Warp’s role.

## Assessment

A city network of post-punk, synth-pop and dance music around groups such as the Human League, Cabaret Voltaire and label Warp. Overall confidence: high.
