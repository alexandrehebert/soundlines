## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Congolese rumba](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/congolese-rumba-01711?RL=01711) — Recognizes a shared tradition across the two Congos and its urban music, dance, and exchange histories.
- [UNESCO Courier: New life for Congolese rumba](https://courier.unesco.org/en/articles/new-life-congolese-rumba) — Describes orchestras and music industries on both banks of the Congo River.
- [UNESCO Creative Cities: Kinshasa](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/kinshasa) — City profile records the capital’s music institutions and living rumba networks.

## Assessment

A shared musical history centered in Kinshasa and Brazzaville, shaped by local musicians, studios, dance bands and transnational exchange. Overall confidence: high.
