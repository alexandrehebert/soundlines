## Summary

All 25 edges have direct supporting evidence for a corresponding recording/performance by the named lead artist or contributor. Recommend high confidence for 18 links and medium for seven where the graph’s artist node omits relevant ensemble or collaborator credit: Goldie/Metalheads/Diane Charlemagne, Desmond Dekker & The Aces, DJ Screw’s multi-rapper freestyle, King Tubby/Augustus Pablo, Prince & The Revolution, Charlie Parker’s ensemble, and Juan Luis Guerra 4.40. “Manteca” is also a collective big-band recording; preserve Chano Pozo’s contribution in the track note even though Dizzy Gillespie’s lead-artist edge is well supported.
