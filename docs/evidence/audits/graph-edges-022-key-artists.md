## Summary

The batch supports 22 direct key-artist claims, with medium confidence appropriate for the more career-spanning or stylistically broad cases. Kraftwerk’s ambient relationship is better represented as influence than as key-artist status; Lil’ Keke is a major Houston/Screwed Up Click figure but not established here as a key artist of the DJ Screw-originated production technique. The previously high Kraftwerk–ambient confidence should be reduced if the edge remains in any form.
