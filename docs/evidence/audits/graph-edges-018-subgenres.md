## Summary

Most relationships are usable when the edge is understood as a broad stylistic taxonomy. The strongest recommended corrections are `hip-hop-funk` (funk influences hip-hop; reverse the edge and use `influences`), `kwaito-house` (house is an influence rather than a complete parent), and `indie-rock-alternative-rock` (overlapping categories do not establish one-way derivation). `glam-rock-rock-and-roll`, `girl-groups-pop`, `impressionism-modern-classical`, and `math-rock-indie-rock` need explicit category caveats. House/disco, garage-house/disco, grime/UK garage, and gqom/house are plausible lineages but should not erase their distinct communities, hybrid origins, or other influences.
