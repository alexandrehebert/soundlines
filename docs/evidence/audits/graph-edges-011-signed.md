# Signing-relationship audit — batch 011

Scope: the 19 live `signed_to` edges left unaudited by batch 009. I checked whether sources establish a signing/recording contract, rather than inferring one from an artist's releases on a label. Findings here are recommendations only; the graph and per-edge notes have not been changed. Where an institutional catalogue blocked direct opening, I used its indexed catalogue metadata and marked the limitation in the finding.

## Summary

19 edges reviewed: 14 supported (including caveated supported edges) and 5 recommended for revision. The revision candidates are Mogwai–Rock Action, Muddy Waters–Chess, Otis Redding–Stax, Phuture–Trax, and Underground Resistance–Tresor; available evidence points to ownership/founding or release/licensing rather than a verified signing. The signed-to audit still needs consolidation into the graph and per-edge notes.
