## Summary

The 20 pending edges were checked. Most are conventional genre hierarchies, but the parent for merengue, salsa, son cubano, and tango is a broad Latin catalog umbrella; nu metal’s relation to heavy metal is contested/overbroad; serialism is a compositional method rather than a genre and should not be a `subgenre_of` edge.
