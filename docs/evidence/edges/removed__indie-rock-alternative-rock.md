# Removed edge: Indie rock → alternative rock

Former graph ID `lineage:indie-rock-alternative-rock`

**Disposition** · Removed (2026-11-05)

The relation encoded a one-way derivation that the sources do not establish. Peer-reviewed genre scholarship treats “indie” as a shifting industry, aesthetic, and social category; “alternative” overlaps with indie but is not a clean parent that makes indie rock a descendant. The edge is removed rather than preserve a misleading hierarchy.

Sources: [peer-reviewed study of indie rock](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/0300776042000300972); [AllMusic alternative/indie taxonomy](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/alternative-indie-rock-ma0000012230); [PLOS One study of genre boundaries](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0155471).
