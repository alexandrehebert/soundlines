**Finding** · The club and Knuckles’ DJ practice were central to house’s emergence; TIME specifically says he pioneered house at the Warehouse by reshaping soul/R&B records with drum-machine loops. This venue-to-genre relation is best read as scene-shaping/central, not as the venue independently causing the genre.
