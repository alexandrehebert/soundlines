Graph ID `key:giorgio-moroder-hi-nrg`

## Sources checked
- [Carroll, “Re-appraising Hi-Nrg” (Dublin City University thesis)](https://www.doras.dcu.ie/27918/1/PhD%20Carroll.pdf) — characterizes Moroder as a forefather of Hi-NRG while noting the style’s multiple pioneers.
- [Hi-NRG queer history paper](https://ler.letras.up.pt/uploads/ficheiros/19288.pdf) — likewise calls him a forefather, but discusses Cowley, Orlando, Summer, and the scene’s collective development.
- [Classic Pop genre history](https://www.classicpopmag.com/features/popscene-hi-nrg/) — traces the sound’s origin to “I Feel Love,” produced by Moroder and Pete Bellotte.

## Assessment
Medium confidence retained. Moroder was an important precursor, but the evidence does not support treating him as the sole or definitive Hi-NRG artist. Removed Wikipedia citations.
