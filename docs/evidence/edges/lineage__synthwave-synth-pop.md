# Synth-pop → Synthwave

**Review status** · Source checked (2026-11-05)

**Graph note** · Synthwave revives and reworks 1980s popular electronic aesthetics; synth-pop is a relevant neighboring influence, alongside film scores and other electronic music. The scholarship supports this broader 1980s revival more directly than a single-parent lineage, so confidence is lowered.

**Sources checked** · [Journal of Popular Music Studies, University of California Press](https://online.ucpress.edu/jpms/article/33/1/70/116330/Reconstructed-NostalgiaAesthetic-Commonalities-and) — scholarly analysis of synthwave’s 1980s nostalgia and aesthetic. [Sound Stage Screen, University of Milan](https://riviste.unimi.it/index.php/sss/article/view/14136) — traces synthwave inspiration to 1980s film composers and broader pop culture. [Routledge Resource Centre](https://resourcecentre.routledge.com/books/9781032732039) — scholarly ethnographic history of synthwave’s online genre community.

**Assessment** · Research treats synthwave as a contemporary reconstruction of 1980s popular culture and electronic aesthetics. Synth-pop is one relevant influence among film-score traditions and other electronic styles; the reviewed sources do not support it as synthwave’s sole or direct parent, so confidence is medium.
