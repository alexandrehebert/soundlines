# Chopin → Nocturne in E-flat major, Op. 9, No. 2

Graph ID `recorded:nocturne-in-e-flat-major-op-9-no-2-chopin`

**Disposition** · Removed after source review (2026-11-05)

**Finding** · The sources cited for the graph describe Chopin as composer, not performer. The Library of Congress recording catalog identifies a 1910s performance of the work but lists it as an audio recording of Chopin’s composition, not Chopin performing it (,). Chopin died in 1849, before sound recording existed.
