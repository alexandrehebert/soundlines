## Sources checked
- [RBMA Daily, Arthur Baker on “Planet Rock” (2013)](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2013/04/key-tracks-arthur-baker-on-planet-rock) — producer Arthur Baker: they were “trying to emulate the sound of Kraftwerk”, built on “Numbers”.
- [Newcastle University / The Conversation, Adam Behr on Florian Schneider (2020)](https://www.ncl.ac.uk/press/articles/archive/2020/05/conversationkraftwerk/) — Numbers and Trans-Europe Express “the lynchpin” of Planet Rock.
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, Jon Savage on Kosmische Musik (2011)](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2011/12/krautrock-feature) — Trans-Europe Express inspired early Bronx hip hop.

## Assessment
Planet Rock’s use of Kraftwerk material is documented by its producer and by academic commentary. Raised to high; 1982 period and note kept.
