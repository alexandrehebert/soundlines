## Assessment

The Library of Congress country history discusses the broader Nashville country scene but does not connect Robertson’s 1922 Victor session to it. The Texas State Historical Association account says Robertson met Henry Gilliland in Richmond, then traveled to New York to record with Victor on June 30, 1922. This session is therefore not an instance of involvement in the Nashville scene; at most, it is relevant background context for early commercial country recording. Confidence is low. The New York location is supported by the TSHA account: https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/robertson-alexander-eck
