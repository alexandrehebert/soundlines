Summary: most component claims are credible, but `fusion_of` overstates co-equal parentage for several edges. Reassess `IDM—techno` (direct evidence not found), electro house’s electro endpoint, and taxonomic overlaps for blues-rock and tech-house. Afrobeat, jazz-rap, new jack swing, and reggaeton are well-supported multi-source hybrids; annotate their additional roots rather than treating the listed pair as exhaustive.
