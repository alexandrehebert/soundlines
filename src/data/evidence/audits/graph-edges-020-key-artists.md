## Summary

Twenty-four links are supportable with tighter scope and confidence adjustments; Jamie xx–UK garage is not supported as a “key artist” edge and should be removed or modeled as influence. Main qualifications: Hardwell spans multiple electronic styles; J Dilla is principally a hip-hop producer with strong jazz-rap/neo-soul influence; Jamie xx and John Digweed fit narrower house substyles better than undifferentiated house; Coltrane’s spiritual-jazz tag applies most clearly to his later work; and Johnny Cash’s Americana association is retrospective.
