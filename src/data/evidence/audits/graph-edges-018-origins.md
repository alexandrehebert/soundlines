Findings assess the exact city/country claim and distinguish a label’s corporate origin from later base, and a genre’s origin from an influential scene. ## Cross-check summary

Most label-city links are direct, but the city claim must track what “origin” means. Ruthless Records’ Compton link is contradicted by contemporaneous reporting placing its founding in the San Fernando Valley. Stax’s Memphis identity followed Satellite’s 1957 founding in Brunswick. Source Records’ Paris address remains unverified. Crydamoure and Roulé align with the Paris-area/French-house scene, while the available evidence does not always establish a legal office inside Paris city limits. Salsa’s Cali genre-origin edge was removed separately by the parent after scholarship identified Cali as a major adoption/performance center rather than salsa’s origin.
