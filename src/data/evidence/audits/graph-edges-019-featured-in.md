## Summary

All 25 edges have direct support for characteristic or notable use. The original medium ratings were raised where institutional, scholarly, or specialist sources directly establish the association (for example sitar, tanpura, jazz trombone, and vibraphone). Confidence remains medium for TB-303 in broad trance/psytrance, triangle in the Classical period, Romantic trumpet, tuba in broad jazz and Romantic music, and turntables in boom-bap because evidence concerns narrower subscenes, transitions, or production practice. Consider more specific genre labels such as acid trance/Goa trance for TB-303; the current broad labels need these caveats.
