## Summary

The 25 associations are supported at varying scopes. Highest-confidence edges represent ensemble-defining or historically central use (hichiriki/gagaku, jinghu/Peking opera, harpsichord/Baroque, mandolin/bluegrass, and Minimoog/progressive rock). Confidence remains medium where the association is niche, regionally variable, or attached to a prominent sub-scene rather than the whole genre (harp/jazz, maracas/cumbia, Minimoog/G-funk, modular synth/ambient or Krautrock, and Romantic harp/oboe). No edges were removed in this source-only batch; several notes should preserve the stated scope limits.
