## Summary

Twenty-three edges are supported with varying scope; two weak career-level claims are removed: Eric Prydz–electro house and Frankie Knuckles–garage house. Explicit caveats preserve the retrospective or umbrella nature of Emmylou Harris–alt-country, Satie–Impressionism, Four Tet–IDM, Fatboy Slim–breakbeat and Eric Prydz–EDM. The graph should keep these distinctions in its individual evidence notes.
