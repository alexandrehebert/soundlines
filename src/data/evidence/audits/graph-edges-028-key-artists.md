## Summary

All 25 edges were reviewed. Recommended raises to high: Shabba Ranks–dancehall, Shang Xiaoyun–Peking opera, Siti binti Saad–taarab, Skream–dubstep, Slayer–thrash metal, So Solid Crew–UK garage, Soundgarden–grunge, and Snoop Dogg–West Coast rap. Retain/recommend medium or medium-high where the genre is secondary, broad, contested, or phase-specific (Selena–cumbia, Slint–math rock, Slipknot–nu metal, Slowdive–dream pop, Snoop Dogg–G-funk, Rolling Stones–blues rock, Stravinsky–modern classical, Sven Väth–trance). Remove or sharply downgrade Sonic Youth–math rock and Squarepusher–breakcore: the available serious sources support adjacent influence or occasional stylistic overlap, not key-artist centrality.
