## Summary

Twenty-three edges are supportable as artist-to-genre associations, generally with high confidence and explicit stylistic caveats. Two are better modeled as influence: King Crimson influenced math rock, and highlife influenced aspects of King Sunny Adé’s juju sound; neither is well described as a key artist of the target genre. Joy Division is proto-goth rather than an unqualified gothic-rock act, and several artists’ multi-style careers warrant medium confidence.
