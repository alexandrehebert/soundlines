## Cross-check summary

All 25 edge IDs were inspected. Strongest anthem-level sources were found for “My Generation,” “Nkalakatha,” “One Love,” “Original Nuttah,” and several historical/canonical works. The most material challenges are exact track credit/version for Nusrat’s “Dam Mast Qalandar,” Romeo Santos being credited through Aventura on “Obsesión,” track-specific khoomei evidence for “Orphan’s Lament,” and genre precision for “Oye Como Va” (mambo/cha-cha-chá traditions vs. the broader Latin-jazz label). Confidence recommendations evaluate the combined track, genre, and anthem/canonical relationship, not just whether a song exists.
