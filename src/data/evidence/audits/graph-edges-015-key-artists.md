## Follow-up cautions

- The `Celia Cruz → Son cubano` claim is the main possible relationship-level overstatement: sources establish son in her recorded repertoire but not its primacy. Consider replacing the genre edge with a repertoire note.
- `Chief Keef → Drill` is supported as association, not as originator; Chicago drill had earlier innovators.
- `Debussy → Impressionism` is a conventional retrospective label, not an uncontested self-identification.
- `Chopin → Romantic music` and `Coltrane → Modal jazz` describe period/style associations across careers, not exclusive styles.
