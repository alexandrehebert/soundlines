# Artist–genre edge removals

## Charlotte de Witte → minimal techno

Removed `key:charlotte-de-witte-minimal-techno`. The graph cited a general biography and a review describing a single EP, neither of which established minimal techno as a durable artist-level genre association. Sources checked for this audit support Charlotte de Witte as a techno artist, but do not substantiate the narrower minimal-techno label. The broader techno edge remains. This avoids promoting a track/release-level description into a career-level classification.

## Ron Hardy → Garage house

Removed `key:ron-hardy-garage-house`. Directly checked sources place Hardy in Chicago house and identify him as an early DJ at the Chicago Music Box. Garage house is tied to New York’s Paradise Garage lineage. The original graph cited Wikipedia only and conflated the two scenes; the Chicago house association remains represented by the separate Ron Hardy → Chicago house edge. Sources: [Chicago Black Social Culture Map](https://cbscmap.omeka.net/items/show/51); [Red Bull Music Academy Daily](https://web03125.microsites03.redbull.com/2014/12/den-one-feature/).
