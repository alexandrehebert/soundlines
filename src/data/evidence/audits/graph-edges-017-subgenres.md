# Subgenre hierarchy audit batch 017

Scope: the next 25 pending `subgenre_of` edges, ending with the two French-house parent edges. Sources checked 2026-11-05. The review tests both direction and the exactness of the proposed parent; geographic umbrellas, production methods, and movement names are flagged where they do not behave like ordinary genre trees. Findings and recommendations only; graph, tracker, and per-edge evidence notes were not changed.

Coverage: 25 of 25 pending edges were checked. The strongest structural challenges are parent choice for acid/deep house (use House, with Chicago as origin context), the French-house/French-Touch relationship (overlapping movement/style labels rather than parent-child), and chopped-and-screwed (a production method/scene practice more than a conventional genre). “Latin” works as an industry/geographic umbrella for several regional genres, but evidence should not imply a single stylistic lineage.
