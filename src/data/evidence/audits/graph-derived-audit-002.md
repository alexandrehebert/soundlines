## Summary

Direct checks support many edges but expose several that should not remain high-confidence `derived_from` claims. Strong candidates for retyping/removal or direction review are post-rock → alternative rock, trance → acid house, trip-hop → hip-hop, and vaporwave → hip-hop. Post-hardcore is more precisely tied to hardcore punk than to punk rock generally. Existing high ratings should be reduced for minimal techno → Detroit techno and post-hardcore → punk rock unless the graph note defines those as broad lineage. No graph or tracker edits were made in this audit.
