## Cross-check notes

Three types of overstatement recur here: Nashville as a later country-music industry hub rather than the genre’s origin; Chennai as the modern institutional center of Carnatic music rather than the tradition’s origin; and regional gamelan associations attached to an overly generic node. Cologne’s minimal-techno edge and Versailles’ French Touch edge describe scene ties, not demonstrated singular origins. Several artist edges (João Gilberto, Lalon Fakir, Lil’ Keke, DJ Minx) describe formative or career communities rather than verified birthplaces.
