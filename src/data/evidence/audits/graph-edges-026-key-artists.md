## Summary

The batch supports most direct artist-to-genre links. Remove Pixies–emo because influence and shared alternative-rock space do not establish key-artist centrality. Keep broad or contested tags at medium: Pete Rock’s jazz rap, Pixies’ indie/noise-rock, Pizzicato Five’s J-pop, Portishead’s trip-hop (which its members reject), Queen’s glam-rock period, QOTSA’s stoner-rock roots, Rammstein’s industrial-metal taxonomy, and the Ramones’ pop-punk precursor role.
