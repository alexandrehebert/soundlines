- Prioritized institutional histories, scholarly works, artist/label sources, and established specialist music outlets. Aggregator taxonomies were used as corroboration when direct sources were limited.
- Confidence reflects the strength and centrality of each artist-to-genre relation. Several genres apply to a career phase, influence, or subset of a catalog rather than defining the artist as a whole.
