## Summary

All 25 edges have support, but several confidence labels should change. The clearest qualifications are Fugazi–emo (historical linkage, not a straightforward core genre act), Moroder–Italo disco (precursor/influence rather than unqualified membership), Goldie–breakbeat (medium rather than high), and GosT–darksynth (medium because the narrower label is fluid). Gang Starr–jazz rap, Goodman–swing, Goldie–jungle, and Green Day–pop-punk have stronger evidence than their former medium ratings suggested.
