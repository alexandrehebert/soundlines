## Overall recommendation

All 25 performer/instrument or production-tool claims are supported by opened evidence. 20 warrant high confidence; five should retain medium pending a narrower check or ontology adjustment: Blakey (drum-kit specificity), Debussy (performance vs. study), El Anka (source form), DJ Shadow (relation semantics), and Future (entity/relation semantics).

## Consolidation
Twenty-four artist/instrument edges were updated with the opened sources and recommended confidence; five remain medium with their stated scope caveats. `plays:future-auto-tune` was removed because it models software vocal processing as an instrument-playing relation; its source and rationale remain in the edge note.
