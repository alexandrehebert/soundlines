## Cross-check summary

The strongest concerns are not unsupported city associations but category slippage: the origin edges for broad genres often identify an influential scene, festival, or hub. Keep those edges only with their scene-level qualifier. Specific caveats: Rai arose across western Algeria before flourishing in Oran; Peking opera was synthesized from traveling and regional forms before consolidating in Beijing; Cali is a salsa center and local dance style rather than salsa’s birthplace; French Touch is Paris-centered with a distinct Versailles-linked artist cluster, and Daft Punk’s Versailles association is inaccurate; amapiano’s origins remain contested across Gauteng townships. Congolese rumba is explicitly shared across Brazzaville and Kinshasa. The most confidently supported exact-scene links are Bronx hip-hop, Birmingham’s Black Sabbath-era metal scene, CBGB’s downtown Manhattan scene, Chennai’s music season, Chicago blues, and Durban gqom.
