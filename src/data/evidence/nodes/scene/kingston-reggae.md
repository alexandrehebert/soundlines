## Sources checked

- [Performance Matters: Jamaican sound systems and knowledge](https://performancematters-thejournal.com/index.php/pm/article/view/427) — Ethnographic research documents Kingston sound-system practice, expertise, and community value.
- [Smithsonian Archives Center: Jamaican music collections](https://www.si.edu/object/archives/components/sova-acma-03-027-ref1934) — Archival holdings document Jamaican recording, sound-system, and performance practices.

## Assessment

A layered Kingston-centered history of Jamaican recording, sound systems, studio practice, and performance. Reggae grew from earlier Jamaican forms and cross-border musical exchange rather than from a single venue or individual. Overall confidence: high.
