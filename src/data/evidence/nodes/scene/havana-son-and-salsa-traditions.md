## Sources checked

- [National Park Service: Salsa Is More Than Salsa](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/salsa-is-more-than-salsa.htm) — Cultural history describes Cuban son and its later influence on salsa arrangements and New York musical practice.
- [CUNY musicology: From Contradanza to Son](https://academicworks.cuny.edu/jj_pubs/315/) — Study complicates simple rural-to-urban origin stories, arguing for son’s consolidation in Havana in the early twentieth century.
