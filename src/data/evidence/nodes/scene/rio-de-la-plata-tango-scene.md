## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Tango](https://ich.unesco.org/en/rl/tango-00258) — States that tango developed among urban lower classes in both Buenos Aires and Montevideo within the Río de la Plata basin.
- [Uruguay UNESCO Commission: Tango](https://comisionunesco.org.uy/rutas-unesco/tango/) — Identifies tango as a shared tradition of Uruguay and Argentina and names popular neighborhoods in Montevideo and Buenos Aires.

## Assessment

A shared urban tradition around Buenos Aires and Montevideo, shaped by migration, dance, song and exchange across the estuary. Overall confidence: high.
