## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Peking opera](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/peking-opera-00418) — States that Beijing, Tianjin, and Shanghai are performance centers; the form is practiced widely across China.
- [National Museum of China: Mei Lanfang exhibition](https://en.chnmuseum.cn/exhibition/exhibition_series/temporary_exhibitions/historical_and_cultural_exhibition/202201/t20220127_253498.html) — Institutional exhibition documents Mei Lanfang’s artistic life and role in the form.
- [Beijing Municipal Government: Peking Opera](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/culture/202005/t20200511_1894129.html) — Historical account traces Anhui troupes’ arrival in Beijing in 1790 and the subsequent development of the form.

## Assessment

A performance tradition with deep roots in Beijing, associated with artists including Mei Lanfang and many other performers and companies. Overall confidence: high.
