## Sources checked

- [UNESCO World Heritage nomination: Historic Centre of Vienna](https://whc.unesco.org/document/1118) — Describes Vienna’s association with the development of European music, especially Viennese Classicism and Romanticism.
- [Vienna Tourist Board: Classical music](https://www.wien.info/en/art-culture/music-stage-shows/classical-music-345730) — Documents Vienna’s musical institutions and composers from different periods.
- [University-press volume: *Vienna: A Musical History*](https://downloads.ctfassets.net/ulsp6w1o06p0/3FQxwDZ5gws44pflYkxaaL/6694122f368da16d7d784c3b70d5c71c/7709_Lowres.pdf) — Scholarly overview emphasizes the city’s varied classical, folk, and popular traditions.

## Assessment

A historically important urban center in which composers from different backgrounds worked; not a single school or exclusive origin for classical music. Overall confidence: high.
