## Sources checked

- [Oxford Academic: *Nashville Cats*](https://academic.oup.com/book/36601) — Travis D. Stimeling’s history documents Nashville record production from 1945–75 and the establishment of a national recording industry.
- [Oxford Handbook of Country Music: Recording industry](https://academic.oup.com/edited-volume/34626/chapter-abstract/295016349) — Charles Hughes analyzes Nashville’s central but complicated role in country music’s broader recording history.
- [Country Music Hall of Fame: RCA Studio B](https://www.countrymusichalloffame.org/experiences/studio-b/about-studio-b) — Museum account documents the studio and Nashville production network.

## Assessment

A recording, publishing and broadcast center for country music, built alongside regional traditions across the American South. Overall confidence: high.
