## Sources checked

- [The Music Academy, Madras: institutional history](https://musicacademymadras.in/history/) — The Academy says its annual conference practice, beginning in 1929, grew into Madras’s December music season.
- [Sangeet Natak Akademi: Carnatic music](https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/public/uploads/announcements/165693456768d344ca86529carnatic_music.pdf) — National academy material places the season within the wider Carnatic concert tradition and institutional history.

## Assessment

The Music Academy dates its annual conference practice to 1929; the wider December season grew around the Academy and other organizations. This is a Chennai-centered event within the wider Carnatic tradition, not the tradition’s origin. Overall confidence: high.
