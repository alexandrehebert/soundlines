## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian Folklife: La Vieja Guardia](https://folklife.si.edu/magazine/la-vieja-guardia-salsa-dancing-cali-colombia) — Ethnographic account shows local dance culture assembled from Caribbean and other influences, not a single-source salsa origin.
- [Cambridge University Press: Record grooves and salsa dance moves](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/popular-music/article/abs/record-grooves-and-salsa-dance-moves-the-viejoteca-phenomenon-in-cali-colombia/D7A176735C85ED848BB332FAC810BFA7) — Peer-reviewed research examines Cali’s distinctive record-centered salsa dancing and social venues.
- [Cali municipal cultural heritage account](https://www.cali.gov.co/cultura/publicaciones/170336/la-salsa-calena-unica-en-el-mundo/) — Documents local clubs, dance schools, festivals, and the city’s recognition of salsa caleña.

## Assessment

A local salsa and dance culture shaped through radio, records, clubs, dance schools and festivals, drawing on music from across the Caribbean and diaspora. Overall confidence: high.
