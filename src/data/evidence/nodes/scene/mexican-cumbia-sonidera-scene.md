## Sources checked

- [INAH journal: Los sonideros en México](https://revistas.inah.gob.mx/index.php/antropologia/article/view/2834) — Anthropological article documents sonideros as a Mexican sound-system movement emerging in the 1960s.
- [Tapuya: Listening across borders in cumbia sonidera](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/25729861.2018.1497273) — Peer-reviewed research connects sonidera practice to Peñón de los Baños, Mexico City, transnational record traffic, and migration.
- [Mexico Ministry of Culture: Los Ángeles Azules](https://www.gob.mx/cultura/prensa/los-angeles-azules-embajadores-de-la-cumbia-mexicana) — Government profile documents the Iztapalapa group’s role in popularizing Mexican cumbia.

## Assessment

A Mexico City sound-system and dance tradition associated with Iztapalapa and Los Ángeles Azules; cumbia itself has multiple regional histories. Overall confidence: high.
