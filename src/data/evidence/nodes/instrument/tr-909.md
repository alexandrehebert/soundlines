# Roland TR-909

Graph ID `instrument:tr-909`

**Review status** · Launch and adoption anchors checked

## Evidence review

Roland dates the launch to 1983. Roland identifies the TR-909 on “Strings of Life,” and the original Transmat label dates that release to 1987. Jeff Mills recalls getting his 909 in 1986. These examples support early adoption. They do not establish a single peak or later transition.
