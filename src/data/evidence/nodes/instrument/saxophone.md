## Sources checked

- [Alto Saxophone — Musical Instruments Museum, Brussels](https://www.mim.be/index.php/en/collection-piece/alto-saxophone) — Museum object records an 1863 Sax alto, cites the 21 March 1846 patent and documents band use despite limited orchestral integration.
- [Alto Saxophone in E-flat — The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/503909?locale=en&pg=1) — Museum essay confirms the 1846 patent, success as a band instrument during Sax’s life and later popularity through jazz and pop.
- [Celebrating Sax — The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2014/celebrating-sax) — Museum exhibition summary confirms the patent date and the saxophone’s later role across musical forms.

## Assessment

The patent date is high confidence. The 1863 “established” phase rests on one surviving military-supplier instrument and is now scoped explicitly to band use; it does not imply full orchestral integration. Removed Wikipedia as a source.

## Remaining uncertainty

The saxophone family has multiple sizes and adoption histories; jazz prominence came much later and should not be conflated with early military-band use.
