## Sources checked

- [Roland JUNO-106 technical specifications](https://support.roland.com/hc/en-us/articles/201966419-JUNO-106-Technical-Specifications) — Manufacturer specifications explicitly date model 1984–1985 and document six voices, patch memory, MIDI and chorus.
- [Roland Australia: JUNO—Legendary Heritage](https://rolandcorp.com.au/blog/juno-legendary-heritage-future-classic) — Manufacturer history places introduction in 1984 and describes four-year production and later influence; promotional popularity language treated cautiously.
- [National Music Centre collection: JUNO-106](https://nmc.emuseum.com/objects/1872/juno-106) — Museum collection object record listed in the graph; inaccessible in this retrieval, so not counted as independently checked.

## Assessment

The 1984 period and technical description are strongly corroborated by Roland’s specifications. Kept one continuing-use phase and clarified that model production dates do not delimit ongoing musical use.

## Remaining uncertainty

The museum record could not be opened in this pass. Roland’s sales/popularity superlatives are manufacturer claims rather than independent adoption measures.
