## Sources checked

- [National Film and Sound Archive of Australia, Sampling Sounds: Fairlight CMI](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection/item/sampling-sounds-fairlight-cmi) — Dates Fairlight CMI invention to 1979 and documents use by Kate Bush, Brian Eno and screen composers.
- [Museum Victoria, Fairlight CMI](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/1095198) — Collection record calls Fairlight first commercially available digital sampling instrument, c.1979.
- [Hugh Davies, A History of Sampling](https://www.ocopy.net/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/davies-hugh_a-history-of-sampling.pdf) — Scholarly historical account documents Mellotron/Optigan and other pre-Fairlight sampling instruments.
- [Edinburgh thesis, Studying Music Technologies/Digital Sampling Instruments](https://era.ed.ac.uk/bitstream/1842/22943/2/Harkins2016.pdf) — Academic source cautions Fairlight was a digital synthesizer/workstation with sampling, not the first sampler in an unrestricted sense.

## Assessment

Prior graph treated Fairlight as beginning of all sampling and attached unsupported MPC-era popularity phases. Now distinguishes tape sampling and influential digital sampling.
