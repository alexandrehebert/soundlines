## Sources checked

- [Wikipedia, original citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accordion) — Page retrieved; broad overview, not sole authority for chronology.
- [Demian patent, edited English summary](https://www.henrydoktorski.com/free-reed/history/demian.html) — Read the published summary: May 6 description and May 23 registration, 1829; names Cyrill, Karl and Guido. Translator explicitly warns this is not a guaranteed literal translation. Supports year and bellows/chord construction; original manuscript images not independently transcribed.
- [Metropolitan Museum of Art, Alexandre accordion](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/503502) — Collection record dates instrument 1850–55 and places major Paris production in the 1830s through 1870–71. Supports early development, not global dominance.
- [Institut pour les Savoir-Faire Français](https://www.institut-savoirfaire.fr/savoir-faire-rare/facture-daccordeon) — Trade heritage page corroborates May 1829 patent and free-reed mechanism. Later musette and production narrative is French-centered; its sweeping disappearance claim is unsuitable globally.
- [Indiana University Archives of Traditional Music](https://libraries.indiana.edu/cumbia) — Read Juan Sebastián Rojas’s transcript and recording references. Documents accordion cumbias and 1968 ensemble interview; distinguishes these from flute-led ensembles.
- [UNESCO merengue listing](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/music-and-dance-of-the-merengue-in-the-dominican-republic-01162?lang=en) — Search excerpt names accordion, but direct retrieval returned a human-verification challenge. Not counted as full-text verification.

## Assessment

Replace circa with year precision for the named patent. High confidence applies to that event, not an exclusive inventor claim for the whole instrument family. Replace arbitrary peak phases with scoped manufacture evidence. Preserve a documented Colombian example without implying every cumbia ensemble uses accordion; remove the uncorroborated migration mechanism and Russian/merengue examples from the short description. Relationships remain outside this pass.

## Remaining uncertainty

Worldwide prominence cannot be summarized as one measured peak. UNESCO full text remains inaccessible; no retained claim depends on it.
