## Evidence review

Roland dates the launch to 1980. The Smithsonian account documents early use in Japan and on Arthur Baker’s “Planet Rock,” which charted in 1982. It also describes production constraints and approximately 12,000 sales. This gives a firmer basis for a recording milestone than for an exact market peak or a later trap-era peak.
