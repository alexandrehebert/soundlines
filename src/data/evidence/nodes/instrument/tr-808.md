## Sources checked

- [The TR-808 Story — Roland](https://www.roland.com/uk/promos/roland_tr-808/?lang=en-GB) — Manufacturer history dates production to 1980–82 and traces later adoption among young Detroit and Chicago producers.
- [The History of the TR-808 Drum Machine — Smithsonian Magazine](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/arts-culture/history-tr-808-drum-machine-180975205/) — Smithsonian history explains the 808’s initially poor sales and later influence; links its sound to hip-hop and electronic music.
- [TR-808 Drum Machine Flashback — Roland U.S.](https://www.rolandus.com/blog/2014/02/13/tr-808/) — Manufacturer account identifies “Planet Rock” as an early influential recording built around the 808.

## Assessment

The graph’s 1980 launch and 1982 “Planet Rock” marker are defensible but should not be mistaken for immediate global establishment. Sources show a short production life and much longer musical afterlife. Removed Wikipedia citation.

## Remaining uncertainty

The instrument’s influence is broad and ongoing, so source-specific recordings illustrate adoption without capturing every regional lineage.
