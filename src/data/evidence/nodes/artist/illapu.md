## Sources checked

- [Illapu official history](https://illapu.cl/historia/) — First-party chronology dates founding to summer 1971, documents early touring and ongoing activity.
- [Memoria Chilena, El grupo Illapu](https://www.memoriachilena.gob.cl/archivos2/pdfs/MC0006509.pdf) — Chile’s National Library digital heritage portal records early history, debut LP and the band’s experience under dictatorship.
- [Revista Musical Chilena / SciELO, Illapu in Exile](https://www.scielo.cl/scielo.php?lng=en&pid=S0719-47492025000100201&script=sci_arttext) — Recent peer-reviewed study details exile and return history, correcting overly simple continuous-residency assumptions.
- [Los Angeles Philharmonic, Illapu artist profile](https://www.laphil.com/people/illapu) — Institutional performance bio corroborates 1971 formation.

## Assessment

Kept 1971, which is independently supported by the group’s official chronology, Chilean library heritage records and a major orchestra profile. Added a useful summary. Exile and attempted returns complicate continuous activity but the group remained a continuing project and has performed since return. Confidence: high.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1973–1988 period included touring/exile and a forced return denial in 1981; a single interval compresses geographic displacement.
