## Assessment

Confirmed the 1991 formation date and added a phase description distinguishing the hiatus from the band’s endpoint. Portishead played again at benefits in 2022 and 2025, so the group cannot be treated as permanently dissolved.

## Remaining uncertainty

Recent activity is limited to rare benefit performances; the band has not announced a sustained return to recording or touring.
