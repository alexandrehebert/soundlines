## Sources checked

- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Joy Division / New Order](https://rockhall.com/inductees/joy-division-new-order/) — Institutional history dates Joy Division’s formation to 1976 and Curtis’s death to 1980.
- [Science Museum Group, Joy Division collection](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/people/ap13960/joy-division) — Museum record confirms formation in 1976 and activity on tour in 1980.
- [Universal Music Publishing Group, Joy Division](https://www.umusicpub.com/us/artists/joy-division) — Rights-holder biography documents 1979 debut album and 1980 releases.

## Assessment

The 1976–1980 band span is accurate: formation follows the 1976 Sex Pistols show, and Curtis died in 1980; the group then ended and the remaining members formed New Order. Confidence: high.
