## Sources checked

- [Insomniac artist profile](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/robert-hood/) — Says Hood co-founded Underground Resistance by the end of the 1980s; his first Vision and X-101 records appeared in 1991.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/robert-hood-mn0000288841/biography) — Corroborates his early Underground Resistance role and dates Minimal Nation to 1994.
- [Red Bull Music Academy lecture](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/robert-hood/) — Describes Hood as a founding UR member and credits Minimal Nation (1994) with pioneering minimal techno.

## Assessment

1989 is the collective’s formation year, not a clearly established first solo-release date; kept it as a broad career onset because Hood was involved from UR’s founding period. Description now distinguishes collective work from the defining 1994 solo album.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ on whether Hood joined UR at its founding or shortly afterward; graph onset represents involvement in the collective, not a solo debut.
