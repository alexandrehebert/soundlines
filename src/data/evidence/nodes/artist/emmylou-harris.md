# Emmylou Harris

## Sources checked

- [Alabama Music Hall of Fame](https://www.alamhof.org/emmylou) — Biography dates debut LP Gliding Bird to 1970 and describes the early solo period; some catalogs use 1969 release.
- [Encyclopedia of Alabama](https://encyclopediaofalabama.org/article/emmylou-harris/) — Institutional reference dates her first album to 1970 and distinguishes later breakthrough via Pieces of the Sky.
- [Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum](https://www.countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/emmylou-harris) — Museum account identifies 1975 Pieces of the Sky as the album that established her enduring artistic direction.
- [Polar Music Prize](https://www.polarmusicprize.org/laureates/emmylou-harris/) — Prize biography corroborates first album around 1969/70 and the later Gram Parsons collaboration.

## Assessment

The 1969 decade estimate is adequate but the release year has catalog discrepancies: several institutional sources say 1970, while some discographies date the album 1969. The graph keeps 1969 as approximate recording/initial-release marker; major country breakthrough is 1975, not her start.

## Remaining uncertainty

Gliding Bird is variously dated 1969 or 1970 across credible accounts; confirm the original Jubilee release date before using year precision.
