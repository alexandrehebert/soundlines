## Assessment

Replaced the 1990s decade marker with the documented 1994 formation year. The debut album came in 1995, with international reach expanding after Sehnsucht in 1997. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

The official history focuses on recordings, while the group bio gives the specific formation year.
