## Sources checked

- [Harvard Divinity School, Center for the Study of World Religions, Baul songs by Lalon](https://cswr.hds.harvard.edu/publications/transcendence-transformation/archive/baul-songs-lalon-dehatattva-principles-body) — Identifies Lalon as a major Baul composer and dates his death to 1890.
- [Cambridge University Press, “Rabindranath Tagore—The Greatest of the Bāuls of Bengal”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-asian-studies/article/abs/rabindranath-tagorethe-greatest-of-the-bauls-of-bengal1/8B084561E815593F492467FB88C768ED) — Scholarly account explicitly cautions that his birth date is not certain and discusses early biography.
- [Correspondences, Keith Cantú, Islamic Esotericism in Bengali Baul Songs](https://correspondencesjournal.com/ojs/ojs/index.php/home/issue/download/10/9) — Peer-reviewed scholarship documents his nineteenth-century songs and the need to work critically with oral and manuscript sources.

## Assessment

The 1890 endpoint is well attested. The graph’s circa-1830 start is not independently established by the checked sources; biographical chronology is largely oral and contested. Retain it only as a low-confidence approximate activity estimate, not a verified first-performance date. Confidence: high for death year, low for start.

## Remaining uncertainty

A stronger dated first-performance or composition record is needed before treating 1830 as an evidentiary milestone; birth year is also disputed.
