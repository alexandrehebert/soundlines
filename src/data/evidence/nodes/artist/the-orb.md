## Sources checked

- [Sound On Sound, Little Fluffy Clouds](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/classic-tracks-orb-little-fluffy-clouds) — Dates the first Orb release to the 1988 Eternity Project One compilation and follows the 1989 EP.
- [Spin oral history of The Orb](https://www.spinmagazine.com/2012/01/orb-look-back-20-years-little-fluffy-clouds/) — Paterson recalls forming The Orb with Jimmy Cauty in summer 1988 and the early WAU! release.
- [MusicRadar retrospective](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/what-were-the-skies-like-when-you-were-young-how-the-orbs-little-fluffy-clouds-showed-the-world-that-sampling-could-be-an-art-form) — Reviews the 1991 album and Paterson’s continuing role in a changing project.

## Assessment

1988 is supported by formation and first-release accounts. Describing The Orb as a continuing project better fits its fluid membership than a fixed-band lineup.
