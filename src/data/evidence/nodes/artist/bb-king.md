## Sources checked

- [Polar Music Prize — B.B. King (2004 laureate)](https://www.polarmusicprize.org/laureates/b-b-king/) — 2004 Polar laureate: performed from the early 1940s and first recorded in the late 1940s.

## Assessment
1942 has no source support as a professional start (he was a local and church performer then). His first recordings and radio career in Memphis in 1949 mark the professional start, so the period now runs 1949–2014. The description now notes the jazz-inflected urban-blues guitar style and the long touring career, and mentions the early-1940s local performing.

## Remaining uncertainty
The 2014 end (illness ended touring in October 2014) comes from Wikipedia and Wikidata; no independent page confirming the last concert was fetched.
