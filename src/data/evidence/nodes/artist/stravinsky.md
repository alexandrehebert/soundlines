## Sources checked

- [Chamber Music Society of Lincoln Center biography](https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/about-the-music/composers/igor-stravinsky) — Identifies Requiem Canticles (1966) as Stravinsky’s last completed work.
- [Atlanta Symphony Orchestra biography](https://www.aso.org/artists/detail/igor-stravinsky) — Dates his early contact with Rimsky-Korsakov to 1902 and describes his later career.
- [Boosey & Hawkes timeline](https://www.boosey.com/pages/Opera/composer/timeline?composerid=2708) — Publisher chronology cross-check for the composer’s career milestones.

## Assessment

Kept 1902 as the early career milestone when Stravinsky showed early pieces to Rimsky-Korsakov, but specified the endpoint is his last completed work in 1966; his life continued to 1971. This is a creative-career span, not a lifespan.
