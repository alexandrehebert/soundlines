## Assessment

Kept 1985 as the group’s formation year, distinguishing it from Chuck D’s earlier college-era project and the 1987 album debut. Some Long Island Hall wording says Chuck formed Public Enemy in 1982, while AllMusic dates the assembled group to 1985; confidence in the formation-versus-project distinction is moderate. Public Enemy remains active, so no end year.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1982 versus 1985 conflict reflects different meanings of “formed”: Chuck D’s early project/name versus the group lineup that recorded and toured.
