## Sources checked

- [Songwriters Hall of Fame: Sam Cooke](https://www.songhall.org/profiles/sam-cooke) — Dates the start of recording and songwriting career with the Soul Stirrers to 1951; confirms death in 1964.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame: Sam Cooke](https://rockhall.com/inductees/sam-cooke/) — Documents five years as Soul Stirrers lead vocalist before first pop release in 1956.
- [Library of Congress National Recording Preservation Board: Jesus Gave Me Water](https://lcweb2.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/Jesus-Gave-Me-Water_Fleming.pdf) — Essays on his early gospel recordings and transition into the pop market.
- [Mississippi Encyclopedia: Sam Cooke](https://mississippiencyclopedia.org/entries/sam-cooke/) — State historical encyclopedia documents gospel/R&B career, 1957 recordings and 1964 death.

## Assessment

The existing 1951–1964 span is supported when start denotes his professional gospel recording career, rather than childhood/church singing. Added clear gospel-to-pop chronology and corrected the weak Wikipedia-only bibliography.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some sources differ between 1950 and 1951 for recruitment/first recording; the year 1951 refers to his Specialty recording debut.
