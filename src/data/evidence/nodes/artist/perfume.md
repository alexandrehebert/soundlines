## Sources checked

- [Perfume official profile](https://www.perfume-web.jp/profile/) — Official group profile dates formation in Hiroshima to 1999.
- [Embassy of Japan in the UK profile](https://www.uk.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/2013perfume.html) — Government profile says Perfume formed in 2000; documents the 2007 breakthrough hit.
- [Universal Music Japan biography](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/perfume/biography/) — Label chronology distinguishes their early independent releases from 2005 major-label debut.

## Assessment

Updated the decade marker to 1999, the official profile’s formation date. An Embassy profile rounds this to 2000; the one-year discrepancy reflects differing biographies, so formation is high confidence within 1999–2000.

## Remaining uncertainty

Official primary source gives 1999; one government cultural profile says 2000. Retained 1999 and explicitly recorded the discrepancy.
