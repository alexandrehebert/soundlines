## Sources checked

- [Long Island Music and Entertainment Hall of Fame](https://www.limusichalloffame.org/inductees/the-shangri-las/) — Dates formation to 1963 and the signature single’s number-one peak to 1964.
- [Library of Congress, Why the Shangri-Las Matter](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-plan/tools-and-resources/documents/Why-the-Shangri-Las-Matter.pdf) — Says the band was largely done by 1968 and assesses its influence on later artists.
- [Associated Press obituary for Mary Weiss](https://apnews.com/article/f8287d8251bcfe482c8d21f2f1c9b6d9) — Reports the group’s Queens origins, lineup and first hit.

## Assessment

Kept the 1963–1968 envelope: 1963 formation is corroborated, and LOC describes the group as largely finished by 1968. The end is an active-period marker, not the date their influence ended.
