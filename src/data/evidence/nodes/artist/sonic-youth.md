## Sources checked

- [Pitchfork report on the hiatus](https://pitchfork.com/news/54207-thurston-moore-speaks-on-kim-gordon-split/) — Describes Sonic Youth as on indefinite hiatus after the 2011 separation of Kim Gordon and Thurston Moore.
- [WCMU/NPR interview with Kim Gordon](https://www.wcmu.org/music/2015-02-22/sonic-youths-kim-gordon-on-marriage-music-and-moving-on) — Gordon states Sonic Youth ended in 2011.
- [Thurston Moore memoir excerpt/profile](https://booksite-699200216060-ap-east-2-an.s3.ap-east-2.amazonaws.com/349846/349846984.pdf) — First-person account identifies the band’s formation in 1981.

## Assessment

Retained 1981 and 2011 as formation and final active-year markers. “Indefinite hiatus” is the most careful phrasing because no formal dissolution was announced, and members have discussed a possible future reunion.
