## Sources checked

- [Bristol City Council Museums, Massive Attack](https://museums.bristol.gov.uk/narratives.php?irn=7067) — Museum record dates Massive Attack’s formation to 1988 from remaining Wild Bunch members.
- [MusicRadar, Massive Attack and Bristol music scene history](https://www.musicradar.com/artists/the-title-came-up-as-a-joke-at-first-but-it-fitted-the-song-perfectly-we-just-had-to-use-it-how-massive-attack-created-the-groundbreaking-90s-hit-that-propelled-bristols-unique-music-scene-into-the-global-spotlight) — History/interview confirms the 1988 spin-off from the Wild Bunch and later Blue Lines work.
- [AllMusic, Massive Attack biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/massive-attack-mn0000372929) — Music reference provides group and album chronology.

## Assessment

The 1988 start is correct for Massive Attack as a group, but its members’ shared Wild Bunch activity began earlier. This is a formation date, not the start of Bristol’s scene. No end date; the group remains active. Confidence: high.
