## Assessment

Kept 1991 for his first documented public DJ performance; some label biographies date professional activity to 1993, while the first album arrived in 1994. The timeline should communicate the initial club career, not imply immediate recording success.

## Remaining uncertainty

The start varies by definition: first club performance (1991), professional DJ career (1993), or album release (1994). 1991 is retained with this distinction.
