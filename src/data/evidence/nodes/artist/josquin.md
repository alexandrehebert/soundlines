## Sources checked

- [Oxford History of Western Music, Josquin and the Humanists](https://academic.oup.com/book/60817/chapter-abstract/531553314) — Scholarly context confirms his historical importance and death in 1521.

## Assessment

Replace the unsupported 1470 start with 1475, the earliest documentary career evidence commonly cited in modern scholarship; retain 1521 death. His birth year and early life are uncertain, and some older biographies conflate him with another Josquin. Confidence: high for documented career start/end, not for birth.
