## Sources checked

- [National Endowment for the Arts biography](https://www.arts.gov/honors/heritage/bill-monroe) — Government biography documents radio work with his brothers after their 1930 move, the Monroe Brothers and the 1939 Opry breakthrough.
- [Bluegrass Music Hall of Fame biography](https://www.bluegrasshall.org/inductees/bill-monroe/) — Hall biography dates the Monroe Brothers to 1934 and documents Opry induction in 1939 and Blue Grass Boys lineage through 1996.
- [Smithsonian Folkways: Bill Monroe](https://folkways.si.edu/bill-monroe-bluegrass-innovator/music/article/smithsonian) — Smithsonian history dates formation of Blue Grass Boys to 1938 and Opry debut shortly thereafter.
- [Existing AllMusic bluegrass genre page](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/bluegrass-ma0000002466) — Genre context only, not a biographical source.

## Assessment

Corrected the node kind to person. The Blue Grass Boys formed in 1938, but the NEA describes earlier radio work with Monroe's brothers after their 1930 move and the Hall dates the Monroe Brothers to 1934. Use circa 1930 as an approximate early-career marker rather than presenting the later band formation as his career start.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact year of his first radio or professional performance remains unclear; 1930 is an approximation, not a precisely documented debut.
