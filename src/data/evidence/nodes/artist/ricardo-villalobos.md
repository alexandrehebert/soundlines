## Sources checked

- [AllMusic Ricardo Villalobos biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/ricardo-villalobos-mn0000472894) — Reference dates his earliest discography to 1993, initially under aliases.
- [SEVEN Berlin artist profile](https://seven.berlin/artists/ricardo-villalobos/) — Club/label biography dates first releases to 1993 and distinguishes his own-name releases.
- [Gray Area artist profile](https://grayarea.co/artists/ricardo-villalobos) — Dance-music profile says his first release under his own name was in 1995.
- [Resident Advisor biography](https://ra.co/dj/ricardo-villalobos/biography) — Specialist dance reference for performance and career milestones.

## Assessment

Confirmed the 1993 onset only when aliases are included. Added that his first release under his own name came later; first professional DJ activity and international recognition were later still. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some biographies use 1995 because they count releases under his own name rather than alias Minta Spacew or collaborators.
