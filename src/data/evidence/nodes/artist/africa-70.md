## Sources checked

- [Fela Kuti official site — 1971](https://felakuti.com/us/story/1971) — Artist-family/estate history gives the exact rename date, describes 1971 as a breakthrough year, and says the group remained a leading West African attraction into the 1980s.
- [Fela Kuti official site — 1981](https://felakuti.com/story/1981) — Official chronology dates the Afrika 70 to Egypt 80 renaming to 1981, refining the group-name endpoint.
- [Philharmonie de Paris — Fela Kuti exhibition works list](https://drop.philharmoniedeparis.fr/CMDE/CMDE000005004/exposition_fela-kuti_liste_oeuvres.pdf) — Museum exhibition catalogue lists Fela with Africa 70 through 1980 and Egypt 80 entries from 1980, showing the transition straddles a year and the naming endpoint is approximate.
- [AllMusic — Fela Kuti & Africa 70](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/fela-kuti-africa-70-mn0000793646) — Existing discography citation retained as supplementary release context.

## Assessment

The source cited before this review was a genre history, not an Africa 70 biography. Replaced the overly broad 1970–80 dates with a 1971 name-change start. The group name transitions to Egypt 80 around 1980–81; the year is fuzzy across catalogues, so mark the endpoint as approximate rather than treating the ensemble as ending outright.
