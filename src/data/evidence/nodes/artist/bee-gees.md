## Assessment

The graph’s 1958 start is reasonable as the Bee Gees name/group-era marker but official history traces public performance to 1956 and the Rattlesnakes; make that distinction explicit. Simplified eleven arbitrary phase marks to a small evidence-based set. 2003 marks end of the original trio, but later surviving-member activity means the single end date of 2012 should be interpreted carefully.

## Remaining uncertainty

Audit the graph period endpoint and post-2003 phases against official tour/release chronology; group activity is intermittent and membership changed after Maurice’s death.
