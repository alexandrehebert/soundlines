## Sources checked

- [Planet Mu release notes, Classics Vol. 1](https://planet.mu/releases/rp-boo-classics-vol-1/) — Label notes say “Baby Come On” was made in 1997 and is often cited as the first footwork track.
- [Red Bull Music Academy lecture](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/rp-boo-lecture/) — Describes RP Boo as a Chicago producer whose 1997 “Baby Come On” helped originate footwork.
- [Resident Advisor feature, The evolution of footwork](https://ra.co/features/1235) — Places his early tracks in Chicago parties from 1997 and notes that many regard him as a footwork originator.

## Assessment

Removed the incompatible decade precision: a specific 1997 track is the cited career landmark. Qualified the genre-origin claim as “widely cited” because multiple DJs and dancers shaped footwork rather than one inventor.
