## Sources checked

- [Constellation Records: Godspeed You! Black Emperor](https://cstrecords.com/pages/artist/godspeed-you-black-emperor) — The group’s long-time label documents its 25-year history, releases and ongoing collective identity.
- [Concordia University dissertation on Montreal post-rock](https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/976402/1/MR63287.pdf) — Academic dissertation dates the band’s formation in Montreal to 1994.
- [Constellation Records: F♯ A♯ ∞](https://cstrecords.com/en-fr/products/godspeed-you-black-emperor-f-a-1995-1997) — Label dates the limited vinyl release of the debut album to August 1997.

## Assessment

Kept 1994 as formation and distinguished it from the 1997 debut album. The band is active again after its hiatus, so no end date was warranted.

## Remaining uncertainty

The group’s first cassette preceded the 1997 commercial LP; early Montreal shows and releases are unevenly documented.
