## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/tunde-nightingale-mn0001649901/biography) — Describes him as an early postwar juju hit performer.
- [University of Pretoria dissertation](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstream/handle/2263/22921/Complete.pdf?sequence=7) — Cites juju scholarship crediting Nightingale with introducing a recognizable juju sound by 1944.
- [Nigerian Journal of Medicine article](https://www.nigerianjournalsonline.com/index.php/jonmed/article/download/1236/1219) — Reports his career began in the early 1940s, his shekere innovation and his death in 1981.

## Assessment

Kept the approximate 1940 onset: sources place his career in the early 1940s and recognizable juju style by 1944. 1981 is supported by a Nigerian journal article. Description now identifies his historical significance rather than leaving the entry blank.
