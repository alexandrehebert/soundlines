## Assessment

Replaced the vague 1960s onset with the Fukuoka Prize’s documented 1965 debut as group lead singer. 1971 marks succession and serious career leadership, not first performance. End year 1997 is established.

## Remaining uncertainty

Different biographies describe early family performances differently; 1965 is a strong documented professional debut.
