## Sources checked

- [Academy of Korean Studies Encyclopedia of Korean Culture](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0072520) — Scholarly Korean reference records study with masters through the 1930s and five complete pansori cycles in the 1960s–70s.
- [Korean Citation Index academic article on his artistic activity](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002414722) — Peer-reviewed study says group activity as singer/teacher began in his twenties and documents 1951 military performances.
- [Korean Citation Index study on his career phases](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002532299) — Academic article explicitly divides his life into career phases; confirms 1916–2003 lifespan.
- [Korea JoongAng Daily obituary](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/korea/a-renowned-performer-of-korean-opera-is-dead/10642931) — Reports pansori training from age six and death in 2003.

## Assessment

The old c.1950 start missed decades of training and performing. Academic sources place group teaching/performance in his twenties (roughly the mid-1930s) and training with masters throughout the 1930s. Set c.1936 to represent the beginning of professional activity; 2003 is confirmed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The source does not name a single first professional performance; c.1936 is an inferred decade midpoint and should remain approximate.
