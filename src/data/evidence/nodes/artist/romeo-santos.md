## Assessment

Replaced imprecise circa-1990 with 1995, the first documented album as Los Tinellers; separated that start from Aventura’s 1999 album identity and the 2011 solo phase. The performer may have formed/performed with the group earlier, so 1995 is a discographic milestone, not a claimed formation date.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources disagree between 1995 and 1996 for Trampa de Amor (recording/edition dating); AllMusic and Apple Music cataloging favor 1995.
