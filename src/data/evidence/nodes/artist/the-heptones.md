## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/the-heptones-p2890/biography) — Dates the trio’s formation to 1965 and first hit “Fattie Fattie” to 1966.
- [The Heptones official biography](https://theheptones.world/biography/) — Earl Morgan’s official biography dates the group’s founding to 1965.
- [Roots Archives discography](https://www.roots-archives.com/artist/12) — Discography places the core trio together in 1965 and its first releases in 1966.

## Assessment

Changed 1960s to 1965, when the familiar Sibbles–Llewellyn–Morgan trio came together; some histories trace an earlier precursor duo to the late 1950s. Removed the unsupported 2000 endpoint because the group has continued in changing form.

## Remaining uncertainty

The earliest precursor configuration predates the classic trio; 1965 refers to the well-documented core group.
