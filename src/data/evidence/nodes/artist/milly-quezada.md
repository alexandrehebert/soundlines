## Sources checked

- [Dominican Music in the United States educational resource](https://dominicanmusicusa.com/educational_resources/milly-quezada-the-queen-of-merengue/222) — Identifies 1975 formation of Milly y los Vecinos as the start of her formal music career.
- [Miami New Times profile](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/music/hear-her-roar-6351856/) — Reports her group’s first album in 1975 and offers career context.
- [Dominican Republic National Copyright Office tribute](https://onda.gob.do/noticias/la-onda-rinde-homenaje-a-merengueros-en-el-dia-nacional-del-merengue/) — Government cultural tribute recognizes her place in merengue.

## Assessment

Replaced the approximate 1970s onset with a documented 1975 group formation and recording milestone. The band phase preceded and should not be confused with her later solo career. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ in group-name form as members and lineup evolved; 1975 formation is consistent.
