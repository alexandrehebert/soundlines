## Sources checked

- [Daniel Bell interview, Higher Frequency](https://higher-frequency.com/e_interview/daniel_bell/index.htm) — Bell says he began making records in 1990; identifies Technarchy as his first 12-inch and describes 1994 Losing Control and his production hiatus.
- [First Avenue artist biography](https://first-avenue.com/performer/daniel-bell-aka-dbx/) — corroborates first +8 12-inch Technarchy in 1990, followed by Accelerate and DBX work.
- [CTM Festival 2009 archive catalogue](https://archive2013-2020.ctm-festival.de/fileadmin/_migrated/content_uploads/CTM09_catalogue_web_01.pdf) — identifies Bell as a key figure in minimal techno and documents DBX revival in 2008.

## Assessment

Changed start from 1989 to 1990: Bell’s own interview identifies that year as when he began making records and dates his first 12-inch, Technarchy. No source found in this review supporting 1989. Description is supported by the contemporary CTM festival profile and Bell’s own account of a stripped-down minimal aesthetic. Left end open because recording pauses and alias revivals make career endpoint inappropriate.

## Remaining uncertainty

Earlier band and hip-hop production activity is mentioned by First Avenue, but not dated; 1990 is a defensible start for his documented electronic recording career, not all musical activity.
