## Sources checked

- [Official Charts So Solid Crew page](https://www.officialcharts.com/artist/9989/so-solid-crew/) — Dates the collective’s formation to 1998 and “21 Seconds” to its 2001 number-one run.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0000752695) — Places the crew’s creation in 1998 and documents the 2001 single.
- [The Guardian, “How we made 21 Seconds”](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2017/oct/17/so-solid-crew-how-we-made-21-seconds-interview) — Member recollections explain the track and the collective’s impact on UK garage.

## Assessment

Corrected the 1997 start to 1998, the formation year in Official Charts and AllMusic. The group’s membership was fluid, so the period describes the collective rather than a fixed lineup.
