## Sources checked

- [North Carolina Music Hall of Fame, John Coltrane](https://northcarolinamusichalloffame.org/inductee-item/john-coltrane/) — Dates his professional debut to 1945 and distinguishes his 1960 solo-career launch.
- [Smithsonian Music, John Coltrane](https://music.si.edu/story/john-coltrane) — Documents post-Navy Philadelphia career and recording with Dizzy Gillespie’s orchestra by 1949.
- [Official John Coltrane biography](https://www.johncoltrane.com/biography) — Confirms his later career and death in July 1967.

## Assessment

The 1945–1967 span is supported for professional activity through death. Clarify that 1945 marks an early professional debut, while leader and solo career milestones came later; sources differ on which milestone to call career start. Confidence: high for endpoint, medium-high for onset.
