## Sources checked

- [Official biography](https://www.johncoltrane.com/biography) — Biography read: early Philadelphia scene, 1946–55 apprenticeship, 1957 leader recordings, subsequent experimental work and death in July 1967.
- [North Carolina Music Hall of Fame](https://northcarolinamusichalloffame.org/inductee-item/john-coltrane/) — Dates professional debut and Navy playing to 1945; death to July 1967. Its earlier move-to-Philadelphia chronology conflicts with the official biography; not adopted.
- [Wikipedia, original citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Coltrane) — Early-career section checked: 1945 professional trio work, 1946 first recordings; distinguishes recording debut from professional debut.

## Assessment

Retain 1945–1967 with high confidence as a professional-career span. Description of bebop/hard-bop, modal and freer improvisation is supported. No authored phases to audit. Avoid equating the 1960 standing quartet with the beginning of leader recordings in 1957.
