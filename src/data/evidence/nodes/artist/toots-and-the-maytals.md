## Sources checked

- [Toots and the Maytals official biography](https://www.tootsandthemaytals.net/extended-toots-bio/) — Dates the original Maytals formation to 1962 and recounts its early releases.
- [LA Philharmonic artist biography](https://www.laphil.com/people/toots-hibbert) — Documents 1962–63 recordings, the 1967 reunion and transition to the Toots and the Maytals name.
- [Encyclopedia.com group history](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/toots-and-maytals) — Notes the 1981 split, 1990s reunion and Toots Hibbert’s 2020 death.

## Assessment

Replaced 1960s decade precision with 1962, the documented trio formation. The career has multiple phases: early Maytals, an early-1980s split, a 1990s reunion and the Toots-led group’s end at Hibbert’s 2020 death. The website retains a legacy brand after his death; that alone does not prove ongoing group activity.
