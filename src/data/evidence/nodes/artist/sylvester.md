## Sources checked

- [Sylvester official biography](https://sylvesterofficial.com/about/) — Confirms 1947–1988 lifespan and describes his move to San Francisco, Cockettes performances and later solo career.
- [Digital Transgender Archive collection](https://www.digitaltransgenderarchive.net/downloads/4b29b627n) — Archival biography documents his dates, performance history and cultural legacy.
- [Legacy Project Chicago profile](https://legacyprojectchicago.org/person/sylvester) — Places his move to San Francisco and Cockettes-era performances in 1969–70; records early albums and later rise.

## Assessment

Changed 1967 to 1970, the clearly documented public San Francisco/Cockettes phase. He had earlier performance activity with the Disquotays, but the reviewed sources do not securely date it to 1967. 1988 is his death year, not a posthumous release endpoint.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some biographies trace his first group activity to Los Angeles in the 1960s; an exact start year remains unverified.
