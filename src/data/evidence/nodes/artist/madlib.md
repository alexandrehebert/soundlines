## Assessment

Retained circa 1993 for first commercial work; the sources distinguish early group activity, 1993 guest production, 1994 credited single, and 1999 album.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1993 versus 1994 exact first public credit depends on whether group appearance or credited production is used.
