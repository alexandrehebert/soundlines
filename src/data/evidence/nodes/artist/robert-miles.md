## Sources checked

- [Insomniac feature](https://www.insomniac.com/music/from-the-crate-robert-miles-children/) — Retrospective on the 1990s “Children” recording and its piano-led sound.

## Assessment

1994–2017 remains defensible as the recording career’s span: “Children” originated in 1994 and Miles died in 2017. The track’s release and breakthrough extended into 1995–96, so the start does not imply its commercial release year.
