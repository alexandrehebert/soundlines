# Gravediggaz

**Review status** · Reviewed: formation, inactivity and comeback documented

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Encyclopedia.com: Gravediggaz](https://www.encyclopedia.com/education/news-wires-white-papers-and-books/gravediggaz) — Biographical entry dates formation to 1992 and describes its members and initial concept.
- [AllMusic: Gravediggaz](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/gravediggaz-mn0000662067) — Music reference dates its debut album to 1994, describes horrorcore labeling and lists a 2002 release.
- [AllMusic: Nightmare in A-Minor](https://www.allmusic.com/album/nightmare-in-a-minor-mw0000219899) — Release history states the group returned after years of inactivity for the 2002 album, its final album.
- [Pitchfork: Prince Paul interview](https://pitchfork.com/features/5-10-15-20/8975-prince-paul/) — First-person interview describes Prince Paul assembling the group with RZA, Too Poetic and Frukwan.

## Assessment

Changed the 1994 start, which dated the debut album, to the documented 1992 formation. Retained 2002 as the final full album after an inactivity break; a later compilation is not equivalent to a continuing group recording career.

## Remaining uncertainty

A later 2010 track is sometimes attributed to the name, so 2002 marks the final full-length release, not an undisputed last musical appearance.
