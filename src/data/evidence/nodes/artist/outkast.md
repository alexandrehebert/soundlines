## Assessment

Kept the existing 1992 year as an approximate formation/signing period, but clarified that the first album came in 1994 and the label signing is documented in 1993. No end date is appropriate given continuing reunions and member activity.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources document early 1990s formation rather than one exact day; 1992 remains a conventional year marker.
