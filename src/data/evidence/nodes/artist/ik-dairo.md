## Assessment

Replaced a circa-1940 start and 1990 endpoint with the documented 1946 first public/professional performance and 1996 death year. The old end was unsupported and wrong. Confidence: medium-high; the 1946 detail is supported by scholarly secondary literature, though early biography remains sparse.

## Remaining uncertainty

Band formation and public/professional debut may not be the same event; 1946 is the earliest cited public performance.
