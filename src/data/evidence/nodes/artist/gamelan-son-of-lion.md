## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian Folkways: Gamelan Son of Lion album notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/FW31312.pdf) — Record notes state that the current ensemble formed in 1976 and describe the American-built instruments.
- [New Music USA: Barbara Benary on orchestrating for the gamelan](https://newmusicusa.org/nmbx/orchestration-composers-reveal-tricks-of-the-trade-barbara-benary/) — Composer Benary recounts the collective and repertory ensemble’s birth in 1976.
- [University of Rochester: Gamelan Community in America](https://iml.esm.rochester.edu/prjc-archive/wp-content/uploads/sites/11/2017/09/GDS-Community-Gamelan-In-America-FINAL.pdf) — Academic project identifies the New York collective, its 1976 founding, and its contemporary Javanese-gamelan repertory.

## Assessment

Confirmed 1976 as the ensemble’s formation, distinct from the 1974 construction of its instruments. Added the group’s composer-collective identity rather than describing it as a conventional traditional ensemble.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources use both ensemble formation and earlier instrument construction; the graph dates the collective’s activity.
