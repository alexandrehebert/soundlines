## Sources checked

- [Fela Anikulapo-Kuti — Oxford Bibliographies in Music](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62400/reference-article-abstract/555412345?login=false) — Scholarly bibliography gives 1938–97 dates, London study from 1958 and the Koola Lobitos lineage; it presents the conventional Afrobeat attribution that newer scene scholarship qualifies.
- [Fela Kuti — Trinity Laban Conservatoire](https://www.trinitylaban.ac.uk/about-us/our-history/fela-kuti/) — Institutional biography confirms 1938–97, study beginning in 1958 and later musical evolution in Nigeria.
- [Reconstructing Afrobeat as a Scene-Based Genre — Journal of World Popular Music](https://doi.org/10.1558/jwpm.25695) — Peer-reviewed study challenges a single-founder account and places formation in a 1960s–70s Lagos scene involving multiple musicians and networks.
- [About Fela — Fela Kuti official site](https://felakuti.com/gb/about) — Estate-linked source dates the first London band to 1959, a small discrepancy with sources that date study and early activity to 1958.
- [Fela Kuti — Rock & Roll Hall of Fame](https://rockhall.com/inductees/fela-kuti/) — Institutional induction biography corroborates life dates, music and continuing influence.

## Assessment

The node’s `kind` was incorrectly set to group; Fela is an individual artist. The 1950s start was too broad and lacked a rationale, so it is now c.1958, when he began music study and early band activity in London. 1997 is corroborated as the end of his life/career. Description credits his major role while avoiding a sole-inventor claim challenged by recent musicology.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ slightly on whether Koola Lobitos formed in 1958 or 1959. Afrobeat’s development is collective and the timing of its naming is distinct from Fela’s early career.
