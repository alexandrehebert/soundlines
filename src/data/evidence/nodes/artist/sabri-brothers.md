## Sources checked

- [Compass Records artist profile](https://compassrecords.com/artist/the-sabri-brothers/) — Says Maqbool formed his first qawwal party in 1956 and his elder brother soon joined, establishing the Sabri Brothers.
- [Islam Museum group profile](https://islam.museum/en/group/sabri-brothers/) — Identifies the Pakistani group as led by Ghulam Farid and Maqbool Sabri from the 1950s.
- [Smithsonian Folkways / Archives catalog](https://www.si.edu/object/archives/components/sova-cfch-unes-ref2141) — Catalogs Sabri Brothers performances in the UNESCO world traditional music collection as qawwali.

## Assessment

Corrected the overbroad 1950s precision to a supported 1956 start. Used Maqbool’s 2011 death as the endpoint of the classic brothers-led act; this does not claim the family’s qawwali tradition stopped then.

## Remaining uncertainty

The graph period describes the original brothers-led act, not later relatives or successor ensembles.
