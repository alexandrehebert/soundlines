## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress, “Le Freak” National Recording Registry essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/LeFreak.pdf) — The LOC essay dates Chic’s formation to about 1976, distinguishes its precursor Big Apple Band, documents its 1977 debut single, and notes later hiatuses and changing lineups.
- [Songwriters Hall of Fame, Nile Rodgers biography](https://www.songhall.org/profiles/nile-rodgers) — Institutional biography dates formation of Chic to 1976, identifies its initial members, says it disbanded in 1983 and regrouped in the early 1990s, and describes Rodgers continuing to tour with Chic.

## Assessment

Retain 1976: two independent institutional sources support it, with the Library of Congress describing the date as approximate and the Songwriters Hall of Fame giving it directly. The existing source was Wikipedia; replace it with the LOC and Songwriters Hall of Fame sources. Do not use 1983 as an end date: the sources document hiatus, regrouping, and later touring. The graph has no phase list to revise.

## Remaining uncertainty

The entry uses the group name Chic, so 1976 marks the new name/lineup rather than the earlier Big Apple Band roots (formed in 1972). Lineup and activity were not continuous; the open-ended period summarizes the ongoing project.
