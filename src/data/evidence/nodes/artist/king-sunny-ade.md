## Sources checked

- [Nigerian Copyright Commission, King Sunny Adé](https://copyright.gov.ng/king-sunny-ade/) — Official profile dates formation of African Beats to 1967 and describes his jùjú innovations.
- [Encyclopedia.com, King Sunny Adé](https://www.encyclopedia.com/people/literature-and-arts/music-popular-and-jazz-biographies/king-sunny-ade) — Career chronology places him in juju bands from 1958, own band in 1965, and records from 1967.
- [Los Angeles Times, 1994 King Sunny Adé profile](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1994-08-04-ol-23277-story.html) — Contemporaneous profile documents the 1966 Green Spots formation and continuing career.

## Assessment

The 1960s start is defensible as rounded career onset, but his music activity began in local bands around 1958; his own group formed in 1965–67 and international recognition followed in the 1980s. No end date: ongoing activity. Confidence: medium-high for the sequence, medium for a single onset year.
