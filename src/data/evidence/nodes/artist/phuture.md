## Sources checked

- [AllMusic Phuture biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/phuture-mn0000336086) — Music reference dates formation to 1985 and the commercial “Acid Trax” single to 1987.
- [Insomniac, “Acid Tracks” history](https://www.insomniac.com/music/from-the-crate-phuture-acid-tracks/) — Dance-music history distinguishes the early cassette/club circulation from the later studio record.
- [DJ Mag, “Acid Tracks” history](https://djmag.com/content/game-changers-phuture-acid-tracks) — Specialist account dates the Trax Records release to 1987.
- [Resident Advisor biography](https://ra.co/dj/phuture/biography) — Independent dance reference notes 1986 club circulation before the 1987 release.

## Assessment

Kept the 1985 formation start, correcting the description’s overclaim that the group single-handedly “invented” acid house. The defining track circulated in clubs before its 1987 record release; both are separate phases.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources differ on whether to date “Acid Tracks” to 1986 club circulation or 1987 commercial release; formation year remains 1985.
