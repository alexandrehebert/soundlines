## Sources checked

- [Notjustok 2011 interview with Davido](https://notjustok.com/article/davido-notjustok-hype/) — first-person interview: Davido says he decided to pursue music professionally in March 2011 and discusses “Back When,” “Dami Duro,” and his self-description as Fuji pop/Afropop.
- [Concord publishing roster](https://concord.com/publishing-roster/davido/) — documents early attention from 2011 “Dami Duro” and debut album Omo Baba Olowo (2012).
- [Sony Music press biography](https://cdn-p.smehost.net/sites/8e1f40b762984a27a20f577d99b7ac97/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Davido_CHAMPION-SOUND_November-19-2021-.pdf) — label biography dates musical debut to 2011 and lists early singles including Back When and Dami Duro.

## Assessment

Changed broad 2010s start to 2011. Davido’s own 2011 interview states he pursued music professionally beginning in March and discusses the first single; Concord and Sony label materials corroborate 2011 debut/early attention. Added nuance to the style description: his contemporary self-description was Fuji pop/Afropop, so “Afrobeats” alone would risk flattening how he described his music. Removed a broad academic genre citation and general AllMusic citation in favor of artist-specific evidence.

## Remaining uncertainty

The interview captures his own genre description at the beginning of his career; later work is stylistically broader. Period is year-level professional career onset, not his earlier informal recordings with a school group.
