## Sources checked

- [The Guardian obituary](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2023/jun/06/astrud-gilberto-obituary) — Documents that she had not sung professionally before the 1963 Getz/Gilberto session; international hit followed in 1964 and solo work began.
- [Washington Post obituary](https://www.washingtonpost.com/obituaries/2023/06/06/astrud-gilberto-samba-ipanema-dies/) — Corroborates global breakthrough, Grammy, and death in 2023.
- [Irish Times obituary](https://www.irishtimes.com/obituaries/2023/06/24/astrud-gilberto-obituary-queen-of-brazilian-bossa-nova/) — Characterizes a four-decade recording career and notes Girl from Ipanema was her first recording.
- [AllMusic artist profile](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/astrud-gilberto-mn0000931086) — Existing artist reference is supplemental; not used to date career start.

## Assessment

Changed the graph start from 1960 to 1963, the documented first professional recording session; 1964 marks the hit, not her first singing. End 2023 is death year. This separates recording career from her earlier personal association with bossa nova musicians.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1963 session and 1964 single dates are well supported; an early label/discography citation would add a primary record source.
