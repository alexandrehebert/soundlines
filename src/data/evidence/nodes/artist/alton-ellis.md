## Sources checked

- [National Library of Jamaica — Alton Ellis](https://www.nlj.gov.jm/REGGAE%20EXHIBITION/Alton%20Ellis.htm) — Library exhibit documents the Alton & Eddie duo and first recording in 1959, key rocksteady recordings, and later career. It internally conflicts on birth year: heading says 1938, body says 1944.
- [The Washington Post — Alton Ellis, 70](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2008/10/19/alton-ellis-70-singer-helped-to-launch-rock-steady-sound/0fe81ce4-ef66-4b9a-8714-5d7abf1c95a3/) — Contemporaneous obituary gives birth year 1938, reports the 1959 talent-show contract and first duo hit, and documents recording and UK relocation.
- [Jamaica Information Service — Jamaica has lost a giant](https://jis.gov.jm/jamaica-has-lost-a-giant-grange/) — Government condolence statement confirms his death and importance to Jamaican popular music.
- [AllMusic — Alton Ellis](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/p2883) — Discography reference independently dates the first single to 1959 and rocksteady recordings to the mid/late 1960s.

## Assessment

Replaced the old 1950–2000 span with his first duo recording and death years. Cross-source check exposed a birth-date conflict inside the National Library page; its heading and the Washington Post say 1938, while its body says 1944. Do not use the inconsistent detail without note.
