## Assessment

The graph’s 1957 professional start is supported by two biographies for his All India Radio appointment; the National Academy record independently confirms his later performance career. Kept the year, open-ended. Confidence: high.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some accounts differ on whether the 1957 AIR posting was at Cuttack or Allahabad; this does not change the career year.
