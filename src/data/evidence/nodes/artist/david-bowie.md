## Sources checked

- [Television Academy biography](https://www.televisionacademy.com/bios/david-bowie) — documents first album in 1967, major traction with Space Oddity in 1969, genre-bending work, and death date in 2016.

## Assessment

Corrected the 1969 graph start, which represented breakthrough rather than career onset. Official Bowie material calls 1964 the start of his professional recording career via Liza Jane; Television Academy dates his first album to 1967 and real traction to 1969. End 2016 is corroborated. Replaced placeholder description with sourced breadth while avoiding an exhaustive genre taxonomy.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 1964 date is for first professional recording; early band activity preceded the release. 1969 remains the first major chart breakthrough, not the start.
