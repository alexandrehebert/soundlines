## Sources checked

- [Buena Vista Social Club, Compay Segundo artist profile](https://www.buenavistasocialclub.com/musicians/compay-segundo/) — Official project biography says he played with leading bands in the 1920s–30s, found fame with Los Compadres in the 1940s, formed his own group in the 1950s, and continued until his 2003 death.
- [Universal Music France, Compay Segundo biography](https://www.universalmusic.fr/artistes/20000318501) — Label biography dates the armónico invention to 1924, notes a 1934 Havana band, first discography activity in the 1940s, Los Compadres in 1942/1947 (its sections conflict), and international rediscovery around Buena Vista Social Club.
- [PBS source previously in graph](https://www.pbs.org/buenavista/music/a_son.html) — Existing PBS citation concerns Cuban son generally, not Compay Segundo’s biography; it was replaced with artist-specific biographies.

## Assessment

The broad 1920 start is supported as a marker for band activity, not discographic fame: the Buena Vista Social Club biography places him in bands during the 1920s and 1930s, while Universal identifies a 1934 Havana band and first discography activity in the 1940s. Keep 2003 as the documented death year. Update the generic source set with artist-specific biographies and distinguish the later Los Compadres and Buena Vista recognition phases in the description. ## Remaining uncertainty

Universal Music’s biography gives different Los Compadres formation dates in its overview (1947) and detailed section (1942); BVSC supports the broader decade only. The 1920 start represents approximate early band activity.
