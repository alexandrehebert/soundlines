## Sources checked

- [Peaches official biography](https://www.teachesofpeaches.com/about) — Official bio frames The Teaches of Peaches (2000) as her breakthrough debut under the Peaches persona.
- [AllMusic artist biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/peaches-esque-mn0000752770) — Reference records earlier Fancypants Hoodlum (1995) under Merrill Nisker and explains adoption of the Peaches name.
- [TIME interview](https://time.com/4051470/peaches-rub-interview-museum-of-sex/) — Profile distinguishes her earlier teaching and music from the 2000 Peaches debut.

## Assessment

Kept 2000 as the start of the Peaches solo persona, but clarified that Nisker released earlier work in 1995. The graph should not silently conflate her earlier recordings with the later Peaches identity. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources do not identify the exact year she adopted the Peaches name; 2000 is a clear documented public debut.
