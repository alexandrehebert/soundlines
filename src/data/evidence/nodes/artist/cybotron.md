## Sources checked

- [Detroit Historical Society, Encyclopedia of Detroit](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/atkins-juan) — dates Cybotron formation with Juan Atkins and Rick Davis to 1980 and their split to 1985; identifies Clear and Techno City in the Detroit techno history.
- [BMG: Cybotron releases new music](https://www.bmg.com/news/techno-pioneers-cybotron-release-first-new-music-in-40-years) — confirms original Atkins/Davis duo, Atkins’ departure after 1983 Enter, 2019–20 live revival, and new Atkins-featured EP in 2023.

## Assessment

Start year 1980 is corroborated by Detroit Historical Society. Retained open-ended period: original duo split in 1985, but the project later revived for shows and recording, with new music in 2023. Existing description already distinguishes the original lineup from later resumed activity and aligns with sources; removed Wikipedia citations and retained no separate Techno citation because the group-specific history suffices for this entry.

## Remaining uncertainty

The Society describes the 1985 split while BMG describes subsequent project revivals; this is an intermittent project history, not continuous activity. Current evidence does not establish a later endpoint.
