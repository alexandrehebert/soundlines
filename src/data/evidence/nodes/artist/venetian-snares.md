## Sources checked

- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/venetian-snares-mn0000317131) — Says he self-released cassettes during the 1990s and dates his first vinyl, Greg Hates Car Culture, to 1999.
- [Igloo Magazine, Greg Hates Car Culture](https://igloomag.com/profiles/venetian-snares-greg-still-hates-car-culture) — Retrospective interview/history dates the first vinyl release to 1999.
- [AllMusic discography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/venetian-snares-mn0000317131) — Discography corroborates the 1999 emergence and later releases.

## Assessment

Kept the broad 1990s start because AllMusic says he self-released cassettes during that decade, but the first documented vinyl milestone is 1999. The start should not imply a widely distributed release in 1995.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact first cassette date is not established by the sources checked; 1999 is the first corroborated commercial vinyl release.
