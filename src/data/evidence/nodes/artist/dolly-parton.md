## Sources checked

- [National Endowment for the Arts](https://www.arts.gov/honors/medals/dolly-parton) — Biography dates her Grand Ole Opry debut to 1959 and first single to 1960.
- [Library of Congress Dolly Parton timeline](https://www.loc.gov/collections/dolly-parton-and-the-roots-of-country-music/articles-and-essays/dolly-parton-timeline/) — Institutional chronology gives early recording/performance milestones and her move to Nashville after school.
- [Country Music Hall of Fame and Museum](https://countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/dolly-parton) — Museum account distinguishes songwriting recognition in 1966 and RCA/Porter Wagoner breakthrough in 1967.

## Assessment

The previous 1960s start obscured her early public career: NEA dates an Opry debut in 1959 and first single in 1960. Her 1966 songwriting and 1967 RCA/Porter Wagoner milestones are later breakthroughs, not career beginnings.

## Remaining uncertainty

Parton performed even earlier as a child; 1959 is a documented professional-stage marker rather than her first performance.
