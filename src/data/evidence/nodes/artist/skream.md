## Sources checked

- [Apple Music album notes](https://music.apple.com/us/album/unreleased-classics-vol-1-2002-2003/1554344691) — Dates debut single “The Judgement” with Benga to 2003 and identifies “Midnight Request Line” as the 2005 breakthrough.
- [Resident Advisor biography](https://ra.co/dj/skream/biography) — Documents his later move from dubstep into disco, house and techno.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0000555075) — Corroborates “Midnight Request Line” (2005) and the 2006 debut album.

## Assessment

Kept 2003 as first release with Benga, while separating it from the solo breakthrough in 2005. Updated description to reflect the later shift across styles rather than imply he remained exclusively a dubstep artist.
