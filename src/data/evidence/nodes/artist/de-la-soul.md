## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress National Recording Registry essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/3-Feet-High-and-Rising.pdf) — documents Tommy Boy signing the group in 1987, first single Plug Tunin’ in 1988, and the breakthrough 1989 debut album.
- [NPR Fresh Air archive, interview with Dave and Mase](https://freshairarchive.org/segments/hippies-hip-hop-de-la-soul) — interviews two members; confirms high-school formation, 1989 debut, Native Tongues relationship, and eclectic sampling. Its short bio labels formation 1985.
- [LA Philharmonic artist profile](https://www.laphil.com/people/de-la-soul) — describes high-school formation in the late 1980s, 1989 debut, Native Tongues role and later stylistic development.

## Assessment

Retained decade-level onset but corrected the graph representation to the 1980s (start 1980 with decade precision): sources disagree on formation year, variously 1985 or broadly late 1980s; the Library of Congress establishes signing in 1987 and first single in 1988. 1987 should not be presented as exact group formation. Replaced AllMusic with LOC and institutional/interview sources. Description summarizes documented sampling, eclecticism, and collective role.

## Remaining uncertainty

Formation is variously dated; use an 1980s decade, while distinguishing signed 1987, first single 1988, and debut album 1989. Group remains active with evolving membership.
