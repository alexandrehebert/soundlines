## Sources checked

- [IRCAM composer biography](https://resources.ircam.fr/en/composer/arnold-schoenberg) — Confirms 1874–1951 lifespan and describes development of his twelve-tone method.
- [Oxford American National Biography](https://academic.oup.com/american-national-biography/reference/63042/reference-article-abstract/570552801) — Dates his decision to pursue music as a career to 1895.
- [Universal Edition biography](https://www.universaledition.com/en/Contacts/Arnold-Schoenberg/) — Publisher biography corroborates lifespan and musical chronology.

## Assessment

Replaced a vague circa-1890 onset with 1895, when the Oxford biography says he chose music as his career. Kept 1951 as death year. Twelve-tone composition was developed later, chiefly in the early 1920s, so it should not be confused with his career start.
