## Sources checked

- [GQ interview: The Survivors—Erykah Badu](https://www.gq.com/story/erykah-badu-gq-music-issue) — First-person interview recalls D’Angelo asking her to open his Dallas show and Kedar Massenburg signing her afterward.

- [Universal Music Japan biography](https://www.universal-music.co.jp/erykah-badu/biography/) — Label bio documents Baduizm’s 1997 release and breakthrough.
- [The New Yorker profile](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2016/04/25/erykah-badu-the-godmother-of-soul) — Long-form profile documents her Erykah Free demo and early development before solo success.

## Assessment

The former 1990 decade start was unsupported. Badu’s first widely documented career breakthrough is a 1994 Dallas opening performance; signing and album release followed, with Baduizm in 1997. If the graph instead intended release activity, 1997 would be clearer, but the note describes the distinction.

## Remaining uncertainty

Earlier local performance and demo activity predate the 1994 opening; 1994 is a public career breakthrough marker, not her first time singing.
