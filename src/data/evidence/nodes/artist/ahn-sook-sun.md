## Sources checked

- [KBS World — Master Singer Ahn Suk-seon](https://world.kbs.co.kr/service/contents_view.htm?board_seq=42446&id=&lang=e&menu_cate=culture) — Korean public broadcaster records birth in 1949, early training from age nine, later study with Kim So-hui, and recognition for pansori/gayageum byeongchang. It dates her complete-cycle performances and wider recognition from 1986.
- [Korea.net — Pansori Master Ahn Sook-sun: A Story of 70 Years](https://www.korea.net/koreanet/fileDownload?fileUrl=pdfdata%2F2015%2F09%2FKoreaMagazine08_en_0903.pdf) — Government cultural magazine reports a career exceeding 55 years in 2015, international performances since 1988, and continuing work at the National Gugak Center.
- [Korea Times — Pansori master looks to create zeitgeist work](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/lifestyle/20160824/pansori-master-looks-to-create-zeitgeist-work) — Interview-based profile says she began singing at nine, performed in theaters and hotels before 1979, and became more serious about pansori after joining the National Changgeuk Company that year.

## Assessment

The 1979 company appointment was a career transition, not her first public performance: the Korea Times interview explicitly describes earlier theater and hotel performances. Removed the unsupported start year rather than treating that later milestone or childhood training as her career inception.

## Remaining uncertainty

The checked sources do not establish the year of her earliest public performances.

## Estimated timeline placement (2026-11-05)

The period now uses an explicitly estimated 1960s-to-present placement, superseding
the earlier undated decision. Korea.net's account of more than 55 years of singing
in 2015 supports this broad context, not an exact professional debut. The period
note preserves that limitation; 1979 remains a later career transition.
