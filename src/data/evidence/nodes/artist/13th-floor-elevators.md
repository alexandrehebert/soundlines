## Sources checked

- [Texas State Historical Association — 13th Floor Elevators](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/13th-floor-elevators) — Texas history reference dates the group’s origin and initial lineup to 1965, first single and national exposure to 1966, and breakup to 1969. Documents Hall’s 1965 use of “psychedelic” to describe their sound.
- [AllMusic — The 13th Floor Elevators](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/thirteenth-floor-elevator-mn0000504928) — Existing source was a specialist artist history but direct access returned 403; TSHA supplies accessible independent corroboration.
- [Boston University — 13th Floor Elevators historical study](https://open.bu.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/6979eeac-f214-4398-aa77-e7bc8924a9d5/content) — Academic study search extract dates an early group formation to December 1965, consistent with TSHA.

## Assessment

Graph’s 1966 start confused first release/public breakthrough with group formation. Primary regional history places formation in 1965 and disbanding in 1969; updated both. The “psychedelic” label is documented as bandleader Tommy Hall’s own description, not an uncontested first-ever claim.
