## Sources checked

- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame — A Tribe Called Quest](https://rockhall.com/inductees/a-tribe-called-quest/) — Institutional biography places the group in the mid-1980s, characterizes its jazz/soul sampling and Afrocentric themes, and highlights its 1990s sound development.
- [CBS News — Q-Tip on A Tribe Called Quest’s final album](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/qtip-a-tribe-called-quest-final-album-tribute-phife-dawg/) — Interview reporting Q-Tip’s account that he formed the group with Ali Shaheed Muhammad, Jarobi White, and Phife Dawg in 1985; confirms its final album announcement.
- [The Guardian — A Tribe Called Quest announce final album](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2016/oct/28/a-tribe-called-quest-final-album-phife-dawg) — Contemporaneous 2016 report quotes Q-Tip calling the forthcoming album the group’s final one.
- [The Guardian — A Tribe Called Quest hint at breakup with “last” shows](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2017/jul/31/a-tribe-called-quest-break-up-new-york-la-show) — Contemporaneous report describes 2017 performances as the group’s last, supporting the period endpoint.

## Assessment

The 1988 start conflated the name/recording-era marker with the group’s earlier formation. Shifted to circa 1985. The 2017 endpoint is a final-performance marker, not a claim that the group had been continuously active; it had an earlier 1998 breakup and later reunions.
