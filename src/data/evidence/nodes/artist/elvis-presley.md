# Elvis Presley

Graph ID `artist:elvis-presley`

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress Information Bulletin](https://www.loc.gov/loc/lcib/0303/sound.html) — LOC identifies the Sun studio sessions from 1954–55 as Presley’s first recordings and the launch of his career.
- [Sun Records official history](https://sunrecords.com/history/) — Label history documents his signing and early Memphis recording activity in 1954.
- [Graceland biography](https://www.graceland.com/biography) — Presley’s estate/museum biography says his singing career began with Sun Records in 1954.

## Assessment

1953 was the private acetate/demo recording year, not public career activity. LOC, Sun Records and Graceland support 1954 as his commercial-career start; his first RCA single and national breakthrough came later in 1956.

## Remaining uncertainty

This entry uses first commercial-career activity, not the private demo made for his mother in 1953.
