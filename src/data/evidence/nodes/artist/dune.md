## Assessment

Dune is a German electronic dance project, not simply a techno act: contemporary press places it in the German dance scene, while the specialist biography also identifies rave and happy hardcore. The 1995 debut single and chart breakthrough are directly supported by the label audio and contemporary trade press. Sources differ on when the project was formed (early studio work in 1993/1994 versus public debut/formation in 1995); the graph’s 1995 period marks the documented release era, not a definitive founding date.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact founding year and original lineup chronology remain inconsistent across the current official history and retrospective catalog biographies. The graph does not claim an exact formation date.
