## Sources checked

- [5 Magazine: Armando Gallop, A Life](https://5mag.net/acid-house/armando-gallop-a-life/) — Long oral-history article with collaborators recounts active scene presence by 1986, tracks, label releases, and his 1996 death.
- [Defected: Mike Dunn interview](https://defected.com/news/post/mike-dunn-house-music-all-life-long) — Label interview corroborates 1988 acid tracks and the Warehouse label context.
- [Martian Arts: Armando](https://martianarts.net/armando/) — Scene-oriented archival account ties his early-teen DJ career and 1988 major releases to Chicago.
- [AllMusic: Armando](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/armando-mn0000925213) — Existing authority citation; not relied on for exact dates in this assessment.

## Assessment

Changed the overbroad 1985 start to 1986, an oral-history marker of his presence in the Chicago club scene. The 1996 endpoint reflects his death, not the end of influence. Strong agreement supports his importance and key 1988 releases.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact first recording date remains uncertain; “151” and “Land of Confusion” chronology varies across secondary accounts and represses. Avoid implying 1986 was his first release.
