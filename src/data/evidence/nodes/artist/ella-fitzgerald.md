## Sources checked

- [National Endowment for the Arts, Ella Fitzgerald](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/ella-fitzgerald) — Confirms her 1996 death and career arc from the Apollo contest to her long performance career.
- [Library of Congress, Ella Fitzgerald Collection](https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/15/resources/1237) — Archival finding aid documents her 1934 Apollo contest and 1996 death.
- [U.S. Copyright Office, “Chance Debut at Apollo Theater Leads to Storied Career”](https://www.copyright.gov/history/lore/pdfs/200911%20CLore_November2009.pdf) — Institutional account dates the Apollo debut to 1934.

## Assessment

The 1934 start is a public breakthrough marker: she won the Apollo amateur contest and was subsequently hired by Chick Webb. NEA/LOC confirm 1996 endpoint. Her solo career began later, after Webb’s death in 1939. Confidence: high.
