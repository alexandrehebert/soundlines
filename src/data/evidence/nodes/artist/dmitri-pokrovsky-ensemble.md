## Sources checked
- [Encyclopedia.com (Encyclopedia of Russian History): Folk music](https://www.encyclopedia.com/history/encyclopedias-almanacs-transcripts-and-maps/folk-music-0) — Pokrovsky (d. 1996) began collecting and performing village-style Russian folk in the 1970s; revival reached international world-music audiences.
- [Wikipedia: Dmitri Pokrovsky Ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Pokrovsky_Ensemble) — founded in Moscow in 1973 with Tamara Smyslova; Les Noces 1994; performances to 2016–2023.
- [Classical Voice America, 2 May 2018](https://classicalvoiceamerica.org/2018/05/01/seattle-so-shows-post-rite-stuff-in-stravinsky-feast/) — "Moscow-based Dmitry Pokrovsky Ensemble" performs Les noces with the Seattle Symphony.

## Assessment
Founding in 1973 is confirmed. The 1996 end date was Dmitri Pokrovsky's death, not the group's end; the ensemble performed in 2018 and later, so the period is now 1973–open with year precision.
