## Assessment

Replaced the inaccurate 1930 start (his birth decade) with c.1941, derived from biographies placing his first band at age 13–14 and birth in 1927. Kept 2005 death endpoint. Confidence: medium; sources differ by about one year, so circa precision is appropriate.

## Remaining uncertainty
