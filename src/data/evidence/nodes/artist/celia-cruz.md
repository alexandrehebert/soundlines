## Sources checked

- [Florida International University Libraries, Celia Cruz biographical notes](https://latinpop.fiu.edu/discography/Celia_Cruz_Biography.cfm) — University library collection biography says she sang on major radio in the 1940s, joined La Sonora Matancera in 1949, and became prominent in salsa from the mid-1970s.
- [Fania Records, Celia Cruz Artist Essentials](https://fania.com/artist-essentials/celia-cruz/) — Label history dates her selection as Sonora Matancera lead singer to 1950 and her death to 2003.

## Assessment

The current 1940 start is defensible only as a broad year marker: FIU places her early public activity in the 1940s, while her move to La Sonora Matancera is dated 1949 by FIU and 1950 by Fania. Preserve 1940 with an explicit decade-level caveat in the note; 2003 is cross-supported. FIU describes a major career transition into salsa in the mid-1970s, but no phase objects are present to revise.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources disagree by one year on joining La Sonora Matancera (1949 versus 1950); the 1940 graph start is an approximate marker, not a precisely established first performance.
