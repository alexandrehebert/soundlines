## Sources checked

- [BMI, “Prince Royce’s Bachata Breakthrough”](https://www.bmi.com/advice/prince_royces_bachata_breakthrough) — Contemporaneous interview dates his debut album to 2010 and describes its first single, “Stand By Me.”
- [Latin Music and Media, single campaign case study](https://www.iamlmp.com/advertise/campaigns/prince-royce-stand-by-me/) — Documents “Stand By Me” appearing on the campaign site on November 19, 2009, before its 2010 single release.
- [Prince Royce official biography](https://www.princeroyce.com/tour/bio.html) — Artist bio confirms the 2010s debut album and continued career.
- [Apple Music debut album listing](https://music.apple.com/us/album/prince-royce/841018262) — Label-distributed catalog dates the self-titled album to March 2, 2010.

## Assessment

Changed the unsupported c.2000 start to 2009, the earliest documented public circulation of his debut single. The signed deal/demo preceded this; 2010 marks formal single/album launch and wider breakthrough. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

A recording of “Stand By Me” was promoted online in 2009, while its official single and album releases are dated 2010; graph uses earliest documented public circulation.
