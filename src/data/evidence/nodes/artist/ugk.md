## Sources checked

- [Texas State Historical Association, Handbook of Texas](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/ugk-underground-kingz) — Documents UGK’s late-1980s Port Arthur formation, Pimp C’s 2007 death and the posthumous 2009 final album.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/ugk-mn0000221637) — Confirms the late-1980s formation and major-label debut in 1992; describes the hiatus around incarceration and return in 2007.
- [UGK official website](https://www.ugk4life.com/) — Artist legacy site for the duo and its catalog.

## Assessment

Kept the duo’s active period through Pimp C’s death in 2007; the 2009 album is posthumous rather than evidence of continued joint activity. 1987 is a widely cited formation year, though some sources only give “late 1980s.”

## Remaining uncertainty

UGK 4 Life was released in 2009 after Pimp C’s death; period end marks the duo’s last active phase rather than last catalog release.
