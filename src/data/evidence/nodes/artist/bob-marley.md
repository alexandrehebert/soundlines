## Sources checked

- [Bob Marley official history](https://www.bobmarley.com/history/) — Official history covers early career and formation of the Wailing Wailers in 1963.
- [National Library of Jamaica biography](https://nlj.gov.jm/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/bn_marley_rn_054.pdf) — National library source says Marley recorded “Judge Not” in 1961 and identifies later Wailers formation as 1964, showing some source disagreement.
- [Jamaica Information Service biography](https://jis.gov.jm/information/famous-jamaicans/robert-nesta-marley/) — Government biography dates group formation to 1963, providing corroboration against the library’s 1964 account.
- [Existing AllMusic reggae genre page](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/reggae-ma0000002820) — Genre page is contextual only; not a source for Marley’s dates.

## Assessment

Changed the vague decade-only period to the reported 1961 first recording and corrected the endpoint from 1980 to death in 1981. Notes flag source disagreement over whether the Wailers formed in 1963 or 1964. The group formation date is distinct from Marley’s solo career marker.
