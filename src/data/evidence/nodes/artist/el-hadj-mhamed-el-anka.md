## Sources checked

- [El Anka authority record, BnF](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb13954501v) — French national library states his fame spread rapidly through records from 1927 and credits him with creating a new popular-song genre.
- [El Anka biographical overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Hadj_M%27Hamed_El_Anka) — Secondary summary dates his first Columbia discs to 1928 and notes earlier activity after 1926.
- [El Hadj M’hamed El Anka profile, L’Echo d’Algérie](https://lechodalgerie.dz/musique-chaabi-el-hadj-mhamed-el-anka-une-institution-musicale/) — Algerian newspaper profile discusses his role and teaching lineage.

## Assessment

Replaced the unsupported circa-1920 start with 1927, the earliest year explicitly tied to his recording-based reputation by the BnF. Some sources date the first Columbia discs to 1928, so 1927 is a documented rise marker rather than a proven first record.

## Remaining uncertainty

The exact first-record year varies between 1927 and 1928 references; first known public musical activity predates both.
