## Sources checked

- [Jam-On Productions / Newcleus official history](https://www.jamonproductions.com/newcleus/) — First-person group history traces origins to a 1976 DJ collective and dates the first Newcleus credited single to 1983.
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily interview](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2015/06/key-tracks-newcleus-jam-on-it/) — Interview with Cozmo D recalls Jam-On Productions forming in 1977 and explains its transition toward group recordings.

## Assessment

Replaced the unsupported 1982 start with 1983, the documented first single credited to Newcleus. The antecedent Jam-On collective dates back to 1976–77; the description preserves this earlier phase without collapsing it into the recording group’s release history. No end date is supported.

## Remaining uncertainty

The date the project adopted the Newcleus name is not clearly specified; 1983 is a defensible public recording milestone.
