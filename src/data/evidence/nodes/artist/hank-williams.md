## Sources checked

- [Country Music Hall of Fame, Hank Williams](https://countrymusichalloffame.org/hall-of-fame/hank-williams) — Museum biography states career was underway by the mid-1930s and places the first Drifting Cowboys band around 1938.
- [Encyclopedia of Alabama, Hank Williams Sr.](https://encyclopediaofalabama.org/article/hank-williams-sr/) — State encyclopedia documents WSFA radio work intermittently from 1937–1942.
- [Pulitzer Prizes, Hank Williams](https://www.pulitzer.org/winners/hank-williams) — Biography notes early radio debut in late 1936 or early 1937 and explicitly says accounts vary.
- [Alabama Music Hall of Fame, Hank Williams](https://www.alamhof.org/hank-williams) — State music hall record gives a later 1941 WSFA date, demonstrating source variation and the risk of false precision.

## Assessment

Replaced the mistaken 1930 start with circa 1936 for the documented beginning of public musical/radio activity; sources disagree between late 1936/1937, and the first band is dated around 1938. Kept 1953 as the biographical endpoint. Confidence: medium-high for a mid-1930s start, medium for the exact year.

## Remaining uncertainty

Early radio and performance chronology is disputed: use circa 1936 rather than treating one recollection or institution’s date as exact.
