## Sources checked

- [David Morales official biography](https://www.djdavidmorales.com/) — describes his New York club DJ work in the 1980s–90s, Def Mix with Frankie Knuckles and Judy Weinstein, and his extensive remix/production career.
- [David Morales interview, Groove.de](https://groove.de/2016/09/20/david-morales-def-mix-interview-finn-johannsen/2/) — Morales recalls joining Judy Weinstein’s For The Record DJ pool in 1983 and being recommended to play Paradise Garage.
- [1998 David Morales interview, Spirit of House](https://www.spiritofhouse.com/interviews/interview-david-morales-1998.html) — reports joining the record pool in 1983 and his breakthrough Paradise Garage bookings; useful contemporary corroboration.

## Assessment

Changed start from 1985 to 1983, supported by both Morales’s first-person recollection and a contemporary interview describing his record-pool membership and Garage opportunity. His later official bio supports role and Def Mix significance. Left end open because he remains active. Replaced Wikipedia.

## Remaining uncertainty

Morales says he began DJing at 13, earlier than 1983; 1983 is used as the documented professional New York club/record-pool milestone, not his first time playing records.
