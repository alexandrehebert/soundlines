## Sources checked

- [Peer-reviewed article, Records as records](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/181660/1/23257962.2021.pdf) — Documents Hardy DJing at Den One from 1974, returning to Chicago and the Music Box in 1982, and his death in 1992.
- [Resident Advisor news obituary](https://ra.co/news/72600) — Reports Hardy died March 2, 1992 and describes the significance of his Music Box sets.
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, Ron Hardy at the Music Box](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2015/05/ron-hardy-at-the-music-box/) — Details Music Box chronology, later club residencies and Hardy’s edits.

## Assessment

1974–1992 is well supported by the academic account and corroborating obituary. Softened the “broke Acid Tracks” claim: his repeated cassette play is a documented part of its early circulation, but a single DJ should not be credited as sole originator of the record’s reputation.
