## Sources checked

- [English National Opera — Alban Berg](https://www.eno.org/people/alban-berg/) — Opera-house biography dates Schoenberg study to 1904, describes Berg’s stylistic development, and records Wozzeck’s 1925 premiere and the 1935–37 composition of Lulu.
- [IRCAM — Alban Berg biography](https://ressources.ircam.fr/fr/composer/alban-berg/biography) — Paris research institute dates Berg’s life 1885–1935, Schoenberg lessons from October 1904, first mature works, and later wartime context.
- [Cambridge University Press — Alban Berg excerpt](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805218/63414/excerpt/9780521863414_excerpt.htm) — Existing specialist book excerpt retained as an additional biographical reference; exact excerpt contents were not re-fetched here.

## Assessment

The 1905 decade-level start obscured a well-documented 1904 turning point: Berg’s composition lessons with Schoenberg. Changed to 1904 as a career-development marker, not his first-ever composition; end date remains his death in 1935.
