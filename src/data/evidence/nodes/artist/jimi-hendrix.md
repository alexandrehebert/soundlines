## Sources checked

- [Official Jimi Hendrix biography](https://www.jimihendrix.com/biography/) — Documents sideman work with the Isley Brothers and Little Richard before his London breakthrough, and death on September 18, 1970.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Jimi Hendrix Experience induction essay](https://rockhall.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/The_Jimi_Hendrix_Experience_1992.pdf) — Institutional biography corroborates early 1960s backing work and the rapid rise of the Experience.
- [AllMusic biography](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/jimi-mn0000354105) — Distinguishes early-1960s backup-guitar work from solo breakthrough in 1967 and confirms death in 1970.

## Assessment

The 1962–1970 span is acceptable as broad professional activity through death, but 1962 should not be mistaken for the start of his solo or internationally known career: he worked as a sideman first and broke through with the Experience from 1966–67. Confidence: high for end, medium for exact start.

## Remaining uncertainty

Career onset may be dated by early paid sideman work or later solo breakthrough; keep that distinction explicit.
