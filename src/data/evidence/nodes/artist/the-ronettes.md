# The Ronettes

## Sources checked

- [Ronnie Spector official biography](https://www.ronniespector.com/about) — Ronnie says she formed the Ronettes as a teenager and made her first records in 1961.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame profile](https://rockhall.com/inductees/ronettes/) — Hall of Fame history traces the act from earlier teenage performances to the name Ronettes and its 1960s hits.
- [Library of Congress, “Be My Baby” at 60](https://blogs.loc.gov/now-see-hear/2023/08/be-my-baby-at-60/) — Describes the trio and notes their first reunion performance under the name at the 2007 induction after 1967.

## Assessment

Kept decade-level 1959 as the earlier group formation period, but the Ronettes name and recording career are better dated to 1961. Original activity largely ended by 1967; a 2007 Hall of Fame appearance was a reunion, not renewed career activity.

## Remaining uncertainty

Early-group formation date varies (1958–61 depending on whether the Dolly Sisters/teenage act or Ronettes-named group is meant).
