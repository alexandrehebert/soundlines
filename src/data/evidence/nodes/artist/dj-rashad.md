## Sources checked

- [DJ Rashad — Warp Publishing](https://warppublishing.net/artists/dj-rashad/) — Publisher profile confirms name, Hammond/Chicago ties, Teklife co-founding with DJ Spinn, footwork role and 2014 death.
- [Footwork Pioneer DJ Rashad Dies, Aged 34 — The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2014/apr/28/footwork-pioneer-dj-rashad-dies-aged-34) — Obituary documents 2004 first EP, 2006–13 albums and Double Cup’s 2013 critical recognition. Its initial account of the cause of death was later corrected; no cause is repeated here.

- [Teklife / Ghettoville / Eski — Goldsmiths, University of London](https://research.gold.ac.uk/id/eprint/28833/7/9781912685790_Teklife_Book.pdf) — Academic book situates Rashad and Teklife within Chicago’s locally grounded footwork scene and its international networks.
- [Double Cup — DJ Rashad / Hyperdub](https://dj-rashad.bandcamp.com/album/double-cup-2) — Artist/label release page confirms the 2013 album and memorial context after Rashad’s 2014 death.

## Assessment

The only existing biography link was AllMusic, and the graph gave a 1990s start without explaining it. Bandcamp Daily and academic work support his roots in local crew/party culture; Guardian documents the 2004 first EP and 2013 recognition. The 1990s remains a broad scene-entry marker, and speculative individual peak phases were removed.

## Remaining uncertainty

Sources vary on when local parties/crews began; 1990s is a broad career-entry window, while his first widely documented EP came in 2004.
