## Sources checked

- [Gnaoua and World Music Festival, Organisation](https://www.festival-gnaoua.net/en/organisation/) — Official festival history dates its creation to 1998.
- [Gnaoua and World Music Festival, The Festival and Essaouira](https://www.festival-gnaoua.net/en/the-festival-and-the-city-of-essaouira/) — Describes the festival’s role in bringing Gnawa masters to wider international audiences and cross-cultural collaborations.
- [UNESCO/UNDP report on Essaouira festival](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/in/rest/annotationSVC/DownloadWatermarkedAttachment/attach_import_3a642ad6-8cca-4230-bcd3-96635c041502?_=224698eng.pdf) — Describes the festival’s 1998 launch and its role in raising Gnawa music’s international profile.

## Assessment

The 1998 start and recurring platform for Gnawa musicians are supported. Cross-tradition exchange is a stated festival feature; phrase effects as opportunity and visibility rather than a guaranteed transformation of the tradition.
