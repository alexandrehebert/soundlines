## Sources checked

- [Beethoven-Haus Bonn: Ninth Symphony](https://www.beethoven.de/en/work/view/5556714292117504/) — Confirms the Vienna premiere on 7 May 1824 and notes the work’s actual first London performance was in 1825.
- [Beethoven-Haus Bonn, 200th anniversary account](https://www.beethoven.de/en/press/view/5715431570538496/BTHVN2024%2B-%2B200%2Byears%2Bof%2BBeethoven%2526%252339%253Bs%2BNinth) — Documents the academy concert and accompanying works on 7 May 1824.
- [Boston Symphony Orchestra work guide](https://www.bso.org/works/symphony-no-9-in-d-minor-op-125-cho) — Provides premiere venue, conductor, soloists, and choral-finale context.

## Assessment

The 7 May 1824 Vienna date is confirmed. The choral finale is an important formal innovation; the broad claim that it expanded public possibilities is interpretive, and should be presented as significance rather than a directly measurable event.
