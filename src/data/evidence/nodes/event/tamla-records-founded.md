## Sources checked

- [Motown Museum archive, Berry Gordy](https://archives.motownmuseum.org/people-acts/people-acts/active_tab/actor/item/gordy-berry-1929-present) — Museum archive dates Gordy’s $800 loan to establish his company to 12 January 1959 and identifies “Come to Me” as the first release on Tamla.
- [Library of Congress, Civil Rights Era timeline](https://www.loc.gov/exhibits/civil-rights-act/civil-rights-era-timeline.html) — Places Berry Gordy’s founding of Motown Records in Detroit in 1959.
- [Motown Museum, Berry Gordy biography](https://www.motownmuseum.org/legacy/berry-gordy/) — Distinguishes the 1959 start from Motown Record Corporation’s formal incorporation in April 1960.

## Assessment

Added as a major music-industry milestone. The graph distinguishes the January 1959 beginning as Tamla, Motown’s later 1959 naming, and the corporation’s 1960 incorporation; it does not collapse these into one legally precise date.
