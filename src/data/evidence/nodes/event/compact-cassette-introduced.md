# Philips introduces the compact cassette

Graph ID `event:compact-cassette-introduced`

**Review status** · reviewed

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Philips, First Philips cassette recorder, 1963](https://www.philips.com/a-w/about/news/media-library/20190101-First-Philips-cassette-recorder-1963.html) — Philips dates introduction of its first compact cassette recorder at the Berlin Funkausstellung to August 1963.
- [Philips backgrounder, Key innovations](https://www.philips.com/consumerfiles/newscenter/main/shared/assets/es/Downloadablefile/Backgrounder%20_Key%20innovations.pdf) — Confirms launch of the Compact Cassette at the 1963 Berlin International Consumer Electronics Fair.

## Assessment

The 1963 introduction is confirmed. The later impact on home recording and portability is well established, though this short event note appropriately describes subsequent effects rather than claiming immediate universal adoption.
