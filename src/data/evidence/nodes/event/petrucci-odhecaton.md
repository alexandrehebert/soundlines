# Petrucci prints the Odhecaton

Graph ID `event:petrucci-odhecaton`

**Review status** · reviewed

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress, Harmonice musices odhecaton A](https://www.loc.gov/item/2008561329/) — Catalog identifies the work as first published in 1501 and preserves a copy in its pre-1700 music collection.
- [University of Cambridge, Copyright History: Petrucci commentary](https://www.copyrighthistory.org/cam/commentary/i_1498/i_1498_com_1220082186.html) — Scholarly commentary clarifies that Petrucci was not the first to print music generally, but pioneered large-scale printed polyphony and a market for it.
- [Polish Music Library, Ottaviano Petrucci](https://polskabibliotekamuzyczna.pl/encyklopedia/petrucci-ottaviano/?lang=en) — Library encyclopedia dates publication to 15 May 1501 and identifies it as a landmark collection of polyphonic works.

## Assessment

1501 and the collection’s importance are confirmed. Avoid “first music print” without qualification: earlier single-line music printing existed; the Odhecaton is widely identified as the first major printed collection of polyphonic music.
