## Sources checked

- [YouTube Blog, A to Z: #HappyBirthdayYouTube](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-to-z-happybirthdayyoutube/) — Company history identifies 23 April 2005 as upload date of the first video, “Me at the Zoo.”
- [Victoria and Albert Museum, early YouTube watch page and first video](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/acquiring-an-early-youtube-watch-page-and-its-first-ever-video) — Museum documents the first video upload and notes YouTube was registered in February and first website launched in May 2005.
- [YouTube Blog, YouTube at 15](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-at-15-my-personal-journey/) — Co-founder retrospective distinguishes website registration, first upload, and public launch.

## Assessment

The year is correct, but “launch” compresses several milestones: company registration in February, beta/site launch in May, and first video upload in April. Make the node’s intended milestone explicit; the platform launch, rather than first upload, is better described as spring 2005.
