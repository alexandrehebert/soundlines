## Sources checked

- [Philharmonie de Paris, L’Orfeo collections record](https://collectionsdumusee.philharmoniedeparis.fr/0752026-l-orfeo-de-claudio-monteverdi.aspx) — Museum record identifies Monteverdi’s opera as performed at the Mantuan ducal palace in 1607.
- [OPERA America, National Opera Calendar](https://www.operaamerica.org/calendar/production/21940/orfeo) — Opera reference identifies its 1607 court-performance context and premiere history.
- [Opéra de Lausanne, L’Orfeo](https://www.opera-lausanne.ch/en/show/lorfeo/) — Provides the commonly cited date, 24 February 1607, and Mantua venue.

## Assessment

The 1607 Mantua performance is established. It was a court event, not a public opera-house premiere. “One of the earliest operas to remain central to the repertory” is better than calling it the first opera.
