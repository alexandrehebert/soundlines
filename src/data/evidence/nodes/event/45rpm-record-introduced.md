# RCA Victor introduces the 45 rpm single

## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress, Audio and Recording Firsts](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-plan/tools-and-resources/documents/The-Sound-of-Being-First.pdf) — Dates RCA Victor’s 45 rpm introduction to 1949 and notes that historians dispute which title was first.
- [RILM Music Encyclopedias: 45-Rpm Discs](https://rme.rilm.org/articles/ers01360/1.0/ers01360) — Music reference entry dates U.S. introduction by RCA Victor to February 1949.
- [U.S. Government Publishing Office history of LP/45](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GPO-TNW-17-1-2008/pdf/GPO-TNW-17-1-2008-5.pdf) — Places RCA’s 7-inch singles after Columbia’s 1948 LP announcement.

## Assessment

1949 is sound for the format’s introduction. The “first 45 record” is contested; the event should describe the launch of a format rather than claim a single first title. Refine the graph description to distinguish 45 rpm discs from Columbia’s 1948 LP.
