# iTunes Music Store opens for downloads

## Sources checked

- [Apple, iTunes Music Store launch press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2003/04/28Apple-Launches-the-iTunes-Music-Store/) — Contemporaneous company release dates the U.S. service launch to 28 April 2003 and describes per-track downloads without subscriptions.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of American History, Apple’s iTunes](https://americanhistory.si.edu/explore/exhibitions/americas-listening/online/apples-itunes) — Museum overview describes licensed digital song sales and the service’s role in a consumer shift.

## Assessment

The launch date and initial U.S.-only scope are established. It helped normalize legal per-track downloads but did not alone create digital music sales or eliminate file sharing.
