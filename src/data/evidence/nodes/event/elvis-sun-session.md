## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress, Elvis Presley Sun Records sessions](https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/recording-registry/registry-by-induction-years/2002/) — Identifies Sun sessions from 1954–55 and their role in Presley’s career and the rock-and-roll era.
- [Library of Congress, Sun Records Studio catalog](https://www.loc.gov/item/2010630851/) — Catalogs the Memphis studio and its history; dates the studio opening, distinct from Presley’s session.

## Assessment

The 1954 Sun period is supported; this graph entry should not imply the studio opened in 1954 (it opened earlier) or that one session alone created rock and roll. “Key early recording” is appropriately scoped.
