## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress, Le Sacre du Printemps](https://www.loc.gov/collections/bronislava-nijinska/articles-and-essays/le-sacre-du-printemps/) — Dates the premiere to 29 May 1913 at Théâtre des Champs-Élysées in Paris.
- [Philharmonie de Paris, Le Sacre du printemps](https://pad.philharmoniedeparis.fr/0743340-le-sacre-du-printemps-de-igor-stravinski.aspx) — Confirms premiere details, Ballets Russes production, and the event’s tumultuous reception.
- [Boston Symphony Orchestra, The Rite of Spring](https://www.bso.org/works/the-rite-of-spring) — Provides work history and premiere context.

## Assessment

The date and location are certain. Contemporary reception accounts agree on disturbance but differ on its extent and cause; the graph appropriately says accounts vary. Avoid repeating the myth that the audience immediately rioted solely because of the music.
