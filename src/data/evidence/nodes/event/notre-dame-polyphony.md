## Sources checked

- [Cambridge History of Medieval Music, Notre Dame](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-history-of-medieval-music/notre-dame/94C93F0FE9B8B2690D8329693A9FCA83) — Scholarly chapter examines Notre-Dame repertory, chronology, and the source-based limits of assigning works to named composers.
- [Notre-Dame de Paris, Polyphony and motets](https://www.notredamedeparis.fr/en/understand/music/polyphony-and-motets/) — Institutional account describes the later retrospective account naming Léonin and Pérotin and development of the so-called Notre-Dame school.
- [University of California eScholarship, Voice of the Cathedral](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/10v1x47m) — Scholarly study contextualizes twelfth-century Notre-Dame sound and polyphonic evidence.

## Assessment

A late-twelfth-century marker is reasonable, but “Notre-Dame school” is a modern label built partly on a later account, and composer attributions/chronology remain debated. Describe the repertory and manuscript evidence rather than a formally organized school with a precise start.
