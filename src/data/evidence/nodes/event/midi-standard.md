## Sources checked

- [MIDI Association, MIDI History: 1981–1983](https://midi.org/midi-history-chapter-6-midi-begins-1981-1983) — Association history traces manufacturer discussions, 1982 agreement, and public demonstration at the 1983 NAMM show.
- [Library of Congress, MIDI Sequence Data](https://wwws.loc.gov/preservation/digital/formats/fdd/fdd000102.shtml) — Technical sustainability description dates the MIDI specification to 1983 and summarizes its purpose.
- [MIDI Association, MIDI 1.0](https://midi.org/midi-1-0) — Identifies the 1983 first publication and manufacturer collaboration.

## Assessment

The 1983 standard publication/demo milestone is supported. Agreement discussions started earlier, so describe the standard as agreed/finalized and publicly demonstrated in 1983, not invented from scratch that year.
