## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress, Berliner gramophone patent specification](https://www.loc.gov/item/berl0124/) — Catalogs U.S. patent no. 372,786, dated 8 November 1887.
- [Library of Congress, Emile Berliner timeline](https://www.loc.gov/collections/emile-berliner/articles-and-essays/timeline/) — Dates the first gramophone patent to 1887 and notes early lateral-cut discs.

## Assessment

The 1887 patent date is confirmed. The patent marks a technology step, while commercial disc adoption and Berliner’s later companies occurred over subsequent years; keep that distinction clear.
