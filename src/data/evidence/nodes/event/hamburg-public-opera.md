# Hamburg’s public opera house opens

**Review status** · reviewed

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Hamburg State Opera, history](https://www.die-hamburgische-staatsoper.de/de/ueberuns/haus) — Institutional history dates the Gänsemarkt public opera house to 1678 and calls it the first privately operated public opera house in Germany.
- [Hamburg city government, Historic Gänsemarkt](https://www.hamburg.de/politik-und-verwaltung/behoerden/finanzbehoerde/wir-ueber-uns/historie-gaensemarkt-203400) — City history confirms the German opera house opened there in 1678 and operated until 1738.

## Assessment

The 1678 opening is confirmed. “Public” and “commercial” distinguish the venue from court opera; avoid broad claims that this was Europe’s first public opera house, since Venice’s San Cassiano predates it.
