## Sources checked

- [National Park Service, Origins of Sound Recording: Thomas Edison](https://www.nps.gov/edis/learn/historyculture/origins-of-sound-recording-thomas-edison.htm) — Documents Edison’s late-1877 phonograph conception and tinfoil cylinder demonstration.
- [Library of Congress, Historical Background Timeline](https://www.loc.gov/programs/national-recording-preservation-plan/tools-and-resources/historical-background/timeline/) — Places Edison’s phonograph in the history of practical sound recording and distinguishes later commercial systems.

## Assessment

1877 is appropriate as the invention milestone. Edison’s tinfoil phonograph could record and play back sound, but durable commercial music distribution depended on later cylinder and disc technologies.
