## Sources checked

- [Journal of the American Musicological Society, The Council of Trent Revisited](https://online.ucpress.edu/jams/article-abstract/55/1/1/49809/The-Council-of-Trent-Revisited) — Musicological re-examination finds limited official pronouncements and stresses local implementation; it rejects the simplistic story of a universal ban on polyphony.
- [Oxford Academic, English Plainchant Revival: historical background](https://academic.oup.com/book/49282/chapter-abstract/422542906) — Dates music-related decrees to sessions in 1562 and 1563.

## Assessment

The graph’s 1545–1563 period captures the Council as a whole, but music resolutions were chiefly discussed in 1562–63. Its wording correctly avoids claiming a general ban on polyphony; retain the broad period only as Council dates, not as the period of the specific music decrees.
