## Sources checked

- [University of California Press, Sounding the Indian Ocean](https://www.luminosoa.org/books/175/files/82efdc1c-2aa8-4890-af54-b7984daccd06.pdf) — Scholarly open-access book documents Siti binti Saad and her collective recording titles in Bombay in 1928–29 for HMV.
- [Verba Africana, Kijiti by Siti binti Saad: author and context](https://verbafricana.org/swahili/b-kijiti-author-and-context.htm) — Academic project notes the group recorded over 250 songs for HMV and Columbia between 1928 and 1930.
- [Archive of Indian Music, Siti Binti Saad recordings](https://retrotan2020.com/siti-binti-saad/) — Provides contextual details of the Gramophone Company’s Bombay sessions with Zanzibar taarab musicians in 1928.

## Assessment

The 1928 Bombay sessions are supported. Recordings helped extend taarab’s circulation, but distribution and reception varied; “helping circulate” is appropriately measured.
