## Sources checked

- [SoundCloud company history](https://soundcloud.com/company/about-us) — dates the company’s founding by Alexander Ljung and Eric Wahlforss in Berlin to 2007.
- [Hesmondhalgh, Jones & Rauh, “SoundCloud and Bandcamp as Alternative Music Platforms”](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2056305119883429) — dates SoundCloud’s launch from Berlin to 2008 and analyzes its later role as an audio-distribution platform, including in EDM and hip-hop.

## Assessment

The graph’s year refers to the company’s 2007 founding, not public launch. The record now states this distinction; research commonly dates the platform launch to 2008.
