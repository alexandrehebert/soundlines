## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress: Jack Kapp Collection finding aid](https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/11/resources/801) — Confirms the 1942 ban and its different settlement dates across companies.
- [Cornell University: National War Labor Board arbitration finding aid](https://rmc.library.cornell.edu/EAD/htmldocs/KCL05315.html) — Dates the nationwide stoppage by union members to 1 August 1942.
- [Oxford Academic, “Facing the Music: Recorded Sound”](https://doi.org/10.1093/acprof:oso/9780190467036.003.0009) — Scholarly overview of the 1942–44 strike and its labor context.

## Assessment

The ban began on 1 August 1942, after AFM president James Petrillo announced that members would stop recording for commercial labels. It did not halt all sound recording: radio broadcasts, non-union sessions, and later V-Disc activity complicate broad claims.
