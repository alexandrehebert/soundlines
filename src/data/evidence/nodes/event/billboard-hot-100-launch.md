# Billboard launches the Hot 100

## Sources checked

- [Billboard 50th anniversary issue](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Billboard/00s/2008/BB-2008-09-20.pdf) — Billboard’s retrospective dates the chart’s debut to August 1958 and reproduces its original methodology explanation.
- [Billboard archive, first Hot 100 chart](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/Archive-Billboard/80s/1988/BB-1988-04-30.pdf) — Historical chart archive identifies the first chart as 4 August 1958.
- [Guinness World Records](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/101513-first-no-1-single-us) — Corroborates the Hot 100 launch date and its first No. 1, “Poor Little Fool.”

## Assessment

The launch year is correct; the first chart was dated 4 August 1958. Its original formula included sales, radio play and jukebox activity; avoid implying the modern chart methodology is unchanged.
