## Sources checked

- [Conservatoire de Paris official history](https://www.conservatoiredeparis.fr/en/school/le-conservatoire/history) — Institutional history dates the law establishing the Conservatoire de Musique to 3 August 1795 and explains its predecessor schools.
- [Bibliothèque nationale de France catalog authority](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb118633265) — Confirms the institution’s 1795 foundation date.

## Assessment

The 1795 founding date is confirmed. The institution’s predecessors date to 1783 and 1792, so the graph should frame 1795 as the creation of the Conservatoire, not the beginning of organized state music education in Paris.
