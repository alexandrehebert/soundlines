## Sources checked

- [UNESCO, Pansori Epic Chant](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/pansori-epic-chant-00070) — UNESCO records the 2003 Masterpiece proclamation and 2008 incorporation into the Representative List.
- [UNESCO, Baul Songs](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/baul-songs-00107) — UNESCO records Baul Songs’ 2008 inscription on the Representative List.
- [UNESCO Republic of Korea periodic report](https://ich.unesco.org/en/state/republic-of-korea-KR?cp=KR&info=periodic-reporting&topic=en-state) — Confirms Pansori was declared a Masterpiece in 2003 and incorporated into the Representative List in 2008.

## Assessment

The combined 2008 Representative List date is correct for both entries, but Pansori had already been proclaimed a Masterpiece in 2003. Keep that earlier milestone explicit if relevant; UNESCO inscription recognizes safeguarding status, not origin.
