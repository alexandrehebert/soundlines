## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress interview, Monterey Pop with Michelle Phillips](https://lcweb2.loc.gov/static/programs/national-film-preservation-board/documents/Interview_Michelle-Phillips.pdf) — Dates the three-day festival at Monterey Fairgrounds to 16–18 June 1967.
- [Smithsonian, 1967 and the Summer of Love](https://music.si.edu/story/1967-and-summer-love) — Connects the festival to the music and cultural context of 1967.
- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame library guide](https://library.rockhall.com/greatest_festival_moments/monterey_1967) — Provides an institutional reference to the festival’s dates and historical framing.

## Assessment

Dates and place are well supported. Its influence on later festival culture is an interpretation supported by music histories; keep “helped turn” rather than claiming it invented the modern festival.
