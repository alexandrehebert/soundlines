# Raï is inscribed on UNESCO’s heritage list

Graph ID `event:rai-unesco-inscription`

**Review status** · reviewed

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Raï](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/ra-popular-folk-song-of-algeria-01894) — Official listing identifies Raï as a popular folk song of Algeria and confirms inscription in 2022.
- [UNESCO Intergovernmental Committee decision 17.COM 7.B.38](https://ich.unesco.org/en/decisions/17.COM/7.B.38) — Records the decision to inscribe Raï on the Representative List in 2022.

## Assessment

The 2022 inscription is confirmed. UNESCO recognizes a living cultural practice and safeguarding aims; it does not establish a definitive date or singular origin for Raï.
