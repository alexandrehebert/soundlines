## Sources checked

- [Prestige Records history, Concord](https://concord.com/concord-albums/prestige-records-story-2/) — Current catalog owner dates Weinstock’s launch of Prestige to 1949 and describes its jazz catalog.
- [Bob Weinstock obituary, Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/local/2006/01/18/bob-weinstock/cdcb916f-ea8e-49d6-b1ae-ea5973ed5984/) — Obituary says the first 1949 session was under New Jazz before the name changed to Prestige.
- [Prestige label history, DownBeat](https://www.downbeat.com/digitaledition/2014/DB1410/_art/DB1410.pdf) — Jazz publication recounts the 1949 founding and importance of Weinstock’s catalog.

## Assessment

Kept 1949 but clarified the New Jazz-to-Prestige naming sequence; the first recording activity predates consistent use of the Prestige name.

## Remaining uncertainty

Label, company, and imprint naming was fluid in the earliest releases; 1949 marks the business/catalog origin.
