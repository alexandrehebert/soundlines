## Sources checked

- [The Wire — Jeff Mills interview](https://www.thewire.co.uk/in-writing/interviews/jeff-mills-interview-by-derek-walmsley) — Mills directly recalls starting Underground Resistance with Mike Banks in 1989.
- [Red Bull Music Academy — Jeff Mills lecture](https://www.redbullmusicacademy.com/lectures/jeff-mills-lecture/) — Mills recounts forming the group and label with Banks; supports their shared origin and studio context.
- [Axios Detroit — Techno’s Black roots](https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-detroit-0ea10f20-f472-11ef-9c81-71a40ad4c40c) — Reports UR as a Detroit label started in 1989.

## Assessment

The graph’s founders and 1989 date are supported by Mills’s retrospective interview. Set precision to circa because collective activity and first releases need not share a formal founding date. UR is both a label and broader collective/network.

## Remaining uncertainty

Mike Banks is central to the history; Mills later left the group, so this description does not imply a fixed or permanent partnership.
