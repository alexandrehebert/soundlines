## Sources checked

- [Rough Trade official story](https://www.roughtrade.com/about/our-story) — Official history dates the record shop’s opening to 1976, distinguishing it from the later label.
- [Rough Trade shop history](https://shop.roughtraderecords.com/about?lang=en_GB) — Store retrospective says the label grew from the shop and later was revived by Geoff Travis and Jeannette Lee.
- [Rough Trade profile, Rough Trade Publishing](https://roughtradepublishing.com/about/) — Related company history dates the record label to 1978.

## Assessment

Kept 1978 for the record label, clarifying that the record shop began in 1976. The label experienced ownership and distribution disruptions and was revived in 1999; the start date does not imply uninterrupted operation.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some sources use 1976 for the shop/founding story and 1978 for label releases; the graph concerns the label.
