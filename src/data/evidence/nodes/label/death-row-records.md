## Sources checked

- [Death Row Records history, official store/archive](https://store.deathrowpictures.com/pages/about) — Label retrospective documents key 1993–94 releases and its influential roster.
- [Death Row Records history, ABC News](https://abcnews.com/Entertainment/SummerConcert/story?id=5406064) — News retrospective dates co-founding to 1991 and describes later bankruptcy.
- [Death Row Records archival project, Iron Mountain](https://www.ironmountain.com/resources/case-studies/d/death-row-records) — Records-management project confirms 1991 founding and describes historical hip-hop catalog.

## Assessment

Kept 1991, corroborated by multiple sources, but replaced vague “and others” with key early principals. The label was originally formed as Future Shock before taking the Death Row name; this distinction merits inclusion if the graph models name lineage.

## Remaining uncertainty

Founding accounts vary on whether to credit Suge Knight, Dr. Dre, The D.O.C., and/or Dick Griffey; the name and legal structure changed during formation.
