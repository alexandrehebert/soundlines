## Sources checked

- [Motown Records, Detroit Historical Society](https://www.detroithistorical.org/learn/online-research/encyclopedia-of-detroit/motown-records) — Museum history distinguishes Tamla’s January 1959 founding from the later Motown imprint.
- [Motown Museum about page](https://www.motownmuseum.org/about/) — Museum dates Motown’s establishment to 1959 and identifies Berry Gordy.
- [Motown timeline, AP News](https://apnews.com/article/fcd844cd1174d0c135e3c8156a1f3405) — Associated Press recounts the 1959 founding and original Tamla name.

## Assessment

Kept 1959 while clarifying that Tamla was the first imprint and Motown Record Corporation followed in 1960. This resolves the common 1959/1960 discrepancy.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some accounts use “Motown” broadly for Gordy’s 1959 company, while formal incorporation dates to 1960.
