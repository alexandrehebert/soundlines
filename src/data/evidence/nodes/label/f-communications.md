## Sources checked

- [F Communications retrospective, Qobuz](https://www.qobuz.com/ch-fr/magazine/story/2020/06/16/f-communications-leveil-de-la-scene-electronique-francaise/) — Retrospective dates its launch to April 1994 and describes the founders’ catalog.

## Assessment

Kept 1994 founding date and added the important status nuance: inactivity/dormancy is not the same as documented legal closure, so no end date was added.

## Remaining uncertainty
