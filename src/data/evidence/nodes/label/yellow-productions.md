## Sources checked

- [Yellow Productions — official history](https://www.yellowprod.fr/) — Label’s own history says it was created in 1993 by DJ Yellow and Chris LeFriant (Bob Sinclar), and outlines its stylistic range.
- [Ovum Recordings — DJ Yellow biography](https://ovumrecordings.com/artists/dj-yellow/) — Label biography says DJ Yellow and Bob Sinclar started Yellow Productions in 1993 and describes its eclectic repertoire.
- [Bibliothèque nationale de France — Yellow label authority record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb13979535s) — National library authority record identifies the imprint and its founders, corroborating label identity and partnership.

## Assessment

Changed 1994 to 1993: the label’s official history, an independent label biography, and BnF authority record support 1993 and the cofounder attribution. Avoid treating French house as the label’s only sound; its own account lists jazz, funk, Brazilian styles, soul, hip-hop, and house.

## Remaining uncertainty

The former 1994 may refer to an early release or catalog appearance; consulted sources support 1993 as founding year.
