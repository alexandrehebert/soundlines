## Sources checked

- [Impulse Records official story](https://www.impulserecords.com/our-story/) — Official history details the label’s Coltrane association and uses 1961–65 as its first historical period.
- [Impulse Records history, JazzTimes](https://www.jazztimes.com/features/profiles/the-impulse-records-story-the-house-that-trane-built/?v=a672068055fa) — History dates preparation and early catalog numbers to December 1960 and first Coltrane releases to 1961.
- [Impulse Records 60th anniversary, Jazz.FM](https://jazz.fm/impulse-records-60th-anniversary-reissues-releases/) — Jazz outlet distinguishes 1960 founding under Creed Taylor from 1961 debut releases.

## Assessment

Changed graph start from 1961 to 1960 for founding; 1961 marks the first year of releases and the start of the label’s Coltrane era.

## Remaining uncertainty

The label’s anniversary is sometimes counted from its 1961 first releases, but founding accounts support 1960.
