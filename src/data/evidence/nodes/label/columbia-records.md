## Sources checked

- [Recorded-sound timeline, Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/static/managed-content/uploads/sites/6/2017/02/recorded_sound_timeline-2013.pdf) — LOC dates organization of Columbia Phonograph Company to 1888 and distinguishes subsequent developments.
- [Columbia Records paperwork finding aid, Library of Congress](https://findingaids.loc.gov/repositories/11/resources/795) — Administrative history describes roots in the late 1880s and distinguishes Columbia Graphophone/Phonograph entities.
- [Columbia history, Association for Recorded Sound Collections](https://www.ibew.org.uk/dvarch/DV01472.pdf) — Historical scholarship dates formal founding/incorporation in Washington to January 1889.

## Assessment

Retained 1888 but clarified it means regional organization; 1889 refers to incorporation and early advertising. This resolves the apparent date conflict.

## Remaining uncertainty

“Columbia Records” as a continuing label identity spans reorganizations and corporate successors; period is brand lineage, not one uninterrupted legal entity.
