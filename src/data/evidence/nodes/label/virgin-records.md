## Sources checked

- [Virgin — Company timeline](https://com.prod.loyalty.virgin.com/about-virgin/timeline) — Official timeline distinguishes the 1970 mail-order business and 1972 Manor studio from Virgin Records and Virgin Music Publishing launching in 1973.
- [Virgin Records — Official label profile](https://com.prod.loyalty.virgin.com/virgin-companies/virgin-records) — Official profile identifies Tubular Bells as the first album Virgin Records released.
- [Music Week archive (1977)](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/UK/Music-Week/1977/Music-Week-1977-11-05.pdf) — Contemporary interview reports Simon Draper began working exclusively on the label launch in 1972 and Tubular Bells was released in May 1973.

## Assessment

Changed 1972 to 1973 for the record label launch, following Virgin’s distinction between precursor business/studio activity and the label. Removed the French-electronic-acts example: it was imprecise for the parent label and needs specific imprint/catalog context.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some secondary accounts call the label founded in 1972, apparently using the preparatory launch period; the graph date now tracks first public release and Virgin’s stated launch.
