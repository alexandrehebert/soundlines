## Assessment

The graph’s 1978 date is unsupported by the sources found and conflicts with cataloged Nusrat recordings. Changed period to 1992, tied to EMI Pakistan’s identified studio-album version, and documented a separately cataloged 1990 variant. Qawwali association is sound.

## Remaining uncertainty

Because the graph title is ambiguous across spellings, albums and live performances, 1992 is a specific documented catalog version, not a claim that Nusrat first performed the composition then. Exact session recording date remains unknown.
