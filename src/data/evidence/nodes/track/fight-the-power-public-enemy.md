## Sources checked

- [Official Charts — Fight the Power](https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/public-enemy-fight-the-power/) — Chart database identifies Def Jam and a June 24, 1989 first UK chart date.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of African American History and Culture — Radio Raheem’s boombox](https://nmaahc.si.edu/explore/stories/radio-raheems-boom-box) — Museum account documents the song’s prominent use in Do the Right Thing (1989).
- [Official artist-channel soundtrack audio](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EYUH2g3bshA) — Universal metadata identifies the Do the Right Thing soundtrack version and its 1989 Def Jam copyright.
- [Apple Music — Fight the Power](https://music.apple.com/us/album/fight-the-power/1531780502) — Catalog dates the Fear of a Black Planet version to 1989/1990 release-era catalog; the graph uses the 1989 soundtrack debut.

## Assessment

1989 is correct for the original soundtrack/single version, while the album version came in 1990. The Smithsonian source supports the film connection; conscious hip-hop fits the song’s political content but remains a genre/critical label.

## Remaining uncertainty

Versions vary in mix and release date; this note anchors the year to the original film/single recording.
