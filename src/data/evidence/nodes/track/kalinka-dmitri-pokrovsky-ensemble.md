## Sources checked

- [PTNA Piano Music Encyclopedia — Kalinka](https://enc.piano.or.jp/en/musics/79888) — Music reference identifies Kalinka as composed by Ivan Larionov in 1860, despite its common folk-song label.
- [Library of Congress — Kalinka](https://www.loc.gov/item/jukebox-248283/) — LOC catalog documents an early Russian folk-song recording from 1910, establishing that the repertoire predates the ensemble.
- [Dmitri Pokrovsky Ensemble — official press archive](https://pokrovsky-ensemble.ru/en/media/press/press-release) — Ensemble's press archive notes its Russian folk tradition and explicitly quotes a review saying not to expect them to sing Kalinka; no supporting evidence located for the graph's alleged 1960 recording.
- [Dmitri Pokrovsky Ensemble — official Earthbeat album](https://pokrovsky-ensemble.ru/ru/media/audio/discography/playlist/earthbeat) — Official track listing for the 1987 Earthbeat album contains no Kalinka; this does not rule out a different recording, but does not support the graph claim.

## Assessment

The existing 1960 date cannot be a recording by Dmitri Pokrovsky Ensemble: the ensemble was founded later, in 1973. No source found verifies this group's recording of Kalinka. Reframed the node to the song's sourced 1860 composition and removed the unsupported recorded_by edge. The slug retains the legacy performer wording for stable IDs.

## Remaining uncertainty

If a particular Pokrovsky Ensemble recording is intended, it needs a release/session citation before the artist edge and recording date can be restored.
