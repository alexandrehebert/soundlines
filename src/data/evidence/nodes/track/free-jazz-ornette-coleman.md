## Sources checked

- [National Endowment for the Arts — Ornette Coleman](https://www.arts.gov/honors/jazz/ornette-coleman) — NEA jazz profile describes Free Jazz as a continuous collective improvisation recorded with a double quartet and notes its influence.
- [JazzDisco — Ornette Coleman catalog](https://www.jazzdisco.org/ornette-coleman/catalog/) — Discography identifies the Atlantic 1364 Double Quartet album and dates its issue to 1961.
- [MusicBrainz — Free Jazz release history](https://musicbrainz.org/release-group/8e921fc1-a775-3875-9f0e-55260c5f835e) — Release group lists a September 1961 US album issue; release variants differ.
- [AllMusic — Free Jazz](https://www.allmusic.com/album/free-jazz-mw0000256161) — Review identifies the 1961 album and contextualizes Coleman’s role in the development of free jazz.

## Assessment

1961 is the album-release year; the session itself took place in December 1960. The track/title is tied to an album-length collective improvisation, rather than a conventional song. Free jazz is historically accurate but also the album’s eponymous term.

## Remaining uncertainty

The 37-minute continuous improvisation is the original album concept; later editions and track divisions may differ.
