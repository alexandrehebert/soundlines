## Sources checked

- [R.E.M.HQ — Losing My Religion turns 30](https://remhq.com/news/losing-my-religion-turns-30/) — Band site gives single release date February 19, 1991 and links it to Out of Time.
- [R.E.M.HQ — Losing My Religion in HD](https://remhq.com/news/check-out-losing-my-religion-in-hd/) — Official site again identifies it as the 1991 lead single from Out of Time.
- [Official R.E.M. video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KOYqf0C4DL8) — Official artist channel audio references the Out of Time track; later digital reissue date is not the original release year.

## Assessment

The 1991 year and R.E.M. attribution are well supported. Alternative rock is suitable broad context; the mandolin-led arrangement should not be treated as evidence that the song is folk.

## Remaining uncertainty

Charting continued into 1992 in some markets; original single year is 1991.
