## Sources checked

- [PBS American Masters — Muddy Waters: Can't Be Satisfied](https://www.pbs.org/wnet/americanmasters/muddy-waters-cant-be-satisfied/730/) — Documents the 1941 Library of Congress field recording under the same title, distinct from Waters's later electric Aristocrat recording.
- [Origins of Modern Music Archive — Muddy Waters catalog raisonné](https://originsarchive.com/muddy-waters/) — Catalog raisonné distinguishes 1941 field recording from the 1947 recording issued in 1948, which gave Waters a breakthrough.
- [Red Saunders Research Foundation — Aristocrat label discography](https://campber.people.clemson.edu/aristocrat.html) — Detailed label/session research lists Aristocrat 1305A, December 1947 recording and June 1948 issue.

## Assessment

The 1948 year is supported for the Aristocrat commercial release, but a same-title version was recorded by Alan Lomax in 1941 and issued by the Library of Congress folk collection. The graph must specify which recording it means. Current performer relationship and release year point to the 1948 Aristocrat electric recording; added this qualification to the note.

## Remaining uncertainty

The recording's precise issue day varies or is not established in reviewed sources; retain 1948 year-level precision.
