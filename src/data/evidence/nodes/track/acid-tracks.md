## Sources checked

- [Roland interview with DJ Pierre](https://articles.roland.com/lifetime-achievement-dj-pierre-and-phuture/) — Confirms Phuture’s 1987 Trax pressing and distinguishes it from the 1985 demo/cassette circulation.
- [DJ Mag retrospective](https://djmag.com/content/game-changers-phuture-acid-tracks) — Describes Ron Hardy’s early club play and 1987 Trax release.
- [Insomniac track history](https://www.insomniac.com/music/from-the-crate-phuture-acid-tracks/) — Explains why some sources say 1986 (pre-release circulation) while the commercial record is dated 1987.

## Assessment

1987 is the commercial Trax release year; the tape circulated in Chicago earlier. Phuture is the recording artist. Acid house remains high confidence, with the exact first-play chronology based partly on participant recollection.
