## Sources checked

- [Qobuz — Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan catalog](https://www.qobuz.com/au-en/interpreter/nusrat-fateh-ali-khan/22860/page/42) — Streaming catalog lists an OSA “Allah Hoo Jalle Shan” recording with a 1980 date, the apparent basis for the graph year; date precision may be platform metadata.
- [Victor Entertainment / JVC — Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan recording](https://www.jvcmusic.co.jp/-/Discography/A008159/VICG-60316.html) — Label discography lists “Allah-Hu Allah-Hu” and states it was digitally recorded at Victor 301 Studio on September 20, 1987.
- [Real World Records — Live at WOMAD 1985](https://realworldrecords.com/features/blogs/a-look-back-on-2019-at-real-world-records/) — Official label history says the live WOMAD 1985 album opens with “Allah Hoo Allah Hoo,” identifying a distinct take from later studio recordings.

## Assessment

Retained 1980 only as the catalog date currently attached to this graph node, while lowering the claim’s specificity in the description. There is no single definitive “Allah Hoo” recording: it is a devotional qawwali performed in multiple takes and settings. Do not interpret 1980 as the composition date or as verified for the JVC version.

## Remaining uncertainty

The Qobuz 1980 entry may be a release-date placeholder; the node needs an edition/recording identifier before a precise version date can be stated confidently.
