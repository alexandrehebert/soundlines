## Assessment

1986 is a sound release year for the original instrumental, while the composition may date to the 1984 session. The graph classification as Chicago/deep house is appropriate; do not merge it with the later MLK spoken-word version.

## Remaining uncertainty

Some discographies date the Trax EP differently by issue; track’s release year is kept at 1986, with variant-specific date uncertainty noted.
