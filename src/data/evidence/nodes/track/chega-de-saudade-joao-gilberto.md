## Sources checked

- [Agência Senado — recording of Chega de Saudade](https://www12.senado.leg.br/noticias/materias/2008/08/01/gravacao-de-chega-de-saudade-marca-o-nascimento-da-bossa-nova) — Brazilian Senate history dates João Gilberto’s Odeon 78-rpm recording to July 10, 1958 and notes Cardoso’s earlier 1958 recording.
- [Revista piauí — the beginning of Bossa Nova](https://piaui.uol.com.br/web/o-marco-zero-da-bossa-nova/) — Music-history essay distinguishes Cardoso’s April 1958 recording from João Gilberto’s following-month version.
- [AllMusic — Chega de Saudade album release](https://www.allmusic.com/album/release/chega-de-saudade-mr0001826007) — Discography review notes the song’s single release in 1958 preceded the 1959 LP.

## Assessment

1958 is correct for João Gilberto’s recording and single, though not the song’s composition date or first-ever recording. The performance is rightly associated with bossa nova, while the earlier Elizeth Cardoso version shows why the performer/version must stay explicit.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact release-day data differs across discographies; no day-level date is asserted.
