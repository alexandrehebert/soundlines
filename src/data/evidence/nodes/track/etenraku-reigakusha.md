## Assessment

Gagaku classification and Reigakusha performance context are supported. The graph year 1992 should be understood as a recording/catalog date, not the age of the traditional melody. Sources do not independently verify that exact Reigakusha issue year, so confidence in the date remains limited.

## Remaining uncertainty

The specific 1992 Reigakusha edition has not been independently identified in accessible catalog records; do not treat 1992 as the composition date or assert an origin beyond the tradition’s documented history.
