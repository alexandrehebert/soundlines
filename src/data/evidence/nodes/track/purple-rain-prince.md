## Sources checked

- [Purple Rain — Prince official discography, Piano & a Microphone 1983](https://discography.prince.com/albums/piano-microphone-1983) — Official site distinguishes an earlier private 1983 rehearsal from the 1984 released studio/live album track.

## Assessment

The entry’s 1984 period is correct for release, while its recorded performance originates in August 1983. Existing Soul genre association is insufficiently precise: LOC instead emphasizes gospel, country, blues, and rock. Lowered confidence from medium to low; the song crosses styles and is not well represented by a single soul label.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact genre taxonomy varies; no single style captures the hybrid arrangement. Artist node credits Prince alone though the track’s recording uses The Revolution.
