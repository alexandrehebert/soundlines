## Assessment

1996 is the date of the recording session, not a conventional retail single release. The tape circulated locally; later chapter reissues/catalog entries can carry later dates. This node describes the freestyle/tape, and DJ Screw is the curator/recordist alongside the featured Screwed Up Click rappers.

## Remaining uncertainty

This is a mixtape performance with multiple rappers rather than a standard single by DJ Screw alone; any artist edge should be read as recording/production credit, not sole vocal performance.
