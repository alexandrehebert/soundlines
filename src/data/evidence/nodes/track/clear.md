## Evidence review

Billboard’s September 24, 1983 issue reports the Fantasy single on its Black Singles chart and records Davis’s own description of the group’s sound. The supplied release credits on Qobuz identify Atkins and Davis as writers and give a 1983 Fantasy phonogram date. The graph drops the claim of an exact stylistic midpoint.
