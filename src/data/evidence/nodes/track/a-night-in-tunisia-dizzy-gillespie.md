## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian — Charismic Productions Records of Dizzy Gillespie](https://americanhistory.si.edu/ne/collections/archival-collection/sova-nmah-ac-0979?edan_fq%5B0%5D=-object_type%3A%22Audio%22&page=0) — National Museum of American History collection identifies Gillespie’s manuscript and dates composition to 1942.
- [Philharmonie de Paris — Night in Tunisia](https://edutheque.philharmoniedeparis.fr/0756969-standards-night-in-tunisia.aspx?_lg=fr-FR) — Music institution recounts the December 1944 session and Gillespie’s January 26, 1945 re-recording under the Night in Tunisia title.
- [Jazz Standards — A Night in Tunisia](https://www.jazzstandards.com/compositions-0/nightintunisia.htm) — Specialist jazz reference dates the composition to 1942 and discusses its development as a jazz standard.

## Assessment

Kept 1944 as the earliest documented recording year, but corrected the description to distinguish composition (1942), an initial take titled “Interlude” (December 1944), and the named arrangement recorded in January 1945. The 1944 date is not the composition date.

## Remaining uncertainty

The composition and recording titles changed across sessions; this node represents the work’s first recording lineage, not only the later 1945 take.
