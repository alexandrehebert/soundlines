## Sources checked

- [Library of Congress National Recording Registry essay](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/Your-Love_Snoman.pdf) — Distinguishes Jamie Principle’s early version from Frankie Knuckles’s influential Trax recording.
- [DJ Mag / Registry announcement](https://djmag.com/news/jamie-principles-chicago-house-classic-your-love-inducted-national-recording-registry) — Explains the 1980s Chicago house context and dual authorship.
- [MusicBrainz release catalog](https://musicbrainz.org/release/26d862e0-8d4d-40af-843b-710c723b7d47) — Release catalog cross-reference for the 1987 Trax version.

## Assessment

The 1987 Trax recording is Frankie Knuckles and Jamie Principle’s collaboration; the earlier Persona version is a different recording. Chicago house is strongly supported. Keep the credit distinction explicit.
