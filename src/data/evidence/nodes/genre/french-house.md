# French house

Graph ID `genre:french-house`

**Review status** · Pending source review

**Graph period** · 1993–2002 (circa)

**Graph claim** · French-produced house associated with looped disco and funk samples, filtering and club production; it overlaps with but does not encompass the broader French Touch label.

**Graph phase rationale** · Approximate international club and press visibility: the style grew from early-1990s French production and reached a broad international profile around Homework, Cassius and Stardust in 1997–99. The phases describe one historical wave, not the ongoing reach of French house or all French Touch music.
