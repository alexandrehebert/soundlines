## Sources checked

- [Merlini, “More 1980s than the 1980s” (Sound Stage Screen)](https://air.unimi.it/retrieve/handle/2434/1025442/2350721/More%201980s%20than%20the%201980s.pdf) — Peer-reviewed article treats darksynth as one of several overlapping synthwave taxonomies and names Perturbator as a representative darker style; cautions against treating labels as rigid musical boundaries.
- [Darksynth in heavy music (University of Vienna thesis)](https://services.phaidra.univie.ac.at/api/object/o%3A1764647/get) — Thesis dates the dark synthwave/darksynth emergence around 2012 and links it to Carpenter Brut and Perturbator with metal/industrial/post-punk influences. Secondary synthesis; useful date corroboration, not definitive genre historiography.
- [Decibel — Carpenter Brut interview (2017)](https://www.decibelmagazine.com/2017/02/21/neon-knights-q-a-with-carpenter-brut/) — Artist says he began making music without knowing the synthwave label and heard it after his first EP; demonstrates labels were community-applied and not necessarily self-defined.
- [Le Monde — Perturbator profile (2021)](https://www.lemonde.fr/culture/article/2021/05/28/musique-l-imperturbable-tempo-de-perturbator_6081832.html) — Dates Perturbator’s first Bandcamp mini-album to 2012 and describes subsequent movement into darkwave/darksynth; supports the circa-2012 marker while showing individual project chronology.
- [Kerrang! — Carpenter Brut profile (2019)](https://www.kerrang.com/how-carpenter-brut-are-revolutionising-heavy-music-at-the-forefront-of-the-synthwave-insurgency) — Dates his Trilogy EPs to 2012, 2013, and 2015; documents underground audience and later crossover to metal audiences, not a genre-wide peak.

## Assessment

The original 2010 start is too early/unsourced for the named darksynth category; a University of Vienna thesis places emergence around 2012, corroborated by foundational Perturbator and Carpenter Brut releases. Removed 2014 golden-age and 2018 niche phases: sources describe artist careers and scene crossover, not a genre-wide prominence curve. AllMusic was inaccessible (403) and removed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The academic literature on this narrowly named microgenre is limited; even a peer-reviewed synthwave article notes taxonomy is overlapping and sometimes mood-based. Start is approximate. No phases retained because evidence does not support a general popularity timeline.
