## Sources checked
- [Google Arts & Culture / Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/ugXxcmEzFVvjKQ?hl=en) — Rotterdam Records founded 1992 by Elstak; charts gabber’s rise and fall; “By the millennium, the entire scene had collapsed”.

- [The Guardian, Thunderdome (2019)](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2019/oct/30/thunderdome-the-dutch-rave-with-the-worlds-fastest-hardest-music) — Dreamteam “most famous hardcore group in the 90s”; Thunderdome continuing.
- [The Guardian, “Gift of the gabber” (2020)](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2020/jan/10/gift-of-the-gabber-the-return-of-dance-musics-gloriously-tasteless-subgenre) — “After years as a niche interest… gabber is finally having its moment in the mainstream” (2019).

## Assessment
The circa-1992 emergence (Rotterdam Records and Euromasters) is confirmed. Earlier Parkzicht activity from 1989 is described as background. The previous sequence had no peak. It now runs emerging 1992, golden-age 1995 for the mid-1990s mass phase, and low-intensity established from 2000 after Sound and Vision’s “collapse.” A further established phase in 2019 reflects the revival described by the Guardian.
