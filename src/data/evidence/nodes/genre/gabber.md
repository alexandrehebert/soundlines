## Evidence review

- The Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision documents a 1991 hardcore precursor event in The Hague and dates Rotterdam Records’ first release to 1992. The graph uses circa 1992 as a medium-confidence marker for recorded Rotterdam gabber. [Sound and Vision exhibit](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/ugXxcmEzFVvjKQ?hl=en)
- The exhibit describes the Thunderdome CD series and the Dutch television program devoted to gabber by 1996. The Dutch historical canon situates this in a Rotterdam culture that reached listeners abroad. The graph uses 1996 as a medium-confidence marker for wider visibility. The sources establish circulation more directly than a peak or endpoint, so the 1998 end date was removed. [Sound and Vision exhibit](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/ugXxcmEzFVvjKQ?hl=en), [Canon van Nederland](https://www.canonvannederland.nl/nl/page/292759/gabber)
