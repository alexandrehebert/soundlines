## Assessment

Replace the exact 1985 start with a decade-level 1980s marker: socially and politically conscious rap is documented in late-1980s/early-1990s scholarship and testimony, but the content predates the narrower subgenre label. Remove the proposed 1997 “golden-age” and 2018 “established” phases; neither is a substantiated genre-wide transition, and the 2018 marker was simply the current-graph date. Remove the LiveAbout source and the mismatched Oxford link in favor of scholarship and an artist interview. The description should remain broad and avoid presenting “conscious” as a fixed, mutually exclusive musical form.

## Remaining uncertainty

Socially conscious content has roots across rap history; the more recent “conscious rap” label can be narrowing and sometimes pejorative. The start date is a decade-level marker for a named tendency, not the beginning of social critique in hip-hop.
