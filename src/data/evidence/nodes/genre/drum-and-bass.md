## Sources checked

- [Christodoulou, “Drum and Bass as Cultural Accelerator” (Cambridge Companion to Electronic Dance Music)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-electronic-dance-music/drum-and-bass-as-cultural-accelerator/D0B5BC519520218AC5034BBA6E83B15D) — Peer-reviewed Cambridge chapter locates emergence in inner-city London clubs and raves in the early 1990s and describes the genre’s high speed and working-class urban context.
- [Christodoulou, “Bring the Break-Beat Back!” (Dancecult)](https://dj.dancecult.net/index.php/dancecult/article/download/695/697?inline=1) — Academic analysis of drum and bass subgenre change traces jungle’s transformation into drum and bass in the late 1990s, the role of Jamaican sound systems, and continual genre mutation rather than a terminal endpoint.
- [“Jungle: The Breakbeat’s Revenge,” Popular Music Genres (Edinburgh University Press)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/popular-music-genres/jungle-the-breakbeats-revenge/965A1EC312D4D61AE7337564835EAE59) — Scholarly chapter highlights contested naming and distinction between jungle and drum and bass, cautioning against a simple one-for-one successor narrative.
- [V&A — The Music Is Black: Large Print Guide](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-music-is-black-large-print-guide) — Museum guide dates jungle and drum and bass alongside the UK rave scene in late 1980s/1990s and summarizes influences, sound, artists and Black British cultural impact.

## Assessment

Replaced Wikipedia and an unverified Resident Advisor citation with peer-reviewed Cambridge and Dancecult scholarship plus the V&A museum guide. The prior 1994 start was too late for early-1990s emergence; reset to decade precision. Removed the arbitrary 2000 endpoint and unsupported one-year golden-age shading; drum and bass is an evolving family, not a short closed period. Description distinguishes the jungle continuum and disputed naming.

## Remaining uncertainty

Jungle and drum and bass overlap, but sources describe a contested shift in terminology and sound, with late-1990s transformation rather than a clean boundary. The period start covers the named genre’s emergence, while roots predate the 1990s.
