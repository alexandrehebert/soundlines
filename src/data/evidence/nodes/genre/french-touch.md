# French Touch

**Review status** · Pending source review

**Graph period** · 1995–2005 (circa)

**Graph claim** · A Paris-centred electronic-music movement and export label of the late 1990s and early 2000s, spanning filter house, disco-inflected house, electro-pop and more. Scholarship treats it variously as a scene, a sound or a brand; it is broader than French house.
