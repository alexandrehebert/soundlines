# French Touch

## Sources checked

- [Musée SACEM: Musiques électroniques](https://musee.sacem.fr/index.php/ExhibitionCMS/ExhibitionCMS/View?id=4881) — Institutional exhibition traces French Touch formation to the 1990s, highlighting 1994 F Communications and Daft Punk releases.
- [Musée des Arts Décoratifs / Google Arts & Culture: French Touch 2.0](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/french-touch-2-0/cwUhArKvQ-smKQ) — Exhibition account explicitly narrows the original movement to 1997–2000 while discussing later heirs.
- [Le Monde: Justice and French Touch](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/m-le-mag/article/2024/04/06/justice-s-new-quest_6667576_117.html) — Historical feature describes the term’s 1990s use, varied artists, international audience and first-wave loss of momentum in early 2000s.
- [Le Monde: Daft Punk and the French Touch label](https://www.lemonde.fr/series-d-ete/article/2023/08/17/daft-punk-l-encombrant-label-french-touch_6185731_3451060.html) — Retrospective identifies late-1990s export rise and a later second wave, including Justice and Human After All as a key 2005 point.
- [Musée des Arts Décoratifs: Génération X](https://madparis.fr/generation-x-une-courte-histoire) — Museum history identifies Daft Punk’s March 1994 first single and distinguishes contemporaneous underground/free-party scenes.

## Assessment

Sources support a Paris/French electronic scene developing before Homework: SACEM points to 1994 infrastructure and Daft Punk’s first single; international visibility broadened around Homework (1997), and one museum exhibition uses 1997–2000 for the original movement. The previous 2004 “niche” and 2005 endpoint were too definitive: early-wave momentum faded, but later French electronic waves and a continuing cultural label remain.

## Remaining uncertainty

“French Touch” is a flexible scene, sound and marketing label. Start dates differ depending on whether one means phrase usage, label infrastructure, first records, or export prominence; current 1994 marks scene documentation, not an uncontested origin.
