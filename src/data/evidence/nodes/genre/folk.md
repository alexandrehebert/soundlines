## Assessment
The 1930 decade start is defensible as the roots of the revival (field recordings, Guthrie, Almanacs), though Wikipedia dates the revival proper to the 1940s. The former golden-age phase ran 1945–2010, which no source supports. New phases: 1930 emerging, 1950 established (Weavers), 1958 golden-age (Kingston Trio, Newport, Baez/Dylan; mid-1960s peak), 1966 established (low confidence; boom recedes into folk-rock). Added the Wikipedia revival article and Guthrie official biography.

## Remaining uncertainty
Exact end of the boom (1964–1966) is interpretive; Smithsonian Folkways and LOC pages returned 403.
