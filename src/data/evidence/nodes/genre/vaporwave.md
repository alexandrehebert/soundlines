## Sources checked

- [University of Virginia, “Vaporwave Is Dead: Sound, Community, and Metadata in Internet Music Genres”](https://libraetd.virginia.edu/public_view/q811kk637) — Research thesis dates emergence to the early 2010s and documents later diversification and debates over definition.
- [Cambridge repository, “Vaporwave Aesthetics: Internet Nostalgia and the Utopian Impulse”](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/a33d041f-0e86-47a2-9763-3b37a147d0aa) — Scholarly article dates the term to the early 2010s and traces its aesthetic lineage.
- [Journal of Popular Music Studies, “Vaporwave Politics, Protest, and Identity”](https://online.ucpress.edu/jpms/article-abstract/30/4/123/106377/VaporwavePolitics-Protest-and-Identity) — Peer-reviewed analysis of defining musical and cultural characteristics, including Floral Shoppe (2011).

## Assessment

Circa 2010 is supported as an approximate beginning of an internet-born scene, while sources describe development and disagreement over genre boundaries rather than fixed stages. Removed the unsupported 2012 “golden age,” 2015 “established,” and 2020 “niche” stages. Confidence: high for early-2010s emergence; low for any rigid subperiodization.

## Remaining uncertainty

A 2009 precursor date also appears in scholarship; keep circa 2010 rather than claiming a definitive first year.
