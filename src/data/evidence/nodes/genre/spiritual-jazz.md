## Sources checked

- [Smithsonian, John Coltrane](https://music.si.edu/story/john-coltrane) — Museum account describes Coltrane’s pursuit of spirituality and musical transcendence, a central influence on the later label.
- [Journal of American History, “John Coltrane and Black America’s Quest for Freedom”](https://academic.oup.com/jah/article-abstract/98/2/581/766095) — Scholarly review frames Coltrane’s 1960s spiritual and social engagement.
- [University of California eScholarship, “Universal Consciousness”](https://repositories.cdlib.org/uc/item/1jb67281) — Research article examines Alice Coltrane, Black jazz, Hindu thought and spirituality in the 1960s/70s.
- [Smithsonian Jazz overview](https://music.si.edu/story/jazz/) — Institutional jazz history places Coltrane’s spiritual evolution in the 1960s and recognizes subsequent avant-garde continuities.

## Assessment

Kept c.1960 as an emergence marker but removed 1975 end and 1966/1973 prominence curve. Spiritual jazz is a later umbrella for varied recordings and practices associated with the 1960s/70s; musicians continued to pursue spiritual jazz in later decades. Confidence: medium, since scene boundaries and label use are retrospective.

## Remaining uncertainty

The graph phrase “especially 1960s and 1970s” is a concentration, not an endpoint; artist-by-artist chronology may clarify distinct currents.
