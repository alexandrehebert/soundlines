# Trip-hop

Graph ID `genre:trip-hop`

**Review status** · Pending source review

**Graph period** · 1993–2001 (circa)

**Graph claim** · A press-coined label for downtempo, sample-based, jazz-, funk- and soul-inflected experimental breakbeat music associated with 1990s UK scenes. The Bristol Sound is related but not identical; Massive Attack, Portishead and Tricky have rejected the trip-hop label.
