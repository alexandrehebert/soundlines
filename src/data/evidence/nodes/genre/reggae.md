## Sources checked

- [Jamaica Information Service, Reggae](https://jis.gov.jm/jamaica/reggae/) — Jamaican government history dates reggae’s origin in Jamaica to the late 1960s and names 1968 hits as early markers.
- [Smithsonian Folklife, “Black History in Roots Reggae Music”](https://folklife.si.edu/magazine/black-history-in-roots-reggae-music) — Cultural history documents a roots-reggae golden age c.1968–1983 while tracing ska, rocksteady and African/Jamaican influences.
- [Smithsonian, Jamaica: Music and Performing Arts](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/travel/jamaica-music-and-performing-arts-17745080/) — Cross-checks ska and rocksteady precursors and the 1968 “Do the Reggay” naming milestone.
- [AllMusic reggae overview](https://www.allmusic.com/genre/reggae-ma0000002820) — Specialist reference retained for classification, while removing unsupported 2010 “established” marker.

## Assessment

Kept 1968 as an approximate distinct-genre start, supported by Jamaican and Smithsonian accounts. The 1983 phase is defensible only for the end of the roots-reggae golden age; it does not end reggae, which continues through many branches. Removed the unsupported 2010 phase. Confidence: high for emergence/golden-age framing; “reggae” remains a broad umbrella.

## Remaining uncertainty

The “golden age” applies specifically to roots reggae and its social themes, not all reggae styles or global scenes.
