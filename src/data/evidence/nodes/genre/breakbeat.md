## Sources checked

- [Cambridge: Funk, the Breakbeat Starts Here](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/popular-music-genres/funk-the-breakbeat-starts-here/55837A608444BA396211AFFCF319C2C2) — Scholarly genre history locates breakbeat lineages in funk-derived breaks rather than one late-1980s genre start.
- [University of West London study of boom bap and MPC](https://repository.uwl.ac.uk/id/eprint/5150/) — Academic study documents sampled drum-break practice and its late-1980s hip-hop lineage.
- [Rietveld, Machine Possession](https://openresearch.lsbu.ac.uk/download/cc22adab77ea67b097c59ac741e3bcc76a2df6238aff27877c6810691013dd3e/217487/RIETVELD-Machine%20Possession%20-%20Accepted%202017.pdf) — Research situates breakbeat-driven UK hardcore as one later incarnation in a sequence of dance styles.

## Assessment

Changed 1988 to a broad 1980s marker, removed the arbitrary 1996 end and unsupported golden-age phase, and clarified that this node covers a rhythm/production family across genres.

## Remaining uncertainty

A graph taxonomy may be mixing technique and genre here. Consider whether “breakbeat” should be modeled as a lineage/family with explicit scene-specific children, rather than a single time-bounded genre.
