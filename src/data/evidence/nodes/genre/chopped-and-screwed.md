## Assessment

The decade-level 1990s start is well supported by the Texas State Historical Association and University of Houston archival materials. Keep the entry open-ended: the style continues beyond DJ Screw’s lifetime. Remove the 2001 “golden-age” and 2018 “established” phases because those years and labels were not supported; the sources only place national recognition after his death in 2000, without a precise year or a measured peak. No confidence field is present to revise.

## Remaining uncertainty

The TSHA biography describes DJ Screw’s production technique and career rather than a genre-wide chronology; individual phase boundaries remain approximate and should not imply a singular creator or clean era split.
