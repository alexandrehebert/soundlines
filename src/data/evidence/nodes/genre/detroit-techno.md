## Evidence review

- Dan Sicko’s 1994 interview dates Atkins’s early work with Cybotron to 1981 and Metroplex to 1985. Carnegie Hall describes earlier local progressive music. Use circa 1981 as a recorded milestone, with medium confidence as a boundary for the wider scene. [WIRED](https://www.wired.com/1994/07/techno/), [Carnegie Hall](https://timeline.carnegiehall.org/genres/detroit-techno)
- Carnegie Hall dates the first of Neil Rushton’s Detroit compilations to 1988. That gives a concrete international exposure marker for the second phase. The golden-age label remains an editorial assessment with medium confidence. [Carnegie Hall](https://timeline.carnegiehall.org/genres/detroit-techno)
- Carnegie Hall describes a 1995 compilation, the Detroit Electronic Music Festival beginning in 2000, and continuing local club activity. These support an open period and give reason to remove the unsupported 1993 niche transition and 1995 end. [Carnegie Hall](https://timeline.carnegiehall.org/genres/detroit-techno)
