## Assessment
The circa-1981 start (Cybotron) and the 1988 golden-age anchor (Rushton’s Virgin/10 compilation and the Music Institute) are well supported. The issue was the open-ended golden-age phase. A third phase, established from 1995, now follows the second-wave peak of about 1988–1994. Carnegie Hall’s 1995 “Beyond the Third Wave” compilation and the DEMF/Movement festival from 2000 show a durable scene, but techno’s centre of gravity had become international by then. The 1995 boundary is editorial, so it has low confidence. DHS DEMF and DMY were added to sources.

## Remaining uncertainty
Some writers extend the peak through the mid-1990s (Drexciya, Moodymann, Planet E). The 1995 boundary could move a few years.
