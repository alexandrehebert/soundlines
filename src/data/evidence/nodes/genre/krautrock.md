## Evidence review

- The Cambridge Companion introduction places the original scene in the late 1960s through the mid-1970s. Its chapter on 1968 connects emergence to both West German and international developments. The graph uses circa 1968 and 1971 as editorial anchors, both at medium confidence. [Introduction](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-krautrock/introduction/53DFF7E1B1FBC4A09925676C4356B96D), [Chapter on 1968](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-krautrock/krautrock-and-the-radical-politics-of-1968/44940BF421701F6A6BCE261BF0519EE3)
- The University of Michigan Press collection documents later musical influence. This supports a continuing legacy, with insufficient evidence for exact alternations between niche and established status in 1984, 1994, 2000, 2008 and 2014. Those markers and the provisional 1975 transition were removed. [Michigan Press collection](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.3998/mpub.9206841)
