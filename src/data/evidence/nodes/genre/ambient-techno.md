## Assessment
The circa-1989 start is supported by Craig’s 1989–90 work and The Orb’s 1989–91 records. The 1996 end matched the classic era, but the graph treats genre end dates as the end of activity, and later Kompakt and GAS records continue the style. The period is now open. Phases now run emerging 1989, golden-age 1992 (SAW 85–92 and Warp AI), and established at low intensity from 1997. Confidence is low to medium because AllMusic and OUP were blocked.
