## Evidence review

- The joint Argentine-Uruguayan UNESCO inscription documents origins among working-class communities in Buenos Aires and Montevideo and describes African, European, and local contributions. It gives regional and social context. The circa-1880 start remains a low-confidence editorial estimate. The 2009 inscription marks heritage recognition. [UNESCO](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL?RL=00258)
- A U.S. dance manual published in 1914 places tango in its title and instruction. This documents circulation abroad by 1914 and gives the established phase a medium-confidence anchor. The date concerns international circulation of the dance and leaves the chronology of musical prominence open. [Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/14010455/)
