## Evidence review

- Roland’s interview dates Ron Hardy’s receipt of a pre-release Phuture tape to 1985. DJ Pierre’s DJ Mag interview distinguishes early club play from the 1987 Trax release. The graph uses circa 1985 as a medium-confidence Chicago emergence marker. The two interviews draw on the same participant. [Roland interview](https://articles.roland.com/lifetime-achievement-dj-pierre-and-phuture/), [DJ Mag interview](https://djmag.com/content/game-changers-phuture-acid-tracks)
- The British Culture Archive documents clubs, warehouse parties and temporary venues by summer 1988. This supports a medium-confidence marker for the British wave. The previous 1990 end date lacked evidence that the style ceased, so the graph leaves the period open. [British Culture Archive](https://britishculturearchive.co.uk/acid-house/)
