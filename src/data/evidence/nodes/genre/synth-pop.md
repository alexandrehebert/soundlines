## Assessment

Kept c.1977 for documented late-1970s emergence, reflecting precursor/term use; the narrower chart-era peak occurred in the early 1980s. Removed the fixed 1985 endpoint, since synth-pop continued and later revived. One early-1980s peak marker retained as a broad historical marker, not a universal global curve. Confidence: medium-high for late-1970s/early-1980s UK/European emergence; medium for broad label boundaries.

## Remaining uncertainty

Synth-pop overlaps with electropop, new wave and New Romantic, and histories differ about whether to start with early electronic pioneers or the late-1970s scene.
