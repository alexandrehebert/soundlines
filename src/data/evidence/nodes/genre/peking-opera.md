## Sources checked

- [University of California Press, Drama Kings introduction](https://content.ucpress.edu/pages/10495/10495.intro.pdf) — States scholarly consensus that Peking opera coalesced around 1845, distinguishing formation from earlier antecedents.
- [Colin Mackerras, “Peking Opera before the Twentieth Century,” Comparative Drama](https://scholarworks.wmich.edu/compdr/vol28/iss1/2/) — Peer-reviewed historical article situates the art form’s formation and development before the 20th century.
- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage, Peking Opera](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/peking-opera-00418) — Confirms the performance tradition and living-practice framing, but not a precise formation year.
- [State Council Information Office of China, Peking Opera](https://english.scio.gov.cn/m/chinafacts/2017-04/13/content_40612142.htm) — Official cultural overview cross-checks conventional origin history; its 1790 milestone is earlier than scholarly coalescence.

## Assessment

Moved the graph start from c.1790 to c.1845 to reflect the cited scholarly consensus on when the form coalesced. The 1790 date remains an antecedent/conventional founding milestone in some official accounts, not a settled date for the formed genre. Removed the misleading single “established” phase at the origin. Confidence: medium, with explicit disagreement about what “origin” means.

## Remaining uncertainty

The official 1790 account and scholarship’s c.1845 coalescence date describe different milestones; keep both distinctions visible.
