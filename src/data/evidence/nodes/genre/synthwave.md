## Sources checked

- [Journal of Popular Music Studies, “Reconstructed Nostalgia”](https://online.ucpress.edu/jpms/article/33/1/70/116330/Reconstructed-NostalgiaAesthetic-Commonalities-and) — Peer-reviewed article dates notable synthwave work to the 2000s and mainstream prominence to the early 2010s, discussing Drive (2011).
- [Studi di estetica, “More 1980s than the 1980s”](https://riviste.unimi.it/index.php/sss/article/view/14136) — Scholarly article places synthwave’s emergence in the mid-2000s and rising popularity in the early 2010s.
- [Routledge, Making Synthwave: How an Online Music Community Invented a Genre](https://www.routledge.com/Making-Synthwave-How-an-Online-Music-Community-Invented-a-Genre/Emmerson/p/book/9781032732039) — Book-length research on the online scene and construction of synthwave as a genre.

## Assessment

The mid-2000s start is scholarly supported. Early-2010s growth is supported, and Drive is a visibility marker, not evidence for a “golden age.” Removed unsupported post-2015 phase markers. Confidence: high for broad start and early-2010s visibility; medium for pinpointing a single origin year.

## Remaining uncertainty

Genre boundaries overlap with retrowave, outrun, and related online scenes; scholars note competing categorizations.
