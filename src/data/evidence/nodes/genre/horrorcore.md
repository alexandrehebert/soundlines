## Assessment

An approximate 1988 start is defensible for horror-themed rap antecedents, but the category name “horrorcore” did not appear until the mid-1990s according to academic research. Esham called his work acid rap; genres now grouped under horrorcore have multiple regional roots. The previous 1999 golden-age and 2018 established claims lacked a consistent evidence basis and are removed.

## Remaining uncertainty

The research literature on horrorcore is limited, and retrospective classification can impose one name on artists who described their music differently. Treat 1988 as precursor-era marker, not a dated genre birth.
