## Evidence review

- The Encyclopedia of Chicago places early house in gay African American clubs in the West Loop. Its separate entry on Frankie Knuckles Way dates his Warehouse residency to 1977–1982. Together these support Chicago club origins and make circa 1980 a defensible scene-formation anchor, with no exact genre birth year implied. [Chicago rap history](https://www.encyclopedia.chicagohistory.org/pages/1041.html), [Frankie Knuckles Way](https://www.encyclopedia.chicagohistory.org/pages/410072.html)
- The residency dates cannot establish international prominence or the later phase transitions. Those require evidence specific to each period and market.

- The UK singles chart places Steve “Silk” Hurley’s “Jack Your Body” at number one in January 1987. This documents UK crossover by 1987. The chart measures one market and record and gives limited evidence for the timing of a global prominence transition.
