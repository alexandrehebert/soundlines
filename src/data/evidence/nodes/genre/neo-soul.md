## Sources checked

- [University of Oregon open textbook: Pay for Play](https://opentext.uoregon.edu/payforplay/open/download?type=print_pdf) — Additional scholarly context for neo-soul as a marketed category.

## Assessment

Kept the 1990s entry, clarified that the name is partly a marketing label, and removed the artificial 2005 endpoint and weak popularity phases. The style is a diverse revival/reworking of soul, not a sharply bounded sound.

## Remaining uncertainty

Further artist-level sources are needed to describe the movement’s diversity without flattening it into a single production formula.
