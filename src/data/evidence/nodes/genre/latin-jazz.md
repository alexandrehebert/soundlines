## Assessment

Britannica dates the fusion to the 1940 founding of Machito and his Afro-Cubans, so precision changes from decade to circa 1940. Golden age now begins with 1947 Cubop and spans the Palladium/Afro-Cuban jazz era (Britannica, NPR); from the 1970s the field diversifies (Irakere, Fort Apache) and remains acclaimed, shaded as established. The former 1955–2010 golden age was removed.

## Remaining uncertainty

Whether Brazilian jazz belongs here is debated (Britannica treats it separately); the 1970 boundary is editorial.
