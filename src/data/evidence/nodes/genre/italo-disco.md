# Italo disco

Graph ID `genre:italo-disco`

**Review status** · Pending source review

**Graph period** · 1977 (decade)

**Graph claim** · An Italian-centered post-disco dance music that took shape through electronic records and independent production in the early 1980s, with an international market and circulation through DJs and wholesalers. “Italo disco” became a category abroad before it became a common self-description in Italy; later reissues and revivals reshaped its reputation.
