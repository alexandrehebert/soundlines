# Disco

Graph ID `genre:disco`

**Review status** · Pending source review

**Graph period** · 1972 (decade)

**Graph claim** · A dance-music family shaped in 1970s club networks, especially in New York and Philadelphia, by Black, Latino, and LGBTQ communities and by producers working across soul, funk, and orchestral pop. Club DJ practice, extended mixes, and the 12-inch single became important to its circulation; its histories continued after the U.S. backlash of 1979.

**Graph phase rationale** · Approximate U.S. mainstream and club visibility: the mid-to-late 1970s brought disco’s mass-market peak and backlash; club scenes and related music continued afterward, with later disco revivals. A single U.S. backlash date does not mark the genre’s end, and the shading is not a global chart measure.
