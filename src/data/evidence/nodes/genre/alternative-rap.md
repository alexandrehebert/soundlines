## Sources checked

- [Oxford Bibliographies: Rap/Hip-Hop](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62400/reference-article-abstract/555393653) — Scholarly bibliography contextualizes rap as a field with varied musical and academic categories; does not establish an alternative-rap start date.
- [Cambridge Popular Music: alternative and underground in Havana hip hop](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/popular-music/article/mala-bizta-sochal-klu-underground-alternative-and-commercial-in-havana-hip-hop/680ED0CA7FD2C52AF79C8CE78B578717) — Peer-reviewed article shows “alternative” is relational and varies across local scenes rather than naming one fixed sound.
- [AllMusic Alternative Rap style page](https://www.allmusic.com/style/alternative-rap-ma0000012203) — Prior taxonomy gives a genre landing page but does not justify exact date or prominence phases.
- [AllMusic De La Soul page](https://www.allmusic.com/artist/de-la-soul-mn0000238322) — One artist cannot by itself establish genre boundaries or origin.

## Assessment

Kept the descriptive umbrella definition but removed unsupported 1988/1999/2018 phase staging. These imply fixed genre-wide periods and measured prominence without supporting scholarship. The current sources support category fluidity, not a precise origin date.

## Remaining uncertainty

A genre history focused on US alternative rap naming and contemporary usage would be needed before restoring an emergence year or phases.
