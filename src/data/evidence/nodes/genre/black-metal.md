## Sources checked

- [Metal Music Studies — Phew – What a blizzard! Black metal and the UK popular press](https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/mms.4.2.329_1) — Peer-reviewed article identifies the notorious Oslo-centred second wave in the early 1990s and documents its media visibility.
- [Journal of Popular Music Studies — “Blacker than Death: Recollecting the Black Turn in Metal Aesthetics”](https://digitalcommons.uri.edu/com_facpubs/35/) — Peer-reviewed article frames 1990s black metal as following the earlier extreme-metal aesthetic and changing international metal context.
- [Metal Music Studies — Norwegian black metal, transgression and sonic abjection](https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/mms.5.1.21_1) — Scholarly article examines Norwegian black metal in the early 1990s, corroborating a regional second-wave phase.

## Assessment

The graph’s 1995 “golden age” and 2018 “established” labels were not source-grounded. Replaced them with distinctions the scholarship actually supports: the 1982 name/first-wave anchor and early-1990s Norwegian second wave. The 1982 year is a decade-level date.
