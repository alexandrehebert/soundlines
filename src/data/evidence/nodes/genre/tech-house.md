## Assessment

Kept c.1994 as an approximate emergence marker but removed 2005 endpoint and 1998/2003 editorial prominence labels. Tech-house is a shifting club category whose names and sound vary by location; 1990s London is a documented formative scene, not a universal origin. Confidence: medium.

## Remaining uncertainty

Further primary club/flyer/label sources could refine the earliest date; current c.1994 should remain approximate.
