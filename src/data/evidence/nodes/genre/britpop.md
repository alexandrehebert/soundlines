## Sources checked

- [Royal Holloway, University of London — Britpop’s Common People](https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/en/publications/britpops-common-people-national-identity-popular-music-and-young-/) — Doctoral thesis examines Britpop’s 1990s mainstream success and cultural meanings using media, album-review, and interview material.
- [University of London — British Indie Music in the 1990s](https://research.gold.ac.uk/id/eprint/28700/1/SOC_thesis_White_2006.pdf) — Thesis identifies August 1995 as Britpop’s chart-domination highpoint, anchored by the Blur–Oasis singles battle; search extract checked.
- [Official Charts Company — Best-selling Britpop songs](https://www.officialcharts.com/chart-news/wonderwall-by-oasis-leads-the-uk-s-official-top-50-best-selling-britpop-songs__30860/) — Official chart compilation confirms the large and continuing UK sales footprint of 1995–96 Britpop releases.
- [Cambridge Companion-related research citation](https://researchonline.ljmu.ac.uk/id/eprint/5606/1/402845.pdf) — Academic extract cautions that “Britpop” had earlier uses and gained particular currency in the mid-1990s; supports treating it as a press category rather than a precise musical invention.
