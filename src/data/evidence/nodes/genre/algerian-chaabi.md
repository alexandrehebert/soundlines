## Sources checked

- [ASJP academic journal, “Les maîtres de chaâbi: La génération des années 20”](https://asjp.cerist.dz/en/downArticle/18/27/2/13612) — Study identifies the 1920s generation as foundational to chaâbi and El Anka as an innovator.
- [OhioLINK dissertation, Algerian chaâbi and El Anka history](https://etd.ohiolink.edu/acprod/odb_etd/ws/send_file/send?accession=osu1483633609856956&disposition=inline) — Research dates the style’s development to after World War I and documents El Anka’s 1920s career and 1929 orchestra.
- [The Journal of North African Studies, “Wine, love or divine love? … Algerian Sha’bî Songs”](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13629387.2018.1517339) — Peer-reviewed article discusses the genre’s repertory and cautions that El Anka’s singular paternity is not universally accepted.

## Assessment

Move onset from 1930s to the 1920s: the academic literature identifies the 1920s founding generation, and one historical study dates development to after World War I. The prior 1950 “golden age” and 1960 established phases were unsupported and removed. Confidence: medium-high for 1920s consolidation; lower for absolute origin.

## Remaining uncertainty

Chaâbi’s antecedents predate its naming and consolidation; scholarship disputes whether El Anka alone can be credited with founding it.
