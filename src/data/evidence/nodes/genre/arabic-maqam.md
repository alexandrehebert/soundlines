## Sources checked
- [USNA Arabic culture page](https://usna.edu/AAT/songs-poetry/introduction/traditional-classical-music.php) — al-Kindi (801–873), al-Farabi; maqam = "station"; re-hosted popular text, weak
- [USC Scalar — Traditional music in the Arab world](https://scalar.usc.edu/works/music-in-global-america/traditional-music-in-the-arab-world.147) — 200; general overview
- [Maqam World (Johnny Farraj)](https://maqamworld.com/en/maqam.php) — maqam as system of scales, habitual phrases, modulation and sayr
- [Smithsonian NMAA — Legacy of Munir Bashir](https://asia-archive.si.edu/podcast/the-legacy-of-munir-bashir-omar-bashir-ud/) — maqam/taqsim as the classical improvisatory core

## Assessment
Maqam as a modal system is well documented; theoretical writing from the 9th–10th centuries (al-Kindi, al-Farabi) supports the circa-900 documentary anchor. Period, description and the single low-confidence established phase are kept. Added Maqam World and the Smithsonian page as stronger supporting sources.
