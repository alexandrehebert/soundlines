## Sources checked

- [Latin American Research Review, Bachata’s Imagined Communities](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/latin-american-research-review/article/from-radio-guarachita-to-el-tieto-eshow-bachatas-imagined-communities/B1507CFD80DC5775668D191F2478AF57) — Peer-reviewed article dates Dominican guitar-based bachata to marginalized Santo Domingo barrios in early 1960s and traces bolero lineage.
- [UNESCO, Music and dance of Dominican Bachata](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/bachata-social-dance-from-the-dominican-republic-01514) — Defines bachata as Dominican musical/dance expression and describes bolero/Afro-Antillean fusion; direct open returned sparse page but indexed record checked.
- [City College of New York, Bachata Music Collection](https://www.ccny.cuny.edu/dsi/deborah-pacini-hernandez-bachata-music-collection) — Documents marginalization since 1960s and eventual international popularity, drawing on researcher Deborah Pacini Hernández’s archive/book.

## Assessment

Peer-reviewed research supports the origin date and social setting. Prior 1975 golden-age and 2010 marker lacked support; retained only documented emergence and cautious later international visibility.
