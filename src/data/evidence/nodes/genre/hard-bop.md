## Assessment

Britannica, Carnegie Hall, Wikipedia and the Guardian agree hard bop crystallised in early-to-mid-1950s Blue Note/Messengers work, so the start moves from c.1950 to c.1953 (precursor sessions 1952–53). The 1965 end was removed: Blakey led the Messengers to 1990, Silver returned to hard bop in the 1990s (NEA) and Carnegie Hall documents the 1980s Young Lions revival. Phases rebuilt as emerging 1953, golden age 1955, lower established 1965 and revival 1981, each with notes and sources.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact onset (1953 vs 1954–55) depends on whether 1952–53 Silver/Brown sessions count; the 1965 and 1981 boundaries are editorial.
