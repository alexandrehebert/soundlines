## Sources checked
- [BBC, A brief history of psychedelia in eight colours](https://www.bbc.co.uk/programmes/articles/4nWcSYlCbk9BDJryvdBZMNg/a-brief-history-of-psychedelia-in-eight-colours) — first self-described psychedelic album Oct 1966; Summer of Love 1967; original era “fizzled out”, leading to hard rock and prog; neo-psychedelia from the early 1980s.

- [Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Grateful Dead](https://rockhall.com/inductees/grateful-dead/) — mid-’60s “psychedelics-fueled” improvisation.
- [Wikipedia, Psychedelic rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychedelic_rock) — supplementary.
- Britannica (blocked, 403).

## Assessment
Circa-1965 emergence and a 1966–1969 peak are supported. The 1970 end date was replaced with an open end because the BBC history documents continuation and revivals (and the graph itself has stoner rock fusing from it). Phases now run emerging 1965, golden-age 1966, established (low) 1970.
