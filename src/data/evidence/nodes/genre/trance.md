## Sources checked

- [Oxford University Press: Music-Machines—A Transatlantic History of German Electronic Music](https://academic.oup.com/book/63077) — Musicological monograph includes dedicated histories of Frankfurt techno/trance, translocal trance, and German trance in US raves.
- [Dance 2 Trance official release archive](https://www.theonecollection.co.uk/dance-2-trance) — Dates the Frankfurt duo to 1990.
- [Jam & Spoon official site: short biography](https://www.jamandspoon.com/) — Artist-managed history places early trance roots in Frankfurt in the early 1990s and documents Dance 2 Trance and later Jam & Spoon work.
- [Transactions of the International Society for Music Information Retrieval: divergent techno paths](https://doi.org/10.5334/tismir.324) — Recent research treats 1992 Frankfurt as a commonly cited trance birth marker, while computationally mapping style divergence across 1991–94.
- [Official Charts: Robert Miles, Children](https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/robert-miles-children/) — Primary UK chart record supports a specific 1996 crossover milestone.

## Assessment

Replaced Wikipedia and generic style-only sourcing with a scholarly history, contemporary artist/label chronology and chart data. This supports Frankfurt activity around 1990–92 but does not justify one definitive birthplace for all trance. The chart phase now states its narrow geographic scope.

## Remaining uncertainty

Subgenre differences and the global circulation of Goa, progressive and vocal trance are not fully represented by two phases.
