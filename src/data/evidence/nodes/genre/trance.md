## Evidence review

- The Dance 2 Trance label profile dates the duo’s formation in Frankfurt to 1990. Jam & Spoon’s artist history describes the wider local scene in the early 1990s. Both support an approximate formation anchor with medium confidence. [Dance 2 Trance label profile](https://www.theonecollection.co.uk/dance-2-trance), [Jam & Spoon artist history](https://www.jamandspoon.com/)
- Robert Miles’s “Children” peaked at number two in the UK in 1996. This supports a medium-confidence crossover anchor and an established phase. A single market offers limited evidence for a worldwide peak. [Official Charts](https://www.officialcharts.com/songs/robert-miles-children/)
- Armin van Buuren’s chart history lists trance-branded releases after 2005. The graph leaves the period open and removes the 2003 niche boundary, for which the reviewed sources give no clear basis. [Official Charts artist history](https://www.officialcharts.com/artist/13043/armin-van-buuren/)
