## Sources checked

- [Reconstructing Afrobeat as a Scene-Based Genre — Journal of World Popular Music](https://doi.org/10.1558/jwpm.25695) — Peer-reviewed study challenges single-origin narratives; locates genre formation in a wider 1960s–70s Lagos scene and documents the 1968 announcement and early-1970s consolidation. Full text checked via author-hosted copy.
- [Afrobeat, Fela and Beyond: Scenes, Style and Ideology — University of Pittsburgh dissertation](https://eprints-prod-01.library.pitt.edu/10261/) — Dissertation abstract dates emergence to late-1960s Lagos and frames its history through scenes and transnational networks; repository page found via search, direct retrieval unreliable.
- [Tony Allen: An Autobiography — Duke University Press](https://www.dukeupress.edu/tony-allen) — Publisher record establishes Allen’s long collaboration with Fela (1964–79); corroborates central partnership, not a sole-inventor claim.
- [Afro-Beat overview — AllMusic](https://www.allmusic.com/style/afro-beat-ma0000004495) — Search excerpt attributes a 1963 naming date; likely confuses Fela’s return to Lagos with the 1968 naming documented by scholarly sources. Direct page blocked.
- [Fela’s Zombie album at 50 — Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/9eacb46f0197a1bf8399716b45a60265) — Earlier citation could not be reliably retrieved; not needed for the revised origin claim.

## Assessment

The graph’s prior description incorrectly said Fela named Afrobeat in 1963. The consulted musicology places a nascent movement in 1960s–70s Lagos, documents a 1968 press announcement, and argues for a scene-based origin; the 1960s decade anchor therefore remains, while confidence in a single-year origin is low. Afrobeat should not be conflated with Afrobeats. Removed weak AllMusic origin citation from graph sources.

## Remaining uncertainty

Exact first use of the term and the relative contributions of Kuti, Allen, Orlando Julius, and other Lagos musicians remain debated; the graph now avoids presenting one uncontested inventor or a falsely precise birth year.
