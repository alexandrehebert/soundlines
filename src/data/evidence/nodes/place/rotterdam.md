## Sources checked

- [Peer-reviewed article: “Gabber: Raising Hell in Technoculture”](https://intellectdiscover.com/content/journals/10.1386/mms_00057_1) — Describes gabber as a Dutch hardcore dance genre with Rotterdam as its epicentre in the early 1990s.
- [Netherlands Institute for Sound and Vision: A Brief History of Gabber](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/ugXxcmEzFVvjKQ?hl=en) — Archives scene details including Paul Elstak founding Rotterdam Records at the city's Mid-Town record shop in 1992.
- [Academic study: “Making a scene: exploring the dimensions of place through Dutch popular music”](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0308518X15595781) — Treats gabber within a comparative analysis of Dutch music scenes, useful for avoiding a single-source local narrative.

## Assessment

The central role of Rotterdam is well supported. Gabber developed within a wider Dutch scene, so “epicentre” is more precise than implying sole origin. Overall confidence: high.
