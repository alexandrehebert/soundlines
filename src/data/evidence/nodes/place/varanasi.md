## Sources checked

- [Sangeet Natak Akademi: Ravi Shankar](https://www.sangeetnatak.gov.in/public/uploads/awardees/docs/1740735753_Ravi%20Shankar.pdf) — National academy biography states he was born in Varanasi in 1920 and describes his international tours and contribution to Hindustani instrumental music.
- [Praemium Imperiale: Ravi Shankar](https://www.praemiumimperiale.org/en/laureate/india/shankar) — Independent international arts award profile documents his career and role in introducing Indian music to wider audiences.
- [Ravi Shankar Opera Project: Key People](https://ravishankaroperaproject.org/key-people/) — Project biography also records his Varanasi birth and musical career.

## Assessment

Birthplace and international influence are directly supported. This is a biographical connection to the city, not a claim that Varanasi alone represents Hindustani music. Overall confidence: high.
