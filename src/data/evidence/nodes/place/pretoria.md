## Sources checked

- [World Bank: South Africa creative economy research](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099020408292536299/pdf/IDU-fcdfc726-4197-4f26-9597-7d3ad27904b3.pdf) — Locates amapiano in Gauteng townships and frames it within youth culture and creative economies.
- [University of Pretoria: “Understanding amapiano and the South African city”](https://repository.up.ac.za/bitstreams/6f82dab5-746e-4017-ac0c-1218153d6a21/download) — Scholarly analysis studies amapiano’s spatial representation and relation to township and city life.
- [“Historicizing Kwaito,” *African Music*](https://journal.ru.ac.za/index.php/africanmusic/article/view/1782) — Comparative study provides context on the neighboring Gauteng-rooted dance-music lineage and debates over pinning genres to a single city.

## Assessment

The Gauteng connection is well supported, but accounts of amapiano’s exact city of origin remain contested. Pretoria should be presented as one regional center, not the sole birthplace. Overall confidence: medium.
