## Sources checked

- [International Mozarteum Foundation: Mozart's birthplace](https://mozarteum.at/en/mozart-museums/mozarts-birthplace) — The foundation's museum materials situate Mozart's birth and childhood at the Salzburg house.
- [International Mozarteum Foundation: Mozart biography](https://mozarteum.at/en/wolfgang-amade-mozart) — Gives his birth date and identifies him as the son of Salzburg court musician Leopold Mozart.
- [City of Salzburg: Mozart's biography](https://www.salzburg.info/en/salzburg/city-of-mozart/wolfgang-amadeus-mozart) — Dates his birth in Salzburg in 1756 and documents his early musical life there.

## Assessment

Birthplace and childhood are directly documented by specialist and municipal institutions. The graph's claim is well supported, while not implying Mozart spent his entire career in Salzburg. Overall confidence: high.
