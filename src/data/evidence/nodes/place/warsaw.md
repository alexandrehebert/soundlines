## Sources checked

- [Fryderyk Chopin Institute: Warsaw Conservatory](https://greatcomposers.nifc.pl/en/chopin/catalogs/places/158_warsaw-conservatory) — Identifies Chopin’s 1826 studies at the Main School of Music attached to the University of Warsaw.
- [Chopin.pl: Biography](https://www.chopin.pl/biography_chopin.en.html) — Documents the family’s move to Warsaw, his studies and public performances there, and his later departure; it separately gives his birthplace as Żelazowa Wola.
- [Poland Travel: In the Footsteps of Chopin](https://www.poland.travel/en/in-the-footsteps-of-chopin-discover-warsaw-and-zelazowa-wola/) — Official tourism account notes his Warsaw performances shortly before departure from Poland.

## Assessment

The graph accurately frames Warsaw as a formative and early-career city, not Chopin’s birthplace or the sole center of his career. Overall confidence: high.
