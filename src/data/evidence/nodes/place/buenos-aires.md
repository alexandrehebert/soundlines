## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Tango](https://ich.unesco.org/es/RL/el-tango-00258?RL=00258) — Argentina and Uruguay’s joint nomination describes tango emerging among popular classes in Buenos Aires and Montevideo in the Río de la Plata basin.
- [Cambridge Companion to Tango: “A Brief History of Tango”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-tango/brief-history-of-tango/46082B9A3C0529CCD1DF68474A7F7ECF) — Scholarly overview situates tango’s mixed Argentine and Uruguayan origins in the Río de la Plata region.
- [Instituto Nacional de Musicología “Carlos Vega”](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/cultura/ina) — Argentina’s national musicology institute provides institutional context for research on Argentine tango and music history.

## Assessment

Buenos Aires is a foundational urban center, but tango’s roots and development are shared with Montevideo and the wider Río de la Plata. Overall confidence: high.
