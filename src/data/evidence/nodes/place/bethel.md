## Sources checked

- [U.S. National Park Service: Woodstock Music Festival Site](https://www.nps.gov/places/woodstock-music-festival-site.htm) — Gives the location as the Bethel vicinity in Sullivan County, New York, and identifies the 1969 festival site and its National Register significance.
- [Bethel Woods Center for the Arts: Woodstock History](https://www.bethelwoodscenter.org/museum/woodstock-history) — Site museum directly answers that Woodstock took place in Bethel, New York, and explains that the festival was originally intended for Woodstock, which is why the name stuck.
