## Sources checked

- [Government of Punjab: Important personalities of Faisalabad](https://faisalabad.punjab.gov.pk/important-personalities) — Official district profile states Khan was born in Faisalabad in 1948.
- [University of Cape Town research repository](https://open.uct.ac.za/server/api/core/bitstreams/3da1fee4-a8ab-4ebf-8023-19af082cad13/content) — Academic thesis independently identifies Lyallpur, now Faisalabad, as his birthplace.
- [Fukuoka Prize: Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan biography](https://fukuoka-prize.org/files/download/en/LaureatesI18n/laureate_blocks/50a8d14b-967c-41ff-a255-19f299dfc400/value01/value05) — Prize biography records his birth in Faisalabad and family’s qawwali tradition.

## Assessment

The birthplace claim is corroborated across independent institutional sources. Overall confidence: high.
