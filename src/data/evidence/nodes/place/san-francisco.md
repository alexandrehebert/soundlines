## Sources checked

- [U.S. Government Publishing Office: The Counterculture and Psychedelic Rock](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-S20-PURL-gpo1267/pdf/GOVPUB-S20-PURL-gpo1267.pdf) — Describes the scene's focus in Haight-Ashbury and names San Francisco bands and venues.
- [San Francisco Heritage: Haight Street Turns On](https://www.sfheritage.org/heritage-in-the-neighborhoods/haight-street-turns-on/) — Documents the neighborhood's countercultural and psychedelic history.
- [Cambridge University Press: American Hippies](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/american-hippies/drugs-music-and-spirituality/1BA131FA9915F651C886A6D41B9B60B8) — Scholarly contextualization of the Haight as one focal neighborhood within the broader hippie movement.

## Assessment

The city was an important, well-documented center, especially around Haight-Ashbury and its venues. Psychedelic rock and counterculture were not exclusive to San Francisco. Overall confidence: high.
