## Sources checked

- [UNESCO: Action Plan for the Safeguarding of Baul Songs](https://ich.unesco.org/en/projects/action-plan-for-the-safeguarding-of-baul-songs-00047) — Places Lalon Shah’s life and intergenerational Baul tradition in the Kushtia region, while noting Bauls live across Bangladesh and West Bengal.
- [Bangladesh Heritage Hub: Kushtia district inventory](https://heritagehub.gov.bd/HeritageInfoes?distCodes=4050&heritageTypeId=0) — Government inventory records Baul heritage and practice in Kushtia.
- [Bangladesh Heritage Hub: Baul Gurubad](https://heritagehub.gov.bd/HeritageInfoes/Details/2026) — Identifies Kushtia among districts where Baul practice remains prominent.

## Assessment

Kushtia is closely associated with Lalon and active Baul communities, but Baul culture is geographically dispersed. Lalon’s precise birthplace is disputed and is not asserted here. Overall confidence: medium-high.
