# Kinshasa

## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Creative Cities Network: Kinshasa](https://www.unesco.org/en/creative-cities/kinshasa) — Calls Kinshasa a cradle of Congolese rumba and documents pioneering studios and bands.
- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Congolese rumba](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/congolese-rumba-01711?RL=01711) — Joint DRC–Republic of the Congo inscription describes rumba as common to urban areas in both countries.
- [Lesley Nicole Braun, *Congo’s Dancers* (University of Wisconsin Press)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv31jm8wn) — Ethnographic scholarship details the place of rumba and dance in Kinshasa’s social and cultural life.

## Assessment

“Cradle” and “pioneering studios” are supported for Kinshasa, but the tradition is shared with Brazzaville and the wider Congolese region. Overall confidence: high.
