## Sources checked

- [Manchester University Press / Cambridge: “The beating heart of soul: Wigan Casino”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/keeping-the-faith/beating-heart-of-soul-wigan-casino/539D6496E9527A25346AD7010D0A81D1) — 2026 scholarly chapter uses primary sources to examine the club's development, all-nighters, localism, and national reach.

- [Wigan Council local history archive](https://www.wigan.gov.uk/Docs/PDF/Resident/Leisure/Museums-and-archives/archives/Past-Forward/pf51.pdf) — Municipal archive places the Casino's Northern Soul/all-nighter period in 1973–81.

## Assessment

The venue became a defining and iconic Northern Soul site, but the scene predated it and depended on multiple clubs in different towns. The graph accurately ties the town to the venue; avoid implying Wigan originated the movement. Overall confidence: high.
