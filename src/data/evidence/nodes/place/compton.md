# Compton

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [Compton Art & History Museum: Sounds of the City](https://www.comptonmuseum.org/previous-exhibitions/sounds-of-the-city) — Local exhibition examines the city’s impact on hip-hop and places N.W.A. within Compton’s history.
- [Library of Congress: *Straight Outta Compton*](https://www.loc.gov/static/programs/national-recording-preservation-board/documents/StraightOuttaCompton.pdf) — National Recording Preservation Board essay documents the album’s release through Eazy-E’s Ruthless Records and its role in popularizing gangsta rap.
- [UC eScholarship: *Gangster Boogie*](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/3hd2d12n) — Scholarly dissertation studies Compton-based N.W.A. within Los Angeles gangsta rap history.

## Assessment

The local lineage is strong, but neither the city museum nor scholarship supports calling Compton the sole origin of gangsta rap. Overall confidence: high for the stated bounded claim.
