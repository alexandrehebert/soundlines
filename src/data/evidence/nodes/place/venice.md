## Sources checked

- [Treccani, *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani*: Antonio Vivaldi](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/antonio-vivaldi_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/) — Music historian Michael Talbot gives Vivaldi's birth in Venice on 4 March 1678 and details his Venetian education and musical work.
- [Oxford Bibliographies in Music: Antonio Vivaldi](https://academic.oup.com/reference/62400/reference-article-abstract/555398242) — Scholarly reference says Vivaldi was based in Venice for most of his known life, despite work in Mantua and travel elsewhere.

## Assessment

The concise claim is supported: Venice was his birthplace and principal base, not his only place of work. Overall confidence: high.
