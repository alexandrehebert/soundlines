## Sources checked

- [KAIST research portal: “Organizing K-Pop”](https://pure.kaist.ac.kr/en/publications/organizing-k-pop-emergence-and-market-making-of-large-korean-ente/) — Peer-reviewed study examines Korean entertainment houses and the creation of the K-pop market.
- [Cambridge University Press: *The Cambridge Companion to K-Pop*, “Itaewon Class, Gangnam Style, and Yeouido Star”](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-companion-to-kpop/itaewon-class-gangnam-style-and-yeouido-star/3E94A922A8A128152012D362D26DD7B7) — Places the formation of export-oriented pop within Seoul's nightlife and production-company networks.
- [Seoul Institute report](https://global.si.re.kr/bbs/view.do?key=2411060001&pstSn=2605210006) — City research institute identifies Seoul as a core hub for Korea's creative industries, including music.

## Assessment

Seoul is a central production, management, and media hub; K-pop's artists, audiences, and cultural roots extend across South Korea and international networks. Overall confidence: high.
