## Sources checked

- [Alejandro Ulloa, *La Salsa en Cali* (Colombia’s Minciencias repository)](https://repositorio.minciencias.gov.co/entities/publication/4b93513a-4767-4c99-8035-c4c8ebc25547) — Foundational urban study of salsa, media, and cultural identity in Cali.
- [Universidad del Valle Digital Library: *La salsa en Cali*](https://bibliotecadigital.univalle.edu.co/entities/publication/5a980d5d-920b-4e80-b3ef-2d0a6d011a2c/full) — Records the research behind Ulloa’s account of the city’s salsa culture.
- [Cali city government: How Cali became a salsa center](https://www.cali.gov.co/cultura/publicaciones/53022/informe-especial-sobre-cmo-cali-se-convirti-en-la-capital-mundial-de-la-salsa/) — Local historical account describes clubs, dancing, festivals, and the city’s adoption of salsa.

## Assessment

The sources support salsa’s local adoption and distinctive dance, club, and festival culture. “Capital” is a cultural identity claim, not an assertion that salsa originated in Cali. Overall confidence: medium-high.
