## Sources checked

- [University of Lagos repository: Fela Kuti and political protest](https://api-ir.unilag.edu.ng/server/api/core/bitstreams/98fc174c-1c75-4be1-b3d3-97f1bc91f40d/content) — Research traces Fela’s return to Lagos, Afrobeat development, Kalakuta Republic, and the African Shrine.
- [University of Pittsburgh: *Afrobeat, Fela and Beyond*](https://eprints-prod-01.library.pitt.edu/10261/) — Scholarly dissertation studies Afrobeat through Lagos-centered scenes and transnational networks.
- [Fela Kuti official archive: About Fela](https://felakuti.com/gb/about) — Documents his Lagos club, the Afrika Shrine, and his performances there until his death.

## Assessment

Lagos is central to Afrobeat’s emergence and Fela’s career, but the music also drew on wider Nigerian, African, and diasporic traditions. Overall confidence: medium-high.
