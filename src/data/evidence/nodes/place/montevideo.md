## Sources checked

- [Uruguay Ministry of Tourism: Tango](https://uruguaynatural.com/es/masqueunpais/el-tango/) — National tourism authority describes tango's formation in both cities and gives examples of cross-river authorship.
- [Uruguay's UNESCO Commission: Tango](https://comisionunesco.org.uy/rutas-unesco/tango/) — National commission identifies the tradition as shared and rooted in Montevideo and Buenos Aires.
