## Sources checked

- [Mexico’s Ministry of Culture: Los Ángeles Azules](https://www.cultura.gob.mx/regiones_de_mexico/sur/detalle.php?act=195459) — Government profile locates the group’s cumbia sonidera lineage in Iztapalapa, Mexico City.
- [Los Ángeles Azules: Official biography](https://losangelesazules.com.mx/bio/) — The group describes its origins in Iztapalapa and its international cumbia career.
- [“Listening across borders: migration, dedications, and voice in cumbia sonidera,” *Tapuya*](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/25729861.2018.1497273) — Academic article studies sonidero communities and cumbia circulation in Mexico City and across borders.

## Assessment

Mexico City’s description is best understood through Iztapalapa and the group’s specific cumbia lineage, not as a claim that the city originated cumbia. Overall confidence: medium-high.
