## Sources checked

- [Fundação Joaquim Nabuco: Dorival Caymmi](https://pesquisaescolar.fundaj.gov.br/pt-br/artigo/dorival-caymmi/) — Researcher Semira Adler Vainsencher records Caymmi's birth in Salvador, Bahia, on 30 April 1914.
- [Instituto Moreira Salles collection record](https://acervos.ims.com.br/index.php/Detail/objects/60298) — Archival collection documentation provides an independent institutional record for Caymmi and his cultural legacy.
- [Fundação Joaquim Nabuco English article](https://pesquisaescolar.fundaj.gov.br/en/artigo/dorival-caymmi/) — The institution's English-language profile also identifies him with Salvador and Bahia.

## Assessment

The birthplace claim is direct and well supported. Caymmi's work drew on Bahia, but one artist does not represent the full musical history of Salvador. Overall confidence: high.
