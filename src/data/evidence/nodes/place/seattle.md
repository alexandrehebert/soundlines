## Sources checked

- [University of Washington Libraries: History of *The Rocket*](https://guides.lib.uw.edu/therocketexhibit/history) — Describes the magazine's role in chronicling the birth of the Seattle Sound and grunge.
- [University of California eScholarship dissertation](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/76t7z7n9) — Studies grunge as a scene and genre rooted in the Pacific Northwest's particular time and place.
- [University of Washington Libraries: Seattle Sounds](https://guides.lib.uw.edu/seattlesounds-class) — Surveys the city's varied musical history and explicitly cautions that Seattle's music is much broader than grunge.
- [Peer-reviewed study on adjacent Olympia and Bellingham scenes](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/27538702241299876) — Shows how nearby communities developed distinct identities alongside Seattle during the grunge era.

## Assessment

Seattle's international association with grunge is strong, but the story is regional and the city's music history is much broader. Graph wording is appropriately qualified; confidence: high.
