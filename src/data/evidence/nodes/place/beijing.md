## Sources checked

- [UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage: Peking opera](https://ich.unesco.org/en/RL/peking-opera-00418?RL=00418) — Identifies Beijing as one of the form’s performance centers and notes its use of Beijing dialect.
- [National Museum of China: Peking Opera Maestro Mei Lanfang](https://en.chnmuseum.cn/exhibition/exhibition_series/temporary_exhibitions/historical_and_cultural_exhibition/202201/t20220127_253498.html) — States Mei Lanfang was born in Beijing and documents his artistic life.
- [Beijing Government: Mei Lanfang Memorial Museum](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/beijinginfo/culture/culturaltreasures/cityofmuseums/list/202604/t20260427_4617242.html) — Confirms his former Beijing residence now preserves archival materials about him.

## Assessment

The graph correctly says “performance center,” not origin or exclusive home of Peking opera, which is practiced throughout China. Overall confidence: high.
