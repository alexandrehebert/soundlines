# Munich

Graph ID `place:munich`

**Review status** · Reviewed · ECM's founding in Munich is corroborated by the label and a local museum's research exhibition.

**Reviewed** · 2026-11-04

## Sources checked

- [ECM Records: About](https://ecmrecords.com/story/) — The label says producer Manfred Eicher founded ECM in 1969 and describes him in 1972 as a Munich-based producer.
- [Haus der Kunst: ECM — A Cultural Archaeology](https://www.hausderkunst.de/en/eintauchen/ecm-a-cultural-archaeology) — Munich's contemporary-art museum explicitly states ECM was founded by Eicher in Munich in 1969.

## Assessment

Both sources directly support the narrow founding-place claim. This documents ECM's origin, not that every part of the label's later operation remained in Munich. Overall confidence: high.
