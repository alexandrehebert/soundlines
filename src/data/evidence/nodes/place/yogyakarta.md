## Sources checked

- [University of Gadjah Mada journal: “The Place of Court Dance and Music in the Javanese Kratons”](https://journal.ugm.ac.id/index.php/jurnal-humaniora/article/view/759) — Examines court music and identifies Yogyakarta's Sultanate and court gamelan traditions.
- [University of Gadjah Mada repository: ethnomusicological study of Yogyakarta court gamelan](https://etd.repository.ugm.ac.id/penelitian/detail/81374) — Research connects the musical aesthetics and tuning of the Yogyakarta court gamelan to the history of the palace.
- [Smithsonian National Museum of Asian Art: Javanese Gamelan Music](https://asia-archive.si.edu/podcast/javanese-gamelan-music/) — Documents contemporary gamelan performance by the Indonesian Arts Institute at Yogyakarta.
- [Ethnomusicologist Roger Vetter: Gamelans of the Kraton Yogyakarta](https://vetter.sites.grinnell.edu/gamelan/) — Research account based on decades studying and teaching Central Javanese gamelan, focused on palace ensembles and their community relationships.

## Assessment

Yogyakarta is an important court and educational center for Central Javanese gamelan, but the tradition is regional and also strongly associated with Surakarta. The graph's “a prominent ... tradition” wording is appropriately scoped. Overall confidence: high.
