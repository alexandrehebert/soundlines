## Sources checked

- [Carnegie Hall: History of Detroit Techno](https://timeline.carnegiehall.org/genres/detroit-techno) — Institutional music-history account traces techno to Detroit, including Black club culture, radio, producers, and the city's social context.
- [University of Michigan Bentley Historical Library: Remixing Detroit History](https://bentley.umich.edu/about/collections-magazine/fall-2016/remixing-detroit-history/) — Archive-based account documents the Electrifying Mojo's radio role and the place of Black Detroit communities in techno's history.
- [Michigan State University: Detroit and the rise of techno](https://cal.msu.edu/news/ask-the-expert-how-detroit-and-its-people-are-integral-to-the-rise-of-techno-music/) — Historian and museum curator situates Detroit techno in Black and queer communities, radio, and warehouse spaces.

## Assessment

The city's centrality is well supported. Keep the account tied to artists, radio and club networks; do not reduce the genre to deindustrialization or a single inventor. Overall confidence: high.
