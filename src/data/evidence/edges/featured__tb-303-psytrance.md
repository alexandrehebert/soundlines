**Finding** · The Bristol thesis connects TB-303 arpeggiation to Goa trance and says later psytrance tags can invoke that antecedent; the Finland study associates the device’s resonant acid sound with classic Goa tracks. This is evidence for lineage and some production, not a defining instrument in psytrance generally.
