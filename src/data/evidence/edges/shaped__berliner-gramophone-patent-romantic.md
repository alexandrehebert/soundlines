## Evidence review

Victor’s 1904 disc of Caruso singing Leoncavallo documents an early recording of late nineteenth-century opera. A 1910 newspaper advertisement in Puerto Rico markets a later take and lists discs of Verdi, Bizet, and others. These sources establish a later commercial use of disc recording and give limited support to a direct link between the patent and Romantic music as a style.
