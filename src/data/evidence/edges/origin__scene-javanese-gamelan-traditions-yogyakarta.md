**Finding** · Grove’s *Music of Indonesia* identifies Yogyakarta and Surakarta as two Central Javanese court centers where gamelan traditions flourished in elaborate forms; Grinnell ethnomusicologist Roger Vetter documents the distinct Yogyakarta court tradition. Gamelan is much wider than either city. [Grove chapter, *Indonesia*](https://musc102.blogs.wesleyan.edu/files/2021/03/Philip-Yampolsky-et-al._Indonesia.pdf); [Grinnell College, Kraton Yogyakarta gamelans](https://vetter.sites.grinnell.edu/gamelan/)
