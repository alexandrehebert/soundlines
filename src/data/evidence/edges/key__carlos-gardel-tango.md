## Evidence review

Uruguay’s nomination dossier hosted by UNESCO describes singing engagements around 1910 and dates Gardel’s early collaboration with José Razzano to 1911. This supports an approximate 1910 career anchor. Both the nomination and the Argentine cultural profile identify his 1917 recording of “Mi noche triste” as formative for tango canción. The graph describes it as a milestone because chronological priority depends on how the genre is defined. [Uruguayan nomination dossier](https://media.unesco.org/sites/default/files/webform/mow001/uruguay_records_carlos_gardel.pdf), [Argentine cultural profile](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/carlos-gardel-memoria-y-vigencia-del-zorzal-criollo)
