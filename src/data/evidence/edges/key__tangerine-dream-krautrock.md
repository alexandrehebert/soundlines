## Sources checked
- [Guardian, 2015, Edgar Froese dies](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2015/jan/23/tangerine-dream-founder-edgar-froese-dies) — “early in their career the group were associated with” krautrock; later spacey synth sound had little in common with it.
- [Red Bull Music Academy Daily, Jon Savage on Kosmische Musik (2011)](https://daily.redbullmusicacademy.com/2011/12/krautrock-feature) — Tangerine Dream on the 1972 Ohr Kosmische Musik compilation.

## Assessment
Association is real but mainly for the early albums; later work is Berlin School electronic music. Kept medium with a note.
