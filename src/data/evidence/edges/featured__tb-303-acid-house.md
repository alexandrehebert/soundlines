## Evidence review

Roland’s product chronology dates launch to 1981. DJ Pierre describes manipulating the filter and passing a tape to Ron Hardy in 1985. His DJ Mag interview dates the Trax release to 1987. The two interviews share a participant. The graph uses 1987 as a documented public milestone at medium confidence for broader adoption. The launch and the direct link to acid house have stronger evidence than the timing of wider prominence.
