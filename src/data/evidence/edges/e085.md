## Evidence review

The study of West German countercultures identifies Kraftwerk with Düsseldorf and treats krautrock as a British umbrella term applied to groups with distinct backgrounds. The Michigan Press collection examines several of those groups and their different settings. Düsseldorf is defensible as an important center. The edge remains low confidence as an origin claim because the evidence supports a wider, multi-city history. [West German countercultures study](https://doi.org/10.1080/13619462.2021.1925551), [Krautrock essay collection](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.3998/mpub.9206841)
