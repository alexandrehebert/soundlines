# Removed edge: Etenraku → Reigakusha

Former graph ID `recorded:etenraku-reigakusha`

**Disposition** · Removed (2026-11-05)

The graph’s 1992 Reigakusha recording was not established. Smithsonian Folkways identifies its 1963 `Etenraku` recording as performed by Tenrikyo Hawaii Dendocho; the Tokyo University of the Arts archive catalogs a distinct tape without confirming Reigakusha or a 1992 date. Reigakusha’s history says the group formed in 1985, while the verified label release with its Etenraku is from 2019. The work is retained generically as `track:etenraku`; the anthem edge is updated to that work rather than a particular performance.

Sources: [Smithsonian Folkways album](https://folkways.si.edu/festival-of-japanese-music-in-hawaii-vol-1/world/music/album/smithsonian); [Folkways liner notes](https://folkways-media.si.edu/docs/folkways/artwork/FW08745.pdf); [Tokyo University of the Arts Koizumi archive](https://koizumi.geidai.ac.jp/db/opentape/or039401/); [Reigakusha history](https://reigakusha.com/home/about); [Nippon Columbia release](https://columbia.jp/prod-info/COCJ-40784/).
