**Finding** · Scholarship traces Zanzibar’s court tradition to late nineteenth-century exchange with Egypt and documents its regional influence. Taarab subsequently developed throughout the Swahili coast, with distinct local forms; Zanzibar should be represented as formative center, not sole origin of every taarab form.
