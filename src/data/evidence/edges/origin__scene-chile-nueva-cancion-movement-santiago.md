**Finding** · Chile’s National Library says Nueva Canción developed through peñas and was named at a 1969 Santiago festival; its account also places later related Canto Nuevo particularly in Santiago. Scholarly and heritage sources characterize Nueva Canción as a national movement with distributed artists and venues., [University of Santiago museum](https://lausachesunmuseo.cl/arte-y-cultura/la-nueva-cancion-chilena-detalle/))

**Recommendation** · Medium-high. Santiago was a major naming, venue, and organizing hub, but the movement is national and should not be reduced to an exclusively urban origin.
