# Clarinet → Saxophone

Graph ID `lineage:saxophone-clarinet`

**Review status** · Source checked (2026-11-05)

**Relationship** · influences · medium

**Graph note** · Adolphe Sax experimented by fitting a bass-clarinet mouthpiece to an ophicleide; this clarinet-derived single-reed idea contributed to the saxophone, alongside a new brass body and bore. This is a design influence, not a claim that the saxophone descends from the clarinet alone.

**Sources checked** · [The Metropolitan Museum of Art](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/504684) — instrument history identifies the bass-clarinet mouthpiece experiment. [Philharmonie de Paris Museum](https://collectionsdumusee.philharmoniedeparis.fr/histoires-d-instruments-le-saxophone.aspx?_lg=fr-FR) — supplementary instrument-history account.

**Assessment** · Adolphe Sax experimented with a bass-clarinet mouthpiece on an ophicleide; the Metropolitan Museum says this idea spawned the saxophone. This supports a specific design influence, not simple descent from clarinet. The Philharmonie de Paris instrument history is an additional museum source.
