# Removed edge: key:osibisa-highlife

Removed 2026-11-05. Sources identify Osibisa as Afro-rock and establish its members’ highlife background, but do not establish the band itself as a key highlife act. Scholarship describes Osibisa as Afro-rock formed by Ghanaian musicians with highlife backgrounds; BFI links the group name to highlife’s Fante term. Evidence supports highlife roots and hybridization, but the band is not primarily a highlife act. Keep only with this caveat, or remap to highlife influence on Afro-rock if available.
