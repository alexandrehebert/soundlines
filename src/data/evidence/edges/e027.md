## Evidence review

The Rotterdam oral history recalls Parkzicht DJs playing Detroit tracks and Plus 8 releases before local variants of hard techno developed. The technoculture study situates gabber in Dutch scene rivalries and the reception of several forms of imported dance music. Detroit influence is documented, while a direct derivation from Detroit techno alone remains weak.
