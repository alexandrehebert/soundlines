## Evidence review

Record Mirror’s September 1986 feature documents early Chicago dance-floor favorites by Loleatta Holloway and Sylvester and compares the emerging house beat to Hi-NRG. The Face’s contemporary coverage records reservations about equating the styles and describes Ron Hardy’s varied sets. The accounts support a shared club repertoire and audible overlap. They leave the extent of direct influence uncertain. [Record Mirror](https://www.worldradiohistory.com/UK/Record-Mirror/80s/86/Record-Mirror-1986-09-06.pdf), [The Face](https://909originals.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/house-the-face-1986.pdf)
