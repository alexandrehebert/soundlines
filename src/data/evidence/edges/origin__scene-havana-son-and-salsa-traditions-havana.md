**Finding** · Musicologist Peter Manuel’s article says Cuban son’s consolidation and many of its defining rhythmic traits developed in Havana, while noting roots in Oriente. Salsa’s later development is trans-Caribbean and especially associated with New York; the two should not be collapsed into a Havana-only salsa origin. [Peter Manuel, *Latin American Music Review*](https://academicworks.cuny.edu/jj_pubs/315/); [Oxford Academic, *Salsa Rising*](https://academic.oup.com/book/6051)

**Recommendation** · **Medium, retain with distinction.** Havana is strongly supported for son’s consolidation. Clarify that the edge maps Cuban son traditions and musical roots, not the exclusive origin of salsa.
