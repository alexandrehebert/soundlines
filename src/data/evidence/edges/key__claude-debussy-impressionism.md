**Findings** · RILM’s music encyclopedia says the term is applied above all to Debussy. Deutsche Grammophon and Royal Holloway explain that the label is a retrospective stylistic shorthand: Debussy rejected it, and it can oversimplify his musical language. Retain as conventional association with an explicit caveat.
