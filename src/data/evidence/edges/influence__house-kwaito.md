# House was a major influence that kwaito localized alongside South African musical practices; this does not reduce kwaito to a house subgenre.

Graph ID `influence:house-kwaito`

**Review status** · Source checked (2026-11-05)

**Relationship** · influences · medium

**Finding** · Kwaito is a distinct post-apartheid South African electronic dance genre that appropriated and localized imported house sounds along with local musical practices. House is a major influence, but a simple “derived from house” link overstates the lineage; prefer `influences` from house to kwaito, medium.
