## Evidence review

Trax issued the Frankie Knuckles version of “Your Love” in 1987. The independent account documents the release and Trax lists the artist in its retrospective catalog. The sources establish a release relationship. Confidence remains low for the signing claim because the sources give no terms or direct evidence of a label contract.
