**Finding** · Mexico City cultural heritage authorities trace sonidera culture to working-class neighborhoods including Tepito, Tacubaya, and Peñón de los Baños. Scholarship describes cumbia sonidera as born in Peñón de los Baños, beside Mexico City’s airport. [Mexico City Cultural Heritage](https://repospatrimonio.cultura.cdmx.gob.mx/declaratorias-de-patrimonio-cdmx/cultura-sonidera/); [*Cumbia Sonidera* study](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/25729861.2018.1497273); [INAH anthropology journal](https://revistas.inah.gob.mx/index.php/antropologia/article/view/2834)

**Recommendation** · **High for the Mexico City sonidero scene; retain.** Do not imply cumbia itself originated in Mexico City; the edge refers to the local sonidera development.
