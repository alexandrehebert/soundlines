## Evidence review

The Mulligan collection dates the sessions to 1949–1950, and the editorial notes to his oral history date Capitol’s Birth of the Cool album to 1957. The album illustrates how the LP packaged cool jazz for listeners. The source record supports this example of distribution and gives limited evidence for the format’s influence on the style itself.
