**Finding** · Research links boom-bap to sampled breaks and a turntable-derived aesthetic, while Smithsonian establishes turntablism’s hip-hop roots. Boom-bap’s production often centers samplers such as the MPC; turntables are part of its DJ/sample lineage, not necessarily the instrument used to create every track.
