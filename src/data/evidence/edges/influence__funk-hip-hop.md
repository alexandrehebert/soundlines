# Funk’s breakbeats, production, and musical vocabulary influenced hip-hop; direction is funk → hip-hop, not hip-hop derived from funk alone.

Graph ID `influence:funk-hip-hop`

**Review status** · Source checked (2026-11-05)

**Relationship** · influences · medium

**Finding** · Sources establish funk as a foundational influence on hip-hop, including sampled breaks and regional West Coast funk culture. They do not establish hip-hop as wholly derived from funk; hip-hop also has other musical and cultural roots. Prefer `influences` from funk to hip-hop rather than `derived_from` from hip-hop to funk.
