# Removed edge: anthem:orphan-s-lament-khoomei-huun-huur-tu

Removed 2026-11-05 because the exact recording/artist match or full anthem relationship is not established. Label and retailer catalogs confirm “The Orphan’s Lament” as a Huun-Huur-Tu title track. Smithsonian explains xöömei/khoomei as both a particular technique and sometimes a general term, and the ensemble is a throat-singing group; the record’s track-specific technique is not demonstrated by the label metadata. Friends of Tuva calls the album contemporary art in traditional Tuvan styles. Nothing checked supports this recording as an anthem of khoomei. Track identity is good; the full anthem relation is not.
