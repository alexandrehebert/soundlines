## Evidence review

The National Recording Registry documents Lionel Mapleson recording Metropolitan Opera performances on cylinders around 1900–1903. The Edison collection separates the 1877 tinfoil machine from later cylinder technology. This supports an indirect preservation link. The sources do not establish an effect on Romantic composition or style.
