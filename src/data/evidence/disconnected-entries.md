# Explicit connectivity exceptions

These stable IDs are retained without invented relationships. This is a
connectivity register, not a claim that every recording attribution is verified.
The integrity test requires the disconnected set to match this table exactly:
new exceptions need an explicit editorial decision, and an entry must leave the
table when a supported relationship is added.

The technology and industry events are independently sourced historical entries.
A general recording, distribution, or legal milestone does not by itself establish
an influence on a particular genre. The tracks distinguish known works or corrected
credits from unverified proposed recordings. Do not reconnect them to their former
performers merely to eliminate an isolated node.

Follow-up research on 2026-11-05 checked the original Apple launch announcements,
Smithsonian's gramophone history, the MIDI Association's first-person history,
the original rave-law text, and reporting on Napster. Recording-catalog searches
also failed to establish the proposed Hamsadhwani, Moner Manush, or The Hut
attributions. Absence from those catalogs is not proof that a recording never
existed. Retain the IDs while these explicit exceptions remain under review.
